Abert's Towhee (Melozone aberti)
================================

Description
-----------
Brown overall with rusty crown and face. Pale throat contrasts with
dark spot on belly. Compact finch-like body, black tail. Subtle coloring
blends into desert brush easily.

Behavior
--------
Ground forager scratching in leaf litter and desert scrub. Moves with
quick hops and pauses. Often stays low in dense vegetation. Usually seen
singly or in pairs year-round.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrubland, mesquite thickets, and dry washes of southwestern
Arizona and New Mexico. Prefers thick brush near water sources. Resident
year-round in its range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Towhee shares habitat but has brown crown and underparts. California
Towhee lacks rusty coloring. Green-tailed Towhee shows rufous crown and
greenish back in migrants.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for rusty cap and face contrasting with brown body. Dark spot on
pale belly is distinctive. Scratching behavior on ground characteristic.
Listen for sharp call notes in desert brush areas.
