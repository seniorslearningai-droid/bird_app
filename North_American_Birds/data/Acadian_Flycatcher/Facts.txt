Acadian Flycatcher (Empidonax virescens)
========================================

Description
-----------
Olive-green upperparts with yellowish underparts.
Pale yellowish eye-ring. Thin pointed bill adapted
for catching insects mid-flight. Small songbird
weighing only 9-12 grams.

Behavior
--------
Sits upright on perches watching for flying insects.
Sallies out quickly to snap insects from the air.
Territorial songbird with distinctive loud call.
Often returns to same perch after catching prey.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests with dense vegetation.
Favors areas near streams and swampy woodlands.
Breeds in eastern United States during spring
and summer migration season.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-bellied Flycatcher has more yellow overall.
Least Flycatcher is grayer with different eye pattern.
Willow Flycatcher resembles Acadian but has
duller coloring and different call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Listen for loud distinct "pit-zup" call.
Note olive-green back with pale eye-ring.
Watch for active insect-catching behavior.
Observe thin pointed bill and upright posture.
