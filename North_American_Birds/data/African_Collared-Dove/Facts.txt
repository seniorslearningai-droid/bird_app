African Collared-Dove (Streptopelia roseogrisea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium pale dove. Very pale sandy-pink body with
thin black hindneck collar. White undertail coverts.
About 11 inches. Origin of domestic Ringneck Dove.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds and grain on ground. Ancestor of
domestic Ringneck Dove. Feral birds found in
Florida and other warm US areas.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub and open areas in native Africa.
In North America found as feral birds in Florida,
occasionally elsewhere in the South.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eurasian Collared-Dove very similar but larger and
darker. African paler and more delicate.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very pale sandy-white plumage with thin black neck
collar. Smaller and paler than Eurasian
Collared-Dove. Found in Florida.
