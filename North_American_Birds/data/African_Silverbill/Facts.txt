African Silverbill (Euodice cantans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch, pale sandy-brown above, white below,
pointed tail, pale silvery
bill. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious in flocks. Nests colonially. Forages
for grass seeds. Introduced
to Hawaii and other areas.

Habitat
-------
Dry grasslands, scrub, and open areas. Introduced
to Hawaii and Florida.
Native to Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indian Silverbill similar but darker brown. Munias
have heavier bills and
different tail shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale sandy-brown with silvery bill and pointed tail.
Tiny size. Gregarious
in flocks. Hawaii introduced.
