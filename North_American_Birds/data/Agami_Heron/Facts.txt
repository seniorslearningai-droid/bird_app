Agami Heron (Agamia agami)
==========================

Description
-----------
A stunning medium-large heron with deep chestnut and green plumage,
a bright blue and white neck, and a long, sharp dagger-like bill.

Behavior
--------
Stands motionless in shallow water or on riverbanks, then strikes
rapidly to catch fish. Solitary and secretive, rarely seen in open areas.

Habitat
-------
Dense tropical rainforest streams, swamps, and slow-moving rivers
from southern Mexico through Central America to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other heron shares its vivid chestnut, green, and blue coloration.
Juvenile birds are brown and streaked, resembling immature night-herons.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for the brilliant blue-white neck, chestnut body, and extremely
long bill. Its preference for shaded forest streams makes it distinctive.
