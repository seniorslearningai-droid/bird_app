Ainley's Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates cheimomnestes)
================================================

Description
-----------
A small, all-dark sooty-brown storm-petrel with a slightly forked tail
and relatively short, rounded wings. Nearly identical to Leach's Storm-Petrel.

Behavior
--------
Flies low over the ocean with erratic, bounding wingbeats. Breeds in
winter on rocky island cliffs, returning to colonies only at night.

Habitat
-------
Open Pacific Ocean waters, breeding exclusively on Guadalupe Island
off Baja California, Mexico. Critically endangered with roughly 500 individuals.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Almost identical to Leach's Storm-Petrel and Townsend's Storm-Petrel.
Best separated by breeding season and subtle differences in wing shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely difficult to identify at sea. Breeds in winter (unlike Leach's),
and shows a slightly less prominent white rump patch with dark central line.
