Akekee (Loxops caeruleirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, yellow-green with black
mask, crossed bill tips. Blue-gray
bill. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Uses crossed bill tips to open flower buds for
nectar and insects. Critically
endangered on Kauai.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Kauai above 3,500 feet.
Very small population remaining.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kauai Amakihi lacks black mask and crossed bill.
No other bird has
bill tips crossing in Hawaii.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black mask, yellow-green body, crossed bill tips.
Only on Kauai native forest.
Critically endangered.
