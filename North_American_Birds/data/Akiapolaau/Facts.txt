Akiapolaau (Hemignathus wilsoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper with unique bill: short
straight lower mandible for
hammering, long curved upper mandible.

Behavior
--------
Uses lower mandible to hammer wood, upper bill
to probe for insects. Critically
endangered on Big Island.

Habitat
-------
Native koa and ohia forests of the Big Island
above 4,000 feet. Very few
individuals remain.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird anywhere has this unique two-part
bill function. Distinctive in
any Hawaiian forest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unique bill: short lower mandible plus long curved
upper. Yellow male. Big Island
koa forest. Critically endangered.
