Akikiki (Oreomystis bairdi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gray-and-white Hawaiian honeycreeper,
4.5 inches long. Pale gray back,
white underparts, pinkish bill.

Behavior
--------
Acrobatic bark creeper foraging for insects.
Tame, often in pairs. Critically
endangered — fewer than 50 remain.

Habitat
-------
Found only on Kauaʻi above 3,500 feet
in native wet forests. Depends on
ʻōhiʻa and koa trees.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Could confuse with bright yellow-green Anianiau
or Kauaʻi ʻAmakihi. Japanese White-eyes
have distinctive white eye-ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Watch for creeping along bark.
Gray-and-white plumage, no bold markings.
High elevation and Kauaʻi location key.
Listen for soft high-pitched calls.
