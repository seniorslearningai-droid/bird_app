Akohekohe (Palmeria dolei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian honeycreeper, dark with orange nape,
white-tipped feathers throughout,
white curly crest. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds primarily on ohia nectar. Critically endangered.
Restricted to high-elevation
forests of east Maui.

Habitat
-------
Native wet ohia forests of east Maui above 4,500
feet. One of most endangered
birds in United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in range. White curly crest and
orange nape unique. Dark
plumage with white tips.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White curly crest and orange nape unique. Dark body
with white speckles. High
Maui forest. Critically endangered.
