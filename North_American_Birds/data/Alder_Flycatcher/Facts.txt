Alder Flycatcher (Empidonax alnorum)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Small olive-green flycatcher with whitish underparts, two white wing
bars, and a faint eye ring. Very similar to Willow Flycatcher.

Behavior
--------
Perches upright on shrub branches, sallying out to catch insects.
Males sing a distinctive "fee-BEE-o" song from exposed perches.

Habitat
-------
Wet thickets of alder and willow along streams, lake edges, and
bogs across northern North America; winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nearly identical to Willow Flycatcher. Also resembles other Empidonax
flycatchers. Voice is the only reliable field distinction.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Listen for the "fee-BEE-o" song. Habitat in wet alder/willow thickets
helps confirm. Eye ring slightly more pronounced than Willow Flycatcher.
