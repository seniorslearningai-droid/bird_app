Aleutian Tern (Onychoprion aleuticus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern. Gray above, white below, black cap
with white forehead, black bill. Very similar
to Arctic Tern in shape. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for small fish. Breeds in Alaska. Migrates
to western Pacific; winters in Southeast Asia.
Migration route poorly known.

Habitat
-------
Coastal Alaska. Breeds on beaches and tundra near
coast. Migrates west across Pacific. Winters
in Southeast Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Arctic Tern very similar. Aleutian Tern has gray
body and black bill (not red).

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body (not white like Arctic Tern). Black
bill. Breeds only in Alaska. Distinct
white forehead patch.
