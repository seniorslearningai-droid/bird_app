Allen's Hummingbird (Selasphorus sasin)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Tiny hummingbird with iridescent green back, metallic red crown, and golden-green underparts.
Males have brilliant red gorget. Females duller with red throat speckles. Length: 3.3 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly aggressive and territorial. Males perform elaborate dive displays producing loud
chirping sounds with tail feathers. Rapid wing beats (40-50 per second) create humming sound.
Feeds on flower nectar.

Habitat
-------
Chaparral, oak woodlands, gardens, and coastal scrub. Prefers areas with flowering plants.
Year-round resident in coastal California. Altitudinal migrant in inland regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rufous Hummingbird (reddish overall), Calliope Hummingbird (smaller with purple gorget),
Costa's Hummingbird (purple crown and gorget). Ruby-throated Hummingbird (eastern range).

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for iridescent red-orange gorget on male. Bright metallic green back distinguishes from
Rufous. Listen for territorial dive display sounds. Females have red spots on throat. Fast,
direct flight pattern.
