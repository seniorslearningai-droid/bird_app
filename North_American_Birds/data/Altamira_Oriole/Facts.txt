Altamira Oriole (Icterus gularis)
==================================

Description
-----------
The largest North American oriole. Brilliant orange body with black
back, wings, and throat. Distinctive orange shoulder patch on wing.

Behavior
--------
Weaves a remarkably long hanging nest (up to 25 inches). Forages
in trees for insects, nectar, and fruit. Often seen in pairs.

Habitat
-------
Tropical woodlands, forest edges, and riparian areas from southern
Texas through Central America. Common in Rio Grande Valley.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Resembles Hooded Oriole but is significantly larger and has an orange
shoulder patch. Audubon's Oriole has a black head, not throat patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for the large orange shoulder patch and massive size. The black
throat combined with all-orange body is distinctive among Texas orioles.
