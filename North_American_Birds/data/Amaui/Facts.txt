Amaui (Myadestes woahensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian thrush, brown-gray plumage. Known
only from Oahu. Extinct since
approximately 1825.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for fruit in native Hawaiian forest.
Extinct before scientific study
could fully document its behavior.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu, Hawaii. Extinct due
to deforestation and hunting
following Western contact.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Hawaiian Myadestes thrushes similar. All
extinct or endangered.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species from Oahu. No specimens with
complete description. Known from
subfossil remains and early accounts.
