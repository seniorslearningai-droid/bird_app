Amazon Kingfisher (Chloroceryle amazona)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Large kingfisher with dark green upperparts and white underparts.
Male has a broad chestnut breast band; female has green-spotted flanks.

Behavior
--------
Perches over water, plunging headfirst to catch fish. Often solitary.
Gives a loud, rattling call. Territorial along riverbanks.

Habitat
-------
Rivers, streams, lakes, and lagoons in tropical lowlands from Mexico
through Central and South America. Prefers larger bodies of water.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Similar to Green Kingfisher but much larger — nearly twice the size.
Ringed Kingfisher is larger still and has rufous on both sexes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size distinguishes it from Green Kingfisher. Male's chestnut
breast band and female's spotted flanks are key field marks.
