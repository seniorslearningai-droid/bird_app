American Barn Owl (Tyto furcata)
=================================

Description
-----------
Distinctive heart-shaped white facial disc, tawny and gray mottled
upperparts, and pale white to buff underparts with fine dark spots.

Behavior
--------
Strictly nocturnal hunter that locates prey by sound alone in
total darkness. Gives eerie screeching calls instead of hooting.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, farmland, marshes, and woodland edges. Nests in
barns, tree cavities, and cliff ledges across North and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American owl has the heart-shaped white face. Barn
Owls worldwide are similar but this species is unique in the Americas.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Heart-shaped white face is unmistakable. Golden-buff back and white
underparts visible in flight. Active after dark near open farmland.
