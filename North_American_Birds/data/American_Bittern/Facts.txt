American Bittern (Botaurus lentiginosus)
========================================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized heron with warm tan-brown and black streaking.
Yellowish-green legs, pale throat with dark stripe on neck.
Yellow-green facial skin around eye. Stocky build.

Behavior
--------
Solitary, secretive bird that hunts in marshes from dawn to dusk.
Makes distinctive "thunder-pump" booming call in breeding season.
Freezes with neck extended vertically when threatened.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater and brackish marshes with dense cattails and reeds.
Requires areas with standing water and tall emergent vegetation.
Prefers large reedbeds away from human disturbance.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Young Night-Heron has different body shape and neck pattern.
Least Bittern is much smaller and more delicate.
Immature herons lack the distinctive dark stripe on neck.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for cryptic brown streaking and stocky heron shape.
Listen for booming call in spring breeding season.
Watch for characteristic freezing posture with extended neck.
