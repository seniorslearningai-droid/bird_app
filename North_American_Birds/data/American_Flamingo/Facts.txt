American Flamingo (Phoenicopterus ruber)
========================================

Description
-----------
Brilliant pink plumage with long curved neck, stilt-like pink legs,
and a distinctive downward-bent black-tipped bill unique among birds.

Behavior
--------
Feeds by sweeping its inverted bill through shallow water, filtering
algae and crustaceans. Nests in dense colonies on mudflats.

Habitat
-------
Coastal lagoons, salt pans, and mudflats in the Caribbean and
extreme southern Florida. Requires warm, shallow alkaline waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Roseate Spoonbill is also pink but has a flat spoon-shaped bill and
lacks the flamingo's long neck and distinctive kinked beak.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unmistakable brilliant pink color, extreme leg and neck length,
and unique bent bill visible at rest or while wading to feed.
