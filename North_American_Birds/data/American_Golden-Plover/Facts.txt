American Golden-Plover (Pluvialis dominica)
===========================================

Description
-----------
Breeding adults have striking black face and underparts with
golden-speckled brown upperparts. Non-breeding birds are brownish
with pale eyebrow and golden spots on back.

Behavior
--------
One of the longest migrants in North America, flying from Arctic
tundra to South American grasslands. Runs along ground to forage.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Migrates through prairies and coastal
mudflats. Winters on pampas grasslands in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-bellied Plover is larger with gray (not golden) upperpart
spots and shows white rump in flight. Pacific Golden-Plover is
nearly identical; best separated by call and primary projection.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Golden-spotted back, slim build, small bill. Black belly and face
in breeding plumage is diagnostic. Look for primary tips extending
past tail tip at rest.
