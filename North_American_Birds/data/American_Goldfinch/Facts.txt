American Goldfinch (Spinus tristis)
===================================

Description
-----------
Small finch with bright yellow plumage in breeding season. Males display vibrant
yellow with black wings and tail. Females and winter birds are duller olive-brown.
White wing bars and markings are distinctive year-round.

Behavior
--------
Acrobatic fliers that often perform undulating flight patterns while calling.
Highly social, forming flocks especially in winter. They feed on seeds and
remain in groups while foraging and traveling.

Habitat
-------
Open fields, meadows, and areas with thistle, dandelion, and weedy growth.
Orchards and gardens attract them when flowers and seeds are abundant. They
prefer semi-open areas with scattered trees and shrubs.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Female Orioles can be confused with female Goldfinches. Pine Siskins are
smaller and streakier. Lesser Goldfinches have white wing patches. Warblers
may appear similar in non-breeding plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Listen for distinctive "per-chick-o-ree" call. Males in breeding season are
unmistakable bright yellow. Look for white wing bars on all birds. Undulating
flight with wings briefly closed is characteristic behavior.
