American Goshawk (Accipiter atricapillus)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Large powerful hawk with slate-gray upperparts, finely gray-barred
pale underparts, bold white eyebrow stripe, and vivid red-orange eyes.

Behavior
--------
Aggressive forest hunter pursuing birds and mammals at high speed
through dense trees. Fiercely defends nest, sometimes striking humans.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in mature boreal and montane conifer forests. Winters at lower
elevations; may irrupt into settled areas when prey populations crash.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cooper's Hawk is smaller with a rounded tail and less bold eyebrow.
Sharp-shinned Hawk is much smaller. Both lack the goshawk's size
and prominent white supercilium.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large accipiter size, bold white eyebrow, red eyes, and heavily
barred chest. Broad rounded wings and long tail visible in flight
through forest canopy.
