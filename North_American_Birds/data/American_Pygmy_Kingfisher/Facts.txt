American Pygmy Kingfisher (Chloroceryle aenea)
===============================================

Description
-----------
Tiny kingfisher with dark metallic green upperparts, rich rufous-orange
breast and flanks, white belly, and a small dagger-shaped bill.

Behavior
--------
Perches motionless on low branches over small forest streams and pools,
plunging headfirst to catch tiny fish, tadpoles, and aquatic insects.

Habitat
-------
Dense tropical lowland forest streams, mangrove edges, and shaded
woodland pools from Mexico through Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Green Kingfisher is noticeably larger with a heavier bill and more
white spotting. Amazon Kingfisher is much larger with a bigger crest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely small size is the best clue — sparrow-sized. Rufous
breast contrasts with green back. Found over shaded, narrow forest
streams rather than open water.
