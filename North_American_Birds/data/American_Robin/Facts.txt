American Robin (Turdus migratorius)
===================================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized thrush with brick-red breast and upper abdomen. Gray-brown back,
white eye-ring, and distinctive white spots under tail. Males have darker head
and brighter coloring than females. Bill is yellow.

Behavior
--------
Hops along the ground searching for invertebrates. Often seen in a characteristic
upright posture. Loud, cheerful warbling song. Highly territorial during breeding
season. Forms flocks in migration and winter.

Habitat
-------
Found in open woodlands, parks, gardens, and urban areas. Breeds in forest edges
and clearings. Prefers areas with short grass for foraging and shrubs for nesting.
Highly adaptable to human-modified landscapes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Towhee has reddish flanks but black back and hood. Orchard Oriole females
are yellowish-green. Varied Thrush has orange wing bars and darker appearance in
western regions.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for rusty-red breast and gray-brown back combination. Distinctive yellow bill
and eye-ring. Hopping behavior on ground is characteristic. Listen for loud, clear
warbling song. White spots under tail visible in flight.
