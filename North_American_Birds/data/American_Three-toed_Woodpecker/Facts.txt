American Three-toed Woodpecker (Picoides dorsalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium woodpecker with black-and-white barred back,
yellow crown on male. Only three toes.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Scales bark off dead trees to reach beetle larvae.
Boreal and mountain forest specialist.
More deliberate feeding than other woodpeckers.

Habitat
-------
Boreal spruce-fir forest and high mountain conifers.
Often found in recently burned
forest areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-backed Woodpecker similar but has solid black
back. American Three-toed has barred back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Barred black-and-white back. Yellow crown patch on male.
Three toes. Boreal and mountain
spruce-fir forest.
