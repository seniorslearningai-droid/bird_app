Amethyst-throated Mountain-gem (Lampornis amethystinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Male green above with amethyst-
purple throat patch and white stripe behind eye.
Female green above, cinnamon below. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar in mountain cloud forests. Rare vagrant
to Arizona from Mexico. Typically forages
at middle height in forest.

Habitat
-------
Cloud forests and pine-oak mountain forests of Mexico.
Very rare vagrant to mountain
canyons of southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-throated Mountain-gem similar habitat. Amethyst-
throated has purple throat and white supercilium.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's amethyst-purple throat patch and white stripe
behind eye. Green back. Very rare
vagrant in Arizona mountains.
