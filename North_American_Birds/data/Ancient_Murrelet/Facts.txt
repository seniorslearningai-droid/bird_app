Ancient Murrelet (Synthliboramphus antiquus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small alcid. Black head and throat, gray back,
white underparts, white streaks on head in breeding.
About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in burrows on islands. Chicks leave at night
and swim to sea with parents. Migrates south in
winter. Winters off Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Pacific coast islands from Alaska south.
Winters offshore from Alaska to California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small Pacific alcids. Black and gray pattern
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray back with black head and throat. White head
streaks in breeding. Small alcid of
Pacific coast.
