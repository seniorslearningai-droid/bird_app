Anianiau (Magumma parva)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny Hawaiian honeycreeper, bright yellow below, olive-yellow
above, short slightly
curved bill. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active nectar feeder in ohia and other flowers. One
of smallest Hawaiian honeycreepers.
Declining on Kauai.

Habitat
-------
Native wet and mesic forests of Kauai. Declining
but still present above
3,000 feet.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kauai Amakihi larger with longer curved bill. Anianiau
smallest yellow honeycreeper
on Kauai.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny bright yellow bird in Kauai native forest.
Short slightly curved bill.
Smallest honeycreeper on Kauai.
