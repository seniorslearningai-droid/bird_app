Anna's Hummingbird (Calypte anna)
==================================

Description
-----------
Small hummingbird with iridescent rose-pink to magenta head and throat in males;
females and juveniles are green above with grayish-white below and spotted throat.

Behavior
--------
Year-round resident; males perform dramatic diving courtship displays
producing loud popping sounds with tail feathers; aggressive defenders of feeders.

Habitat
-------
Gardens, parks, open woodlands, and chaparral along the Pacific Coast
from Baja California to British Columbia; often near flowering plants.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Costa's Hummingbird has purple gorget extending to side tufts; Rufous Hummingbird
males are orange-rufous rather than deep rose-magenta.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rose-magenta crown and gorget on males; larger size than most western hummingbirds;
metallic chirping song; present year-round on the West Coast.
