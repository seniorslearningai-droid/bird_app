Antillean Nighthawk (Chordeiles gundlachii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium nighthawk with cryptically patterned brown,
black and buff plumage. White throat patch,
white wing bar in flight. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aerial insect catcher; forages at dusk and dawn.
Breeds in Florida Keys; winters in South America.
Call is distinctive "pilly-will-o-willy."

Habitat
-------
Open rocky areas, rooftops, and scrubby habitat
in Florida Keys and Caribbean islands. Winters
in northern South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Nighthawk nearly identical. Antillean best
separated by call and range in Florida Keys.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Best identified by distinctive "pilly-will-o-willy" call.
Nearly identical to Common Nighthawk visually.
Breeds in Florida Keys only.
