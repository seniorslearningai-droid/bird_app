Antillean Palm Swift (Tachornis phoenicobia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny swift. Dark above with white rump, white
flanks and belly. Very small size and deeply
forked tail. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in palm fronds in Caribbean. Fast twinkling
flight while foraging for aerial insects.
Rare vagrant to Florida from Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Found wherever palms grow in Caribbean islands,
especially Cuba, Hispaniola, and Jamaica. Rare
vagrant to southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small swifts. White rump and flanks distinguish
Antillean Palm Swift.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size with white rump, white flanks, and dark
back. Deeply forked tail. Extremely rare
vagrant in southern Florida.
