Apapane (Himatione sanguinea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, brilliant crimson-red
with black wings and tail,
white undertail. Curved bill. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active nectar feeder in ohia trees. The most abundant
native Hawaiian bird. Chatters
and sings from treetops.

Habitat
-------
Native ohia forests throughout Hawaiian Islands from
low to high elevations.
Most common native Hawaiian bird.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Iiwi larger with long curved salmon-pink bill.
Female not as brightly
colored but same pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant crimson-red with black wings. White undertail.
Curved bill. Abundant in
ohia flowers throughout Hawaii.
