Aplomado Falcon (Falco femoralis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant medium falcon, long tail, gray above with
distinctive facial pattern. Rufous belly
band, white below with dark flanks. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Reintroduced in southern Texas; hunts birds in open
grassland. Fast agile pursuit predator.

Habitat
-------
Open grassland and coastal prairies. Reintroduced population
in southern Texas; also Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Peregrine Falcon heavier without rufous belly band.
Aplomado Falcon's distinctive face pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive black-and-white face pattern with cream
supercilium. Rufous belly flanks. Elegant
long-tailed falcon.
