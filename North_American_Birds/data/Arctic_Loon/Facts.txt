Arctic Loon (Gavia arctica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium loon, gray head in breeding, black and white
checkered back, distinctive white flank
patch. About 27 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish in deep water. Breeds on Eurasian
and western Alaska tundra lakes.
Winters on Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Tundra lakes in Eurasia and western Alaska. Winters
primarily on Pacific coast, especially
in Alaska waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pacific Loon very similar. Arctic Loon has gray head
contrasting with black throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray crown and hindneck contrasting with black throat.
White flank patch visible on water.
Pacific coast in winter.
