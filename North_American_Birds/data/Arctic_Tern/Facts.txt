Arctic Tern (Sterna paradisaea)
================================

Description
-----------
Slender seabird with pale gray body, white cheeks, blood-red bill and legs,
black cap, and deeply forked tail; underparts whitish with translucent wingtips.

Behavior
--------
Performs the longest migration of any animal — from Arctic breeding grounds
to Antarctic wintering areas and back, traveling up to 70,000 miles yearly.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra and rocky coastlines; winters at sea near Antarctica;
found over open ocean during migration along both coasts and offshore.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Tern has orange-red bill with dark tip and less translucent wingtips;
Forster's Tern has frostier wing tips and is only found in North America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-red bill (no dark tip), translucent wingtip "windows" from below,
very long tail streamers, and buoyant graceful flight low over water.
