Arctic Warbler (Phylloscopus borealis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium leaf warbler, olive-green above, whitish
below with single wing bar, long
supercilium. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in willow scrub in Alaska; migrates across
Asia. Only Phylloscopus warbler
breeding in North America.

Habitat
-------
Willow and alder thickets in western and northern
Alaska. Winters in Southeast
Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Willow Warbler lacks wing bar. Yellow-browed Warbler
has two wing bars.
Kamchatka Warbler very similar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Single wing bar in Alaska breeding habitat. Long
supercilium, olive-green above.
Only breeder of its genus in Alaska.
