Arizona Woodpecker (Leuconotopicus arizonae)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Small woodpecker with solid brown back (unique among North American woodpeckers).
White underparts with dark spots. Males have red nape patch; females lack it.

Behavior
--------
Forages on bark of oak and pine trees searching for insects. Drums on dead
wood. Pairs stay together year-round in their territory.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak woodlands in mountain canyons of southeastern Arizona and New Mexico.
Found at elevations of 4,000–7,000 feet. Closely associated with Arizona sycamore.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ladder-backed Woodpecker has barred black-and-white back. Hairy Woodpecker
has white back stripe. No other woodpecker has solid brown back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Solid brown back is diagnostic — no other North American woodpecker has this.
Look in pine-oak canyons of southeastern Arizona. Listen for sharp 'peek' call.
