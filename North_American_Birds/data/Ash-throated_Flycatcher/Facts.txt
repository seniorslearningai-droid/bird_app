Ash-throated Flycatcher (Myiarchus cinerascens)
================================================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher with pale gray-white throat and breast, brownish-gray back,
rusty-red tail, and two faint whitish wing bars; belly faintly yellowish.

Behavior
--------
Catches insects by sallying from a perch; nests in tree cavities and nest boxes;
vocal with a variety of harsh "ka-brick" and rolling calls.

Habitat
-------
Dry open woodlands, desert scrub, and chaparral of the western United States
and Mexico; often near oaks or mesquite.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown-crested Flycatcher is larger with stronger yellow belly; Great Crested
Flycatcher is brighter yellow below and found in the East.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale ashy-white throat, rusty tail panels visible in flight, and dry open
western habitat; distinctive harsh "ka-brick" call.
