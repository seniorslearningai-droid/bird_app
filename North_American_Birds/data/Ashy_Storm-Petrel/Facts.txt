Ashy Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates homochroa)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Small, all-dark sooty brown seabird with forked tail. Pale gray wing linings
visible in flight. Slender build, fluttering wingbeats. About 8 inches long.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on small fish and crustaceans at sea surface, pattering feet on water.
Nests in rock crevices on offshore islands. Nocturnal at breeding colonies.

Habitat
-------
Pelagic waters of the California Current, mainly offshore California and Baja.
Breeds on Channel Islands and Farallon Islands. Rare vagrant elsewhere.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Storm-Petrel is larger with slower wingbeats. Least Storm-Petrel is
smaller with wedge-shaped tail. Leach's Storm-Petrel has white rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-dark coloring, forked tail, and small size in California offshore waters.
Pale wing linings visible in good light. Rapid, fluttery flight pattern.
