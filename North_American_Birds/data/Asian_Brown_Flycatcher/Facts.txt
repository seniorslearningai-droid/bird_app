Asian Brown Flycatcher (Muscicapa dauurica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, gray-brown above, whitish below
with grayish-buff wash on sides.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from exposed perch for flying insects.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Woodland and forest edges of Siberia and East
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray-streaked Flycatcher has distinct breast streaking.
Dark-sided has darker sides.
Spotted Flycatcher streaked above.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain gray-brown above, pale below with buff wash
on sides. No streaking.
Very rare Alaska vagrant.
