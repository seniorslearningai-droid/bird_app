Asian Rosy-Finch (Leucosticte arctoa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch, brown with pink wing patches and
rump, gray crown. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages at snowfields and rocky alpine terrain for
seeds. Very rare vagrant to
western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Alpine and arctic terrain of Siberia and Asia. Very
rare vagrant to Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black, Brown, and Gray-crowned Rosy-Finches all similar.
Pink wing patches shared
with all rosy-finches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown body with pink wing patches and rump. Gray
crown. Very rare Alaska
vagrant from Siberia.
