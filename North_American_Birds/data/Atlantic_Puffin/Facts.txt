Atlantic Puffin (Fratercula arctica)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Stocky seabird with striking black-and-white plumage; breeding birds have
colorful triangular bill banded orange, yellow, and blue-gray; orange feet.

Behavior
--------
Dives underwater to catch small fish, carrying multiple fish crosswise in bill;
nests in burrows on rocky islands; silent at sea, grunting at colonies.

Habitat
-------
Open North Atlantic Ocean when not breeding; nests on rocky islands and sea
cliffs from Maine and Maritime Canada to northern Europe and Iceland.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Razorbill has a thicker black bill; Common Murre is slimmer with no colorful
bill; Tufted Puffin has golden head tufts and is found in the Pacific.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Clown-like colorful triangular bill, round body, black back with white face,
and orange feet; seen flying low and fast over Atlantic waters.
