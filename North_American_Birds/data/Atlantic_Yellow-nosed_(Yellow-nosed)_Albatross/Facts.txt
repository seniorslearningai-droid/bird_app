Atlantic Yellow-nosed Albatross (Thalassarche chlororhynchos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium albatross, dark gray-black back and wings, white
body and head, black bill with yellow
ridge and red tip. About 32 inches.

Behavior
--------
Long-ranging pelagic seabird. Follows ships. Rare
vagrant to North Atlantic. Breeds on
South Atlantic islands.

Habitat
-------
South Atlantic Ocean. Rare vagrant to North Atlantic
coast; occasionally found off northeastern
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-browed Albatross similar but has yellow-orange
bill. Yellow-nosed has thin yellow bill ridge.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Thin yellow ridge on black bill with red tip. White
head and underparts. Medium albatross
in the North Atlantic.
