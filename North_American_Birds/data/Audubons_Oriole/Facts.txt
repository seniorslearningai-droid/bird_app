Audubon's Oriole (Icterus graduacauda)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large oriole, yellow-green body, black head, wings,
and tail. White wing bars.
About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages quietly in dense thorn scrub. Secretive for
an oriole. Resident in south
Texas brush country.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub and mesquite woodland of the
Rio Grande Valley and south
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scott's Oriole brighter yellow with different range.
No other yellow-green oriole
in Texas thorn scrub.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow-green body with all-black head and tail. Secretive
in dense Texas thorn
scrub. Resident species.
