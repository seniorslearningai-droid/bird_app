Audubon's Warbler (Setophaga coronata auduboni)
================================================

Description
-----------
Yellow-rumped Warbler subspecies with yellow throat (not white). Males in
breeding plumage show yellow crown, rump, and sides. Gray back with streaks.

Behavior
--------
Active forager catching insects in flight and gleaning from foliage. Forms
large flocks in winter. Eats waxy myrtle berries when insects scarce.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests in western North America during breeding season.
Winters in open woodlands, shrublands, and coastal areas. Very adaptable.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Myrtle Warbler (eastern Yellow-rumped) has white throat. Townsend's Warbler
has yellow face with dark cheek patch. Cape May Warbler has chestnut cheeks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow throat combined with yellow rump is key. White wing patch visible in
flight. Western range separates from Myrtle Warbler. Chip call is sharp.
