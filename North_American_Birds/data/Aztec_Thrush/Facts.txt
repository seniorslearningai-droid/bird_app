Aztec Thrush (Ridgwayia pinicola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, dark brown above, white below with
bold brown spotting on breast,
white wing patches. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for fruit in pine-oak forests. Rare but
regular vagrant to Arizona and
Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forests of the Sierra Madre Occidental
in Mexico. Rare vagrant to
Arizona and west Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin lacks bold white wing patches. Spotted
thrushes smaller and more
slender without wing patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing patches obvious in flight. Spotted breast.
Dark brown above. Rare
Arizona vagrant from Mexico.
