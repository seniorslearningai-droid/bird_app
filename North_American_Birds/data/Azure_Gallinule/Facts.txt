Azure Gallinule (Porphyrio flavirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gallinule, pale blue with white underparts,
yellow bill, pink legs.
About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on floating vegetation. Very rare vagrant to
eastern North America from
South America.

Habitat
-------
Floating marshes and grasslands of South America.
Very rare vagrant to eastern
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Gallinule more colorful with red-and-yellow
bill. Common Moorhen darker.
Pale blue color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale blue with white underparts and yellow bill. Very
rare South American vagrant.
Small gallinule.
