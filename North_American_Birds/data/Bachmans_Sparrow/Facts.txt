Bachman's Sparrow (Peucaea aestivalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, rusty-streaked above, plain buff
below, gray face, long rounded
tail. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings beautiful, complex song from low perch. Extremely
secretive otherwise. Open
pine woodland specialist.

Habitat
-------
Open pine woodland with grass understory in southeastern
United States. Declining due
to habitat loss.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Botteri's Sparrow plainer above without rusty streaks.
Cassin's Sparrow in western
grasslands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty streaks on gray-brown back. Plain buff below.
Open pine woodland. Beautiful
clear whistled song.
