Bachman's Warbler (Vermivora bachmanii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler. Male has black cap and bib, yellow
face and underparts, olive back.
Female greenish. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dense swamp vegetation. Possibly extinct;
last confirmed in early 1960s.
One of rarest North American birds.

Habitat
-------
Bottomland swamp forests of southeastern United States.
Critically endangered or extinct.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hooded Warbler has complete hood not partial. Wilson's
Warbler smaller. Yellow with
black cap and bib.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Possibly extinct. Male yellow with black cap and
bib. Bottomland swamp. Last
confirmed record early 1960s.
