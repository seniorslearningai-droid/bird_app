Bahama Mockingbird (Mimus gundlachii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large mockingbird, gray-brown above with streaked
sides, white underparts with streaking.
Two white wing bars. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and aggressive, defending territory vigorously.
Rare visitor to Florida Keys
and south Florida from Bahamas.

Habitat
-------
Thickets and scrub in the Bahamas. Rare visitor
to Florida Keys and extreme
southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Mockingbird lacks streaking on sides and
has more white in wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked sides and flanks distinguish from plain
Northern Mockingbird. Florida Keys
vagrant. Larger overall.
