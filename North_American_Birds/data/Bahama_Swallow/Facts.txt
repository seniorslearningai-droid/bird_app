Bahama Swallow (Tachycineta cyaneoviridis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium swallow with iridescent blue-green back, pure
white underparts, deeply forked tail.
About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages over open areas and pine forests. Rare
visitor to southern Florida.
Nests in tree cavities.

Habitat
-------
Pine forests of the Bahamas. Rare but regular
visitor to Florida Keys and
southern tip of Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tree Swallow similar but less deeply forked tail
and different range. Cliff
Swallow has rusty rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pure white below, deeply forked tail. Iridescent
blue-green above. Florida Keys
visitor from Bahamas.
