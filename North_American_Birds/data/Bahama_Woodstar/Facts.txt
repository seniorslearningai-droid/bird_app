Bahama Woodstar (Nesophlox evelynae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small hummingbird from Bahamas. Male has iridescent
rose-purple gorget, green back, white belly with
rufous flanks. Female greenish with spotted throat. About 3.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar from various flowers. Found in
Bahamas; rare vagrant to Florida. Makes
seasonal movements among Bahamian islands.

Habitat
-------
Scrub forest, gardens, and woodland in the Bahamas.
Rare visitor to southern Florida, particularly
the Florida Keys.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruby-throated Hummingbird similar size. Male Bahama
Woodstar's rose gorget and rufous flanks distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's rose-purple gorget and rufous sides distinctive.
Female's spotted throat. Very rare
vagrant in southern Florida.
