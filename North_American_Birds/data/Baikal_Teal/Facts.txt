Baikal Teal (Sibirionetta formosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dabbling duck. Male has intricate mosaic head
pattern: green, buff, black and white panels. Female
brown with white spot near bill base. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles for seeds and aquatic plants in shallow water.
Forms massive flocks in East Asia during migration.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberian taiga wetlands and tundra marshes.
Winters in East Asian lakes, wetlands, and
flooded rice paddies.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Garganey similar size and shape. Female could
be confused with other female dabbling ducks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's complex multi-colored head pattern unique in
North America. Female's white facial spot
at bill base and dark eye line distinctive.
