Baird's Sandpiper (Calidris bairdii)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Medium-small sandpiper with buffy-brown upperparts, scaly back pattern, and
whitish underparts with light breast streaking. Long wingtips extend past tail.
Slender, horizontal posture. About 7.5 inches long.

Behavior
--------
Forages on dry upland areas and mudflats picking invertebrates. Long-distance
migrant traveling from Arctic to South America. Often in small flocks inland.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra breeder. Migrates through Great Plains and mountain meadows.
Prefers dry, short-grass areas near water. Winters on South American grasslands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-rumped Sandpiper also has long wingtips but has white rump. Pectoral
Sandpiper is larger with sharp breast band. Least Sandpiper has yellow legs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long wingtips projecting well past tail tip — key feature. Buffy-brown scaly
back. Slender horizontal posture. Dark legs. Migrates through interior plains.
