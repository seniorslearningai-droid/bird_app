Baird's Sparrow (Centronyx bairdii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, ochre-orange head with narrow streaks,
necklace of streaks on breast,
flat head. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely secretive in grass, rarely flushing. Walks
on ground under dense grass.
Declining rapidly.

Habitat
-------
Native mixed-grass and short-grass prairie of northern
Great Plains. Declining due
to grassland loss.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Savannah Sparrow has bolder supercilium. Henslow's
Sparrow olive-green head.
Orange head with necklace streaks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Ochre-orange head with fine dark streaks. Necklace
of breast streaks. Northern
Great Plains grassland specialist.
