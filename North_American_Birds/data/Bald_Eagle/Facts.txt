Bald Eagle (Haliaeetus leucocephalus)
======================================

Description
-----------
Large raptor with distinctive white head and tail contrasting with dark brown
body; bright yellow hooked bill and talons; wingspan up to 8 feet.

Behavior
--------
Soars on flat wings; plucks fish from water with talons; steals food from other
birds (kleptoparasitism); builds massive stick nests reused for many years.

Habitat
-------
Near large bodies of open water with abundant fish — coasts, rivers, lakes,
and reservoirs; nests in tall trees or on cliffs near water.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Immature Bald Eagles are mottled brown; Osprey is smaller with crooked wings
and a dark eye stripe; Golden Eagle has golden nape and darker tail base.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White head and tail on brown body (adults); flat-winged soar; massive size;
yellow bill; found near water throughout North America.
