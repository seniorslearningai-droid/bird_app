Bananaquit (Coereba flaveola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny bird, black above with bold white supercilium,
bright yellow below, white wing
patch. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pierces flower bases with sharp bill for nectar.
Very active and bold. Common
Caribbean species.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges, gardens, and flowering trees. Established
in southern Florida. Common
throughout Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird with black above and yellow below
plus bold white supercilium.
Distinctive pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black above with bold white supercilium and bright
yellow below. Tiny size.
Florida and Caribbean.
