Band-rumped Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates castro)
=============================================

Description
-----------
Medium storm-petrel with dark brown plumage and distinctive white rump band.
Square-tipped tail, long wings. Larger and heavier-looking than Leach's.
About 9 inches long with buoyant, shearwater-like flight.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on small fish and squid by surface-pattering or dipping. Nests in
burrows on oceanic islands. Has two separate breeding cycles per island.

Habitat
-------
Tropical and subtropical Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Rare but regular off
U.S. Atlantic coast, especially after storms. Breeds on Galapagos and Hawaii.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Leach's Storm-Petrel has forked tail and erratic flight. Wilson's Storm-Petrel
has yellow-webbed feet visible in flight. Ashy is smaller and darker overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump band with square tail (not forked like Leach's). Shearwater-like
gliding flight. Larger size than most storm-petrels. Atlantic offshore records.
