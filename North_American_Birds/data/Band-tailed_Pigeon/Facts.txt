Band-tailed Pigeon (Patagioenas fasciata)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Large pigeon with gray body, purplish head and breast, yellow bill with dark
tip, yellow feet, and distinctive white crescent on nape; gray tail band.

Behavior
--------
Flocks heavily in fall to feed on acorns and berries; migrates in large flocks;
makes deep cooing calls; nests in Douglas fir and other conifers.

Habitat
-------
Mountain forests of the Pacific Coast and Southwest; found in oak and mixed
conifer forests; sometimes descends to lowland orchards in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rock Pigeon (city pigeon) has iridescent neck and lacks white nape crescent;
Mourning Dove is slimmer with pointed tail and spotted wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large gray pigeon with white crescent on nape, yellow bill tip, and yellow
feet; flocks in mountain oak and conifer forests of the West.
