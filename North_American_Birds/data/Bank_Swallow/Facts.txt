Bank Swallow (Riparia riparia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small swallow, brown above, white below with brown
chest band. Small bill.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests colonially in burrows in riverbanks and sand
cliffs. Forages over water for
flying insects.

Habitat
-------
Riverbanks with sandy bluffs for nesting; forages
over water bodies across North
America. Migratory.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rough-winged Swallow lacks chest band, has brown
throat. Tree Swallow has
iridescent blue-green back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown chest band across white underparts. Brown
back. Colonial nester in
sand bank burrows.
