Bar-tailed Godwit (Limosa lapponica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large godwit. Breeding male has reddish-orange underparts,
brown streaked back. Long slightly upturned bill.
Barred tail. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Makes longest known non-stop migration of any bird:
Alaska to New Zealand (11,000 km non-stop). Forages
on mudflats for invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Migrates through Pacific.
Winters on coasts of Australia and New Zealand.
Uncommon along Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hudsonian Godwit has white wing stripe. Bar-tailed
has barred tail and streaked back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Barred tail and streaked (not white) back. No
white wing stripe visible in flight.
Longest non-stop migration of any bird.
