Bare-throated Tiger-Heron (Tigrisoma mexicanum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large heron with finely barred brown and black plumage
("tiger" pattern), bare orange-yellow
throat. About 32 inches.

Behavior
--------
Stalks fish in tropical waterways. Stands motionless
then strikes. Very rare vagrant
from Mexico to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Tropical rivers and mangroves from Mexico to South
America. Very rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American heron has tiger-barred plumage
with bare yellow throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Finely barred black-and-brown plumage distinctive.
Bare orange-yellow throat. Very rare
Texas vagrant from Mexico.
