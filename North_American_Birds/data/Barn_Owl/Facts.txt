Barn Owl (Tyto alba)
=====================

Description
-----------
Ghost-pale owl with distinctive heart-shaped white facial disc; golden-buff
above with gray and fine dark markings; white to pale buff below.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal; hunts by sound alone in total darkness; swallows prey whole and
regurgitates compact pellets; screams rather than hoots; nests in barns and cavities.

Habitat
-------
Open country — farmland, grasslands, marshes, and lightly wooded areas; one
of the world's most widely distributed birds, found on every continent except Antarctica.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Barred Owl has streaked brown plumage and dark eyes; Snowy Owl is much
larger and all-white; Short-eared Owl has yellow eyes and streaked plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Heart-shaped white face disc, ghostly pale plumage, and silent moth-like
flight; screaming call; associates with open farmland and old buildings.
