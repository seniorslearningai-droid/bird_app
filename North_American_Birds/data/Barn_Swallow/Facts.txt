Barn Swallow (Hirundo rustica)
===============================

Description
-----------
Sleek swallow with deep blue-black back, rusty-orange throat and forehead,
pale buff belly, and long deeply forked tail streamers (longer in males).

Behavior
--------
Catches insects in graceful aerial pursuit; nests in open buildings, bridges,
and culverts using mud pellets; the world's most widespread swallow species.

Habitat
-------
Open country near water — farmland, meadows, marshes, and ponds; found
throughout North America in summer; winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cliff Swallow has square tail and white forehead; Purple Martin is much larger
and all-dark; Tree Swallow has white belly and green-blue back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Deep forked tail, rusty-orange throat, blue-black back; graceful swooping
flight over fields and water; nests in barns and under bridges.
