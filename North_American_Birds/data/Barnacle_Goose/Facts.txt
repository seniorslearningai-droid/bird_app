Barnacle Goose (Branta leucopsis)
==================================

Description
-----------
Medium goose with white face and forehead contrasting with black crown, neck,
and breast. Gray barred back. White belly. Short black bill. About 27 inches.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grasses and agricultural fields. Highly social, forming large flocks.
Nests on cliff ledges in the Arctic to avoid predators. Strong migratory flier.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra for breeding; coastal grasslands and estuaries in winter. Rare
visitor to northeastern North America, mainly Atlantic coast. Eurasian species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canada Goose has brown body and lacks white face. Cackling Goose is similar
but has no white face. Brant has dark belly and no white face pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White face and forehead on black head is unmistakable. Gray barred back, black
breast. In North America, check eastern coastal areas for rare vagrant records.
