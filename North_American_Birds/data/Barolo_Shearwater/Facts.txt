Barolo Shearwater (Puffinus baroli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny shearwater, black above, white below with white
wrapping around rear of face and neck.
Black bill and feet. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Macaronesian islands (Azores, Canaries).
Rare vagrant to North American Atlantic
coast.

Habitat
-------
Eastern North Atlantic. Rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic coast, particularly
off North Carolina.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Manx Shearwater larger. Barolo very tiny, white wrapping
around rear of face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, black above with white below wrapping around
face sides. Rare Atlantic vagrant.
Smallest Atlantic shearwater.
