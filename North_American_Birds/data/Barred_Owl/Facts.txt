Barred Owl (Strix varia)
=========================

Description
-----------
Large, stocky, round-headed owl with brown-and-white barred upper chest
and brown-streaked white belly; dark eyes; no ear tufts; brown-gray overall.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal hunter of small mammals, frogs, and birds; famous "who cooks for
you, who cooks for you-all" hooting call; expanding range westward.

Habitat
-------
Dense mature forests, swamps, and river bottomlands of eastern North America
and now Pacific Northwest; roosts in dense foliage during the day.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Owl (closely related) has spots rather than streaks on belly; Great
Horned Owl has ear tufts and yellow eyes; Barn Owl has heart-shaped face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Horizontal barring on chest, vertical brown streaking on belly, dark eyes,
and distinctive loud "who cooks for you" hooting call.
