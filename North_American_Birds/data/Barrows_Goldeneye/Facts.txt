Barrow's Goldeneye (Bucephala islandica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium diving duck. Male has black and white
plumage, purplish-green iridescent head with white
crescent spot near bill. Female gray-brown. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for aquatic invertebrates and fish. Nests
in tree cavities near lakes. Less common
than Common Goldeneye in most areas.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on montane lakes in western North America
and Iceland. Winters along Pacific coast and
inland western lakes and rivers.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Goldeneye nearly identical. Male has circular
head spot and greenish head gloss.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's crescent-shaped face spot versus round spot
in Common Goldeneye. Steeper forehead
profile. Female's mostly yellow bill.
