Bat Falcon (Falco rufigularis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small falcon, black above, white throat and chest,
rufous belly and thighs. White spots
on crown. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts bats and small birds at dawn and dusk. Very
rare vagrant from Mexico to
southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges and clearings of Mexico and Central
America. Very rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Kestrel different plumage. Bat Falcon's black-
and-rufous pattern distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black above, rufous belly, white throat. Very small
falcon. Crepuscular habits. Rare
vagrant from Mexico.
