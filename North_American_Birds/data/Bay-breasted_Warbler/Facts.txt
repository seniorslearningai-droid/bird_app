Bay-breasted Warbler (Setophaga castanea)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Breeding male has chestnut (bay) crown, throat, and sides; black face; cream
neck patch; white wing bars. Female and fall birds are much duller.

Behavior
--------
Forages methodically through dense conifer foliage for caterpillars; a spruce
budworm specialist whose populations fluctuate with budworm outbreaks.

Habitat
-------
Boreal spruce-fir forests in summer; migrates through eastern North America;
winters in Panama and northern South America in forest canopy.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chestnut-sided Warbler has yellow cap and white face; fall Bay-breasted is
very similar to fall Blackpoll Warbler — look for buffy flanks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding male's deep chestnut crown and sides are diagnostic; fall birds show
buffy flanks and faint streaking; creeping foraging style in spruce tops.
