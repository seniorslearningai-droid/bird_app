Belcher's Gull (Larus belcheri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull. Black back and wings, white head and
underparts, yellow bill with black and red bands,
yellow legs. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages along Pacific coast of South America. Rare
vagrant to Pacific coast of North America from
Peru and Chile.

Habitat
-------
Pacific coast of Peru and Chile. Rare vagrant to
California and Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kelp Gull similar pattern. Belcher's has yellow
(not greenish) legs and different bill bands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black back, white head, and distinctive banded yellow
bill. Yellow legs. Rare vagrant
from South American Pacific coast.
