Bell's Sparrow (Artemisiospiza belli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, gray-brown above, white below with
dark malar stripe, central breast
spot, dark lores. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Runs on ground under dense scrub rather than flying.
Forages for seeds and insects.
Secretive.

Habitat
-------
Coastal sage scrub, saltbush, and dense chaparral
of California and northern
Baja California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sagebrush Sparrow very similar but in different habitat.
Black-throated Sparrow has
black throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark malar stripe, central breast spot. Dense coastal
sage scrub California. Runs
on ground rather than flying.
