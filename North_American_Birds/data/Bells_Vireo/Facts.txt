Bell's Vireo (Vireo bellii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small vireo, grayish above with two wing bars and
pale eye ring. Yellowish sides and
flanks. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense scrub. Song a fast rambling chatter.
Declining due to Brown-headed Cowbird
parasitism.

Habitat
-------
Dense willow and mesquite scrub along streams. Breeding
range from Great Plains to
California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-eyed Vireo has white iris. Bell's Vireo has
dark eye and faint wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small secretive vireo in dense low scrub. Faint wing
bars, yellowish wash. Fast
chattering song.
