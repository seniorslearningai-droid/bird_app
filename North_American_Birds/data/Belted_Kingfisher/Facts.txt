Belted Kingfisher (Megaceryle alcyon)
======================================

Description
-----------
Large-headed blue-gray bird with shaggy crest, heavy dagger bill, white collar
and underparts; male has single blue breast band; female adds rusty band.

Behavior
--------
Hovers over water then plunges headfirst to catch fish; rattling call in flight;
excavates nesting burrows in earthen banks; solitary and territorial.

Habitat
-------
Along rivers, streams, ponds, lakes, and estuaries throughout North America;
needs clear water for hunting and earthen banks for nesting.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other kingfisher species regularly overlaps in most of North America; the
much rarer Green Kingfisher is tiny and lacks the shaggy crest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large blue-gray head with shaggy crest, dagger bill, rattling call in flight,
and habit of hovering then diving into water headfirst.
