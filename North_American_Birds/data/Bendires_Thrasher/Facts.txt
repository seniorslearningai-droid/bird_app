Bendire's Thrasher (Toxostoma bendirei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrasher, gray-brown above with lightly spotted
breast, yellow eye, relatively
short bill. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in desert scrub. Early nester.
Song varied and melodious.
Uses specific desert plant communities.

Habitat
-------
Desert grassland and sparse desert scrub in Arizona,
New Mexico, and southeastern California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Curve-billed Thrasher heavier bill more curved.
Sage Thrasher smaller, streaked,
different habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Shorter bill than other thrashers. Yellow eye.
Lightly spotted breast. Desert
scrub and grassland in Southwest.
