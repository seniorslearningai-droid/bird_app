Bermuda Petrel (Pterodroma cahow)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gadfly petrel, gray-brown above, white rump
and underparts, gray breast band, dark
cap. Critically endangered. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Critically endangered; once thought extinct, rediscovered
1951. Breeds only on Bermuda.
Forages in Atlantic Ocean.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean; breeds only on Bermuda islands.
One of world's rarest and most
endangered seabirds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Petrel has more white on head. Bermuda
has gray cap with white forehead.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray cap and back with white underparts. Rump patch.
Only breeds on Bermuda. Very
rare Atlantic seabird.
