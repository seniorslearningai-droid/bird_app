Berylline Hummingbird (Saucerottia beryllina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Iridescent green head and breast,
gray-brown belly, rufous wings and tail with
purple gloss. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar and insects in mountain forests.
Rare summer visitor to mountain canyons of
southeastern Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak mountain forests of Mexico and Central
America. Rare visitor to Huachuca and Chiricahua
mountains of southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-billed Hummingbird in similar habitat. Berylline's
rufous wings distinctive in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous wing panels flash distinctively in flight.
Green head and breast with gray-brown belly.
Rare in Arizona mountain canyons.
