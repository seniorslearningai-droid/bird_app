Bewick's Wren (Thryomanes bewickii)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Small wren with rich brown back, clean white eyebrow (supercilium), white
underparts, and long tail with white spots on outer corners.

Behavior
--------
Active and curious; cocks tail up; explores brush piles and low shrubs; males
sing complex varied songs; common in gardens and brushy yards.

Habitat
-------
Brushy areas, open woodlands, chaparral, and suburban gardens; common in
the western U.S.; formerly widespread in the East but declined there.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
House Wren is smaller with shorter eyebrow and browner overall; Carolina Wren
has rich buff underparts; Marsh Wren has streaked cap and back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white eyebrow, white-spotted tail corners when spread, clean white belly,
and energetic tail-cocking behavior in brushy western habitats.
