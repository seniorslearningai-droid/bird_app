Bicknell's Thrush (Catharus bicknelli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small thrush, brown above with spotted breast,
yellow base to bill. Extremely
similar to Gray-cheeked. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in dense stunted forest on high mountain
peaks. One of most restricted
breeding habitats in North America.

Habitat
-------
Dense balsam fir krummholz on mountaintops of
northeastern United States and southern
Canada. Winters in Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray-cheeked Thrush nearly identical; best separated
by range and breeding habitat.
Call note and song differ.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
High mountaintop stunted forest breeder. Nearly
identical to Gray-cheeked. Yellow
lower mandible. Specific call.
