Bishop's Oo (Moho bishopi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeyeater, black with yellow ear
tufts and yellow undertail coverts.
Long bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for nectar in native Hawaiian forest flowers.
Extinct since approximately 1904.
Last recorded on Maui.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Molokai and later Maui. Extinct
due to habitat destruction and
introduced avian diseases.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Oo species on different islands. All Hawaiian
Oo now extinct.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species from Molokai and Maui. Black with
yellow ear tufts. No longer
exists in nature.
