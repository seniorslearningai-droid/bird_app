Black-and-white Warbler (Mniotilta varia)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Boldly striped black and white overall; male has black cheek and throat;
female has white cheek; both have black-streaked white underparts.

Behavior
--------
Creeps along tree trunks and branches like a nuthatch, probing bark for insects;
one of the earliest spring warblers to arrive; "wee-see" thin high song.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests of eastern North America; nests on the ground
among tree roots; winters in Florida, Caribbean, and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blackpoll Warbler (fall) has similar streaking but lacks the creeping behavior;
Black-throated Gray Warbler has yellow spot near bill; Downy Woodpecker has red patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Creeping along bark like a nuthatch; bold black-and-white stripey pattern;
thin squeaky song; one of the earliest spring warblers.
