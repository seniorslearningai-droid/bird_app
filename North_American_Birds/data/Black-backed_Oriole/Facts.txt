Black-backed Oriole (Icterus abeillei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium oriole, orange with entirely black back, black
throat and wings, white wing
bars. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in flowering trees. Very rare vagrant to
southwestern United States from
central Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forests and highlands of central Mexico.
Very rare vagrant to southwestern
United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Streak-backed Oriole has streaked back. Bullocks Oriole
different pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entirely black back contrasting with orange body.
Very rare vagrant from highland
Mexico.
