Black-backed Woodpecker (Picoides arcticus)
============================================

Description
-----------
Medium woodpecker with solid black back (no white stripe). Males have yellow
crown patch. Heavily barred flanks. White face with black cheek stripe.
About 9.5 inches. Three toes instead of four.

Behavior
--------
Specializes in foraging on recently burned forest, stripping bark for wood-
boring beetle larvae. Hitches along trunks. Drums loudly. Usually solitary.

Habitat
-------
Recently burned boreal and montane conifer forests of northern and western
North America. Often appears in forests 1–5 years after fire.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Three-toed Woodpecker has barred black-and-white back. Hairy
Woodpecker has white back stripe. Black back is unique to this species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Solid black back with no white stripe is diagnostic. Found in burned forest.
Males show yellow crown. Three-toed foot (check if possible). Loud rapping.
