Black-bellied Plover (Pluvialis squatarola)
============================================

Description
-----------
Large plover; breeding adult has striking black face, neck, and belly contrasting
with speckled silver-gray back; nonbreeding birds are gray overall.

Behavior
--------
Forages on tidal flats and beaches by running and pausing; loud mournful
three-note whistle call; gathers in large flocks on migration.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra; migrates along both coasts and interior; winters
on beaches, mudflats, and estuaries of both coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Golden-Plover has golden-speckled back in breeding plumage and
lacks the black "wingpit" (axillaries) visible in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large plover with silver-gray speckled back, breeding black belly, and black
"wingpits" visible in flight; loud mournful whistle call.
