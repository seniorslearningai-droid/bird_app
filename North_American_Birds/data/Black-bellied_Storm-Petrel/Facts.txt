Black-bellied Storm-Petrel (Fregetta tropica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Dark storm-petrel with white belly patch bisected
by dark central streak, white rump.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims and feeds on ocean surface, different from
other storm-petrels. Very rare vagrant
to North American Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean and south Atlantic. Very rare vagrant
to North American Atlantic
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-bellied Storm-Petrel similar but lacks dark
central streak on belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White belly with black central streak. White rump.
Swims on surface rather than pattering.
Very rare southern vagrant.
