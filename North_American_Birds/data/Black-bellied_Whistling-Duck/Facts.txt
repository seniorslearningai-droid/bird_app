Black-bellied Whistling-Duck (Dendrocygna autumnalis)
======================================================

Description
-----------
Distinctive duck with bright orange-red bill, chestnut breast, black belly,
and bold white wing stripe. Long pink legs. Upright posture. About 21 inches.
Adults have gray face with white eye ring.

Behavior
--------
Perches in trees; nests in tree cavities. Highly social in large flocks.
Makes loud, whistling calls in flight. Nocturnal feeder on seeds and grains.

Habitat
-------
Ponds, lakes, marshes, and flooded fields in southern Texas, Louisiana, Arizona,
and Florida. Expanding northward. Tropical species common in Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Fulvous Whistling-Duck is tawny overall without black belly. No other duck
has combination of orange bill, chestnut breast, and black belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange bill and bold white wing stripe in flight are unmistakable. Black belly
visible at rest. Loud whistle calls. Tree-perching behavior unusual for ducks.
