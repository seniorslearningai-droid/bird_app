Black-billed Cuckoo (Coccyzus erythropthalmus)
================================================

Description
-----------
Slender long-tailed bird with brown upperparts, plain white underparts, all-black
bill, narrow red eye ring, and small white tail spots below.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense vegetation; eats caterpillars including hairy tent caterpillars
other birds avoid; secretive but vocal with repetitive "cu-cu-cu" calls.

Habitat
-------
Dense thickets, deciduous forests, and shrubby edges of eastern North America;
winters in South America; often responds to rainy weather (rain crow).

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-billed Cuckoo has yellow lower mandible, rusty wing panels in flight,
and larger tail spots; Mangrove Cuckoo has buff belly and is coastal.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black bill, narrow red eye ring, small white tail spots, and plain white
belly; skulking behavior in eastern thickets; distinctive hollow calls.
