Black-billed Magpie (Pica hudsonia)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Bold black-and-white bird with iridescent blue-green wings and long wedge-shaped
tail; black head, breast, and back; white belly, scapulars, and wing patches.

Behavior
--------
Intelligent and curious; caches food; builds a domed stick nest; loud
chattering calls; omnivorous; often forages on the ground near livestock.

Habitat
-------
Open country, shrublands, forest edges, and rural areas of western North
America from Alaska to the Great Basin; common near farms and ranches.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-billed Magpie is nearly identical but found only in California's
Central Valley; American Crow lacks white patches and long tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large black-and-white bird with enormously long iridescent tail and bold
white wing patches flashing in flight; loud chattering calls.
