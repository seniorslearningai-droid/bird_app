Black-browed Albatross (Thalassarche melanophris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Most common mollymawk albatross. White body and head,
dark back and wings, yellow-orange bill,
black eyebrow stripe. About 35 inches.

Behavior
--------
Most likely mollymawk to reach North America. Follows
ships, feeds on fish and squid.
Southern hemisphere breeder.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean. Rare vagrant to North Atlantic and
Pacific; occasional off Atlantic
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Atlantic Yellow-nosed Albatross has slimmer bill and
thinner dark eyebrow stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow-orange bill with black tip, black eyebrow stripe.
White body, dark back.
Most frequently seen mollymawk vagrant.
