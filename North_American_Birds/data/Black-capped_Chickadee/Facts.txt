Black-capped Chickadee (Poecile atricapillus)
==============================================

Description
-----------
Small energetic bird with black cap and bib, white cheeks, gray back and
wings, and buffy sides; white edging on wing feathers.

Behavior
--------
Bold and acrobatic; hangs upside down to forage for insects and seeds;
caches food; visits feeders; gives "chick-a-dee-dee" alarm calls.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests, parks, and suburban areas across the northern
United States and Canada; year-round resident; forms winter flocks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Carolina Chickadee has less white on wing and different call; Mountain
Chickadee has white eyebrow; Boreal Chickadee has brown cap and sides.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black cap and bib, white cheeks, gray back, and lively "chick-a-dee"
and two-note whistled "fee-bee" song; very common at feeders.
