Black-capped Gnatcatcher (Polioptila nigriceps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny gnatcatcher, blue-gray above, white below.
Male has large black cap extending
below eye. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active and vocal in dense scrub. Rare visitor
to Arizona from Mexico. Pairs
forage together year-round.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub and thorny woodland of Mexico.
Rare visitor to extreme southern
Arizona near border.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-tailed Gnatcatcher has less extensive cap.
Blue-gray Gnatcatcher much whiter
below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large black cap extending well below eye. White
underparts. Southern Arizona rare
visitor from Mexico.
