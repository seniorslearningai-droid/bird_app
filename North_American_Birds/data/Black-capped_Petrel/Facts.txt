Black-capped Petrel (Pterodroma hasitata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gadfly petrel, dark gray-black back, black
cap, white collar and rump, white underparts.
About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Caribbean island cliffs. Forages in Gulf
Stream; regular offshore Atlantic
from Florida to New England.

Habitat
-------
Gulf Stream waters off Atlantic coast. Breeds on
Haiti and Dominica; ranges widely
in western North Atlantic.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bermuda Petrel smaller with gray cap. Black-capped has
bold black cap and white collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black cap with bright white collar and rump.
Dark back. Gulf Stream specialist
off Atlantic coast.
