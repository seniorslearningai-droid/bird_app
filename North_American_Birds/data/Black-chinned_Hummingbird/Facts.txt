Black-chinned Hummingbird (Archilochus alexandri)
==================================================

Description
-----------
Small hummingbird with metallic green back; male has black throat with a
narrow iridescent purple lower band; female is pale green-gray below.

Behavior
--------
Pumps tail while hovering; males perform a J-shaped pendulum display dive;
visits flowers and feeders; catches insects in flight; highly territorial.

Habitat
-------
Streamside thickets, mountain canyons, dry woodlands, and suburban areas
of the western United States; winters in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruby-throated Hummingbird overlaps little in range; Calliope Hummingbird
is smaller with streaked gorget; Costa's has purple crown and long side tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Purple throat band below black chin on males; western range; tail pumping
while hovering; J-shaped dive display; common at western feeders.
