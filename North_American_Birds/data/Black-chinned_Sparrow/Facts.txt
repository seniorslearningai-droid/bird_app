Black-chinned Sparrow (Spizella atrogularis)
=============================================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow with gray head, breast, and upperparts. Males have black chin
and face patch. Back streaked brown and black. Pink bill. About 5.5 inches.
Females lack black chin, have paler gray faces.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground and in shrubs for seeds and insects. Secretive, often
staying low in dense chaparral. Males sing from exposed perches in spring.

Habitat
-------
Dense chaparral, sagebrush, and mountain scrub on brushy hillsides in
California, Arizona, and New Mexico. Winters in southwestern deserts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dark-eyed Junco has white outer tail feathers. Chipping Sparrow has rufous
cap. Other gray sparrows lack the combination of all-gray head and pink bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray head and breast with pink bill in chaparral habitat. Males show black
chin patch. Plaintive, accelerating trill song. Brushy mountain slopes in west.
