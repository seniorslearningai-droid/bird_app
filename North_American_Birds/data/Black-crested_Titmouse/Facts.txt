Black-crested Titmouse (Baeolophus atricristatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small titmouse with distinctive black crest, gray
body, white forehead patch, rusty
flanks. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in oaks and mesquite. Visits feeders
readily. Loud and vocal.
Replaces Tufted Titmouse in Texas range.

Habitat
-------
Oak woodlands, mesquite thickets, and suburban areas
of central and southern Texas
and northeastern Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tufted Titmouse has gray crest, not black. Overlaps
with Tufted Titmouse in
narrow hybrid zone in Texas.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black crest contrasting with white forehead. Central
Texas range. Rusty flanks.
Very similar to Tufted Titmouse.
