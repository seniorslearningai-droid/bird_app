Black-crowned Night-Heron (Nycticorax nycticorax)
==================================================

Description
-----------
Stocky heron with black crown and back, white face and underparts, gray wings,
and red eyes; short thick neck; long white head plumes in breeding season.

Behavior
--------
Most active at dusk and dawn; roosts in trees during the day in groups;
gives a barking "quok" call in flight; nests in colonies.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, swamps, estuaries, and lake edges throughout North
America and much of the world; very widespread distribution.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-crowned Night-Heron has black-and-white head pattern and yellow
crown; adult herons in flight may be confused with other species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black crown and back, white face, red eyes, stocky short-necked profile;
active at night and dusk; loud "quok" call in flight.
