Black-faced Grassquit (Melanospiza bicolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch. Male dark olive with black face and
breast. Female plain olive-brown.
About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for grass seeds. Rare visitor to Florida
from Caribbean islands. Can
be locally established.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, gardens, and open areas. Caribbean resident.
Rare visitor to
southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-faced Grassquit has yellow on face. Blue-black
Grassquit male all blue-black.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male dark olive with black face. Tiny size. Rare
Florida visitor from Caribbean.
Female plain olive-brown.
