Black-footed Albatross (Phoebastria nigripes)
==============================================

Description
-----------
Large seabird, mostly dark sooty brown with white around bill and eye, and
white at base of tail. Long narrow wings spanning 7 feet. Black feet. Hooked
bill. About 28 inches long.

Behavior
--------
Soars effortlessly on ocean updrafts. Follows ships for refuse. Breeds on
Hawaiian islands, returning to same nest site each year. Long-lived species.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean, common off the west coast of North America. Pelagic,
coming to land only to breed. Abundant on pelagic birding trips offshore.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Laysan Albatross is white-bodied with dark back and wings. Short-tailed
Albatross is larger with pink bill. Black-footed is the common dark albatross.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large, all-dark albatross with white around bill and eye. White tail base.
Long gliding flight on stiff wings. Common on North Pacific pelagic trips.
