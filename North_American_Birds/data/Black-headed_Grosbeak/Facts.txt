Black-headed Grosbeak (Pheucticus melanocephalus)
==================================================

Description
-----------
Male has black head, orange-cinnamon breast and collar, black and white wings,
and large pale bill; female is streaked brown with buff eyebrow.

Behavior
--------
Eats seeds, insects, and berries with powerful bill; one of few birds that
can safely eat toxic Monarch butterflies; robin-like rich melodious song.

Habitat
-------
Mixed and deciduous forests, orchards, and wooded parks of western North
America; winters in Mexico; common at feeders during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rose-breasted Grosbeak (eastern) male has rosy-red breast triangle;
female grosbeaks are similar — look for bold eyebrow and large bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's orange-cinnamon breast and black head are distinctive; large pale
conical bill; robin-like song in western forests; common at feeders.
