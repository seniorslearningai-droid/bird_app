Black-headed Gull (Chroicocephalus ridibundus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gull. Breeding adult has dark brown hood,
red bill and legs, gray back, white underparts.
About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common in Eurasia; rare but regular vagrant to
eastern North America. Flocks with Bonaparte's Gulls
on Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Coastal and inland waters of Eurasia. Regular but
uncommon vagrant to Atlantic coast of
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bonaparte's Gull similar. Black-headed has dark brown
hood and larger size.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark brown hood in breeding. Red (not black) bill.
Slightly larger than Bonaparte's Gull.
Regular vagrant on Atlantic coast.
