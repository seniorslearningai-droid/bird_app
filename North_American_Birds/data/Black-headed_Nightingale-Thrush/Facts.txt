Black-headed Nightingale-Thrush (Catharus mexicanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small thrush with black head and back contrasting
with gray underparts. Orange bill.
About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dense forest undergrowth. Rare vagrant
to Texas from Mexico. Beautiful
song.

Habitat
-------
Dense humid mountain forest of Mexico and Central
America. Very rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Orange-billed Nightingale-Thrush lacks black head.
Other spotted thrushes have spotted
underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head contrasting with gray body. Orange bill.
Very rare Texas vagrant
from mountain Mexico.
