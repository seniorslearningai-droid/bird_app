Black-legged Kittiwake (Rissa tridactyla)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Medium gull with clean white head and body, gray back and wings, and solid
black wingtips (no white spots). Yellow bill, black legs. In winter, gray
smudge behind eye. About 17 inches long.

Behavior
--------
Feeds by plunge-diving for small fish and marine invertebrates. Highly
pelagic outside breeding season. Nests colonially on narrow cliff ledges.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Breeds on Arctic and sub-Arctic cliffs.
Winters offshore, regularly seen from shore during storms. Most oceanic gull.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-billed Gull has ring on bill and pale wingtips. Mew Gull is smaller.
Bonaparte's Gull is smaller with black head in summer. Kittiwake has clean
solid black wingtips.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Clean solid black wingtips with no white mirrors. Yellow bill, dark legs.
Buoyant, tern-like flight. Most commonly seen gull far offshore in winter.
