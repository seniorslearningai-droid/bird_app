Black-necked Stilt (Himantopus mexicanus)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Elegant long-legged shorebird with black back and head, white underparts,
and remarkably long bright pink-red legs; red eye; straight thin bill.

Behavior
--------
Wades in shallow water probing for invertebrates; aggressively defends nest
by dive-bombing intruders; loud "kek-kek-kek" alarm calls; gregarious.

Habitat
-------
Shallow freshwater and saltwater wetlands, flooded fields, and sewage ponds
throughout the West, Gulf Coast, and Florida; increasingly common inland.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Avocet has upturned bill and rusty head; Black Oystercatcher
is all black with red bill; Willet is gray with white wing stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely long pink-red legs (one of longest-legged birds relative to body),
bold black and white pattern, and loud alarm calls at wetlands.
