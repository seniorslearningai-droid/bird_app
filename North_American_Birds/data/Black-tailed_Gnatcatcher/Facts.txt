Black-tailed Gnatcatcher (Polioptila melanura)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny long-tailed bird, blue-gray above, pale below.
Male has black cap in breeding.
Much black on tail. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in desert scrub, constantly flicking
tail. Pairs remain together year-round.
Harsh insect-like calls.

Habitat
-------
Arid desert scrub, washes, and thorny thickets
of the Sonoran and Chihuahuan
deserts in southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-gray Gnatcatcher has more white in tail.
California Gnatcatcher coastal with
different call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Mostly black tail from below (white-sided in Blue-gray).
Desert scrub habitat. Harsh
calls. Black cap on male.
