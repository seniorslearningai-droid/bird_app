Black-tailed Godwit (Limosa limosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large godwit. Breeding adult has orange-red head and
neck, black and white tail, bold white wing
stripe. Long straight bill. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in shallow water for invertebrates. Rare vagrant
to eastern North America from Eurasia. Wades
deeply when feeding.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on European and Asian wet meadows. Winters
on African and South Asian coasts. Rare
vagrant to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hudsonian Godwit has similar wing stripe pattern.
Black-tailed has straight bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black and white tail pattern and bold white wing
stripe distinctive. Straight (not upturned)
bill. Rare vagrant on Atlantic coast.
