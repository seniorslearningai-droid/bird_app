Black-tailed Gull (Larus crassirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull, pale gray back, white body, distinctive
black tail band, yellow bill with red and black
tip. Yellow eyes. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common in Asian coastal waters. Rare vagrant to
North American coasts. Rests on rocks and
jetties; scavenges widely.

Habitat
-------
Rocky coasts, harbors, and fishing ports of eastern
Asia. Very rare vagrant to Pacific and
Atlantic coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mew Gull and Ring-billed Gull similar. Black-tailed
Gull's black tail band and yellow
eyes distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black tail band contrasting with white rump distinctive.
Yellow eyes, black-and-red bill tip.
Asian vagrant to North America.
