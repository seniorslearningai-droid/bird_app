Black-throated Blue Warbler (Setophaga caerulescens)
=====================================================

Description
-----------
Male is deep blue above with black face, throat, and sides; clean white belly;
small white wing spot. Female is olive-brown with subtle pale eyebrow.

Behavior
--------
Forages methodically in dense understory shrubs; males sing a buzzy rising
"I am la-zy" song; male and female look so different they were once considered separate species.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest understory of eastern North America; prefers mountain forests
in Appalachians and Adirondacks; winters in the Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting male is all blue; female Black-throated Blue resembles
Orange-crowned Warbler but has small white wing spot.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's deep blue and black with white belly and tiny white wing spot are
diagnostic; female's plain olive-brown with white wing spot is subtle.
