Black-throated Gray Warbler (Setophaga nigrescens)
==================================================

Description
-----------
Black-and-white patterned warbler with gray back; black throat, cheeks,
and crown; white eyebrow and white sides; tiny yellow dot near bill.

Behavior
--------
Forages in oaks and other trees for insects; males sing a buzzy song with
rising or falling quality; monochrome appearance is distinctive in the West.

Habitat
-------
Dry woodlands, shrubby hillsides, pinyon-juniper, and chaparral of the
western United States; winters in Mexico and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-and-white Warbler creeps on bark and has striped crown;
Townsend's Warbler has yellow face and breast; Hermit Warbler has yellow face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black-and-gray pattern with white eyebrow, tiny yellow dot near bill,
and western range; buzzy song in dry western woodlands.
