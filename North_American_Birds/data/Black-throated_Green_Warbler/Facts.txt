Black-throated Green Warbler (Setophaga virens)
================================================

Description
-----------
Bright yellow face with olive-green crown; black throat and breast sides;
white wing bars; male has solid black throat, female has partial black throat.

Behavior
--------
Forages in tree canopy for insects; males sing persistent buzzy "zee-zee-zee-
zoo-zee" song; a common spring migrant through the eastern U.S.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests of the northeast and Appalachians; migrates
through eastern North America; winters from Mexico to Colombia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Townsend's Warbler (western) has black cap and cheek patch; Hermit Warbler
has yellow face but white underparts and less black; Yellow-rumped Warbler lacks yellow face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow face, black throat, and buzzy "zee-zee-zee-zoo-zee" song;
conifer forests; one of the most reliably heard spring warblers.
