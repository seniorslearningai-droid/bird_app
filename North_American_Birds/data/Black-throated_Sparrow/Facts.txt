Black-throated Sparrow (Amphispiza bilineata)
=============================================

Description
-----------
Striking sparrow with gray back, black throat and breast, white facial stripes
above and below eye, and white underparts. Bold head pattern. About 5.5 inches.
Immatures lack black throat, show streaked breast.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds and insects. Highly adapted to desert heat and
can survive without free water. Pairs or small groups. Melodic, complex song.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub, rocky slopes, and arid grasslands of the Sonoran and Chihuahuan
deserts. Common in creosote and cholla cactus habitat. Year-round resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lark Sparrow has complex head pattern but no black throat. Black-chinned
Sparrow has gray head without white facial stripes. Unique pattern in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black throat and white facial stripes are unmistakable in any plumage.
Desert habitat. Melodic, bell-like song heard at dawn. Perches on cactus.
