Black-vented Oriole (Icterus wagleri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium oriole, black hood, black back and wings,
orange-yellow below, black undertail
coverts. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in flowering trees for nectar and insects.
Rare vagrant to Texas and
New Mexico from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and forest edge of Mexico and Central
America. Rare vagrant to
southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hooded Oriole has orange back and white wing bars.
Scott's Oriole has yellow-black
pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black back and hood with orange-yellow body. Black
undertail coverts. Rare Mexico
vagrant to Texas.
