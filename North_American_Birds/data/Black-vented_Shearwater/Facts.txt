Black-vented Shearwater (Puffinus opisthomelas)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small shearwater, dark brown above, white below with
dark undertail coverts (vent). Dark
sides of breast. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common offshore California especially in winter.
Breeds on islands off Baja California.
Often in large flocks offshore.

Habitat
-------
California coast offshore; breeds on Baja California
islands. Very common California
winter shearwater.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Manx Shearwater has white undertail. Black-vented's
dark undertail coverts diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark undertail coverts on otherwise white-bellied
shearwater. Common off California
in winter.
