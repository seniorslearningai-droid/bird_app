Black-whiskered Vireo (Vireo altiloquus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium vireo similar to Red-eyed but with black
malar stripe (whisker). Olive-green
above, white below. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings a monotonous repeated phrase from mangroves
and coastal trees. Florida's most
common summer vireo in that habitat.

Habitat
-------
Mangroves, coastal hammocks, and tropical forests.
Breeds in Florida Keys and south
Florida, winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-eyed Vireo lacks the black malar stripe.
Yucatan Vireo similar but
occurs in different region.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black whisker mark below eye unique among vireos.
Florida mangrove habitat. Olive
above, white below, red eye.
