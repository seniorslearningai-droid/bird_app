Black-winged Petrel (Pterodroma nigripennis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gadfly petrel, pale gray above, bold black
M-pattern on wings, white below.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Lord Howe and Kermadec Islands. Forages
in tropical Pacific; very rare vagrant
to North American Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cook's Petrel similar but has different underwing
pattern. Black-winged has bolder
upper wing pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black M-pattern on pale gray wings. Small gadfly
petrel in tropical Pacific.
Very rare vagrant.
