Black-winged Stilt (Himantopus himantopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant wader with extremely long pink legs, black
back and wings, white underparts and head,
long straight black bill. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Wades in shallow water picking invertebrates from
surface. Very noisy when disturbed. Rare vagrant
to North America from Eurasia and Africa.

Habitat
-------
Shallow freshwater and coastal wetlands. Breeds across
Eurasia and Africa. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-necked Stilt nearly identical; replaces it in
North America. Black-winged has white head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely long pink legs, black and white plumage.
Black-winged has all-white head vs. black
nape of Black-necked Stilt.
