Black Catbird (Melanoptila glabrirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small mimic thrush, entirely glossy black with dark
bill, red iris. Slim build.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense vegetation. Catbird-like behavior.
Rare vagrant to Texas from
Yucatan Peninsula of Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense coastal scrub and second growth of the Yucatan
Peninsula. Rare vagrant to
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other all-black catbird or mimic thrush in range.
Gray Catbird gray not black.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entirely glossy black, slim catbird shape. Red iris.
Rare Texas vagrant from
Yucatan Peninsula.
