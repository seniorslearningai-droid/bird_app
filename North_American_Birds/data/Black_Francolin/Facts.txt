Black Francolin (Francolinus francolinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium partridge. Male has black breast and face,
chestnut collar, white cheek patch, spotted flanks.
Female brown and streaked. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense grass. Loud distinctive call in
early morning. Forages for seeds and insects.
Introduced to Hawaiian islands and Louisiana.

Habitat
-------
Tall grasslands, agricultural fields, and dense
scrub. In Hawaii found on Kauai, Maui, and
other islands; also feral in Louisiana.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray Francolin paler. Male Black Francolin's
bold coloring unique in Hawaii.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's black chest with distinctive white ear patch
and chestnut collar. Loud territorial
call in early morning.
