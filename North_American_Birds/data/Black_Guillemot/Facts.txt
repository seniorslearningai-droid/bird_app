Black Guillemot (Cepphus grylle)
=================================

Description
-----------
Medium seabird, all black in summer with bold white wing patch. Bright red
feet and red mouth lining. Winter plumage is white with black back and wings.
About 13 inches. Strong, whirring wingbeats.

Behavior
--------
Dives from surface to catch fish and invertebrates near rocky shores. Nests
in rock crevices, often in small colonies. Stays near coast year-round.

Habitat
-------
Rocky coasts and fjords of the Arctic and northern Atlantic. Less pelagic
than other auks, rarely far from shore. Common in Maine and Canadian Maritimes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pigeon Guillemot (Pacific) is nearly identical but separated by range.
Dovekie is much smaller. Razorbill and Common Murre are larger, more black.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black body with striking white wing patch. Bright red feet visible when
swimming or landing. Rocky coastal habitat. Often seen close to shore.
