Black Kite (Milvus migrans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium brown raptor with slightly forked tail. Dark
brown overall with pale head.
About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Scavenger and opportunist. Extremely common in Africa,
Europe, and Asia. Rare vagrant to
Alaska and Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, coasts, and urban areas of Old World.
Rare vagrant to Alaska and
Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red Kite has deeper forked tail and reddish plumage.
Black Kite brown with slight fork.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown overall with shallow forked tail. Highly variable
plumage. Common Old World raptor,
rare North American vagrant.
