Black Mamo (Drepanis funerea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Hawaiian honeycreeper, entirely black with
very long deeply curved bill.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on flowers of native plants with very long
bill. Extinct since approximately
1907 on Molokai.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Molokai. Extinct due to habitat
destruction and introduced diseases.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hawaii Mamo had yellow patches. No other all-black
honeycreeper with extremely
long curved bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. All-black with very long curved bill. From
Molokai only. No longer
exists in any forest.
