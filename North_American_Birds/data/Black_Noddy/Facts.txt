Black Noddy (Anous minutus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small tern-like seabird. Entirely black with small
white cap on forehead. Slender bill. Smaller
and blacker than Brown Noddy. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Picks prey from water surface. Nests in trees on
tropical islands. Uncommon visitor to Florida
from Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Tropical oceanic islands. Occasional visitor to Dry
Tortugas, Florida, from Caribbean. Nests on
tropical Pacific and Atlantic islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Noddy larger and browner with more extensive
pale cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black plumage with small white cap on forehead.
Smaller than Brown Noddy. Slender
black bill.
