Black Oystercatcher (Haematopus bachmani)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Large all-black shorebird with a vivid long red-orange bill, yellow eyes with
red orbital ring, and pale pinkish legs; stocky and crow-sized.

Behavior
--------
Pries open mussels and limpets from rocks with its strong laterally flattened
bill; loud "kleep" calls; nests in a simple scrape on rocky shorelines.

Habitat
-------
Rocky Pacific Coast shores from Alaska to Baja California; closely tied to
wave-washed rocky intertidal zones; rarely found on sandy beaches.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Oystercatcher has white on wings and belly; American Crow and
Common Raven are all dark but lack the red bill and are found inland.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black body with bright red-orange bill; yellow eye; found exclusively on
rocky Pacific Coast shoreline; loud piping call.
