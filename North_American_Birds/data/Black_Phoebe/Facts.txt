Black Phoebe (Sayornis nigricans)
==================================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher with entirely sooty black head, breast, back, and wings;
white belly and lower flanks; no wing bars; faint white wing edging.

Behavior
--------
Perches upright on low rocks or branches near water, wagging tail; sallies
out to catch flying insects; builds mud-and-grass cup nest on bridges and walls.

Habitat
-------
Near water in the western U.S. — streams, rivers, ponds, and coastal areas;
year-round resident from California through Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Phoebe is brownish-gray (not black) with white belly; Dark-eyed
Junco has white outer tail feathers and a conical seed-eating bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black-and-white pattern (clean black above, white below), tail-wagging
habit, and association with water in the western U.S.
