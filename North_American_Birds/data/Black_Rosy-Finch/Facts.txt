Black Rosy-Finch (Leucosticte atrata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch, black body with pink wing patches
and rump, gray crown.
About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages at snowfields and talus slopes. Breeds at
highest elevations in central
Rocky Mountains.

Habitat
-------
Alpine tundra and rocky slopes above treeline in
central Rockies. Winters lower
in flocks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown and Gray-crowned Rosy-Finches similar structure.
Black body distinguishes from
brownish relatives.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black body with pink wing patches and rump. Gray
crown. High Rocky Mountain
alpine specialist.
