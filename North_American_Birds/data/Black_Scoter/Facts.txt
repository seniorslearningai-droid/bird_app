Black Scoter (Melanitta americana)
====================================

Description
-----------
Medium sea duck, males entirely black with bright orange-yellow knob at base
of bill. Females are dark brown with pale cheeks contrasting with dark cap.
About 19 inches. Compact, heavy-bodied duck.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mollusks, crustaceans, and aquatic insects. Migrates in large flocks
along coasts. Rarely seen inland. Winters at sea in nearshore waters.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on boreal lakes and tundra ponds. Winters along both coasts in nearshore
ocean waters. Less common than Surf or White-winged Scoter on Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Surf Scoter male has orange and white bill pattern. White-winged Scoter has
white wing patch. Ruddy Duck is smaller. Female Black Scoter has cleaner
pale cheeks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: all black with orange knob on bill. Female: two-toned head, pale cheeks.
Coastal ocean habitat in winter. Often in flocks with other scoters.
