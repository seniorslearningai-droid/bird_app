Black Skimmer (Rynchops niger)
================================

Description
-----------
Striking black-and-white bird with extraordinary bill — lower mandible much
longer than upper; black above, white below; red-orange bill with dark tip.

Behavior
--------
Flies low over calm water with lower bill slicing the surface to catch fish;
nests in colonies on sandy beaches; loud barking calls at colonies.

Habitat
-------
Coastal beaches, bays, estuaries, and tidal rivers; nests on sand spits and
barrier beaches; found along both U.S. coasts and Gulf of Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American bird has the distinctive knife-blade bill shape;
terns and gulls have symmetrical bills; the skimming flight is unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unique elongated lower mandible skimming the water surface; black back and
white underparts; red-orange bill with black tip; coastal habitat.
