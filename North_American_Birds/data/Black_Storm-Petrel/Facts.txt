Black Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates melania)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-dark storm-petrel with slow languid wingbeats,
pale upperwing bar, forked tail.
About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on islands off Baja California. Largest dark
storm-petrel in eastern Pacific. Slow
deep wingbeats distinctive.

Habitat
-------
Eastern Pacific off California and Baja California.
Forages over warm Pacific waters;
breeds on Baja islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ashy Storm-Petrel smaller and grayer. Black Storm-Petrel
largest dark Pacific storm-petrel.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest all-dark storm-petrel in eastern Pacific. Slow
languid wingbeats. Forked tail,
pale upperwing bar.
