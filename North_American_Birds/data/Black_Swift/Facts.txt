Black Swift (Cypseloides niger)
================================

Description
-----------
Large, all-black swift with forked tail (appearing square when not spread).
Long, scythe-shaped wings. About 7 inches. Noticeably larger than other
swifts. Slow, deep wingbeats compared to Vaux's Swift.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on aerial insects high in the sky. Nests on wet cliff faces near
waterfalls in small colonies. Highly aerial; rarely lands except at nest.

Habitat
-------
Mountain cliffs near waterfalls and sea cliffs in western North America.
Forages widely over mountains and valleys. Rare and poorly known species.
Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Vaux's Swift is much smaller with pale throat and rump. White-throated Swift
has white throat and sides. Chimney Swift is smaller. Purple Martin is a
swallow with different flight profile.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large all-black swift with slow, rowing wingbeats. Look near mountain waterfalls.
Much larger than Vaux's Swift. Rare; finding nest waterfall locations helps.
