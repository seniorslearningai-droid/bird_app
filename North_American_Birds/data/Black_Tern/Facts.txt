Black Tern (Chlidonias niger)
==============================

Description
-----------
Small tern with black head and body in breeding plumage. Gray wings and tail.
White undertail. In fall, white replaces black on head and underparts. Short
bill, shallowly forked tail. About 9.5 inches long.

Behavior
--------
Dips to water surface to snatch insects and small fish. Feeds over freshwater
marshes. Loose colonies for nesting on floating vegetation. Gregarious migrant.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes and lakes in interior North America for breeding. Migrates
along coasts and inland. Winters at sea off Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sooty Tern is larger, ocean-going, with white forehead. Bridled Tern similar
but with white forehead. Other terns have white bodies. Distinctive in breeding.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black body with gray wings in summer — unmistakable. In fall, dark cap with
white forehead. Dipping, buoyant flight over freshwater marshes. Small size.
