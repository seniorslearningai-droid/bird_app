Black Turnstone (Arenaria melanocephala)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Stocky shorebird with black head, breast, and back in breeding plumage. White
belly and white markings on face. Bill slightly upturned. Orange-red legs.
About 9.5 inches. Winter birds lose white face markings.

Behavior
--------
Flips rocks, shells, and seaweed to find invertebrates, as name suggests.
Highly territorial on wintering grounds. Loud, rattling calls in flight.

Habitat
-------
Rocky Pacific coastline — breeds on Alaska tidal marshes, winters on rocky
shores south to Baja California. Almost exclusively coastal in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruddy Turnstone has orange-rufous back pattern and is found on sandy beaches.
Surfbird is larger with yellow legs. Both species flip debris for food.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black upperparts with white belly on Pacific rocky shores. Distinctive
wing stripe pattern in flight. Flipping behavior with strong bill. Loud rattle.
