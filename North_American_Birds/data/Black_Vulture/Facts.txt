Black Vulture (Coragyps atratus)
==================================

Description
-----------
Large all-black bird with bald, wrinkled gray-black head; short hooked bill;
short square tail; white wing patches at wingtips visible in flight.

Behavior
--------
Soars on flat wings, interspersed with frequent flapping; roosts communally;
often follows Turkey Vultures to carrion; aggressive and social at food.

Habitat
-------
Open country, roadsides, farms, and urban areas; common in the southeastern
U.S. and spreading northward; also found throughout Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Turkey Vulture is larger with red head, two-toned wings, and soars in a
strong dihedral (V-shape); Zone-tailed Hawk mimics vultures in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black with white wingtip patches, gray bald head, short square tail,
flap-flap-glide flight style, and flat (not V-shaped) soaring posture.
