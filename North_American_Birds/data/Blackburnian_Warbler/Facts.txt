Blackburnian Warbler (Setophaga fusca)
========================================

Description
-----------
Male has brilliant flame-orange throat and face, black back with white
streaking, black cheeks, and white wing patch. Female is dull yellow-orange.

Behavior
--------
Forages high in the crowns of tall conifers, difficult to see; males
sing a very high thin ascending "zee-zee-zee-zeeeeee" song.

Habitat
-------
Mature conifer forests of the Northeast and Appalachians in summer; a
common fall migrant; winters in Andean forests of South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Redstart male has orange on wings and tail; no other warbler
has the fiery-orange throat; female resembles other streaked warblers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unmistakable fiery-orange throat on males; look high in spruce and
hemlock canopy; very thin ascending song; most brilliantly colored warbler.
