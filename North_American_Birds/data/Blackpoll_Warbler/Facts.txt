Blackpoll Warbler (Setophaga striata)
========================================

Description
-----------
Breeding male has black cap, white cheeks, black streaking on white underparts;
white wing bars. Fall birds are olive-green with streaking and orange legs.

Behavior
--------
Performs one of the longest migrations of any songbird — nonstop over the
Atlantic Ocean to South America in fall; thin high "tseet" song.

Habitat
-------
Boreal spruce forests in summer; migrates through eastern North America;
fall migrants make a trans-oceanic flight to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Fall Blackpoll is very similar to fall Bay-breasted Warbler — look for
orange-yellow (not dark) feet; Black-and-white Warbler has striped crown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding male's black cap and white cheeks are diagnostic; fall birds
have orange-yellow feet; look for heavy streaking; common fall migrant.
