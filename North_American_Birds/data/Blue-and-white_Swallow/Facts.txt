Blue-and-white Swallow (Pygochelidon cyanoleuca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small swallow, deep blue-black above, white below
with black undertail coverts. Small
bill. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages over open areas and forest. Rare vagrant
to North America. Highly
migratory in South America.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, forest edges, and urban environments
in Central and South America.
Very rare vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tree Swallow larger with green gloss above, white
undertail coverts. Barn Swallow
has rusty throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark black undertail coverts contrast with white
belly. Deep blue-black above.
Very small swallow.
