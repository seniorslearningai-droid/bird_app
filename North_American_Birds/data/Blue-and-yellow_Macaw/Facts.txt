Blue-and-yellow Macaw (Ara ararauna)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large macaw, brilliant blue above, bright yellow below,
green forehead, white face.
About 33 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly intelligent. Loud and conspicuous. Escaped birds
occasionally seen; no established
feral population.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forests. Occasional escaped individuals in
United States. Native to
South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other large blue-and-yellow bird in North America.
Unmistakable combination of
colors.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant blue above and yellow below. Large size.
Unmistakable. Occasional escaped
birds in United States.
