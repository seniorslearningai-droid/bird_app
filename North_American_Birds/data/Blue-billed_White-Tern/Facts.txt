Blue-billed White-Tern (Gygis microrhyncha)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pure white tern. All white with blue-based
black-tipped bill, large dark eye. Delicate
and fairy-like appearance. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Lays single egg on bare branch without nest. Picks
fish from surface. Found on Pacific atolls
and Hawaiian islands.

Habitat
-------
Low tropical Pacific atolls and islands. Found in
Hawaiian Northwestern Islands and other
Pacific atolls.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Fairy Tern similar. Blue-billed has blue bill base.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white plumage with blue-based bill. Large dark
eye. Fairy-like flight over
tropical Pacific islands.
