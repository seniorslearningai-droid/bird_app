Blue-black Grassquit (Volatinia jacarina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch. Male brilliant blue-black throughout. Female
streaked brown. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Male displays by leaping vertically singing. Forages
for grass seeds. Rare vagrant
to southern Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, weedy fields, and roadsides from
Mexico to South America. Rare
vagrant to Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting male brighter blue. No other tiny
all-blue-black finch in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male tiny and all blue-black. Vertical leaping display.
Rare Texas vagrant. Female
streaked brown.
