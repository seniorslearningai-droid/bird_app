Blue-crowned Parakeet (Thectocercus acuticaudatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large parakeet, green with blue crown and face, long
pointed tail, reddish bill.
About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious in flocks. Noisy. Feral populations in
southern Florida and Miami area.

Habitat
-------
Dry woodland and savanna. Feral populations in
Florida. Native to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mitred Parakeet has red face. White-eyed Parakeet
has white eye ring. Blue
crown distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Blue crown and face on green parakeet. Long tail.
Florida feral population.
