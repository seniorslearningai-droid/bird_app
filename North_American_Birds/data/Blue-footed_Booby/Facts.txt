Blue-footed Booby (Sula nebouxii)
===================================

Description
-----------
Large seabird with unmistakable bright blue feet. Brown and white plumage,
long pointed wings, pointed tail. Males have smaller pupil than females.
About 32 inches. Bill is bluish-gray, pointed.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives from height for fish, hitting water like a missile. Males display
their blue feet in courtship dances. Nests on ground on oceanic islands.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific Ocean. Rare visitor to California coast, especially in warm
water years. Breeds primarily on Galapagos and Mexican Pacific islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Booby has brown hood and yellow feet. Northern Gannet is all white with
black wingtips and yellow head. Masked Booby has cleaner white body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright blue feet are unmistakable — nothing else looks like this. Brown and
white plumage. Plunge-diving behavior. Rare vagrant to California coast.
