Blue-gray Noddy (Procelsterna cerulea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small delicate seabird. Pale blue-gray plumage with
darker wingtips, white eye ring, black bill and
legs. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Picks small fish and invertebrates from water surface.
Breeds on Hawaiian and Pacific atolls. Gentle
and delicate in flight.

Habitat
-------
Low-lying tropical Pacific islands and atolls. Breeds
on Hawaiian Northwestern Islands and other
Pacific atolls.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White Terns similar habitat. Blue-gray Noddy's pale
blue-gray color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale blue-gray plumage distinctive among tropical seabirds.
Delicate flight. Found on Hawaiian
Northwestern atolls.
