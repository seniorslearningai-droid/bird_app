Blue-gray Tanager (Thraupis episcopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tanager, pale blue-gray throughout with brighter
blue wings and tail. About
6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and conspicuous in gardens and parks. Forages
for fruit and insects. Introduced
to Miami, Florida.

Habitat
-------
Gardens, parks, forest edges, and suburban areas.
Introduced to Miami, Florida.
Native to Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-gray Gnatcatcher much smaller and slender. No
similar pale blue tanager in
its Florida range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale blue-gray throughout with brighter blue wings.
Introduced to Miami, Florida.
Bold garden bird.
