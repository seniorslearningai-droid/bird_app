Blue-headed Vireo (Vireo solitarius)
======================================

Description
-----------
Olive-green back, blue-gray head, bold white spectacles (eye ring + loral
stripe), white throat and belly, yellowish sides, and two white wing bars.

Behavior
--------
Sings persistently with rich slurred phrases; deliberately forages for
caterpillars and insects in foliage; hangs nest from a forked branch.

Habitat
-------
Mature mixed and coniferous forests of northeastern North America and
Appalachians; winters in the Southeast, Caribbean, and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cassin's and Plumbeous Vireos (formerly grouped as "Solitary Vireo");
Blue-headed has brightest white spectacles and most contrasting head pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright white spectacles on blue-gray head; olive back; persistent rich
melodious song; deliberate foraging in mixed forest canopy.
