Blue-throated Mountain-gem (Lampornis clemenciae)
==================================================

Description
-----------
Largest hummingbird in the United States. Males have bright blue throat (gorget),
green back, gray underparts, and bold white facial stripes. Large white tail
spots. Female has gray throat, white stripes. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on flower nectar and insects in mountain canyons. Males defend feeding
territories aggressively. Loud, squeaky call. Moves seasonally with flowers.

Habitat
-------
Mountain stream canyons with sycamores and maples in southeastern Arizona
and southwestern New Mexico. Found at middle elevations in pine-oak zones.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rivoli's Hummingbird is larger with purple crown. Black-chinned Hummingbird
is smaller with purple-black throat. Female mountain-gem has white facial stripes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest U.S. hummingbird. Bold white facial stripes on both sexes. Blue throat
on male. White tail spots. Found in southern Arizona mountain stream canyons.
