Blue-winged Teal (Spatula discors)
====================================

Description
-----------
Small duck; male has blue-gray head with white crescent in front of eye,
brown-spotted body, and iridescent green speculum; female is mottled brown.

Behavior
--------
Early fall migrant; one of the first ducks to head south; flies in tight
swift flocks; feeds on aquatic plants and invertebrates in shallow water.

Habitat
-------
Shallow freshwater marshes, prairie potholes, and flooded fields across
North America; winters from the southern U.S. to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cinnamon Teal male is deep reddish-brown; female Blue-winged resembles
other female dabblers — look for the blue forewing patch in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Blue forewing patch visible in flight; male's white facial crescent; small
size; early fall departure; prairie marsh habitat.
