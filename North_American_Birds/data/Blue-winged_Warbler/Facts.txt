Blue-winged Warbler (Vermivora cyanoptera)
===========================================

Description
-----------
Yellow head and underparts with black eye line; blue-gray wings with two
white wing bars; white undertail; slim body; fairly long thin bill.

Behavior
--------
Forages in shrubby second growth; song is a buzzy inhaled-exhaled "bee-bzzz";
hybridizes with Golden-winged Warbler to produce "Brewster's" and "Lawrence's" hybrids.

Habitat
-------
Shrubby forest edges, overgrown fields, and brushy hillsides of the eastern
U.S.; winters in Central America; declining in areas of Golden-winged contact.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Golden-winged Warbler has black throat and cheek patch; hybrids show
intermediate characters; Yellow Warbler lacks black eye line and wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow face and underparts with black eye line, blue-gray wings, two white
wing bars, and distinctive buzzy "bee-bzzz" song.
