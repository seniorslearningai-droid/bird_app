Blue Bunting (Cyanocompsa parellina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male deep blue throughout with brighter
blue supercilium and rump.
Female plain brown. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dense scrub. Rare vagrant to Texas from
northeastern Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub and brushy woodland of northeastern
Mexico. Rare vagrant to
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting smaller and lighter blue. Blue Grosbeak
larger with wing bars. Deep
blue color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Deep rich blue male throughout. Brighter blue on
brow and rump. Female plain
brown. Rare Texas vagrant.
