Blue Grosbeak (Passerina caerulea)
====================================

Description
-----------
Male is deep rich blue with two chestnut wing bars and a large silver bill;
female is warm brown with buffy wing bars and a large bill.

Behavior
--------
Forages on seeds in weedy fields; males sing a long rich warbled song from
elevated perches; flicks and spreads tail; often in brushy edge habitats.

Habitat
-------
Shrubby grasslands, weedy fields, hedgerows, and woodland edges of the
southern U.S.; winters in Central America; expanding range northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting is smaller and lacks chestnut wing bars; Eastern Bluebird has
rusty breast and is not deep blue overall; female resembles female Indigo.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's rich deep blue with chestnut wing bars and large silver bill; tail-
flicking habit; brushy edge habitat in southern and central U.S.
