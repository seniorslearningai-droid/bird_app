Blue Jay (Cyanocitta cristata)
================================

Description
-----------
Bright blue above with white underparts; bold black necklace; blue crest; black
bar on wing and tail; white wing bars and tail corners; blue, black, and white.

Behavior
--------
Loud and bold; mimics hawk calls; caches thousands of acorns annually,
helping forest regeneration; intelligent; aggressive at feeders.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests, parks, suburbs, and backyards throughout the
eastern and central U.S.; year-round resident; some migrate.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Steller's Jay (western) has dark sooty head and body; scrub-jays lack the
crest; Western Scrub-Jay is blue and gray without a crest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright blue crest, blue-and-white pattern, bold black necklace, and loud
"jay-jay" screaming call; extremely common in eastern North America.
