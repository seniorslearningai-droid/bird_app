Blue Mockingbird (Melanotis caerulescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, all deep blue with black mask and
red eye. Long tail, curved
bill. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in dense scrub. Mimics other
species. Rare vagrant to Arizona
and New Mexico from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub, forest undergrowth, and brushy hillsides
of Mexico. Rare vagrant to
Arizona and New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-gray Gnatcatcher much smaller. No other all-blue
bird with these proportions
in North America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-blue plumage with black mask and red eye.
Long tail. Rare Mexican vagrant
to Arizona scrub.
