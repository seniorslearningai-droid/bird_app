Blue Rock-Thrush (Monticola solitarius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush. Male deep blue throughout with darker
wings. Female brown with
scaled pattern. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches on rocky outcrops and buildings. Sings melodiously
from exposed perch. Rare vagrant
to North America.

Habitat
-------
Rocky cliffs, coastal bluffs, and buildings in
Europe, Asia, and Africa. Rare
vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rufous-tailed Rock-Thrush male has orange underparts.
Blue Grosbeak smaller with
wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male uniformly deep blue on rocky habitat. Female
scaled brown. Very rare
vagrant from Europe/Asia.
