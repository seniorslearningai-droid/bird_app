Bluethroat (Luscinia svecica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small robin-like bird. Male has brilliant blue throat
with red or white central
spot. Breeds in western Alaska.

Behavior
--------
Hops through dense willow scrub. Males sing complex
song from exposed perch.
Cocks and fans rufous-based tail.

Habitat
-------
Dense willow and alder scrub along rivers in
western and northern Alaska. Winters
in Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
European Robin has orange-red face, different range.
No other blue-throated bird
in Alaska willow scrub.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant blue throat with central spot on male.
Rufous tail base. Western
Alaska breeder. Bobs and fans tail.
