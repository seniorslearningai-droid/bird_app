Blyth's Reed Warbler (Acrocephalus dumetorum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain brown warbler, olive-brown above, buff below
with slightly darker breast sides.
Pale supercilium. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense scrub and hedges. Slow deliberate
movements. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub, hedgerows, and garden vegetation in
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Marsh Warbler and Reed Warbler very similar.
Differences in primary
projection and song.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain brown warbler with short primary projection.
Alaska vagrant. Indistinct
supercilium. Very difficult identification.
