Boat-tailed Grackle (Quiscalus major)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large grackle. Male iridescent blue-black with huge
keel-shaped tail. Female brown.
About 16 inches (male).

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious, noisy. Forages on beaches and
marshes. Male displays with wings
drooped and tail cocked.

Habitat
-------
Coastal marshes, beaches, and adjacent areas along
Atlantic and Gulf coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great-tailed Grackle very similar. Boat-tailed has
dark eye (in most of range)
vs yellow eye.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Huge keel-shaped tail. Eye color: dark in most of
range. Coastal saltmarsh habitat
specialist.
