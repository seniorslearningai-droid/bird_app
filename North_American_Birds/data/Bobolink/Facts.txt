Bobolink (Dolichonyx oryzivorus)
==================================

Description
-----------
Breeding male is unique — black below with white back and rump, creamy-yellow
nape; female and fall male are streaked buffy-brown with striped crown.

Behavior
--------
Males perform display flight over meadows giving bubbling metallic song;
long-distance migrant to South America; flocks in rice fields in fall.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, hayfields, and meadows of northern North America in summer;
migrates to South America via the eastern U.S. coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird has the male's inverted black-below, white-above pattern;
females resemble sparrows — look for wide buffy eyebrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unique male with black underparts and white back; bubbling metallic flight
song over meadows; large flocks in grasslands during migration.
