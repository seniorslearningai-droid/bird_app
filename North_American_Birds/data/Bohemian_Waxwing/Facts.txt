Bohemian Waxwing (Bombycilla garrulus)
========================================

Description
-----------
Sleek crested gray-brown bird with black mask and throat; yellow tail tip;
red waxy wing tips; rusty undertail coverts; yellow and white wing markings.

Behavior
--------
Nomadic flocks roam in search of berries; irrupts into the U.S. in winter
when northern berry crops fail; trills constantly; gorges on mountain ash and crabapples.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forests of northern North America and Eurasia; irruptive winter visitor
to northern U.S. cities and towns when berry crops are scarce.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cedar Waxwing is smaller and browner with yellow belly and lacks rusty
undertail; both species sometimes flock together in winter.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty undertail coverts (Cedar Waxwing has white), gray body, crest, red
wax wing tips; nomadic winter flocks stripping berry trees.
