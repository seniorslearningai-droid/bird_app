Bonaparte's Gull (Chroicocephalus philadelphia)
================================================

Description
-----------
Small graceful gull with black bill, pale gray back, white underparts, and
black hood in summer; black bill and ear spot in winter; flashing white wing wedges.

Behavior
--------
Buoyant tern-like flight; dips and plunges to catch small fish; migrates in
large flocks; nests in spruce trees near boreal lakes — unusual for a gull.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forest lakes in summer; migrates along both coasts and interior rivers
and lakes; winters on coasts, bays, and large lakes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Laughing Gull is much larger; Little Gull is smaller with dark underwings;
Black-headed Gull has a red bill; Franklin's Gull has half-hood pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny gull with white triangular wing flash in flight, black bill, buoyant
flight style, and large migrating flocks; common on fall coasts.
