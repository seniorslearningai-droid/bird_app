Bonin Petrel (Pterodroma hypoleuca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gadfly petrel, pale gray above with dark M-pattern,
white underparts, grayish sides
of breast. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern Islands. Common in
Hawaiian offshore waters; occasional
off mainland Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
North central Pacific Ocean. Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern
Islands; forages in Hawaiian
offshore waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cook's Petrel similar. Bonin Petrel has more extensive
gray on sides of breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small pale gadfly petrel with gray breast sides. Dark
M-pattern above. Hawaiian
Northwestern Islands breeder.
