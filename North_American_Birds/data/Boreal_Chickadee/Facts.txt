Boreal Chickadee (Poecile hudsonicus)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Small chickadee with brown cap (not black), brown back, black bib, gray cheeks,
and rich rusty-brown flanks; dull overall compared to Black-capped.

Behavior
--------
Tame and curious; often found in small flocks; caches food; moves south in
irruption years; nasal "tch-day-day" call distinct from Black-capped.

Habitat
-------
Boreal spruce-fir forests of Canada and the northern United States; rarely
strays south except in winter irruptions; closely tied to conifers.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Chickadee has black (not brown) cap and back, whiter cheeks,
and different call; Chestnut-backed Chickadee has richer chestnut back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown cap instead of black, rusty flanks, nasal "tch-day" call, and
boreal conifer forest habitat; less contrasting than Black-capped.
