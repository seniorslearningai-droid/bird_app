Boreal Owl (Aegolius funereus)
================================

Description
-----------
Small owl with large square head, chocolate brown spotted with white above,
white face disk with dark border, yellow eyes, and white-streaked brown breast.

Behavior
--------
Strictly nocturnal; hunts small mammals in dense boreal forest; irrupts
southward in winter; male's prolonged tooting call carries far.

Habitat
-------
Dense boreal spruce-fir forests of Canada and high-elevation mountains in
the West; difficult to find; roosts in cavities during the day.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Saw-whet Owl is smaller with unspotted forehead; flammulated Owl
is tiny; Tengmalm's Owl is the Eurasian equivalent.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Square head with dark-bordered white face disk, spotted crown, yellow eyes;
dense northern conifer forest; nocturnal; tooting advertising call in spring.
