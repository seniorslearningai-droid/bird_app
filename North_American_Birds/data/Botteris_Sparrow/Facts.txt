Botteri's Sparrow (Peucaea botterii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, streaked brown above, plain buff
below, flat head, long rounded
tail. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings from grass clumps or low shrubs. Very difficult
to see on ground.
Restricted to specific grass habitats.

Habitat
-------
Native sacaton grass flats and coastal grass fields
of southern Arizona and southern
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cassin's Sparrow sings elaborate display flight. Bachman's
Sparrow has rusty back.
Grasshopper Sparrow has flat head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain buff below, streaked back, flat head. Specific
sacaton grass habitat in
Arizona. Bouncing ball song.
