Brambling (Fringilla montifringilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch, black head and back in breeding, orange
shoulder and breast, white
rump. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in beech woodland and fields.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and occasionally farther east.

Habitat
-------
Birch and mixed forest of Eurasia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska; occasional
further into North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Chaffinch has no orange shoulder and different
rump pattern. Different body
pattern overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange shoulder and white rump distinctive. Black
head in breeding. Rare Alaska
vagrant from Siberia.
