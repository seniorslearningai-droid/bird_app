Brandt's Cormorant (Urile penicillatus)
========================================

Description
-----------
Large dark seabird with black plumage and buffy throat pouch; breeding adults
show bright blue throat pouch and tan-buff chin; white filoplume feathers.

Behavior
--------
Dives from the surface to pursue fish underwater; roosts communally on offshore
rocks; breeds in large coastal colonies; silent except at nest.

Habitat
-------
Pacific Coast of North America from Alaska to Baja California; feeds offshore
in cold upwelling waters; nests on rocky islands and sea stacks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pelagic Cormorant is smaller with iridescent green gloss and red face skin;
Double-crested Cormorant has orange-yellow face skin and often found inland.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-dark body with blue throat pouch (breeding), buffy chin, and Pacific
Coast ocean habitat; often in large flocks on sea rocks.
