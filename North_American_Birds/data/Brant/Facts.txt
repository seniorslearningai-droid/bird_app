Brant (Branta bernicla)
========================

Description
-----------
Small dark goose with black head, neck, and breast; white neck patch;
dark brown back; pale gray-white belly; short bill; stubby appearance.

Behavior
--------
Highly dependent on eelgrass; coastal; migrates in low flocks over water;
rarely found inland; gives low rolling "r-r-r-ruk" calls in flocks.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on high Arctic tundra; winters on coastal bays, estuaries, and tidal
flats where eelgrass grows; Pacific and Atlantic populations differ slightly.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canada Goose is much larger with white chin strap; Cackling Goose is smaller
but has white cheek patch; Black Brant (Pacific) has darker belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small dark goose with black head and neck, white neck patch, found in
coastal salt bays in winter; low rolling calls; never far from saltwater.
