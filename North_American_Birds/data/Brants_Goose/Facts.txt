Brant (Branta bernicla)
========================

Description
-----------
Small, dark goose with black head, neck, and breast. White neck patch (not
complete collar). Dark brown back, pale flanks, white undertail. About 25
inches. Stubby bill. Two subspecies differ in belly color.

Behavior
--------
Highly specialized on eelgrass and sea lettuce. Forms large flocks in coastal
bays. Strong migrant between Arctic breeding grounds and coastal wintering areas.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra for breeding; coastal bays, estuaries, and tidal flats in winter.
Pacific coast birds winter in Baja California lagoons. Atlantic birds winter on
mid-Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canada Goose is much larger with white cheek patch. Cackling Goose has white
cheek patch. Barnacle Goose has white face. Brant has all-dark head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small, dark-headed goose in coastal saltwater. Black head, neck and breast.
White neck patch (not complete). Flocks fly in irregular bunches. Coastal winter.
