Brewer's Blackbird (Euphagus cyanocephalus)
============================================

Description
-----------
Male is glossy purplish-black on head and greenish-black on body with
yellow eyes; female is plain brown-gray with dark eyes.

Behavior
--------
Walks on ground probing for food near livestock and in parking lots; forms
large flocks; bold and approachable; common in open country.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, farmland, parking lots, parks, and urban areas of western
North America; winters across the South and sometimes northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Grackle has longer keel-shaped tail; Rusty Blackbird is similar but
has fall rusty edges and brown eye; Brown-headed Cowbird has brown head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's glossy purple head and yellow eye walking confidently on the ground;
female's plain brownish-gray with dark eye; western range; flocks.
