Brewer's Sparrow (Spizella breweri)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Small plain sparrow with fine brown streaking on crown and back, thin white
eye ring, light brown face, and white wing bars; very nondescript.

Behavior
--------
Forages on the ground in sagebrush; male sings long buzzy trilling canary-like
song; often difficult to find despite the lengthy song.

Habitat
-------
Sagebrush steppes of the Great Basin and western mountains; one of the most
sagebrush-dependent birds in North America; winters in desert shrublands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Clay-colored Sparrow has stronger face pattern; Chipping Sparrow has
rusty cap; Field Sparrow has pink bill and plain face; all are more patterned.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain fine-streaked sparrow in sagebrush; thin white eye ring; long
trilling canary-like song; the plainest sparrow in sagebrush country.
