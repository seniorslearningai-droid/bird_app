Bridled Tern (Onychoprion anaethetus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern. Dark gray-brown back and wings, black
cap with white forehead extending to eye, white
underparts. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Picks fish from surface; rarely dives. Breeds on
tropical islands. Regular in Gulf Stream waters
off southeastern US.

Habitat
-------
Tropical and subtropical oceanic waters. Regular in
Gulf Stream off Atlantic coast. Breeds on
Dry Tortugas and Caribbean islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray-backed Tern paler. Sooty Tern darker overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark brown-gray back with black cap and white
forehead. White extends past eye to nape.
Regular in Gulf Stream.
