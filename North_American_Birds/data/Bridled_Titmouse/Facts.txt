Bridled Titmouse (Baeolophus wollweberi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small crested titmouse with distinctive black and
white bridled face pattern. Gray
above, white below. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in oak and pine-oak woodlands.
Often joins mixed-species flocks.
Chickadee-like behavior but with crest.

Habitat
-------
Oak and pine-oak woodlands of the Madrean sky
islands in Arizona and New Mexico
south into Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other titmice lack bold black-and-white face pattern.
No other titmouse in its
sky island range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive black-and-white bridle face pattern
with pointed crest. Small size.
Sky island oak woodland habitat.
