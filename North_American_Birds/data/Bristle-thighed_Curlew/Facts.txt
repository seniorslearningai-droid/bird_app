Bristle-thighed Curlew (Numenius tahitiensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large curlew. Brown streaked plumage with buff-orange
rump and tail, downcurved bill. Named for
bristle-like thigh feathers. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Only shorebird known to use tools; rolls eggs with
stones. Breeds in Alaska; winters on Pacific
islands. Rare vagrant on Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Alaskan tundra. Migrates across open Pacific
Ocean. Winters on Pacific islands including
Hawaii and Fiji.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whimbrel similar but lacks orange rump. Bristle-thighed
has buffy rump visible in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Warm buff-orange rump patch visible in flight.
Larger than Whimbrel with warmer overall
tones. Breeds only in Alaska.
