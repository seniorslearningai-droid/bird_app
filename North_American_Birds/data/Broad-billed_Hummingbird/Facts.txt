Broad-billed Hummingbird (Cynanthus latirostris)
=================================================

Description
-----------
Male has brilliant blue-green gorget and underparts, green back, red bill with
black tip. Female is greenish above, gray below with white eye stripe and red-
based bill. About 4 inches. Notched tail.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on flower nectar from a variety of blooms. Hovers and also perch-feeds.
Males chase intruders with chattering calls. Visible tail pumping while feeding.

Habitat
-------
Desert canyons with streams, sycamores, and flowering plants in southeastern
Arizona. Also suburban gardens. Extends into Mexico. Some year-round residents.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-throated Mountain-gem is much larger. Magnificent Hummingbird has purple
crown. Berylline Hummingbird has rufous wings. Female similar to other female hummers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red bill with black tip (both sexes) is key. Male's brilliant blue-green color.
Female has distinctive red-based bill and clean gray underparts. Canyon habitat.
