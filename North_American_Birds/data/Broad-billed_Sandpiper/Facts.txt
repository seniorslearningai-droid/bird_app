Broad-billed Sandpiper (Calidris falcinellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sandpiper with distinctive broad kinked bill.
Dark brown streaked upperparts with double white
supercilium, streaked breast. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats picking invertebrates. Rare vagrant
to North America from Eurasian breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Scandinavian and Siberian bogs. Winters
on Asian and African coasts. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dunlin similar size. Broad-billed has distinctive
double supercilium and bent bill tip.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Kinked bill tip (bent downward at end) distinctive.
Double white supercilium. Dark upperparts.
Rare vagrant.
