Broad-tailed Hummingbird (Selasphorus platycercus)
===================================================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird with iridescent rose-magenta gorget on male; green back;
white breast; broad tail; female is green above with buffy-white below.

Behavior
--------
Males produce a distinctive loud metallic cricket-like wing trill in flight;
breeds in mountain meadows; visits red tubular flowers and sugar-water feeders.

Habitat
-------
Mountain meadows, pine-oak forests, and riparian zones of the Rocky Mountains
and Great Basin; winters in Mexico; common at mountain feeders.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rufous Hummingbird male has orange-rufous sides; Calliope is smaller;
Anna's Hummingbird is a West Coast resident with different habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's loud metallic wing trill is unique and diagnostic; rose gorget;
common at mountain feeders in the West; green-and-white female in mountains.
