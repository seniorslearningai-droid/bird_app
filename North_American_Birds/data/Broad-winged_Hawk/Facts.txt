Broad-winged Hawk (Buteo platypterus)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Small crow-sized hawk with brown back, reddish-brown barring on breast,
bold black-and-white tail bands, and broad wings with pointed tips.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in spectacular "kettles" of thousands at hawk watch sites; summers
quietly in forests; calls with a thin high-pitched whistle.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests of eastern North America in summer; migrates in enormous
concentrations; winters in Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-tailed Hawk is much larger; Red-shouldered Hawk has orange barring and
longer tail; Cooper's Hawk has rounded tail and accipiter shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Broad black-and-white tail bands; spectacular fall migration kettles;
small buteo size; thin whistle call; deciduous forest in summer.
