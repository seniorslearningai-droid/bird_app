Bronzed Cowbird (Molothrus aeneus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium cowbird, male all glossy bronze-black with
red eye and neck ruff. Female
gray-brown. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Brood parasite. Male displays with distinctive hunchback
posture and inflated neck
ruff. Expanding range.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, farmland, and woodland edges in southwestern
United States, Texas, and
Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown-headed Cowbird male has brown head. Shiny Cowbird
lacks neck ruff. Red
eye distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red eye on all-black male. Neck ruff inflated in
display. Hunchback posture.
Southwestern United States.
