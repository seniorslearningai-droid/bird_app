Brown-backed Solitaire (Myadestes occidentalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush-like bird, brown above, gray below,
white eye ring, orange-based
bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Beautiful, haunting song from forest understory.
Rare vagrant to Arizona and
Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Mountain forests of Mexico and Central America.
Very rare vagrant to Arizona
sky islands and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Townsend's Solitaire has white outer tail feathers
and buff wing patches.
Different family relationship.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eye ring, brown above, gray below. Haunting
beautiful song. Rare Mexican
vagrant to mountain forests.
