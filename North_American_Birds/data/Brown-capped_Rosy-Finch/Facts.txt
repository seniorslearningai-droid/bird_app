Brown-capped Rosy-Finch (Leucosticte australis)
================================================

Description
-----------
Medium finch endemic to Colorado mountains. Brown cap (not black or gray).
Rose-pink wash on wings, rump, and belly. Males brighter; females duller.
About 6 inches. Pale bill in winter, dark in summer.

Behavior
--------
Forages on snowfields and talus for seeds and insects blown onto snow.
Forms flocks in winter moving to lower elevations. Nests in cliff crevices.

Habitat
-------
Alpine tundra and rocky mountaintops in Colorado and southern Wyoming. Found
above treeline. In winter, descends to foothill feeders and towns.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Rosy-Finch has black cap. Gray-crowned Rosy-Finch has gray crown and
brown body. Brown-capped has brown cap with no gray, endemic to Colorado.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown cap (no gray or black). Rose-pink on wings and rump. Alpine Colorado
habitat. In winter, visits feeders in mountain towns with other rosy-finches.
