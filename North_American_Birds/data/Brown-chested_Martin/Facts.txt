Brown-chested Martin (Progne tapera)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium martin, brown above, white below with brown
breast band and brown streaks
on sides. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aerial insect forager over open areas. Very rare
vagrant to eastern North America
from South America.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, farmland, and forest edges in South
America. Very rare vagrant to
northeastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bank Swallow smaller with narrower breast band.
Purple Martin male all-dark,
female has scaled throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown breast band, brown streaks on flanks. Brown
above. Larger than Bank Swallow.
Very rare vagrant from South America.
