Brown-crested Flycatcher (Myiarchus tyrannulus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Myiarchus flycatcher, brown above with rufous
tail, pale gray throat, yellow belly,
prominent crest. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gleans insects; nests in large tree cavities. Summer
resident in saguaro desert and
riparian forest of Southwest.

Habitat
-------
Saguaro desert, cottonwood riparian forest. Southwestern
US summer resident; winters
in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ash-throated Flycatcher smaller. Brown-crested larger
with heavier bill and brighter yellow belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest Myiarchus flycatcher in range. Heavy bill,
bright yellow belly, rufous tail.
Saguaro desert specialist.
