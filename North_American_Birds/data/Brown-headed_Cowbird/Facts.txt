Brown-headed Cowbird (Molothrus ater)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Male has glossy black body with brown head; female is plain gray-brown with
faint streaking; short conical bill; stubby tail; walks with head bobbing.

Behavior
--------
Brood parasite — lays eggs in other birds' nests, abandoning eggs for hosts
to raise; follows cattle to catch disturbed insects; forms large flocks.

Habitat
-------
Open country, farmland, forest edges, and suburbs throughout North America;
followed bison historically; now hugely expanded with forest fragmentation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
European Starling is similar at a distance but has longer bill and no brown
head; Rusty Blackbird and Brewer's Blackbird have yellow eyes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's brown head on glossy black body; female's plain gray-brown; following
cattle or horses; short bill; common brood parasite everywhere.
