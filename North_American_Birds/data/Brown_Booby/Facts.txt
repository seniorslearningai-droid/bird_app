Brown Booby (Sula leucogaster)
================================

Description
-----------
Large seabird with chocolate-brown hood, neck, and upperparts contrasting
sharply with clean white belly and underwings. Yellow feet, blue-gray bill
in adults. About 30 inches. Pointed wings and tail.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives from height for flying fish and squid. Rests on buoys, boats,
and rocky outcroppings. Breeds on tropical islands in ground colonies.

Habitat
-------
Tropical and subtropical oceans worldwide. Rare but regular in Gulf of Mexico
and off California. Wanders north after tropical storms. Nests on Caribbean islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-footed Booby has blue feet and brown-and-white wings (not clean contrast).
Masked Booby has white body with black mask and wingtips. Northern Gannet is white.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sharp brown-white contrast between hood and belly. Yellow feet. Plunge-diving
behavior. Tropical oceans and Gulf waters. Often perches on boats or buoys.
