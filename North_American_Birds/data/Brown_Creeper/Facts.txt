Brown Creeper (Certhia americana)
==================================

Description
-----------
Tiny brown bird with heavily streaked brown-and-buff plumage above;
white underparts; long curved bill; stiff pointed tail used for support.

Behavior
--------
Spirals upward on tree trunks probing bark crevices for insects; flies to
base of next tree; extremely well camouflaged; thin high "seeee" call.

Habitat
-------
Mature forests with large trees throughout North America; more common in
winter when joins mixed flocks; nests behind bark plates of dead trees.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American bird creeps up tree bark in quite this way; nuthatches
go down tree trunks; wrens are brighter and shorter-tailed.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Spiraling up tree trunks with curved bill; heavily streaked brown camouflage;
thin high "seeee" call; stiff tail pressed against bark.
