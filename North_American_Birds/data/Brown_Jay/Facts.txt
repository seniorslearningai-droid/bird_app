Brown Jay (Psilorhinus morio)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large, noisy jay, dark brown above and below
with white belly. Yellow bill, blue
eye ring. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious, travels in noisy flocks. Cooperative
breeder with helpers at nest.
Very loud and raucous calls.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forests, woodlands, and forest edges from
Mexico to Panama. Small population
resident in extreme southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar species in its range. Much larger
than other jays. Dark brown
color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large dark brown jay with white belly. Very
noisy and gregarious. Yellow
bill. South Texas resident.
