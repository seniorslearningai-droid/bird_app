Brown Noddy (Anous stolidus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern-like seabird. All dark brown with pale
white cap and white crescent below eye. Long
graduated tail. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Picks fish from surface; rarely dives. Nests in
colonies on tropical islands. Breeds on Dry Tortugas,
Florida.

Habitat
-------
Tropical oceanic islands. Breeds on Dry Tortugas
in Florida and Caribbean islands. Forages offshore
in warm tropical waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Noddy smaller with all-black plumage. Brown
Noddy browner with more extensive pale cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All brown except pale cap. Long graduated tail.
Breeds on Dry Tortugas, Florida.
Tropical seabird.
