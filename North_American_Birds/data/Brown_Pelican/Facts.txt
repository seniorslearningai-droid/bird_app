Brown Pelican (Pelecanus occidentalis)
========================================

Description
-----------
Large seabird with dark brown body, pale yellowish-white head, and enormous
pouched bill; breeding adults have chestnut nape; wingspan up to 7 feet.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives from height into ocean for fish, filling pouch then draining it;
flies in formation just above waves; colonies nest on coastal islands.

Habitat
-------
Coastal ocean, bays, estuaries, and fishing piers along both coasts and Gulf;
once nearly wiped out by DDT, now fully recovered and common.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American White Pelican is all white and doesn't plunge-dive; Double-crested
Cormorant is smaller, darker, and has an orange face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown body with huge pouched bill; spectacular plunge-dives into the ocean;
formation flight over waves; ubiquitous along warm coastal waters.
