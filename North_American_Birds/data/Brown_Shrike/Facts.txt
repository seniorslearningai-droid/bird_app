Brown Shrike (Lanius cristatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shrike, warm brown above, buff below with
fine barring. Black mask through eye,
white supercilium. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches on exposed branches watching for prey.
Impales insects and small vertebrates
on thorns. Rare vagrant to Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Open country, forest edges, and scrubland in Asia.
Rare but regular vagrant to
western Alaska and occasionally farther east.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-backed Shrike similar but male has gray head.
Loggerhead Shrike gray
above with heavy bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Warm brown tones throughout with buff underparts.
Black mask, white brow.
Perches upright on exposed branches.
