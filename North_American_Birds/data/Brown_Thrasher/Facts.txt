Brown Thrasher (Toxostoma rufum)
==================================

Description
-----------
Large thrush-like bird with rich rufous-brown upperparts, bold dark brown
streaking on white underparts, long tail, curved bill, and yellow eyes.

Behavior
--------
Forages by tossing leaf litter with its bill; males sing one of the largest
songbird repertoires — over 1,000 song types; secretive in thickets.

Habitat
-------
Dense shrubby thickets, forest edges, and hedgerows of eastern North America;
uncommon in the open; state bird of Georgia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wood Thrush has round spots (not streaks) and shorter tail; Hermit Thrush
is smaller with spotted breast; Long-billed Thrasher is found in Texas.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rich rufous-brown above, heavily streaked below, long tail, curved bill,
yellow eyes; thickets in the East; sings in doubled phrases.
