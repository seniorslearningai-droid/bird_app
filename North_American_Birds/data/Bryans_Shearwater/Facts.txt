Bryan's Shearwater (Puffinus bryani)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny shearwater, black above, white below. Smallest
shearwater in Pacific. Newly described
species. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
One of world's rarest birds; fewer than 10 known
specimens. Breeds on small Pacific
island; very poorly known.

Habitat
-------
Central North Pacific. Extremely rare; only known
from small islands in Pacific.
Among least-known birds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small black-and-white shearwaters larger. Bryan's
Shearwater smallest Pacific shearwater.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, black above with white below. Extremely
rare; one of world's least-known
bird species.
