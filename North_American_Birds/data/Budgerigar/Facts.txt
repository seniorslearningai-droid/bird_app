Budgerigar (Melopsittacus undulatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small parrot, wild type green and yellow with black
barring, blue cheek patches. Captive
birds vary. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nomadic flocking parrot of Australian grasslands.
Established feral population in southwestern
Florida (now declining).

Habitat
-------
Australian grassland and scrub. Former feral population
in southwestern Florida; very
few remain.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No wild parakeet has the same barred pattern with
blue cheek patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Wild-type green and yellow with black barring, blue
cheeks. Very small parrot. Former
Florida feral population.
