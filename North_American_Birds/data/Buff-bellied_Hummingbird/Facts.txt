Buff-bellied Hummingbird (Amazilia yucatanensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Iridescent green throat and
upperparts, buffy-cinnamon belly, red bill with
dark tip, rufous tail. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar; aggressive at feeders. Nests in
southern Texas. Moves north along Gulf Coast
after breeding in some years.

Habitat
-------
Woodland edges, riparian areas, and gardens in
Lower Rio Grande Valley, Texas, and south along
Gulf Coast of Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Berylline Hummingbird. Buff-bellied has rufous tail
and clean separation of green and buff colors.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Green throat contrasting with buff-cinnamon belly.
Rufous tail visible in flight. Red bill.
Found in Texas near Mexican border.
