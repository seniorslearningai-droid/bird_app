Buff-breasted Flycatcher (Empidonax fulvifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny Empidonax flycatcher with distinctive pale buff-
orange wash on breast. Small size,
two wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in open pine woodland. Regular rare summer resident
in Chiricahua and Huachuca mountain
ranges of southeastern Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Open pine woodland in mountains of southeastern Arizona.
Rare but regular summer
resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Empidonax lack strong buff wash on breast.
Buff-breasted has distinctive warm buff tone.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Warm buff wash on breast and belly unique among Empidonax.
Small size. Southeastern Arizona
mountain pine forest.
