Buff-breasted Sandpiper (Calidris subruficollis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper with buffy face, neck and underparts,
scaly brown back, yellow legs. Short bill.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dry grasslands and short grass fields,
not mudflats. Males display at leks. Migrates
through Great Plains.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Migrates through Great
Plains grasslands. Winters on South American
pampas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruff (non-breeding) similar buffy tones. Buff-breasted
uniform buff with scaly brown back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entirely buffy face and underparts with scaly brown
back. Grassland habitat. Yellow
legs. Elegant long-necked appearance.
