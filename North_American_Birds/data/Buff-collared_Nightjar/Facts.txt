Buff-collared Nightjar (Antrostomus ridgwayi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium nightjar. Brown, gray, and black cryptic
plumage with pale buff collar around hindneck.
About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal insect catcher. Roosts on ground by day.
Rare in US; found in dry rocky canyons of
southern Arizona and New Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dry rocky canyon slopes with sparse scrub in
southern Arizona, New Mexico, and adjacent Mexico.
Uncommon and local.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whip-poor-will similar patterning. Buff-collared has
distinctive pale buff collar on hindneck.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale buff hindneck collar distinctive among nightjars.
Dry rocky canyon habitat in Arizona.
Loud repeated nocturnal call.
