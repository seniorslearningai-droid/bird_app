Bufflehead (Bucephala albeola)
==============================

Description
-----------
A tiny diving duck. Males show bold black-and-white plumage
with a large iridescent green and purple head patch.
Females are brown with a small white cheek spot.

Behavior
--------
Dives frequently for aquatic invertebrates. Takes flight
easily from water. Often seen in small flocks. Monogamous
and returns to same nest sites yearly.

Habitat
-------
Breeds near forested lakes and ponds in Canada. Winters
along coasts, bays, and large inland lakes across
the United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Goldeneye is larger with a rounder head. Hooded
Merganser has a fan-shaped crest. Male Ruddy Duck is
reddish with a white cheek.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest North American diving duck. Males unmistakable
with large white head patch. Fast wingbeats create
a buzzing sound in flight.
