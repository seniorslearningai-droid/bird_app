Buller's Shearwater (Ardenna bulleri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant shearwater, pale gray above with bold dark
M-pattern, clean white below, dark
gray cap. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Poor Knights Islands, New Zealand. Migrates
north into Pacific; uncommon
offshore California in fall.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific in non-breeding season. Offshore California
and Oregon coast in fall.
Breeds on New Zealand islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cook's Petrel smaller. Buller's Shearwater larger with
bold M-pattern and clean white below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold dark M-pattern on pale gray upperwings. Clean
white below, dark cap. Elegant
Pacific shearwater offshore California.
