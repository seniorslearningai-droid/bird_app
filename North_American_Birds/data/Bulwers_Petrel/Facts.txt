Bulwer's Petrel (Bulweria bulwerii)
===========================

Description
-----------
All-dark petrel with long wedge-shaped tail. Brown-black
overall with pale diagonal wing bar.
About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Erratic bat-like flight low over water. Breeds on
Atlantic and Pacific tropical islands;
disperses widely.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. Offshore Hawaiian
waters; rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dark storm-petrels smaller. Bulwer's distinctive long
wedge tail unlike all storm-petrels.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long wedge-shaped tail unique. All-dark with pale
diagonal wing bar. Bat-like erratic
flight near ocean surface.
