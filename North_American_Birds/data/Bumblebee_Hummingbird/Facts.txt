Bumblebee Hummingbird (Atthis heloisa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny hummingbird, one of world's smallest birds.
Male has rose-purple gorget, green back, white
below with green spots. About 3 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar from small flowers. Extremely rare
vagrant to Arizona from Mexico. Easily overlooked
due to tiny size.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak mountain forests of Mexico. Extremely rare
vagrant to mountain canyons of
southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Calliope Hummingbird similar tiny size. Bumblebee male's
round gorget vs. streaked in Calliope.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely small size. Male's round rose-purple gorget
and short bill. Very rare vagrant
in Arizona mountains.
