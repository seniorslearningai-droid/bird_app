Burrowing Owl (Athene cunicularia)
===================================

Description
-----------
A small, long-legged owl standing about 9 inches tall.
Brown and white spotted plumage. Bright yellow eyes
and white eyebrows give it a perpetually surprised look.

Behavior
--------
Active day and night; often seen standing at burrow
entrance. Bobs head when alarmed. Nests underground
in old prairie dog or ground squirrel burrows.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, deserts, agricultural fields, and
golf courses. Found from western Canada through
Florida and throughout Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Short-eared Owl is larger and rarely stands on ground.
Ferruginous Pygmy-Owl lacks long legs. Burrowing Owl's
ground habitat is distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long legs, round head without ear tufts, and ground-
dwelling habit are key. Often perches on fence posts
near burrow entrances in open terrain.
