Burrowing Parakeet (Cyanoliseus patagonus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large parakeet, olive-brown above, yellow and red
belly patch, white breast patches.
About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in burrows in cliffs and embankments. Gregarious.
Very small feral population
in California.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands and cliffs. Tiny feral population
in California. Native to
Argentina and Chile.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Monk Parakeet all green. Conures more uniformly colored.
Olive-brown color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-brown with yellow and red belly. Burrow-nesting
habit. Very rare feral
bird in California.
