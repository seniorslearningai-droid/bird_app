Bushtit (Psaltriparus minimus)
==============================

Description
-----------
Tiny gray bird, about 4.5 inches long with a long tail.
Plain gray-brown overall. Males have dark eyes; females
have pale yellow eyes. No bold markings.

Behavior
--------
Highly social; travels in noisy flocks of 10-40 birds.
Gleans insects from leaves and branches. Builds
an intricate hanging sock-shaped nest.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, chaparral, parks, and suburban gardens
from southern British Columbia through Central America.
Avoids dense forests and open grasslands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Plain gray color can suggest gnatcatchers, but Bushtit
lacks the blue-gray tone and white eye ring. Its tiny
size and long tail distinguish it from most wrens.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, plain gray plumage, and long tail are
distinctive. Almost always found in active, chattering
flocks moving through shrubs.
