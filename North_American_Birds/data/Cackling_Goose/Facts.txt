Cackling Goose (Branta hutchinsii)
====================================

Description
-----------
Formerly considered a small Canada Goose race, now a separate species. Black
head and neck, white cheek patch, brown body, white undertail. Very small —
about 24 inches (much smaller than Canada Goose). Short, stubby bill.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grasses and grains in flocks. Migrates in large flocks, often with
Canada Geese. High-pitched, cackling call (higher than Canada Goose honk).

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Winters in Pacific lowlands, fields, and wetlands.
Common in California's Central Valley and Pacific Northwest in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canada Goose is larger with longer neck and bill. Brant has all-dark head.
Distinguishing from small Canada Goose subspecies can be very difficult.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Much smaller size and stubby bill compared to Canada Goose. Cackling, high-
pitched call. Short neck. Often mixed in flocks with Canada Geese in winter.
