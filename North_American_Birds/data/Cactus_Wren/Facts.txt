Cactus Wren (Campylorhynchus brunneicapillus)
=============================================

Description
-----------
Largest wren in North America, about 8 inches long.
Brown back with white streaks. Bold white eyebrow stripe.
Heavily spotted breast and barred wings and tail.

Behavior
--------
Loud and bold; calls frequently with a harsh, repetitive
churr. Builds bulky grass nests in cholla or saguaro
cacti. Defends territory year-round.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub and arid lowlands of the southwestern
United States and Mexico. Closely associated with
cacti, especially cholla and saguaro.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sage Thrasher is slimmer with a shorter bill and less
bold spotting. Curve-billed Thrasher has a longer
curved bill. Other wrens are much smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large for a wren, with bold white supercilium
and heavily spotted underparts. Its loud raspy call
and cactus habitat make it easy to find.
