California Condor (Gymnogyps californianus)
============================================

Description
-----------
Enormous black vulture with 9.5-foot wingspan. Bald orange-red
head on adults. Large white triangular patches on underwings.

Behavior
--------
Soars on thermals for hours without flapping. Feeds on large
carrion. Highly social; nests in cliff caves and large trees.

Habitat
-------
Rocky shrublands, coniferous forests, and oak savannas of
California, Arizona, Utah, and Baja California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Turkey Vulture is much smaller with a red head and two-toned
flight feathers. Black Vulture is smaller with white wingtips.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive size and wingspan are unmistakable. White underwing
triangles and bare orange head on soaring adults are definitive.
