California Gnatcatcher (Polioptila californica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny gnatcatcher, blue-gray above, gray below without
white. Black cap on male.
Mostly black tail. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely restricted to coastal sage scrub habitat.
Endangered. Pairs defend territory
year-round with cat-like calls.

Habitat
-------
Coastal sage scrub of southern California and
Baja California. Threatened by
development and invasive grasses.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-gray Gnatcatcher lighter below with white tail
edges. Black-tailed in desert.
Range and habitat important.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Coastal sage scrub of southern California. Darker
gray below than Blue-gray Gnatcatcher.
Cat-like mewing call.
