California Gull (Larus californicus)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Medium-large gull with white head and body, gray back,
and yellow bill with red and black spots near tip.

Behavior
--------
Omnivorous scavenger; follows plows and forages at landfills,
lakes, and coasts. Nests colonially on islands in inland lakes.

Habitat
-------
Breeds at inland lakes and marshes in the western U.S.
Winters along the Pacific Coast from British Columbia to Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Herring Gull is larger with a pinkish bill. Ring-billed Gull
is smaller with a complete black ring on the bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Medium size with yellow bill showing both red and black spots.
Dark eye gives a gentle expression compared to pale-eyed gulls.
