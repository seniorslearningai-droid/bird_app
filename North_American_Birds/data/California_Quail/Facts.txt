California Quail (Callipepla californica)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Plump, gray-brown quail with a teardrop-shaped black plume.
Males have a black face with white stripe; females are plain.

Behavior
--------
Travels in coveys of 10-200 birds. Scratches ground for seeds.
Runs rather than flies when threatened. Roosts in dense shrubs.

Habitat
-------
Chaparral, open woodlands, and scrubby parks from southern
British Columbia through Baja California. Common in suburbs.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gambel's Quail has a chestnut crown and rusty flanks. Mountain
Quail has a straight vertical plume and bold white side stripes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Teardrop topknot is distinctive. Males show black-and-white face
pattern. Often seen in large, noisy groups on the ground.
