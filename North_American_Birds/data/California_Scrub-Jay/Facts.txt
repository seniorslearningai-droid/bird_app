California Scrub-Jay (Aphelocoma californica)
==============================================

Description
-----------
Long-tailed jay with blue head, back, and tail. Gray-brown
back patch. White throat outlined by a blue necklace band.

Behavior
--------
Bold and inquisitive; visits feeders regularly. Caches acorns
for winter. Loud, raspy calls. Hops on ground to forage.

Habitat
-------
Coastal scrub, chaparral, oak woodlands, and suburban gardens
in the western United States and Pacific Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Steller's Jay has a crest and entirely dark head. Woodhouse's
Scrub-Jay is paler and limited to interior regions.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright blue with gray-brown back patch and no crest. White
supercilium and blue necklace are distinctive field marks.
