California Thrasher (Toxostoma redivivum)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Large, long-tailed brown bird with a very long, downcurved
bill. Pale buff underparts with a darker brown back.

Behavior
--------
Uses bill to dig and toss leaf litter for insects. Stays
low in dense shrubs. Loud, melodious, mimicking song.

Habitat
-------
Dense chaparral and coastal scrub of California and northern
Baja California. Rarely found outside its core range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Curve-billed Thrasher has spotted breast and spotted orange
eyes. Crissal Thrasher has rusty undertail. Both are inland.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely long, sharply curved bill and long tail are key.
Plain brown above with buff belly. Found only in California.
