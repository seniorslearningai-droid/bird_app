California Towhee (Melozone crissalis)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Large, plain brown sparrow with a long tail. Buffy-orange
throat patch outlined with dark streaks. Rusty undertail.

Behavior
--------
Forages on the ground by scratching in leaf litter. Stays
near dense cover. Often seen in pairs year-round.

Habitat
-------
Chaparral, brushy gardens, and oak woodlands of California
and Oregon. A common backyard bird in much of its range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canyon Towhee is very similar but grayer with a rusty crown
patch and found inland. Abert's Towhee has a black mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain brown overall with a buffy-orange throat and rusty
undertail. Long tail and ground-foraging habits are useful.
