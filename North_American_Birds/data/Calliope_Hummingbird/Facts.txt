Calliope Hummingbird (Selasphorus calliope)
============================================

Description
-----------
Smallest bird in North America. Males have magenta-streaked
gorget. Females are greenish above with peachy-buff underparts.

Behavior
--------
Migrates to high mountain meadows to breed. Hovers at flowers
and feeders. Males perform steep U-shaped display dives.

Habitat
-------
Mountain meadows, willow thickets, and shrubby forest edges
from 4,000 to 11,000 feet in the western United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-tailed Hummingbird is larger and males have a solid
rosy-red gorget. Rufous Hummingbird has rufous sides and tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size is distinctive. Male's streaked magenta gorget is
unique. Short bill and tail barely extends beyond wingtips.
