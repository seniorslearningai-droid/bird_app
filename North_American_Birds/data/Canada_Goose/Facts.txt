Canada Goose (Branta canadensis)
=================================

Description
-----------
Large goose with black head and neck and a distinctive white
chinstrap. Brown body, pale chest, and white undertail.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grass; highly adaptable to urban parks and golf
courses. Honks loudly in flight. Flies in V-formation.

Habitat
-------
Lakes, ponds, rivers, golf courses, and parks across most
of North America. Many populations are now year-round residents.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cackling Goose is much smaller with a stubbier bill. Brant is
smaller with no white chinstrap and darker underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head and neck with bold white chinstrap are unmistakable.
Large size and loud honking call. Common in urban parks.
