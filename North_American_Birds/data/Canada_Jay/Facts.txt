Canada Jay (Perisoreus canadensis)
===================================

Description
-----------
Fluffy, medium-sized gray bird with a darker gray back and
white forehead. Dark nape and short bill. No crest.

Behavior
--------
Bold camp robber; pilfers food from campsites. Caches food
in bark crevices for winter. Tame and curious around humans.

Habitat
-------
Boreal and subalpine coniferous forests across Canada and
the northern United States, especially spruce-fir forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Clark's Nutcracker is larger with white wing patches and
black-and-white wings. Gray Jay (old name) refers to same bird.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Fluffy gray plumage with white face and dark nape cap. Found
in boreal forests and often approaches humans without fear.
