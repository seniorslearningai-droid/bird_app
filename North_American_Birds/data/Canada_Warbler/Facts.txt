Canada Warbler (Cardellina canadensis)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Yellow underparts with a bold black necklace of streaks.
Blue-gray upperparts. Yellow spectacles around the dark eye.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in dense understory; often fans tail. Migrates
long distances to South America. Males sing complex songs.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in moist forest understory with ferns near streams
in Canada and northeastern U.S. Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Magnolia Warbler has yellow rump and white tail spots. Wilson's
Warbler has a black cap but no necklace. Lacks wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black necklace on bright yellow breast is distinctive.
Yellow spectacles and blue-gray back complete the field marks.
