Canvasback (Aythya valisineria)
================================

Description
-----------
Large diving duck. Males have a chestnut-red head and neck,
black breast, and white body. Females are brown with pale flanks.

Behavior
--------
Dives for aquatic plants, especially wild celery. Fast flier
with rapid wingbeats. Forms large flocks on open water.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in prairie potholes and marshes. Winters on large
bays, lakes, and estuaries across the United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Redhead has a rounder red head and gray back. Ring-necked Duck
is smaller and darker. Male Canvasback has a distinctive profile.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long sloping forehead creates a distinctive wedge-shaped profile.
Male's red head and white back are unmistakable at a distance.
