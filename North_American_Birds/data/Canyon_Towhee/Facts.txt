Canyon Towhee (Melozone fusca)
===============================

Description
-----------
Large, gray-brown sparrow with a rusty-orange crown patch
and buffy throat outlined by a necklace of dark spots.

Behavior
--------
Forages on the ground in rocky terrain. Often seen in pairs
year-round. Scratches in leaf litter; sings from low perches.

Habitat
-------
Rocky desert canyons, brushy hillsides, and arid scrublands
of the southwestern U.S. and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
California Towhee is nearly identical but browner and found
along the coast. Abert's Towhee has a black mask and bill base.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty crown patch, buffy throat, and dark necklace spots are
key. Found in rocky desert areas, not coastal chaparral.
