Canyon Wren (Catherpes mexicanus)
==================================

Description
-----------
Rich rufous body with a bright white throat and breast.
Dark brown barring on back and tail. Long, slightly curved bill.

Behavior
--------
Creeps along rocky cliff faces probing crevices for insects.
Loud, descending cascade of whistled notes echoes in canyons.

Habitat
-------
Rocky canyons, cliff faces, and boulder fields from southern
British Columbia to Mexico in the western United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rock Wren is paler and grayer with a buffy rump. Bewick's Wren
lacks the white throat and rufous body. Both prefer rocks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White bib contrasting with rufous body is unmistakable. Its
cascading, descending song is one of the most distinctive.
