Cape May Warbler (Setophaga tigrina)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Breeding male has chestnut cheek patch, yellow neck, and
heavily streaked breast. Female is duller with yellow-green tones.

Behavior
--------
Feeds heavily on spruce budworms during irruption years.
Has a unique tubular tongue for sipping nectar in winter.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in boreal spruce-fir forests. Winters in Caribbean
forests and gardens. Migrates through eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-rumped Warbler has a yellow rump patch. Palm Warbler
has yellow undertail. Breeding male Cape May is unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut cheek patch and yellow neck stripe on breeding male
are unique. Yellow rump and streaked breast visible year-round.
