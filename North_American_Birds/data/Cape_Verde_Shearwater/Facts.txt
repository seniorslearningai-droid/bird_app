Cape Verde Shearwater (Calonectris edwardsii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shearwater, brown above, white below. Darker
than Scopoli's/Cory's with more prominent
dark bill. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Cape Verde Islands; migrates into Atlantic.
Rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic Ocean. Rare vagrant to North American Atlantic
coast. Breeds on Cape Verde
Islands off Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scopoli's and Cory's Shearwaters very similar but
slightly larger and paler.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Similar to Cory's/Scopoli's but smaller and darker
with more prominent bill markings.
Cape Verde endemic, rare Atlantic vagrant.
