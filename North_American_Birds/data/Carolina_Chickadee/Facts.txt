Carolina Chickadee (Poecile carolinensis)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Tiny bird with black cap and bib, white cheeks, gray back,
and pale buff flanks. Nearly identical to Black-capped Chickadee.

Behavior
--------
Active and acrobatic; clings upside down to search for insects.
Forms mixed flocks in winter. Visits feeders for sunflower seeds.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests, parks, and suburbs of the
southeastern United States from New Jersey to Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Chickadee is nearly identical but has wider white
wing edging and a slower, lower-pitched chick-a-dee call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
In range, the only chickadee in the Southeast. Contact zone
with Black-capped Chickadee in mid-Atlantic; call note is key.
