Carolina Parakeet (Conuropsis carolinensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
EXTINCT. Green parrot with yellow and orange head.
North America's only native parrot.
About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extinct since 1918; last wild birds collected 1904.
Flocked in deciduous forest. Hunted
for feathers and as crop pests.

Habitat
-------
Bottomland forest of eastern North America. Extinct
since 1918; last captive bird died
Cincinnati Zoo 1918.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Green parakeets established as escapes. Carolina Parakeet
was North America's only native parrot.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct since 1918. Green with orange-yellow head.
Only known from museum specimens and
historical accounts.
