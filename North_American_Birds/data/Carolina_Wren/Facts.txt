Carolina Wren (Thryothorus ludovicianus)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Rich rufous-brown above, warm buff below. Bold white supercilium
eyebrow stripe. Short, slightly curved bill.

Behavior
--------
Loud singer year-round; sings teakettle-teakettle repeatedly.
Forages low in dense cover. Often cocks tail upward while moving.

Habitat
-------
Dense shrubby undergrowth, brushy forest edges, and suburban
thickets of the eastern United States and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bewick's Wren has a longer tail with white spots and is more
gray-brown. House Wren is smaller and plainer with no eyebrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white eyebrow, rufous tones, and loud repetitive song
are distinctive. Tail often cocked upright while perching.
