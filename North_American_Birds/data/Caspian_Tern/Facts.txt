Caspian Tern (Hydroprogne caspia)
==================================

Description
-----------
Largest tern in the world. Heavy red-orange bill with dark tip. Black cap in
breeding plumage (streaked in winter). Gray back and wings. White body.
About 21 inches with 50-inch wingspan. Crow-sized.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives for fish. Raucous, harsh calls — sounds like a large crow or heron.
Aggressive to other birds at colonies. Kleptoparasitizes other terns.

Habitat
-------
Large lakes, reservoirs, rivers, estuaries, and coasts. Breeds colonially on
islands and sandbars. Present on all coasts and Great Lakes. Widespread migrant.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Royal Tern is similar but smaller with orange (not red) bill and often crested.
Elegant Tern is slimmer, more orange. Caspian is the largest and heaviest tern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive red bill, large size. Harsh, croaking calls unlike any other tern.
Black cap streaked in winter. Widespread on large water bodies year-round.
