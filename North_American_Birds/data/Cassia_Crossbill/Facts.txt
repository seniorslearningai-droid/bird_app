Cassia Crossbill (Loxia sinesciuris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch nearly identical to Red Crossbill.
Male brick-red, female olive-green,
crossed bill tips. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Restricted to South Hills and Albion Mountains where
lodgepole pine lacks squirrel
seed predators. Non-migratory.

Habitat
-------
Lodgepole pine forests of Cassia County, Idaho.
Resident, non-migratory, unlike
other crossbills.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red Crossbill nearly identical. Best identified
by call type, range, and
non-migratory behavior.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Cassia County Idaho lodgepole pine only. Nearly
identical to Red Crossbill.
Non-migratory resident.
