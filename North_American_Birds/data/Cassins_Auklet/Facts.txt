Cassin's Auklet (Ptychoramphus aleuticus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gray alcid. Uniform dark gray above and
below, white spot above eye, white crescent below
eye. Short thick bill. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly pelagic; rarely seen from shore. Dives for
krill and small fish. Nests in burrows on
Pacific coast islands.

Habitat
-------
Offshore Pacific waters. Nests on islands from
Alaska to Baja California. Rarely seen from
shore except near colonies.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small auklets. Cassin's distinctive uniform gray
color with white eye marks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Uniform dark gray color with white spot above
eye. Rarely seen from shore. Pelagic
Pacific species.
