Cassin's Finch (Haemorhous cassinii)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Male has bright crimson crown, rosy-pink breast, and streaked
brown back. Female is heavily streaked brown and white.

Behavior
--------
Forages in conifer treetops for seeds and buds. Forms flocks
in winter. Male sings a rich, varied warbling song.

Habitat
-------
Mountain conifer forests of the western United States, from
the Rockies to the Sierra Nevada. Winters at lower elevations.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Finch is similar but smaller with a less peaked crown.
House Finch male has brown streaking on flanks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Peaked head profile and bright crimson (not rosy) crown on
male are key. Larger and longer-billed than House Finch.
