Cassin's Kingbird (Tyrannus vociferans)
========================================

Description
-----------
Gray head and breast, olive-green back, bright yellow belly.
White chin contrasts with gray chest. No white tail edges.

Behavior
--------
Perches conspicuously to hawk flying insects. Aggressive
defender of territory. Loud chi-bew call is distinctive.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, pinyon-juniper scrub, and riparian areas in
the southwestern United States and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Kingbird has white outer tail feathers and a paler
gray breast. Tropical Kingbird is very similar but larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray breast contrasting with bright yellow belly and
white chin. Dark tail without white edges distinguishes it.
