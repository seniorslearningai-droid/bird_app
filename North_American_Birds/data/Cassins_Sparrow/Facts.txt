Cassin's Sparrow (Peucaea cassinii)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Streaked brown and gray, pale below with faint streaking on
breast. Flat crown, long rounded tail with white tips.

Behavior
--------
Males skylarks in flight song, rising and parachuting while
singing. Secretive on ground; flushes reluctantly.

Habitat
-------
Arid grasslands with scattered shrubs in the southern Great
Plains and Chihuahuan Desert of the United States and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Botteri's Sparrow is very similar but has plain brown tail.
Grasshopper Sparrow is smaller with flat head and stubby tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Skylark display flight and sweet trilled song are distinctive.
White tips on rounded tail visible in flight.
