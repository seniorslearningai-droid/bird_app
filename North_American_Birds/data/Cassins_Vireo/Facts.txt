Cassin's Vireo (Vireo cassinii)
=================================

Description
-----------
Gray head, white spectacles, olive-green back, white wingbars,
and yellow-washed flanks. White throat and underparts.

Behavior
--------
Forages slowly through foliage for insects and berries.
Sings a slow, burry series of phrases with pauses between.

Habitat
-------
Mixed and coniferous forests of the Pacific Coast mountains
and western slopes from British Columbia to Baja California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-headed Vireo is nearly identical but has a blue-gray head.
Plumbeous Vireo is grayer with less olive tones on back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white spectacles and wingbars, olive-green back, and
yellow flanks. Found in western mountains, not east.
