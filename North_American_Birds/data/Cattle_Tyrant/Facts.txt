Cattle Tyrant (Machetornis rixosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher, brown above, bright yellow below
with rusty crown patch. Walks
on ground near cattle. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground near cattle for insects disturbed
by hooves. Very rare vagrant
to North America from South America.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands and ranchland near livestock in South
America. Extremely rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Kiskadee larger with black-and-white head. Western
Kingbird lacks brown back
and ground foraging.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow below, brown above, ground forager near cattle.
Extremely rare South American
vagrant.
