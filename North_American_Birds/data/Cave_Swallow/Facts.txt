Cave Swallow (Petrochelidon fulva)
===================================

Description
-----------
Square tail, dark blue back, rusty-orange rump, pale orange
throat and forehead. Buffy underparts with chestnut cheek.

Behavior
--------
Colonial nester in caves, culverts, and bridges. Hawking
insects in flight over open terrain. Fast, agile flier.

Habitat
-------
Open country near cliffs, caves, and bridges in Texas,
New Mexico, Florida, and Caribbean. Expanding range northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cliff Swallow has a dark throat and white forehead.
Barn Swallow has deeply forked tail and blue-black breast band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale orange forehead and throat (not dark like Cliff Swallow)
combined with rusty rump and square tail are key.
