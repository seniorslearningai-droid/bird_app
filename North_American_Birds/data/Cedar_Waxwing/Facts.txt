Cedar Waxwing (Bombycilla cedrorum)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Sleek brown-gray bird with silky plumage, black mask, yellow
tail tip, and waxy red wing tips. Crested head.

Behavior
--------
Highly social; travels in flocks. Feeds heavily on berries.
Passes berries along a perched flock. High-pitched trilled call.

Habitat
-------
Woodland edges, orchards, and berry-producing shrubs across
North America. Nomadic; follows fruit crops.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bohemian Waxwing is grayer with rusty undertail and yellow
and white wing markings. Larger and only in the north.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sleek crested silhouette, black mask, yellow tail tip, and
waxy red wing spots make this species unmistakable.
