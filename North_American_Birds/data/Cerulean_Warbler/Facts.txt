Cerulean Warbler (Setophaga cerulea)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Male is sky blue above with white underparts and black streaks.
Female is blue-green above with yellowish eyebrow.

Behavior
--------
Forages high in forest canopy, hard to see. Male sings a
buzzy ascending trill with a final upward slur.

Habitat
-------
Mature deciduous forests with tall trees, especially in the
Appalachians and Midwest. Winters in Andean cloud forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-throated Blue Warbler male has black throat and face.
Blackburnian Warbler has orange throat. Female resembles
female Black-throated Blue but bluer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's sky-blue back and narrow black breast band are unique.
Look and listen high in forest canopy in breeding season.
