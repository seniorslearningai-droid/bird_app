Chatham Albatross (Thalassarche eremita)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium mollymawk albatross. White head with gray
cheek, dark back, distinctive yellow bill
with dark culmen. About 35 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds only on Pyramid Rock, Chatham Islands. Very
rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Southern Pacific Ocean, Chatham Islands. Very rare
vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other mollymawk albatrosses similar. Chatham has distinctive
dark-tipped yellow bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow bill with dark tip. Gray wash on head
sides. Very rare Pacific vagrant.
Critically endangered species.
