Chestnut-backed Chickadee (Poecile rufescens)
==============================================

Description
-----------
Black cap and bib, white cheeks, rich chestnut back and
sides. Small and compact with gray wings and tail.

Behavior
--------
Active and acrobatic in conifers. Joins mixed flocks in
winter. Responds to pishing. High-pitched chick-a-dee call.

Habitat
-------
Moist conifer and mixed forests of the Pacific Coast from
Alaska to central California. Common in coastal redwoods.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Chickadee lacks chestnut on back and sides.
Mountain Chickadee has white eyebrow. Both lack chestnut back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unmistakable rich chestnut back and flanks set against black
cap and white cheeks. Only chickadee with chestnut back.
