Chestnut-bellied Sandgrouse (Pterocles exustus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Pigeon-sized bird with cryptic sandy-brown plumage,
chestnut belly, long pointed tail. Male has
thin black breast band. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Flies long distances to water at dawn. Male soaks
breast feathers to carry water to chicks.
Introduced to Hawaii from Africa and Asia.

Habitat
-------
Arid grasslands and scrubland. Introduced to Hawaii,
found on dry leeward slopes of several islands.
Native to African and Asian drylands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar sandgrouse in Hawaii. Dove-like
in shape but flight pattern very different.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sandy-brown cryptic plumage with chestnut belly.
Long pointed tail. Fast direct flight.
Found in Hawaii on dry grassy slopes.
