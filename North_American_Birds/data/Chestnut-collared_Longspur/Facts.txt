Chestnut-collared Longspur (Calcarius ornatus)
===============================================

Description
-----------
Breeding male has black and white face, chestnut nape, black
belly. Female is streaked brown. White tail with black triangle.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in flocks. Male displays in hovering song
flight. Runs rather than hops across grassland.

Habitat
-------
Short-grass prairies and mixed-grass plains of the Great
Plains. Winters in southwestern grasslands and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
McCown's Longspur has more white in tail. Lapland Longspur
female is similar but has heavier streaking.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding male's black belly and chestnut collar unmistakable.
White tail with black triangle visible in all plumages in flight.
