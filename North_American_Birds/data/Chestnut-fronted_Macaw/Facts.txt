Chestnut-fronted Macaw (Ara severus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium macaw, green with chestnut forehead and chin,
red under wings and tail.
About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious in flocks. Loud calls. Small feral population
in Florida.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest and savanna. Small feral population
in southern Florida. Native
to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Severe Macaw is same species. Military Macaw larger.
Smaller macaw with chestnut
forehead.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Green with chestnut forehead and red under wings.
Medium-sized macaw. Florida
feral population.
