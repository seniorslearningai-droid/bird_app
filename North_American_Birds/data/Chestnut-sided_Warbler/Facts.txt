Chestnut-sided Warbler (Setophaga pensylvanica)
================================================

Description
-----------
Breeding male has yellow crown, black eye stripe, white face,
and chestnut sides. Fall birds are lime-green with white eye ring.

Behavior
--------
Forages at mid-level in shrubby forest edges. Cocks tail
upward. Sings a distinctive please-please-pleased to MEET you.

Habitat
-------
Shrubby second-growth forests and woodland edges in the
eastern United States and southern Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bay-breasted Warbler has chestnut on breast not just sides.
Fall birds unique with lime-green back and white eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut sides and yellow crown in breeding plumage unique.
Fall birds' lime-green back and clean white eye ring are key.
