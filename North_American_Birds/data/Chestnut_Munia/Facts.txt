Chestnut Munia (Lonchura atricapilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, black head, deep chestnut body, whitish
lower belly. Heavy gray bill.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious in large flocks. Forages for grass
seeds. Introduced to Hawaii;
very common on several islands.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, open areas, and gardens. Common introduced
species in Hawaii.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tricolored Munia has black belly, white sides. Scaly-breasted
Munia has pale scaly
breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head with uniform deep chestnut body. Heavy
bill. Gregarious flocks
in Hawaiian grasslands.
