Chihuahuan Meadowlark (Sturnella lilianae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, streaked brown above, bright yellow below
with black V on breast.
About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings from fence posts and shrubs in grassland.
Recently split from Eastern Meadowlark.
Song different from relatives.

Habitat
-------
Chihuahuan Desert grasslands of southwestern Texas
and New Mexico, and adjacent
Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Meadowlark very similar. Western Meadowlark
has different song pattern.
Range important for identification.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Eastern Meadowlark. Chihuahuan
Desert grassland range. Song
and genetic differences key.
