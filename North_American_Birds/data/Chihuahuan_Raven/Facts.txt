Chihuahuan Raven (Corvus cryptoleucus)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Medium-large raven, smaller than Common Raven but larger than American Crow.
All black with white feather bases (usually hidden). Wedge-shaped tail.
About 19.5 inches. Rounded head, strong bill.

Behavior
--------
Highly omnivorous and opportunistic. Forms large winter flocks, sometimes
thousands. Follows livestock and agricultural operations for food scraps.

Habitat
-------
Arid grasslands, deserts, and scrublands of the Chihuahuan desert region —
Texas, New Mexico, Arizona, and northern Mexico. Avoids forests and mountains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Raven is larger with deeper voice and more prominent bill. American
Crow is smaller with fan-shaped tail. Fish Crow is smaller and coastal.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smaller than Common Raven, larger than crow. Chihuahuan desert grasslands.
Forms large winter flocks. White feather bases sometimes visible on neck.
