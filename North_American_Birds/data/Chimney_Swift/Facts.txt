Chimney Swift (Chaetura pelagica)
==================================

Description
-----------
All dark sooty-brown, tiny feet, very short tail. Wings
long and swept back. Cigar-shaped body in flight.

Behavior
--------
Almost never lands except to nest and roost. Feeds entirely
on the wing. Loud chattering calls in flight.

Habitat
-------
Urban and suburban areas with chimneys and hollow trees across
the eastern United States. Winters in the Amazon basin.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Vaux's Swift is smaller and found in the West. Swallows have
longer tails and perch on wires. Purple Martin is bigger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Twinkling flight on stiff bowed wings with rapid shallow beats.
Chittering calls and cigar body shape are distinctive.
