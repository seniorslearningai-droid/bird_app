Chinese Egret (Egretta eulophotes)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium white egret, yellow bill with dark tip, blue-gray
facial skin in breeding, yellow
feet. About 26 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in coastal wetlands and tidal flats. Endangered
in Asia. Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Coastal East Asia. Very rare vagrant to western Alaska,
particularly Aleutian Islands.
Endangered Asian species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snowy Egret has black bill and yellow feet. Chinese
Egret has yellow bill with dark tip.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow bill with dark tip, blue-gray facial skin in
breeding. Yellow feet. Endangered
Asian vagrant to western Alaska.
