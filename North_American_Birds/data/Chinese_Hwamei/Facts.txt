Chinese Hwamei (Garrulax canorus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium brown bird with streaked pattern, white eye
ring with streak extending back
(spectacles). About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly vocal with beautiful varied song. Skulks
in dense vegetation. Introduced
to Hawaii, especially Oahu.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest undergrowth and scrub. Introduced to
Hawaiian Islands, particularly common
on Oahu and Kauai.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in Hawaii. White spectacle markings
make it distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White spectacle stripe from eye distinctive. Brown
streaked plumage. Loud musical
song. Hawaiian forest.
