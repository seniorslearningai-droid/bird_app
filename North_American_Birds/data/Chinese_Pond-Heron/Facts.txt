Chinese Pond-Heron (Ardeola bacchus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small compact heron, brown and white streaked body,
brilliant white wings in flight, chestnut
and black breeding plumage. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Stands motionless then strikes for fish. Very rare
vagrant to Alaska from East Asia.
Distinctive white wings in flight.

Habitat
-------
Ponds and rice paddies of East Asia. Very rare vagrant
to western Alaska from
East Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small herons lack the brilliant white wings.
Chinese Pond-Heron's white wings unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant white wings contrasting with brown body
in flight. Compact pond heron.
Rare Alaska vagrant from Asia.
