Chinese Sparrowhawk (Accipiter soloensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small accipiter. Male blue-gray above, pale below
with unmarked white underparts.
Red eyes. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migratory raptor of East Asia. Rare vagrant to Alaska
from East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland of East and Southeast Asia. Very
rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sharp-shinned Hawk similar size. Chinese Sparrowhawk
has all-white unmarked underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale unmarked underparts, blue-gray above. Red iris.
Small accipiter. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Asia.
