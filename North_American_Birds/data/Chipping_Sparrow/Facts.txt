Chipping Sparrow (Spizella passerina)
======================================

Description
-----------
Rufous cap, white eyebrow, black eye line, and clean gray
breast. Small and slim with notched tail.

Behavior
--------
Forages on the ground near trees. Sings a long dry trill.
Common in lawns and parks. Forms flocks in winter.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, parks, gardens, and forest edges across
North America. One of the most widespread sparrows.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Tree Sparrow has rufous cap but also a central
breast spot. Clay-colored Sparrow has brown cheek patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Clean gray breast, bright rufous cap, and white eyebrow
bordered by black are distinctive. Very common in suburbs.
