Christmas Shearwater (Puffinus nativitatis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small all-dark brown shearwater with rounded wings
and relatively short tail. Dark bill
and feet. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern Islands. Forages in
central Pacific; occasional in
Hawaiian waters.

Habitat
-------
Central North Pacific Ocean. Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern
Islands including Midway and
Laysan.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other all-dark shearwaters larger. Christmas Shearwater
small, rounded wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small all-dark shearwater with rounded wings. Central
Pacific; Hawaiian Northwestern
Islands breeder.
