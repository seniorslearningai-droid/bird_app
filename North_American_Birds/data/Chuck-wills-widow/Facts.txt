Chuck-will's-widow (Antrostomus carolinensis)
===============================================

Description
-----------
Large nightjar with cryptic brown mottled plumage. Buffy
collar, large flat head, and very wide gape.

Behavior
--------
Strictly nocturnal. Calls its name chuck-will's-widow loudly
at night. Sits on ground or low branches in daytime.

Habitat
-------
Open pine and mixed forests, woodland edges in the southeastern
United States. Winters in the Caribbean and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whip-poor-will is smaller with less buff coloring and a white
tail tip on males. Eastern Whip-poor-will has similar call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very loud repeated chuck-will's-widow call at dusk and night.
Larger than Whip-poor-will with more buffy-brown tones.
