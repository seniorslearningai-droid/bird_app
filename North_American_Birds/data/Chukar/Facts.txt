Chukar (Alectoris chukar)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium partridge. Gray-brown body with bold black and
white barred flanks, black necklace on white face
and throat, red bill and legs. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Runs quickly over rocky terrain. Calls loudly
"chuk-chuk-chukar." Gregarious; found in coveys.
Widely introduced for upland game hunting.

Habitat
-------
Rocky, steep, arid hillsides with sparse vegetation.
Introduced across western US including Nevada,
Idaho, Oregon, and other western states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Himalayan Snowcock much larger. Hungarian Partridge
lacks bold flank barring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black-and-white flank bars, black necklace on
white face, red bill and legs.
Rocky arid habitat in western US.
