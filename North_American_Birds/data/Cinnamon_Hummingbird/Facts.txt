Cinnamon Hummingbird (Amazilia rutila)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Entirely cinnamon-rufous underparts,
iridescent green upperparts, red bill with dark tip.
Vivid cinnamon coloring. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar from various flowers. Rare vagrant
to southern Arizona from Mexico and Central America.
Active and conspicuous.

Habitat
-------
Dry coastal scrub, woodland, and forest edges of
Mexico and Central America. Very rare
vagrant to southern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Buff-bellied Hummingbird has similar reddish tones
but greener breast. Cinnamon brighter overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entirely vivid cinnamon underparts with metallic green
back unique among hummingbirds. Red bill.
Very rare vagrant in Arizona.
