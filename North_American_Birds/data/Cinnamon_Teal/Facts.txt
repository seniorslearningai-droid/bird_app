Cinnamon Teal (Spatula cyanoptera)
====================================

Description
-----------
Male is rich cinnamon-red with blue wing patches and red eye.
Female is mottled brown. Spatula-shaped bill.

Behavior
--------
Feeds by dabbling and tipping in shallow wetlands. Often
in pairs or small groups. Somewhat secretive.

Habitat
-------
Shallow marshes, ponds, and wetlands in the western United
States. Winters in Mexico and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-winged Teal female is almost identical to female Cinnamon.
Ruddy Duck male is also cinnamon but has stiff upright tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's deep cinnamon-red head and body with blue wing patch
are unmistakable. Female best told by range and bill shape.
