Citrine Wagtail (Motacilla citreola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small wagtail, gray back, bright yellow head and
underparts, white wing bars.
About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Wagtail behavior: bobs tail constantly, runs on
ground. Very rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows and marshes in Asia and Siberia. Very
rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Yellow Wagtail less yellow on head. White
Wagtail has black and white
pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow head contrasting with gray back. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Asia.
Constant tail wagging.
