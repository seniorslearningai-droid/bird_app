Clark's Grebe (Aechmophorus clarkii)
======================================

Description
-----------
Black and white with long thin neck, orange-yellow bill, and
red eye. White face with black cap above the eye.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish. Famous for rushing courtship display with
mate. Forms large flocks on open water in winter.

Habitat
-------
Large western lakes and reservoirs for nesting; coastal
bays and open ocean in winter along the Pacific Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Grebe is nearly identical but has a black face that
extends below the eye and a duller olive-yellow bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White face extends above eye and bright orange-yellow bill
distinguish Clark's from Western Grebe. Best told at close range.
