Clark's Nutcracker (Nucifraga columbiana)
==========================================

Description
-----------
Gray body with black wings, white wing patches, and black tail
with white outer feathers. Long sharp bill.

Behavior
--------
Caches thousands of pine seeds per year. Remarkable spatial
memory. Loud harsh kraaa call in mountain forests.

Habitat
-------
High-elevation conifer forests of the Rocky Mountains and
Pacific ranges, particularly near whitebark and limber pines.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray Jay is softer gray without black wings. Nuthatches are
much smaller. Unmistakable in its mountain habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black-and-white wing pattern on gray body, with long
bill and white outer tail feathers in flight.
