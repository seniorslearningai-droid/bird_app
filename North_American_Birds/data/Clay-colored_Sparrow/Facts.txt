Clay-colored Sparrow (Spizella pallida)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Striped crown, brown cheek patch outlined in white, whitish
median crown stripe, and pale unstreaked breast.

Behavior
--------
Forages in low shrubs and on ground. Sings a flat mechanical
buzz. Males perch conspicuously to sing.

Habitat
-------
Brushy fields, prairie edges, and shrubby areas of the Great
Plains and north-central Canada. Winters in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chipping Sparrow has rufous cap and lacks brown cheek patch.
Brewer's Sparrow is grayer and lacks the facial pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown cheek patch with white border, striped crown, and buzzy
insect-like song are key identification features.
