Clay-colored Thrush (Turdus grayi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, plain warm brown throughout above
and below. Yellow-green bill,
dull orange-yellow legs. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for fruit and invertebrates.
Bold and conspicuous in gardens
and parks. Beautiful melodious song.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges, gardens, parks, and second growth
from Mexico through Central America.
Established in southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin much brighter with red-orange breast.
White-throated Thrush has white
throat. Veery has spotted breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain dull brown throughout with yellow-green bill.
South Texas resident. No bold
markings. Very plain thrush.
