Cliff Swallow (Petrochelidon pyrrhonota)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Square tail, blue-black back, orange-buff rump, dark throat,
and white forehead patch. Pale underparts.

Behavior
--------
Highly colonial nester on cliff faces and bridges. Builds
gourd-shaped mud nests. Gregarious in large flocks.

Habitat
-------
Open country near cliffs, bridges, and buildings across
North America. Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cave Swallow has pale orange throat instead of dark. Barn
Swallow has forked tail and chestnut throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright rusty-orange rump, square tail, and white forehead
on dark head identify this colonial nesting swallow.
