Cocos Booby (Sula brewsteri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium booby. Dark brown above and on head and neck,
white belly, pale yellowish bill.
About 28 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pacific subspecies/species of Brown Booby. Plunge-dives
for fish. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America.

Habitat
-------
Eastern tropical Pacific, Cocos Island and adjacent
waters. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Booby nearly identical. Cocos Booby paler overall
with lighter head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown and white booby, similar to Brown Booby. Paler
head than typical Brown Booby.
Eastern Pacific species.
