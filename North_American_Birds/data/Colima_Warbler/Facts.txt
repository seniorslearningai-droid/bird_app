Colima Warbler (Leiothlypis crissalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, gray above, yellow undertail coverts,
rusty crown patch, plain grayish-
white below. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in oak and pinyon-juniper scrub. Breeds only
in Chisos Mountains of Big
Bend National Park.

Habitat
-------
Oak woodland in the Chisos Mountains of Big Bend
National Park, Texas. Breeds
nowhere else in United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Virginia's Warbler similar but white not yellow undertail.
Nashville Warbler has
yellow below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow undertail coverts, rusty crown, gray body.
Chisos Mountains only in U.S.
Big Bend National Park specialist.
