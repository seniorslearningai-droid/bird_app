Collared Forest-Falcon (Micrastur semitorquatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large forest falcon, black above with white collar
and underparts. Long tail, small rounded
wings. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pursues prey through dense forest. Heard more often
than seen. Rare vagrant from Mexico
to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Dense tropical forest of Mexico and Central America.
Rare vagrant to southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other falcons in open habitat. Collared Forest-Falcon
a dense forest specialist.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black above with white collar and underparts. Forest
interior, often heard not seen.
Very rare vagrant from Mexico.
