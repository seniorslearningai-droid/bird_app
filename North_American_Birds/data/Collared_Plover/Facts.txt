Collared Plover (Charadrius collaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plover. Brown above with rufous tones, white
underparts with black breast band, white forehead,
rufous crown. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on bare ground and river margins for
invertebrates. Rare vagrant to southern US and
Caribbean from Central and South America.

Habitat
-------
Sandy riverbanks, gravel bars, and beaches in
Central and South America. Rare vagrant
to southern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated and other small plovers. Rufous tones
and facial pattern distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous forecrown and neck sides with black breast
band. Small size. Rare vagrant from
Central America.
