Common Chaffinch (Fringilla coelebs)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch. Male has blue-gray head, pinkish breast,
chestnut back, white wing
bars. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds. Common garden bird in
Europe. Very rare vagrant
to northeastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Woodland, gardens, and farmland across Europe. Very
rare vagrant to northeastern
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brambling has white rump and orange shoulder. No
similar brightly patterned finch
in North America range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male blue-gray head with pinkish breast and chestnut
back. White wing bars. Very
rare European vagrant.
