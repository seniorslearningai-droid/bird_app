Common Chiffchaff (Phylloscopus collybita)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small leaf warbler, olive-brown above, off-white
below with yellow-buff tones. Dark
legs. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active insect forager. Named for repetitive chiff-chaff
song. Rare vagrant to eastern
and western North America.

Habitat
-------
Woodland, scrub, and gardens across Europe and
Asia. Rare vagrant to both
coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Willow Warbler has flesh-colored legs, more yellow
below. Arctic Warbler larger
with wing bar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black legs distinctive versus Willow Warbler. Olive-brown
above, dirty white below.
Repetitive chiff-chaff song.
