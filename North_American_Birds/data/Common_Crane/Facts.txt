Common Crane (Grus grus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large gray crane. Gray body, black and white
head with red crown patch, long neck and legs.
About 45 inches with 7-foot wingspan.

Behavior
--------
Migratory; travels in large V-shaped flocks with loud
bugling calls. Rare vagrant to North America from
Eurasia; sometimes mixes with Sandhill Cranes.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Eurasian wetlands and boreal forests.
Winters on European farmland. Rare vagrant to
North America, often with Sandhill Cranes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sandhill Crane very similar but browner. Common
Crane has black and white head pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-gray body with distinctive black and white head
pattern. Often mixes with Sandhill Crane
flocks in migration.
