Common Cuckoo (Cuculus canorus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium slender bird. Male gray above, barred black
and white below, yellow eye ring. Female can
be reddish-brown. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Brood parasite; lays eggs in other birds' nests.
Famous "cuckoo" call. Migrates to Africa for winter.
Rare vagrant to western North America.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in open woodland, moorland, and farmland
across Eurasia. Winters in Africa. Rare vagrant
to Alaska and Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Accipiter hawks similar in silhouette. Oriental Cuckoo
nearly identical; separated by call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Hawk-like silhouette with drooping wingtips and
long tail. Barred underparts. Distinctive "cuck-oo"
call. Rare vagrant in North America.
