Common Eider (Somateria mollissima)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Large, heavy sea duck. Males have white back and breast, black belly and cap,
green nape, and distinctive sloping forehead with orange bill shield. Females
brown with heavy barring. About 24 inches. Largest North American duck.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mussels and other mollusks in coastal waters. Highly social in large
flocks. Females famous for soft down feathers used in duvets. Distinctive
growling display calls.

Habitat
-------
Arctic and sub-Arctic coastal waters. Winters along rocky coastlines south to
mid-Atlantic states on East Coast. Common off New England and Maritime coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
King Eider male has round orange bill knob. Spectacled Eider has pale eye
patch. Female eiders are similar brown ducks; bill shape and size help.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive size and sloping forehead profile unique. Male's white-and-black
pattern with green nape. Rocky coastal habitats. Large flocks offshore in winter.
