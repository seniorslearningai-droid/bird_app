Common Gallinule (Gallinula galeata)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Medium waterbird with slate-gray plumage, brown back, white flank stripe, and
distinctive red frontal shield and bill with yellow tip. Green legs with
red garter ring. About 14 inches. Formerly called Common Moorhen.

Behavior
--------
Swims on open water and walks on lily pads, pumping head with each step.
Clucks and squawks from marshes. Builds floating nests in vegetation. Omnivore.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, ponds, lakes with emergent vegetation across much of
the United States. Year-round in South; migratory in North. Common in wetlands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Coot has white bill and no red shield. Purple Gallinule has purple
plumage and yellow legs. Common Gallinule's red bill and shield are distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red frontal shield and bill with yellow tip. White flank stripe. Gray-brown
plumage. Head-bobbing swimming behavior. Common in freshwater marshes.
