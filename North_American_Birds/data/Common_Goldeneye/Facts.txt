Common Goldeneye (Bucephala clangula)
======================================

Description
-----------
Male has iridescent green head, round white cheek spot, and
yellow eye. Female has brown head and gray body.

Behavior
--------
Dives for aquatic invertebrates. Wings produce loud whistling
in flight. Nests in tree cavities near boreal lakes.

Habitat
-------
Boreal lakes and forest ponds for breeding. Winters on coastal
bays, lakes, and rivers across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Barrow's Goldeneye male has purple head and crescent cheek
spot. Female very similar but Barrow's has shorter bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Round white cheek spot on green head (male) or brown head
with bright golden eye and high rounded crown (female).
