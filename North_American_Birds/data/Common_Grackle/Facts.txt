Common Grackle (Quiscalus quiscula)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Large iridescent blackbird with long keel-shaped tail. Purple-
bronze iridescence on head. Pale yellow eye.

Behavior
--------
Walks boldly and forages on the ground. Highly gregarious;
forms enormous roosts. Loud squeaky gate call.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, farms, suburban lawns, and woodland edges across
the eastern United States and southern Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Boat-tailed Grackle is larger with a bigger tail, found in
coastal areas. Bronzed Cowbird has red eye.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Keel-shaped tail, yellow eye, and iridescent purple-bronze
head on male make this the most familiar eastern grackle.
