Common Greenshank (Tringa nebularia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sandpiper. Gray-brown above, white below with
streaked neck and breast, upturned bill tip,
green-gray legs. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages by running through shallow water with bill
sweeping side to side. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Eurasian bogs and moorland. Winters on
African and Asian coasts. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Yellowlegs similar. Greenshank has slightly
upturned bill and green-gray (not yellow) legs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray-green legs (not yellow). Slightly upturned bill
tip. White back visible in flight.
Rare vagrant on Atlantic coast.
