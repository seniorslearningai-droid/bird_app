Common Ground-Dove (Columbina passerina)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Tiny dove with scaly-patterned head, pinkish bill, and
rufous wing patches visible in flight.

Behavior
--------
Walks on the ground bobbing head. Forages for seeds. Flushes
low with rapid wingbeats and rufous flash in wings.

Habitat
-------
Open sandy areas, brush, roadsides, and agricultural fields
in the southern United States and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Inca Dove has scaled back and longer tail with white edges.
Ruddy Ground-Dove male is more rufous throughout.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, scaly head pattern, and bright rufous wing flash
in flight are distinctive. Walks on ground like a mouse.
