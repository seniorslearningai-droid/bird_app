Common Gull (Larus canus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull, pale gray back, white head and body,
yellow-green bill without red spot in winter.
Gentle dove-like expression. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on fields and coasts. European counterpart
to Ring-billed Gull. Uncommon winter
visitor to northeastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Coasts, fields, and harbors. Regular in winter in
western Europe; rare visitor to northeast
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-billed Gull very similar. Common Gull has smaller
bill, darker eye, gentler expression.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small bill without black ring; darker eye than Ring-billed.
Rounder head, gentler face.
Rare winter visitor to northeast.
