Common Loon (Gavia immer)
==========================

Description
-----------
Breeding adult has black-and-white checkered back, black head
with red eye, and white necklace. Winter is gray-brown.

Behavior
--------
Expert diver pursuing fish underwater. Famous yodeling wail
and tremolo calls on northern lakes. Awkward on land.

Habitat
-------
Clear northern lakes for breeding. Winters on coastal waters
and large southern lakes across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-throated Loon is smaller with upturned bill. Yellow-billed
Loon is larger with yellow bill. Both rare except in North.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding plumage unmistakable. Winter birds identified by
large size, flat crown, and dagger-like bill held level.
