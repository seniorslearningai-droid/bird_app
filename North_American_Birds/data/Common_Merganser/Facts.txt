Common Merganser (Mergus merganser)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Male has green head, white body, and orange-red serrated bill.
Female has rusty head with shaggy crest and gray body.

Behavior
--------
Dives and chases fish underwater. Often seen in rivers.
Nests in tree cavities near water. Flies in lines low over water.

Habitat
-------
Clear rivers and lakes in northern forests for nesting. Winters
on large lakes and rivers across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-breasted Merganser male has shaggy crest and streaked
breast. Hooded Merganser is much smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, green head, white body, and long red serrated bill
on male. Female's rusty head with clean white chin is key.
