Common Murre (Uria aalge)
==========================

Description
-----------
Large auk with dark brown-black head, back, and wings; white underparts.
Thin, pointed bill. About 17.5 inches. "Bridled" form has white eye ring.
In winter, white extends up throat with dark line behind eye.

Behavior
--------
Expert diver, using wings to propel underwater to depths of 600 feet. Breeds
in dense colonies on narrow cliff ledges. Lays single pear-shaped egg.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific and Atlantic oceans. Breeds on sea cliffs; winters offshore
and along coasts. Common from Alaska south to California in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Thick-billed Murre has white bill stripe and stockier bill. Razorbill has
deep, laterally compressed bill. Brünnich's Murre is similar on Atlantic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large auk, dark above and white below, with thin pointed bill. Dense cliff
colonies in summer. Open ocean in winter. Expert diving and underwater flight.
