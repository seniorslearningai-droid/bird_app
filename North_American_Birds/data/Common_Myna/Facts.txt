Common Myna (Acridotheres tristis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, brown body with black head, yellow
bill and eye skin, white wing
patch visible in flight. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and aggressive introduced species. Highly vocal.
Competes with native birds
for nesting cavities.

Habitat
-------
Introduced to Hawaii and South Florida. Urban areas,
parks, farms, and open country.
Invasive pest species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Crested Myna has white crest. No other brown bird
with black head and
yellow bare skin in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head, brown body, yellow bill, white wing
patch. Bold and noisy.
Hawaiian and Florida invasive.
