Common Nighthawk (Chordeiles minor)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Mottled brown-gray cryptic plumage, long wings with white
bar, notched tail. Flies at dusk and dawn.

Behavior
--------
Aerial insect hunter active at dusk. Distinctive peent call
in flight. Males dive and boom with wings at breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, cities, and grasslands across North America.
Winters in South America. Roosts on flat rooftops and branches.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Nighthawk is smaller with wing bar closer to tip.
Chuck-will's-widow is much larger and nocturnal only.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Erratic buoyant flight with white wing bars on long pointed
wings. Nasal peent call and booming dive display.
