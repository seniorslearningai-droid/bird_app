Common Pauraque (Nyctidromus albicollis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large nightjar with intricate brown, black, buff, and
gray patterned plumage. Long tail and wings,
large dark eyes. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Roosts and nests on ground; active at night catching
flying insects. Calls loudly at night. Year-round
resident in southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Brushy woodland and thorny scrub in South Texas
and tropical Americas. Common in Lower
Rio Grande Valley.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chuck-will's-widow and Whip-poor-will similar. Pauraque
has distinctive call and longer tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long tail and white tail patches visible in
flight. Roosts on ground in brush.
Distinctive loud night call in South Texas.
