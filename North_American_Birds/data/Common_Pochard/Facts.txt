Common Pochard (Aythya ferina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium diving duck. Male has chestnut-red head,
black breast, pale gray body. Female brown with
paler face. Sloping forehead. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for aquatic plants, seeds, and invertebrates.
Forms large flocks in Eurasian winters. Rare
vagrant to eastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on freshwater lakes and marshes across
Eurasia. Winters on European lakes and reservoirs.
Vagrant to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Redhead very similar. Males nearly identical;
distinguished by head shape and bill pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's rust-red head with sloping forehead and
gray body. Peak-crowned head profile
separates from Redhead.
