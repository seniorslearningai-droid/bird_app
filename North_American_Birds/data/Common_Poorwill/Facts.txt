Common Poorwill (Phalaenoptilus nuttallii)
===========================================

Description
-----------
Smallest North American nightjar. Mottled brown-gray, short
rounded tail with white corners. Wide flat head.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal; calls poor-will repeatedly at night. Can enter
torpor in winter, the only North American hibernating bird.

Habitat
-------
Dry rocky hillsides, desert scrub, and open pine forests in
the western United States and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chuck-will's-widow and Whip-poor-will are both much larger.
Eastern Whip-poor-will has different call and range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest nightjar with short rounded tail. Poor-will call at
night in western desert scrub. White tail corners visible.
