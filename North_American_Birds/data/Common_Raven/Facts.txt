Common Raven (Corvus corax)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-black bird with wedge-shaped tail, heavy curved bill,
and shaggy throat feathers. Deep glossy iridescence.

Behavior
--------
Highly intelligent; uses tools and plays. Loud deep cronk call.
Soars on thermals. Pairs for life. Caches food.

Habitat
-------
Mountains, forests, deserts, and tundra across North America
and Eurasia. Generalist and adaptable scavenger.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Crow is smaller with rounded tail and higher-pitched
caw. Fish Crow and Chihuahuan Raven are both smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive size, wedge-shaped tail, and deep cronk call.
Soaring flight with flat wings distinguishes it from crows.
