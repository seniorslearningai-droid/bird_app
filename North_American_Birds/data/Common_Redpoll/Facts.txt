Common Redpoll (Acanthis flammea)
==================================

Description
-----------
Small finch with red forehead cap, black chin, and streaked
brown body. Male has rosy-pink wash on breast.

Behavior
--------
Highly nomadic; irrupts south in winter following seed crops.
Forms large flocks in birch and alder. Acrobatic feeder.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forests and tundra scrub for nesting. Winters in
birch forests and weedy fields across northern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hoary Redpoll is paler and frostier. Pine Siskin is streaked
but lacks the red cap. House Finch is larger and redder.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size with bright red forehead spot and streaked brown
plumage. Pink breast on males. Often in flocks in birches.
