Common Redshank (Tringa totanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper. Brown above, white below with
streaked breast, distinctive white wing patches
in flight, orange-red legs and bill base. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Noisy and alert; often the first to call alarm.
Forages on mudflats. Rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on European wet meadows and coasts. Winters
on European and African coasts. Rare vagrant
to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Yellowlegs similar shape but yellow legs.
Redshank's white wing patches distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Broad white wing patches visible in flight. Orange-
red bill base and legs. Noisy alarm
calls.
