Common Redstart (Phoenicurus phoenicurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat. Male has black face, white forehead,
orange-red breast and tail. Female
brown. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Constantly quivers the rufous tail. Flycatches from
perch. Very rare vagrant to
northeastern North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and gardens in Europe and Asia. Very
rare vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Redstart has different pattern. Bluethroat
has blue throat. No similar
bird in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-red tail constantly quivering. Male black face
with orange-red breast. Very
rare European vagrant.
