Common Ringed Plover (Charadrius hiaticula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plover. Brown above, white below with black
breast band, orange bill with black tip, orange
legs. About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Runs and stops to pick invertebrates. Breeds in
Arctic; rare vagrant to eastern North America
from Eurasia. Visits Atlantic beaches.

Habitat
-------
Sandy and pebbly beaches and tundra. Breeds in
Greenland and Eurasia. Rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated Plover nearly identical; Common Ringed
has complete breast band and different call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely similar to Semipalmated Plover. Complete
breast band and absence of webbing between
toes on Common Ringed Plover.
