Common Rosefinch (Carpodacus erythrinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch. Male rosy-red head and breast with
streaked brown back. Female
streaked brown. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds and berries. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska and Aleutian Islands
from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Scrub, woodland edges, and gardens of Europe and
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Finch male more extensive red. House Finch
male has streaked flanks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male rosy-red head and breast. Female streaked brown
without distinctive marks. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
