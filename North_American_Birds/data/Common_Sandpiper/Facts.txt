Common Sandpiper (Actitis hypoleucos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sandpiper. Brown above, white below with
brown breast patch. Constantly bobs tail. Horizontal
posture. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Constantly bobs tail while walking. Low flight with
stiff shallow wingbeats. Eurasian counterpart of
Spotted Sandpiper. Rare vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Breeds along Eurasian rivers and lakeshores. Winters
on Asian and African coasts. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic and Pacific coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Sandpiper nearly identical. Common Sandpiper
lacks spots in breeding and has longer wing stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Spotted Sandpiper but lacks
spots in breeding. Longer white wing stripe
in flight. Rare vagrant.
