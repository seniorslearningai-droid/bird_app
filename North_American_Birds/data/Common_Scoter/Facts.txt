Common Scoter (Melanitta nigra)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sea duck. Male entirely black with yellow-orange
knob on bill base. Female dark brown with pale
cheeks and dark cap. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mollusks in coastal and offshore waters.
Breeds on subarctic lakes. Rare vagrant to
North America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on subarctic and boreal lakes in Europe
and Asia. Winters on European coastlines.
Vagrant to Atlantic and Gulf coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Scoter nearly identical; male separated
by different bill knob coloring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male all-black plumage with yellow-orange bill knob.
Female's distinctive pale cheek contrasting
with dark cap.
