Common Shelduck (Tadorna tadorna)
===========================

Description
-----------
A large, goose-like duck with striking white body, dark green head, broad chestnut breast band, and bright red bill. Males have a knob on bill.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on invertebrates and small fish in estuaries by sweeping bill through mud. Nests in burrows. Gathers in large molting flocks.

Habitat
-------
Coastal estuaries, mudflats, salt marshes, and shallow lakes across Europe and Asia. Prefers sandy or muddy shorelines.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Distinctive coloration makes it hard to confuse. Egyptian Goose has similar size but very different pattern. Ruddy Shelduck is all-orange.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold tricolor pattern of green head, chestnut chest band, and white body is unique. Red bill and large size separate it from all ducks.
