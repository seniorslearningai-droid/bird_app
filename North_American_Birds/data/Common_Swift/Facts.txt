Common Swift (Apus apus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium swift. All dark sooty brown with pale white
throat patch. Long scythe-shaped wings and forked
tail. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Almost entirely aerial; lands only to nest. Screaming
calls in flight. Rare vagrant to eastern North America
from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Breeds throughout Europe and Asia, often in buildings.
Winters in sub-Saharan Africa. Rare vagrant
to Atlantic coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chimney and Vaux's Swifts similar but smaller.
Common Swift paler throat distinguishes it.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All dark with pale throat patch. Long scythe-shaped
wings. Screaming call. Rare European vagrant
on Atlantic coast.
