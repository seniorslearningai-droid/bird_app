Common Tern (Sterna hirundo)
=============================

Description
-----------
Gray above, white below, black cap, and orange-red bill with
dark tip. Long forked tail and graceful flight.

Behavior
--------
Dives headfirst for fish. Colonial nester on beaches and islands.
Aggressive defender of nest. Migrates long distances.

Habitat
-------
Coastal beaches, islands, and inland lakes across North America.
Winters in South America. Very common coastal tern.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Arctic Tern has all-red bill, shorter legs, and longer tail.
Forster's Tern has white primaries and orange bill in breeding.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-red bill with black tip and black outer primaries in
flight. Long tail streamers and graceful buoyant flight.
