Common Waxbill (Estrilda astrild)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch, brown with fine barring, red face mask,
red bill, pinkish-red belly.
About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious in large flocks. Forages for small
seeds in grasslands. Introduced
to Hawaii and Puerto Rico.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, open areas, and roadsides. Introduced
to Hawaii and Puerto Rico.
Native to sub-Saharan Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lavender Waxbill gray without barring. Orange-cheeked
Waxbill has orange cheeks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red face mask, finely barred brown body, red bill.
Very small. Gregarious
flocks in Hawaiian grasslands.
