Common Wood-Pigeon (Columba palumbus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large pigeon. Blue-gray overall with white neck patch
bordered by iridescent green and pink, white wing
bars visible in flight. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on crops, berries, and acorns. Forms large
flocks outside breeding season. Rare vagrant to
North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Farmland, woodland edges, parks, and gardens across
Europe. Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Band-tailed Pigeon similar size. Common Wood-Pigeon
has distinctive white neck patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, blue-gray plumage, and distinctive white
neck patch bordered by iridescent green.
White wing bars visible in flight.
