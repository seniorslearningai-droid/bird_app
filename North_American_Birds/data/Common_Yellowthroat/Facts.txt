Common Yellowthroat (Geothlypis trichas)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Male has bright yellow throat, white border above black mask.
Female is olive-brown with yellow throat and no mask.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense marsh vegetation. Male often sings from
exposed perch. Song is a distinctive witchety-witchety.

Habitat
-------
Marshes, wetland edges, and dense shrubby areas across
North America. One of the most common warblers.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kentucky Warbler has yellow spectacles and no black mask.
Nashville Warbler has gray head and no mask. Males unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's bold black mask with yellow throat is unmistakable.
Witchety-witchety song in marshy habitats very distinctive.
