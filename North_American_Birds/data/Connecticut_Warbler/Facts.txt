Connecticut Warbler (Oporornis agilis)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Complete white eye ring on gray hood, yellow underparts,
and olive-green back. Bold complete eye ring is key.

Behavior
--------
Walks on ground rather than hops. Very secretive; rarely
seen. Pumps tail while walking. Loud chip call.

Habitat
-------
Boreal bogs and wet forests for nesting. Migrates through
the Midwest. Winters in South America. Rare and secretive.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
MacGillivray's Warbler has broken eye ring and is found in
the West. Mourning Warbler has no eye ring and darker hood.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold complete white eye ring on gray hood and walking gait.
One of the most sought-after and difficult warblers to find.
