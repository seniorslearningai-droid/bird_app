Cook's Petrel (Pterodroma cookii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gadfly petrel, pale gray above with dark M-pattern,
bright white below, gray cap.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on New Zealand islands; migrates through North
Pacific. Uncommon offshore California
in late summer and fall.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean. Offshore California and Pacific
coast in migration.
Breeds on New Zealand islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Stejneger's Petrel very similar. Cook's has cleaner
white underparts and paler cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale gray above with bold M-pattern, very white below.
Small gadfly petrel. Offshore
California in late summer.
