Cooper's Hawk (Accipiter cooperii)
====================================

Description
-----------
Blue-gray above, rusty-orange barred below, rounded tail with
white tip. Red eye in adults. Rounded wings.

Behavior
--------
Agile forest hunter chasing birds through dense cover. Visits
bird feeders to catch prey. Flap-flap-glide flight pattern.

Habitat
-------
Forests, woodland edges, and suburban areas across North
America. Increasingly common in cities.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sharp-shinned Hawk is smaller with square tail. Northern
Goshawk is much larger with a white eyebrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rounded tail (Sharp-shin has square tail) and larger size.
Heavier head projection in flight. Red eye on adults.
