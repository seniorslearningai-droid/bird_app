Corn Crake (Crex crex)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium rail. Brown streaked upperparts, gray face
and breast, rufous-chestnut wing patches distinctive
in flight. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely secretive; heard much more often than seen.
Loud rasping "crex-crex" call. Rare vagrant to
North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in European grasslands and hay meadows. Winters
in Africa. Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other rails and crakes. Corn Crake's chestnut wing
patches distinctive in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut wing patches visible in flight distinctive.
Loud rasping "crex-crex" call heard from
tall grass at night.
