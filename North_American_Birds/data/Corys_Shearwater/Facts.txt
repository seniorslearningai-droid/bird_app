Cory's Shearwater (Calonectris diomedea)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Large shearwater with pale gray-brown upperparts, white underparts, and yellow
bill with dark tip. Lacks sharp contrast between dark and light. About 18 inches
with 44-inch wingspan. Lazy, arching flight on stiff wings.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on fish, squid, and offal at sea surface or shallow dives. Follows ships.
Breeds on Mediterranean islands; transoceanic migrant. Occurs in flocks.

Habitat
-------
Open Atlantic Ocean; common off U.S. East Coast in summer and fall. Pelagic,
rarely seen from shore except during strong storms.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Shearwater has dark cap and white neck collar. Sooty Shearwater is
all dark. Cory's pale, uniform coloring and yellow bill are distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large shearwater with uniform pale brown-gray above and yellow bill. Lazy,
banking flight. Atlantic pelagic, common on summer offshore trips. No dark cap.
