Costa's Hummingbird (Calypte costae)
======================================

Description
-----------
Male has iridescent purple cap and gorget with elongated
flared sides. Female is gray-green with white underparts.

Behavior
--------
Male performs steep U-shaped dive display with a shrill
whistling sound at the bottom. Found year-round in desert.

Habitat
-------
Desert washes, Sonoran desert scrub, and chaparral of the
southwestern United States and Baja California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Anna's Hummingbird male has a red-rose gorget and cap.
Black-chinned Hummingbird has black chin with purple lower band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Purple flared gorget and cap on male are unique. Female's
small size and desert range help narrow identification.
