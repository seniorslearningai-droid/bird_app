Couch's Kingbird (Tyrannus couchii)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Large flycatcher with gray head, olive-green back, yellow belly, and white
throat. Heavy bill. Brown-notched tail (no rufous). About 9.5 inches. Nearly
identical to Tropical Kingbird in appearance.

Behavior
--------
Perches openly on wires and treetops, sallying out to catch large insects.
Very aggressive, chasing larger birds. Loud, distinctive calls.

Habitat
-------
Lower Rio Grande Valley of southern Texas and northeastern Mexico. Woodlands,
forest edges, and towns. Year-round resident in Texas, some migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tropical Kingbird nearly identical; best distinguished by call. Cassin's Kingbird
has darker gray breast. Western Kingbird has white outer tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
In range (south Texas): gray head, yellow belly, heavy bill, notched brown tail.
Voice is critical — Couch's gives a breezy, rolling call; Tropical gives a loud
chittering. Both very similar; location and calls separate them.
