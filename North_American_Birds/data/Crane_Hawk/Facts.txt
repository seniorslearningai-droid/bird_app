Crane Hawk (Geranospiza caerulescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender long-legged gray hawk with two white tail
bands. Orange-red eyes. Long double-
jointed legs. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Uses long double-jointed legs to probe tree cavities
for prey. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest of Mexico and Central America. Rare
vagrant to southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mississippi Kite similar gray color but shorter legs.
Crane Hawk's very long legs distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long orange-red legs, slender build. Two white
tail bands. Rare vagrant from
Mexico to Texas.
