Craveri's Murrelet (Synthliboramphus craveri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small alcid. Black above, white below. Partial white
collar, dark partial chin bar. Slightly different
from Scripps's. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests on islands in Gulf of California. Post-breeding
dispersal brings birds into California waters
in late summer.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on islands in the Gulf of California, Mexico.
Post-breeding dispersal to California coastal
waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scripps's and Guadalupe Murrelets similar. Craveri's
has partial dark chin bar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Partial dark chin bar on white throat. Slimmer
than Scripps's. Found in California
waters in late summer.
