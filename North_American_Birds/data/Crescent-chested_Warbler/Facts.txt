Crescent-chested Warbler (Oreothlypis superciliosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, gray above, yellow-orange below with
chestnut crescent on breast,
bold white supercilium. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages actively in pine-oak forest. Rare vagrant
to Arizona and New Mexico
from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Montane pine-oak forests of Mexico. Rare vagrant
to southeastern Arizona and
New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nashville Warbler lacks chestnut crescent. Tennessee
Warbler plain below.
Chestnut crescent unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut crescent on yellow breast distinctive. Gray
above, bold white supercilium.
Rare Arizona vagrant from Mexico.
