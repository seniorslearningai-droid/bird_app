Crested Auklet (Aethia cristatella)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium auklet. Entirely dark gray with forward-curling
orange crest, white facial plume, orange bill.
About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in enormous colonies on Alaskan islands. Emits
citrus-like odor during breeding. Dives for
copepods. Forms massive flocks.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on rocky islands in Bering Sea and Aleutian
chain. Winters in adjacent North Pacific waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whiskered Auklet smaller with different plumes. Crested
Auklet's forward-curling crest unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Forward-curling orange-yellow crest unique. All dark
gray with orange bill. Citrus scent
in breeding season.
