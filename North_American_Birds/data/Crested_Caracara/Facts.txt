Crested Caracara (Caracara plancus)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Black cap, bare orange-red face, white neck and breast with
black barring. Long yellow legs. Bold white wing patches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on the ground foraging for carrion and prey. Often
seen with vultures. Aggressive at carcasses.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, prairies, and ranchlands in Florida, Texas,
Arizona, and Mexico. Expanding range northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other raptor in North America looks like a Crested Caracara.
The bold black, white, and orange-red pattern is unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold pied pattern, bare orange-red face, and flat-winged soaring
flight. White wing patches flash in flight from above.
