Crested Myna (Acridotheres cristatellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, black throughout with white wing patch,
yellow bill, short white
feather crest on forehead. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Formerly established in Vancouver, British Columbia
but now nearly or completely
extirpated from North America.

Habitat
-------
Urban areas, parks, and farms. Introduced from
China. Former population in
Vancouver essentially gone.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Myna brown-bodied. European Starling iridescent
green-black with short bill
and different shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black with white wing patch and small frontal
crest. Yellow bill. Very
rare now in North America.
