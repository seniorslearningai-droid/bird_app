Crimson-collared Grosbeak (Rhodothraupis celaeno)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium grosbeak. Male black with crimson collar and
underparts. Female olive-green
with yellow collar. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dense thorn scrub. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas from northeastern
Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub of northeastern Mexico. Rare vagrant
to Rio Grande Valley, Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar black bird with crimson collar in range.
Cardinal family relative but
unique pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black with crimson collar and belly. Very rare Texas
vagrant from Mexico. Distinctive
crimson collar.
