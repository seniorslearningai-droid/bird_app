Crissal Thrasher (Toxostoma crissale)
======================================

Description
-----------
Large, long-tailed thrasher, plain gray-brown overall with pale gray underparts.
Distinctive chestnut undertail coverts (crissum) visible from below. Very long,
strongly decurved bill. About 11.5 inches. Dark malar streak.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground beneath dense desert shrubs, rarely exposing itself. Runs
quickly from cover to cover. Rich, melodious song given from shrub tops.

Habitat
-------
Dense desert scrub — mesquite thickets, desert washes with dense shrubs in
Arizona, New Mexico, California, and Baja California. Secretive resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Curve-billed Thrasher has spotted breast and orange eye. LeConte's Thrasher
is paler with less obvious crissum. Long bill and chestnut undertail are key.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long, strongly curved bill. Chestnut undertail coverts (look from behind).
Plain gray-brown plumage in dense desert scrub. Rich, varied musical song.
