Crowned Slaty Flycatcher (Empidonomus aurantioatrocristatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher, slate gray above and below, black
crown with orange-yellow crown patch.
About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
South American migrant species. Very rare vagrant to
North American Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and cerrado of South America. Very rare
vagrant to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kingbirds similar size. Crowned Slaty Flycatcher has
slate-gray plumage with orange crown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slate-gray plumage with orange-yellow crown patch. Rare
South American vagrant to
Atlantic coast.
