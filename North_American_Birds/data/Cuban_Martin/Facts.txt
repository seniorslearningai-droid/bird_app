Cuban Martin (Progne cryptoleuca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large swallow endemic to Cuba. Male glossy blue-black.
Female brown above with white
belly, concealed white bases to feathers. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages aerially over open areas. Rare vagrant
to Florida. Nests in cliff
cavities and buildings in Cuba.

Habitat
-------
Open areas and towns of Cuba. Rare vagrant to
Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Martin male nearly identical. Female Purple
Martin distinguished by
streaked underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Female: white belly with concealed white feather
bases on breast. Male all-dark.
Very rare Florida vagrant from Cuba.
