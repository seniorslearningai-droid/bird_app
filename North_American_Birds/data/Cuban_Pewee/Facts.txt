Cuban Pewee (Contopus caribaeus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pewee, olive-brown above, grayish white below.
Prominent white crescent behind eye.
About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies for insects. Caribbean species; rare vagrant
to southern Florida from Cuba and
Bahamas.

Habitat
-------
Woodland of Cuba and Bahamas. Rare vagrant to
southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Wood-Pewee similar but lacks white crescent
behind eye.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White crescent behind eye. Small olive pewee. Rare
vagrant to Florida from Caribbean
islands.
