Cuban Vireo (Vireo gundlachii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small vireo, olive-green above, pale below with yellowish
sides, white supercilium, no
wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Caribbean island vireo. Very rare vagrant to southern
Florida from Cuba.

Habitat
-------
Forest and thicket of Cuba. Very rare vagrant to
southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other vireos have wing bars. Cuban Vireo lacks wing
bars with olive plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive vireo without wing bars. White supercilium.
Very rare Florida vagrant from
Cuba.
