Curlew Sandpiper (Calidris ferruginea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper with noticeably decurved bill. Breeding
adult brick-red below; non-breeding gray above,
white below. White rump. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats wading deeper than Dunlin. Rare
but regular vagrant to both coasts from
Eurasian breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Siberian Arctic tundra. Winters on African
coasts. Uncommon vagrant to both coasts
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dunlin similar with decurved bill. Curlew Sandpiper
has white rump and more curved bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Decurved bill longer than Dunlin's. White rump
distinctive in flight. Bright reddish breeding
plumage unmistakable.
