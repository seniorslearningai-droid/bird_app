Curve-billed Thrasher (Toxostoma curvirostre)
==============================================

Description
-----------
Gray-brown above, spotted below with orange-yellow eye.
Distinctly curved bill. Somewhat cryptic on the ground.

Behavior
--------
Forages by tossing leaf litter with its bill. Loud two-note
whit-wheet call. Sings a complex melodious song.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub, cacti, and chaparral of the Sonoran and
Chihuahuan deserts in the southwestern United States and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bendire's Thrasher has a straighter bill and faint breast spots.
California Thrasher has a longer bill and lacks spots.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Curved bill, spotted breast, and orange eye in desert habitat.
Distinctive two-note whit-wheet whistle call.
