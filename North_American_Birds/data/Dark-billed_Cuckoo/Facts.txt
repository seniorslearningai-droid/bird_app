Dark-billed Cuckoo (Coccyzus melacoryphus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender cuckoo. Brown above, whitish below with
buff wash on breast, dark cap. Bill entirely
dark. Long tail with white spots. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive; forages in dense vegetation for insects
and caterpillars. Rare vagrant to North America
from South America.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in South American forests and woodland.
Rare vagrant to North America; most records from
eastern US and Caribbean islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-billed and Black-billed Cuckoos similar. Dark-
billed has all-dark bill and buffier underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-dark bill distinguishes from Yellow-billed Cuckoo.
Buff wash on underparts. Rare vagrant
from South America.
