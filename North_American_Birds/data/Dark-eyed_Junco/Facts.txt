Dark-eyed Junco (Junco hyemalis)
==================================

Description
-----------
Variable plumage by region; typically dark gray hood and back
with white belly and white outer tail feathers.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground below feeders. Flashes white outer tail
feathers in flight. Forms flocks in winter. Twittering call.

Habitat
-------
Conifer forests for breeding. Winters widely across North
America in woodlands, suburbs, and gardens.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oregon, Slate-colored, Pink-sided, White-winged, and Gray-headed
forms vary regionally but all show white outer tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White outer tail feathers flashing in flight are always present.
Hooded dark gray head and pink bill identify most forms.
