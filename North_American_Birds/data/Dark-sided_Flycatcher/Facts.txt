Dark-sided Flycatcher (Muscicapa sibirica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, dark gray-brown above, white below
with dark brownish-gray sides
and breast patches. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from forest perch for flying insects. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska from
East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forest of Siberia and East
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Asian Brown Flycatcher paler, less contrasting sides.
Gray-streaked Flycatcher has
distinct breast streaking.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark smudgy sides contrasting with white center.
Dark brown above. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
