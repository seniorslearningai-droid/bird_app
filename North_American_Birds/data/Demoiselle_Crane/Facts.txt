Demoiselle Crane (Grus virgo)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium crane, gray with black head and neck, white
ear tufts, long black breast
feathers. About 35 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in massive flocks over Himalayas. Very rare
vagrant to North America,
likely from escapes.

Habitat
-------
Dry grasslands and wetlands of Central Asia and Africa.
Extremely rare vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sandhill Crane all gray without black head. Whooping
Crane white. Black head
and neck distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray crane with black head, neck, and breast feathers.
White ear tufts. Very
rare vagrant or escaped bird.
