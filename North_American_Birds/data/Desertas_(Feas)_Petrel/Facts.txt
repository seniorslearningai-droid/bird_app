Desertas (Fea's) Petrel (Pterodroma feae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gadfly petrel, gray-brown above, gray breast
band, white underparts, dark cap.
About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Desertas and Cape Verde Islands. Migrates
into North Atlantic; rare vagrant
off Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean. Rare vagrant offshore Atlantic
coast of North America, particularly
off Carolinas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Zino's Petrel very similar but smaller. Bermuda Petrel
has different head pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large gadfly petrel with gray breast band and white
underparts. Dark cap. Rare Atlantic
vagrant, particularly off Carolinas.
