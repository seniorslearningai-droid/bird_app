Dickcissel (Spiza americana)
=============================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow-like bird. Males have yellow breast with black V-shaped bib,
gray head with yellow eyebrow stripe, and chestnut wing patch. Females are
paler without black bib. About 6 inches. Stout bill.

Behavior
--------
Males sing incessant "dick-dick-cissel" from grass or weed stalks. Forms huge
flocks in migration and on winter grounds. Nomadic; numbers vary by year.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, meadows, alfalfa and clover fields in central North America. Breeds
in Great Plains and Midwest. Winters in Central and South America savannas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Meadowlarks are larger with different pattern. House Sparrow lacks yellow and
black bib. Female Dickcissel resembles large sparrow but has yellow eyebrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black bib and yellow breast on male — distinctive. "Dick-cissel" song is
unmistakable. Chestnut wing patch. Open grasslands and agricultural fields.
