Double-crested Cormorant (Nannopterum auritum)
===============================================

Description
-----------
All black with orange-yellow facial skin and throat pouch.
Breeding adults develop small double crests on head.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish from the surface. Holds wings out to dry after
diving. Flies in V-formation. Colonial nester.

Habitat
-------
Coastal waters, bays, lakes, and rivers across North America.
Most common and widespread cormorant in North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Neotropic Cormorant is smaller with longer tail. Great Cormorant
is larger with white flank patches in breeding plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-yellow facial skin, kinked neck in flight, and wings-
spread drying posture. Most common cormorant inland.
