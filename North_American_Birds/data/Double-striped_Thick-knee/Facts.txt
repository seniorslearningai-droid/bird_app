Double-striped Thick-knee (Burhinus bistriatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large wader with long yellow legs, large yellow
eye, cryptic brown and buff streaked plumage,
two white facial stripes. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active at dusk and night. Runs rather than flies.
Rare vagrant to Texas from Mexico and Central
America. Calls loudly at night.

Habitat
-------
Dry grasslands, savannas, and open scrub in Mexico
and Central America. Rare vagrant to
South Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar large-eyed wader in US range. Double
head stripes distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Two bold white facial stripes, large yellow eye,
and long yellow legs on large
cryptic brown bird.
