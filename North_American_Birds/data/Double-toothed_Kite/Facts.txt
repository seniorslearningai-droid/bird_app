Double-toothed Kite (Harpagus bidentatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small kite with two tooth-like notches on bill.
Gray above, rufous-streaked below, white
throat stripe. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts lizards and insects in tropical forest. Very
rare vagrant to Texas and
Florida from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest of Mexico and Central America. Very
rare vagrant to southern Texas
and Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sharp-shinned Hawk similar size. Double-toothed Kite
has distinctive tooth-notched bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Two notches on bill sides. Rufous streaking below,
white median throat stripe. Very
rare tropical vagrant.
