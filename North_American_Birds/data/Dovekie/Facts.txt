Dovekie (Alle alle)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny alcid. Black above, white below, very small
round body with stubby bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims and dives for copepods and small crustaceans.
Winters offshore in huge numbers. Occasionally
blown onshore during storms.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in enormous colonies on Arctic cliffs. Winters
offshore in North Atlantic. Occasional winter
visitor to Atlantic coast after storms.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Much smaller than any puffin or murre. Ancient
Murrelet similar tiny size but in Pacific.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, black above and white below, stubby
bill. Like a tiny penguin swimming.
North Atlantic species.
