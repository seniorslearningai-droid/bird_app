Downy Woodpecker (Dryobates pubescens)
=======================================

Description
-----------
Small woodpecker with black and white pattern, white back stripe,
and short stubby bill. Male has red nape spot.

Behavior
--------
Forages on small branches and weed stems. Common at feeders.
Drums on wood to communicate. High-pitched pik call.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests, parks, orchards, and suburbs across North
America. The most common woodpecker at feeders.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hairy Woodpecker is larger with a longer bill. Ladder-backed
Woodpecker in the West has barred back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size and stubby bill distinguish it from Hairy Woodpecker.
White back stripe and small red nape spot on male.
