Dunlin (Calidris alpina)
========================

Description
-----------
Breeding adult has rufous back, black belly patch, and drooped
bill tip. Winter is plain gray-brown above, white below.

Behavior
--------
Forages in tight flocks on mudflats, probing mud with bill.
Spectacular murmurations in flight over tidal flats.

Habitat
-------
Tidal mudflats, beaches, and wetlands during migration and winter.
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Very common coastal shorebird.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Sandpiper and Sanderling lack drooped bill tip.
Curlew Sandpiper has more curved bill and white rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Drooped bill tip is diagnostic. Black belly patch in breeding
plumage unique. Common winter shorebird on coastal mudflats.
