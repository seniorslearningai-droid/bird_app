Dusky-capped Flycatcher (Myiarchus tuberculifer)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Myiarchus flycatcher, brown above, gray throat,
yellow belly, dark cap. No rufous
in tail. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gleans insects and small fruit. Common in oak-pine
forest in southeastern Arizona and
southwestern New Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Oak and pine-oak woodland canyons in southeastern
Arizona. Year-round in some locations,
summer visitor in others.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ash-throated Flycatcher has rufous in tail. Dusky-capped
has dark cap with no rufous tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark dusky cap, no rufous in tail. Yellow belly, gray
throat. Southeastern Arizona oak
woodland.
