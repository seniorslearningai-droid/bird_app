Dusky Grouse (Dendragapus obscurus)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Large grouse, dark gray overall with fine vermiculations. Males have yellow-
orange comb over eye, gray tail with dark subterminal band, and purplish neck
sacs inflated during display. Females brown and barred. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks forest floor; males display with deep booming calls. Eats conifer needles
in winter, high in trees. Polygynous; males display on ridges. Moves to higher
elevations after breeding.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests of western mountains, from sagebrush foothills
to high alpine. Found in Rocky Mountains and Pacific coast ranges.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sooty Grouse (coastal) nearly identical but darker; separated by range. Spruce
Grouse has black throat and red comb. Blue Grouse was the old name for both.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large, dark grouse in western mountain conifer forests. Gray overall with
yellow comb. Male's deep, resonant hooting display. Dense conifer habitat.
