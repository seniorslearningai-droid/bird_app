Dusky Thrush (Turdus eunomus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, dark brown above with rufous wing
panel, heavily spotted and scalloped
below. White supercilium. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for invertebrates. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and scrub in Siberia and Japan.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Naumann's Thrush similar but different in belly
pattern. Eyebrowed Thrush cleaner
pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark brown with rufous wings, heavily spotted below.
Bold white supercilium. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Asia.
