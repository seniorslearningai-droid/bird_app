Dusky Warbler (Phylloscopus fuscatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plain leaf warbler, brown above, buff-white
below with brown wash on flanks.
Pale supercilium. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in low dense scrub near ground. Occasional
hard tick call. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Dense low scrub, willows, and riverine vegetation
in Asia. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska and Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chiffchaff has more olive tones. Radde's Warbler
larger and buffer below.
Separation can be very difficult.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Uniformly brown above without green or olive tones.
Skulks near ground. Hard
tick call note. Alaska vagrant.
