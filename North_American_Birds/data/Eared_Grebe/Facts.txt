Eared Grebe (Podiceps nigricollis)
===================================

Description
-----------
Breeding adult has black neck and back, red eye, and golden
fan-shaped ear tufts. Winter is dusky gray and white.

Behavior
--------
Dives for aquatic invertebrates. Masses in enormous flocks
on alkaline lakes during molt. Rarely flies during migration.

Habitat
-------
Prairie lakes and alkaline wetlands in the western United States
for nesting. Winters on bays and coastal waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Horned Grebe has flat golden ear tufts, not fan-shaped.
Black-necked Grebe is the same species name worldwide.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding adult's golden ear fans on black head unmistakable.
Winter birds show dusky neck and high rounded crown.
