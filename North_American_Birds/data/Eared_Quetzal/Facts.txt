Eared Quetzal (Euptilotis neoxenus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large trogon, brilliant metallic green above, red belly.
Male has no tail plumes unlike
Resplendent Quetzal. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for fruit and insects in mountain pine-oak
forest. Rare and irregular visitor
to southeastern Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Mountain pine-oak forest. Rare irruptive visitor to
Chiricahua and other mountain ranges
in southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Elegant Trogon has copper-green back. Eared Quetzal
brighter metallic green with ear tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant metallic green with red belly, ear tufts.
Rare irruptive visitor to Arizona
mountain pine-oak forest.
