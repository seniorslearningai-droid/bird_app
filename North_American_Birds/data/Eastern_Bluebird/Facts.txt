Eastern Bluebird (Sialia sialis)
==================================

Description
-----------
Male is brilliant blue above with rusty-orange breast and white
belly. Female is gray-blue with washed orange breast.

Behavior
--------
Perches on wires and fences, dropping to ground for insects.
Nests in cavities. Soft musical warbling song.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, farmlands, and meadows with scattered trees
across the eastern United States. Nests in tree cavities.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Bluebird male has blue throat and orange on back.
Mountain Bluebird male lacks orange and is all sky blue.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant blue back and wings with rusty-orange breast on male
are unmistakable. Female shows blue tones in wings and tail.
