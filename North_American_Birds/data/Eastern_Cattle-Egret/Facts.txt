Eastern Cattle-Egret (Bubulcus coromandus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small white egret, orange-buff breeding plumes on
head and neck. Very similar to Western
Cattle-Egret. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Follows large animals. East Asian counterpart to Western
Cattle-Egret. Vagrant to western
Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Pastures and open areas of East and Southeast Asia.
Vagrant to western Alaska and
Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Cattle-Egret nearly identical. Eastern has
more orange on head and
neck in breeding.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Western Cattle-Egret. More extensive
orange-buff head and neck in breeding.
East Asian species.
