Eastern Kingbird (Tyrannus tyrannus)
======================================

Description
-----------
Black above with white underparts and white-tipped black tail.
Concealed orange-red crown stripe rarely visible.

Behavior
--------
Very aggressive toward large birds and raptors. Sallies from
exposed perches to catch flying insects. Buzzy calls.

Habitat
-------
Open country, forest edges, and orchards across the eastern
United States and Canada. Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Kingbird has yellow belly and gray breast. Gray Kingbird
lacks white tail tip and is found only coastally.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black-and-white pattern with white tail tip is distinctive.
No other common flycatcher has this crisp pied coloring.
