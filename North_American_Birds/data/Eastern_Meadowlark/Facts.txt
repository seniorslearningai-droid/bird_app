Eastern Meadowlark (Sturnella magna)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Yellow breast with black V-shaped necklace, streaked brown back,
white outer tail feathers visible in flight.

Behavior
--------
Walks through grass probing for insects. Males sing from fence
posts. Loud flute-like spring-of-the-year song.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, meadows, and farm fields of the eastern United
States. Declining due to agricultural changes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Meadowlark is nearly identical but has a different
song. Both have yellow breast with black V.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow breast with black V is unmistakable in open fields.
Song is the best way to distinguish from Western Meadowlark.
