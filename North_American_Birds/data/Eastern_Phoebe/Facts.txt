Eastern Phoebe (Sayornis phoebe)
==================================

Description
-----------
Sooty-brown above, dirty white below with darker breast.
No eye ring or wingbars. Constantly pumps tail downward.

Behavior
--------
Tail-pumping is distinctive. Often nests on bridges and eaves.
One of the first spring migrants. Fee-bee song.

Habitat
-------
Woodland edges, farms, and areas near water across the eastern
United States. Often nests under bridges.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Wood-Pewee is similar but has wingbars. Empidonax
flycatchers have eye rings and wingbars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Constant downward tail pumping is unique among eastern
flycatchers. Fee-bee song and lack of wingbars or eye ring.
