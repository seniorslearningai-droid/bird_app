Eastern Screech-Owl (Megascops asio)
=====================================

Description
-----------
Small owl with ear tufts, yellow eyes, and streaked-and-barred plumage in
either gray or rufous color morphs. About 8.5 inches. White-spotted scapulars.
Streaked underparts. Camouflaged against tree bark.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal hunter of insects, small rodents, birds, and earthworms. Roosts in
tree cavities by day, resembling broken branch. Descending whinny call and
long, even trill are distinctive.

Habitat
-------
Forests, woodlands, suburban parks, and orchards throughout eastern North
America. Very adaptable; uses nest boxes readily. Year-round resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Screech-Owl nearly identical but gray only, different call. Long-eared
Owl much larger. Northern Saw-whet Owl lacks ear tufts. Flammulated Owl smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small, tufted owl in Eastern woodlands. Two color morphs (gray and rufous).
Descending whinny call at night. Often seen roosting in tree cavities by day.
