Eastern Spot-billed Duck (Anas zonorhyncha)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dabbling duck resembling female Mallard.
Brown body, dark crown and eye stripe, yellow-tipped
dark bill with orange-red spots at base. About 23 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles in shallow freshwater for plants, invertebrates,
and seeds. Gregarious outside breeding season.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska from East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds across East Asia in freshwater wetlands,
lakes, and rivers. Winters in eastern China
and Japan. Vagrant to Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Female Mallard nearly identical. Chinese Spot-billed
Duck very similar. Distinguished by bill
pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow bill tip with red basal spots distinctive.
Dark body with pale face and
prominent eye stripe.
