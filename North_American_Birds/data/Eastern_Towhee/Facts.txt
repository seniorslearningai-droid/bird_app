Eastern Towhee (Pipilo erythrophthalmus)
=========================================

Description
-----------
Male has black hood, rufous sides, white belly, and white wing
spots. Female is brown where male is black.

Behavior
--------
Scratches in leaf litter with both feet simultaneously.
Drink-your-tea song and chewink call note. Secretive in brush.

Habitat
-------
Dense shrubby forest understory and thickets across the
eastern United States. Common in brushy habitats.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Towhee is nearly identical but has white spots on back.
Range overlaps slightly in the Great Plains.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous sides and black hood on male. Drink-your-tea song and
two-footed scratch in leaf litter are behavioral clues.
