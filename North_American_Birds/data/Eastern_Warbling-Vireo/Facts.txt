Eastern Warbling-Vireo (Vireo gilvus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plain vireo, olive-gray above, whitish below
with faint yellowish wash on sides.
No wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Males sing a distinctive rambling warble almost
continuously. Forages in canopy for
caterpillars and insects.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests, orchards, parks, and suburban
trees across eastern and central
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Warbling-Vireo nearly identical but western range.
Red-eyed Vireo has bold eye stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain vireo with no wing bars. Persistent warbling
song. Eastern range. Faint
supercilium but no bold eye stripe.
