Eastern Wood-Pewee (Contopus virens)
======================================

Description
-----------
Olive-gray above, pale below with two whitish wingbars and
no eye ring. Flat crown and medium-sized bill.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from mid-canopy perch to catch insects in flight.
Returns to same perch. Mournful pee-a-wee song.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests across the eastern United States.
Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Phoebe lacks wingbars and pumps tail. Empidonax
flycatchers have eye rings. Western Wood-Pewee identical.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Wingbars with no eye ring in forest interior. Distinctive
mournful pee-a-wee song heard throughout summer.
