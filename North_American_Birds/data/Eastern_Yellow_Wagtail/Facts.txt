Eastern Yellow Wagtail (Motacilla tschutschensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small wagtail, olive-green above, bright yellow below,
gray or olive head. Long tail
constantly wagging. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bobs tail up and down constantly. Forages in open
wet areas. Breeds in western
Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows, marshes, and tundra in western Alaska.
Winters in Southeast Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Citrine Wagtail has yellow head. White Wagtail gray
above without yellow. Gray
Wagtail has gray back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow underparts with olive or grayish head. Constant
tail wagging. Western Alaska
breeder on wet tundra.
