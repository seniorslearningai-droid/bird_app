Egyptian Goose (Alopochen aegyptiaca)
===========================

Description
-----------
A large, long-legged waterfowl with brown body, conspicuous brown eye patches, and a pink bill. Shows white wing patches in flight.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grass and grain. Highly territorial and aggressive. Often seen perching on trees and buildings, unusual for a goose.

Habitat
-------
Native to Africa and the Nile Valley. Introduced and established in Europe and North America near lakes, rivers, and parks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Could be confused with Ruddy Shelduck in flight. The large white wing patches and brown eye patches are distinctive features.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown eye patches (spectacles), pink bill, white wing patches visible in flight, and long pink legs make this species unmistakable.
