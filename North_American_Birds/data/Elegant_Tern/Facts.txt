Elegant Tern (Thalasseus elegans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large tern. White with gray back, shaggy black
crest, long slender orange bill. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish. Breeds in small colonies in
Baja California. Post-breeding dispersal north along
Pacific coast in late summer.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on islands in Gulf of California. Post-breeding
dispersal to California coast. Winters
along South American Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Royal Tern larger with stouter bill. Elegant Tern
slimmer with longer drooping bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slender drooping orange bill. Shaggy black crest.
Post-breeding flocks on California beaches
in late summer.
