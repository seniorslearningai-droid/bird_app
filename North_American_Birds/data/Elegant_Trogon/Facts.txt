Elegant Trogon (Trogon elegans)
================================

Description
-----------
Male has metallic green head and back, red belly, white breast
band, and long barred tail. Female is brown with red belly.

Behavior
--------
Sits motionless in forest, hard to spot despite bright colors.
Croaking kow-kow-kow call. Nests in woodpecker holes.

Habitat
-------
Riparian canyon forests in southeastern Arizona, especially
sycamore canyons. Winters in Mexico. Highly sought by birders.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird in the United States has the same combination.
Eared Quetzal is extremely rare visitor to Arizona.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant red belly, green back, and long barred tail are
unmistakable in Arizona canyon forests.
