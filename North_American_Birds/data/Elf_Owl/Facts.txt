Elf Owl (Micrathene whitneyi)
===========================

Description
-----------
World's smallest owl. Tiny, brown streaked, pale
eyebrows, no ear tufts, very short
tail. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal insect hunter. Nests in saguaro cactus cavities
and tree holes. Summer resident
in desert Southwest.

Habitat
-------
Saguaro desert, oak woodland, and riparian canyons
of Arizona, New Mexico, and Texas.
Summer resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pygmy-owls larger with longer tail. Elf Owl tiny with
very short tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size with very short tail. Nocturnal. Nests in
saguaro cactus holes. Desert Southwest
summer resident.
