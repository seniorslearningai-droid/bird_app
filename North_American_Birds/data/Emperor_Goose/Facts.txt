Emperor Goose (Anser canagicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized goose with blue-gray body, white head and hindneck,
orange-yellow legs. Black throat and foreneck. Adults have
distinctly scaly patterned plumage.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on intertidal invertebrates, algae, and plant material.
Forms large flocks during migration. Nests in low tundra
vegetation near coastal areas.

Habitat
-------
Breeds along western Alaska coast and Siberia. Winters on
Aleutian Islands and Alaska Peninsula shorelines in shallow
coastal waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater White-fronted Goose has similar size but lacks the
white head. Snow Goose has all-white body. Cackling Goose
is smaller with different head coloring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for the distinctive white head contrasting with dark
body. The scaly blue-gray plumage pattern and orange legs
are unique among North American geese.
