Erckel's Spurfowl (Pternistis erckelii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large francolin from East Africa. Brown with chestnut-
streaked underparts and distinctive chestnut crown.
Males have leg spurs. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Found in dense forest understory. Forages for
seeds, berries, and invertebrates on ground.
Introduced to Hawaii in 1957 for hunting.

Habitat
-------
Dense montane forest and woodland. In Hawaii found
on Big Island and Maui in forested
slopes and areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Hawaiian francolins. Erckel's larger with
distinctive chestnut streaking on breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, chestnut streaking on breast and flanks,
chestnut crown. Loud calls
in Hawaiian forest habitat.
