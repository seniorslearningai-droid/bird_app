Eskimo Curlew (Numenius borealis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small curlew, possibly extinct. Brown streaked plumage,
slightly downcurved bill, cinnamon wing lining.
About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migrated in huge flocks historically; hunted nearly
to extinction. Last confirmed sighting 1987.
May still survive but probably extinct.

Habitat
-------
Bred on Arctic tundra of northwest Canada.
Wintered in South America. Once very common;
now possibly extinct.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whimbrel and Little Curlew similar. Eskimo Curlew
smaller with cinnamon wing linings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Possibly extinct; last confirmed 1987. Small curlew
with cinnamon underwing. Any sighting
would be extraordinary.
