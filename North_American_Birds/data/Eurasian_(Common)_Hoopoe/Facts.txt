Eurasian (Common) Hoopoe (Upupa epops)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unmistakable bird with large fan-shaped crest, long
curved bill, black-and-white barred wings
and tail, pinkish-buff body. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Probes soil for insects and grubs with long curved
bill. Common in Europe; rare vagrant
to Alaska and Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and farmland of Europe and Asia. Rare
vagrant to Alaska and eastern
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American bird resembles Hoopoe. Fan
crest, barred wings completely distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unmistakable: fan crest, curved bill, black-and-white
barred wings. Cannot be confused
with any other bird.
