Eurasian (Common) Moorhen (Gallinula chloropus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium waterbird. Dark blackish with brown back,
white flank stripe, distinctive red frontal shield
and bill with yellow tip. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims and walks along water edges. Nods head while
swimming. Common in Europe and Asia; rare vagrant
to northeastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater ponds, marshes, and slow rivers with
emergent vegetation. Common in Europe; rare
vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Moorhen (North American race) nearly identical.
American Coot lacks white flank stripes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red frontal shield and yellow-tipped red bill. White
flank stripe on dark body. Head-
bobbing swim motion.
