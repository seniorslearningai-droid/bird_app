Eurasian Blackbird (Turdus merula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male entirely black with yellow bill and eye ring.
Female brown with spotted
breast. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common garden bird in Europe. Bold forager on
ground. Very rare vagrant to
eastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Gardens, parks, woodland, and scrub in Europe
and Asia. Very rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin similar body shape but has red
breast. Rusty Blackbird lacks
yellow bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: entirely black with yellow bill and eye
ring. Female: brown with spotted breast.
European vagrant.
