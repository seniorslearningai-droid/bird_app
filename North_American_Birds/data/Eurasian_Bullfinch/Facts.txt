Eurasian Bullfinch (Pyrrhula pyrrhula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky finch. Male bright pink-red breast, black
cap, gray back, white rump.
Female similar with brownish-pink. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Eats buds and seeds. Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands from
Asia.

Habitat
-------
Woodland and scrub of Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Finch male pinkish-red but streaked. No similar
black-capped red finch
in Alaska range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pink-red breast, black cap, gray back, white rump.
Stocky shape. Rare Alaska
vagrant from Siberia.
