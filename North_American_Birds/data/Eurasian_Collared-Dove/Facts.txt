Eurasian Collared-Dove (Streptopelia decaocto)
===============================================

Description
-----------
Pale grayish-tan dove with narrow black half-collar on nape.
Squared tail with white corners and dark base.

Behavior
--------
Coos a repetitive coo-COO-coo. Very common around human
settlements. Aggressive toward other dove species.

Habitat
-------
Towns, suburbs, farms, and urban areas across North America.
Introduced from Europe; rapidly expanding continent-wide.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mourning Dove is slimmer with a pointed tail and no collar.
Ringed Turtle-Dove is similar but paler and less common.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black half-collar on nape and square tail with white corners.
Repetitive three-note coo-COO-coo call in urban areas.
