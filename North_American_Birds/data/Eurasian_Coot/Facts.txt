Eurasian Coot (Fulica atra)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium waterbird. Entirely sooty black with brilliant
white bill and white frontal shield. Red eye.
About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims in open water; dives for aquatic plants.
Aggressive and quarrelsome. Rare vagrant to
eastern North America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater and coastal lakes and ponds across Eurasia.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast
and interior North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Coot nearly identical; nearly impossible to
tell apart. Both black with white bill and shield.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to American Coot. Eurasian has
slightly different bill and frontal shield shape.
Rare vagrant from Eurasia.
