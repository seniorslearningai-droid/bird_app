Eurasian Curlew (Numenius arquata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large curlew. Brown streaked plumage, white
rump, very long strongly downcurved bill. About
22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats and grasslands for invertebrates
and worms. Loud "cur-lee" call. Rare vagrant
to Atlantic coast from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on European and Asian moorland and wet
meadows. Winters on European coasts. Rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whimbrel smaller. Far Eastern Curlew has dark rump.
White rump distinguishes Eurasian Curlew.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large curlew with white rump and lower
back visible in flight. Loud "cur-lee"
call distinctive.
