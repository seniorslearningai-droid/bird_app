Eurasian Dotterel (Charadrius morinellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium plover. Breeding adult has rusty-orange breast
with white breast band, gray-brown upperparts,
bold white supercilium. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on insects and invertebrates. Tame and approachable.
Female more colorful; roles reversed in breeding.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on high Arctic tundra in Eurasia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and Pacific coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Charadrius plovers. Eurasian Dotterel's white
breast band and supercilium distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white supercilium and white breast band across
orange-rufous breast. Tame behavior.
Rare vagrant in western Alaska.
