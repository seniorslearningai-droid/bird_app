Eurasian Hobby (Falco subbuteo)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant small falcon, dark gray above, white below
with heavy black streaks, rufous thighs
and vent. Long pointed wings. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swift aerial hunter catching swallows and large insects
in flight. Common in Europe; rare
vagrant to Alaska and Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Open country with scattered trees in Europe and Asia.
Rare vagrant to Alaska and
eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Merlin similar size. Eurasian Hobby has rufous thighs
and more elegant shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous thighs and undertail, heavy streaking below.
Long slender wings. Rare vagrant
from Europe and Asia.
