Eurasian Jackdaw (Coloeus monedula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small crow, glossy black with gray nape and sides
of head. Pale eye distinctive.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly social, nests in chimneys and cliff cavities.
Uses tools and solves complex
problems. Rare vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Open farmland, cliffs, urban areas across Europe
and Asia. Very rare vagrant
to northeastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Crow all black without gray nape. Common
Raven much larger. No
similar crow in North America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small crow with gray nape and pale silvery eye.
Very rare vagrant from
Europe. Distinctive two-tone pattern.
