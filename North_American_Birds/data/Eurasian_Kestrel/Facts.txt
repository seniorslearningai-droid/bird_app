Eurasian Kestrel (Falco tinnunculus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small falcon, male with rufous back and gray head
and tail, spots below. Female brown
barred throughout. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hovers while hunting small mammals and insects in
open areas. Common in Europe; rare
vagrant to Alaska and Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Open grassland and farmland of Europe and Asia. Rare
vagrant to Atlantic coast and
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Kestrel similar but smaller and more colorful.
Eurasian Kestrel larger with less
colorful face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Hovering kestrel behavior. Male's gray head with rufous
back. Larger and less colorful than
American Kestrel.
