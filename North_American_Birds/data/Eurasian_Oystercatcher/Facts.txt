Eurasian Oystercatcher (Haematopus ostralegus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large black and white shorebird with long orange-
red bill, pink legs, and orange eye ring.
About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pries open mollusks and probes for worms on
rocky shores and mudflats. Rare vagrant to
eastern North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Rocky and sandy coasts of Eurasia. Rare vagrant
to northeastern North America, mostly Atlantic
maritime provinces.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Oystercatcher nearly identical; has brown
back rather than black.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black upperparts with white underparts and orange-
red bill. Rare vagrant; American Oystercatcher
has brown back.
