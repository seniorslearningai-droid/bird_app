Eurasian Siskin (Spinus spinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, yellow-green with black crown and chin
on male, streaked olive on female,
yellow wing bars. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in alders and birches for seeds. Gregarious
in flocks. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forest of Europe and Asia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pine Siskin streaked without black crown. American
Goldfinch larger without
streaking.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male black crown and chin with yellow-green. Yellow
wing bars. Rare Alaska vagrant
from Asia.
