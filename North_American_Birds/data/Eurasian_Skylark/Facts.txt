Eurasian Skylark (Alauda arvensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium lark, streaked brown above, pale below with
streaked breast, small crest. Long
hind claw. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Delivers prolonged song while hovering high over
open fields. Introduced to Vancouver
Island, British Columbia.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, agricultural fields, meadows. Small
introduced population on southern
Vancouver Island, British Columbia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Horned Lark has bold face pattern with no crest.
Pipits lack crest and are
slimmer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small crest, streaked brown plumage, song-flight
hovering high over open fields.
Vancouver Island introduced population.
