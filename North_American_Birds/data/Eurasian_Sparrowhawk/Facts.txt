Eurasian Sparrowhawk (Accipiter nisus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small accipiter. Male blue-gray above with orange-
barred underparts; female brown above,
brown-barred below. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dashes through woodland to catch small birds. Common
in Europe; rare vagrant to Alaska
and Atlantic Canada.

Habitat
-------
Woodland and forest of Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant
to Alaska and Atlantic Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sharp-shinned Hawk very similar. Eurasian Sparrowhawk
male has orange barring below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: blue-gray above with orange-barred underparts.
Old World counterpart to Sharp-shinned.
Rare vagrant to Alaska.
