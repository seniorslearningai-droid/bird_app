Eurasian Tree Sparrow (Passer montanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow similar to House Sparrow. Brown above
with chestnut cap, white cheeks
with black spot. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in cavities and crevices. Forages for seeds
in flocks. Introduced population
in St. Louis, Missouri area.

Habitat
-------
Farmland, villages, and open areas. Small introduced
population in Missouri and
adjacent states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
House Sparrow lacks chestnut cap and cheek spot.
Male House Sparrow has gray
crown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut cap and black spot on white cheek distinctive.
Missouri area introduced
population. Similar size to House Sparrow.
