Eurasian Whimbrel (Numenius phaeopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large curlew with downcurved bill, striped crown,
brown streaked plumage, white lower back and rump.
About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats and rocky shores for crabs and
invertebrates. Rare vagrant to North America;
white rump distinguishes it from Hudsonian Whimbrel.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Eurasian tundra. Winters on African and
Asian coasts. Rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hudsonian Whimbrel nearly identical but has dark
rump. White rump is key.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump and lower back visible in flight
distinguishes from Hudsonian Whimbrel. Otherwise
nearly identical curlew.
