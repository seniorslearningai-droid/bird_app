Eurasian Wigeon (Mareca penelope)
===================================

Description
-----------
Medium dabbling duck. Male has chestnut head with creamy-yellow forehead, gray
body, pink breast, and white belly. White wing patch. Female is brown, similar
to American Wigeon female. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grass and aquatic vegetation in flocks. Often mixes with American
Wigeons. Male gives distinctive whistling call. Dives occasionally.

Habitat
-------
Eurasian breeder; regular winter visitor to both U.S. coasts, especially
Pacific coast. Found in estuaries, marshes, ponds, and flooded fields.
Rare inland.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Wigeon male has green eye patch and white crown. Female Eurasian
Wigeon extremely similar to female American; head color and axillary feathers
differ subtly. Often only one or two Eurasian in a mixed wigeon flock.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's chestnut head with cream forehead (not white crown of American). Look
carefully through wigeon flocks on Pacific coast. Whistling male call differs.
