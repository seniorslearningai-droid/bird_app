Eurasian Woodcock (Scolopax rusticola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large cryptic woodland bird. Reddish-brown plumage
with complex pattern, long bill, large eye positioned
high on head. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on earthworms by probing in forest floor.
Nocturnal. Males perform "roding" display flights
at dusk. Rare vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous woodland in Eurasia. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast of North America, mostly
in fall.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Woodcock similar but smaller and shorter
billed.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger than American Woodcock with stronger, bolder
barring. Very similar; best identified by
size and subtle plumage details.
