Eurasian Wryneck (Jynx torquilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Cryptically patterned woodpecker relative, gray-brown
mottled plumage with dark back stripe.
About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Eats ants; twists neck dramatically when threatened.
Rare vagrant to Alaska and Atlantic
coast from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and gardens of Europe and Asia. Rare
vagrant to Alaska and coastal
eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nightjars have similar cryptic plumage. Wryneck has
woodpecker-like feet and ant-eating habits.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Cryptic mottled brown-gray, no red on head. Unusual
neck-twisting behavior. Rare vagrant
from Europe and Asia.
