European Golden-Plover (Pluvialis apricaria)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium plover. Golden-speckled brown upperparts,
breeding male has black face and underparts
bordered white. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on fields and moorland for invertebrates
and berries. Forms large winter flocks. Rare
vagrant to eastern North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on European moorland and tundra. Winters
on European farmland and grasslands. Rare vagrant
to Atlantic coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Golden-Plover very similar. European lacks
axillar markings; white underwing.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White underwing coverts distinguish from American
Golden-Plover in flight. Rare vagrant on
Atlantic coast of North America.
