European Goldfinch (Carduelis carduelis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, red face, black-and-white head, brown
body, bright yellow wing band.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds especially thistles and teasel.
Gregarious in flocks. Small
introduced populations in North America.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, gardens, and woodland edges. Small introduced
populations in New York and
possibly other areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Goldfinch lacks red face and different pattern.
No other bird has red
face plus yellow wing band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red face, black-and-white head, bright yellow wing
band. Unmistakable. Small introduced
North American populations.
