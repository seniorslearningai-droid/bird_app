European Herring Gull (Larus argentatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, pale gray back, pink legs, yellow bill
with red spot, pale eye. White head
and underparts. About 24 inches.

Behavior
--------
Very common gull in Europe. Rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast of North America. Scavenges
in harbors and coastal areas.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic and North Sea coasts of Europe. Rare vagrant
to northeastern North America especially
in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Herring Gull nearly identical. European Herring
Gull often indistinguishable in the field.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very similar to American Herring Gull. Differences subtle
in wing tip pattern. Rare
Atlantic vagrant best identified carefully.
