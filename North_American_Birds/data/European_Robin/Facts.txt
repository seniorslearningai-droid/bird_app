European Robin (Erithacus rubecula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small round bird with orange-red face and breast,
brown above, white belly.
About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and tame in gardens. Sings year-round from
prominent perch. Very rare vagrant
to northeastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Woodland, gardens, and parks across Europe. Very
rare vagrant to northeastern
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin much larger. No similar bird with
orange-red face in North
America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright orange-red face and breast distinctive. Round
shape. Very rare European
vagrant to East Coast.
