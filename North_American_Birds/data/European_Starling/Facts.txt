European Starling (Sturnus vulgaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, iridescent black with green and purple
gloss, yellow bill in summer,
spotted in winter. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Introduced from Europe in 1890; now extremely abundant.
Forms massive murmurations. Aggressive
nest competitor.

Habitat
-------
Cities, suburbs, farms, parks, and open areas throughout
North America. One of commonest
birds in the continent.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Grackles have longer tails and different bill shape.
Blackbirds lack iridescence.
Short tail distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Short tail, iridescent black, yellow bill in summer.
Heavy spotting in winter.
Walking gait, not hopping.
