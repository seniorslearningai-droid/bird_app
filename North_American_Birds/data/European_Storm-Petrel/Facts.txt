European Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates pelagicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny dark storm-petrel with white rump, square tail,
weak fluttery flight. Smallest seabird
in Europe. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on European Atlantic islands. Very rare vagrant
to North American Atlantic coast.
Fluttery butterfly-like flight.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic and Mediterranean. Very rare vagrant
to offshore North American
Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wilson's Storm-Petrel has longer legs, more direct
flight. European is smaller and fluttery.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, fluttery flight, white rump, square tail.
Very rare Atlantic vagrant.
Smallest ocean seabird.
