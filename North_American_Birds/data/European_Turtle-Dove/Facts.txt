European Turtle-Dove (Streptopelia turtur)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dove with orange-brown scaly upperparts, pink
breast, and black and white striped neck patch.
Long graduated tail. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on seeds and plant material on ground. Long-
distance migrant. Rare vagrant to North America;
most records from Atlantic coast islands.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in European farmland, hedgerows, and open
woodland. Winters in sub-Saharan Africa. Rare vagrant
to eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oriental Turtle-Dove larger and darker. Mourning Dove
slimmer without neck patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-brown scaly back with striped neck patch. Small
size and purring flight call. Rare
vagrant in eastern North America.
