Evening Grosbeak (Coccothraustes vespertinus)
==============================================

Description
-----------
Large finch with massive pale yellow-green bill. Male is
yellow with black wings and white wing patch.

Behavior
--------
Nomadic; irrupts south following cone crops. Very loud call.
Cracks large seeds with powerful bill. Visits feeders.

Habitat
-------
Conifer and mixed forests of the western mountains and boreal
north. Winters irregularly across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Goldfinch is much smaller. Yellow Grosbeak is
similar but found only in Mexico and is rare in the US.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive pale bill, bold yellow-and-black pattern, and white
wing patch on male. Very loud ringing call in flocks.
