Eyebrowed Thrush (Turdus obscurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, brown above with bold white supercilium,
orange-buff breast and flanks,
white belly. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for earthworms and berries.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Forests of Siberia and East Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and occasionally
farther into North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dusky Thrush has more complex pattern. American
Robin has red breast without
bold supercilium.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white supercilium on brown thrush. Orange-buff
breast contrasting with white
belly. Rare Asia vagrant.
