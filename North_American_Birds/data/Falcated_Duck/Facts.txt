Falcated Duck (Mareca falcata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dabbling duck. Male has iridescent green and
chestnut head, white throat patch, long sickle-shaped
tertial feathers. Female mottled brown. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles and grazes on aquatic vegetation. Migrates
through East Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska and Pacific Coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds near rivers and lakes in East Asian forests.
Winters in East Asian coastal bays, estuaries,
and rice fields.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gadwall somewhat similar in female plumage. Male's
long drooping tertials unique among ducks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's elaborate head pattern and long curved
tertial feathers unmistakable. Female's finely
barred plumage with dark bill.
