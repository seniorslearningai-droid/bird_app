Fan-tailed Warbler (Basileuterus lachrymosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large ground warbler, dark gray above, bright orange-rufous
below, white-tipped tail
fanned and bobbed. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on or near ground, constantly fanning white-tipped
tail. Rare vagrant to Arizona
from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Rocky canyon streams in western Mexico. Rare vagrant
to southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird fans tail like this. Orange-rufous
below with white tail tips
unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White-tipped tail constantly fanned and bobbed. Orange-rufous
below. Dark gray above.
Rare Arizona vagrant.
