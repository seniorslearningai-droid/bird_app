Far Eastern Curlew (Numenius madagascariensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest curlew in the world. Brown streaked plumage,
very long strongly downcurved bill, brownish rump.
About 24 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats for crabs and invertebrates.
Rare vagrant to Pacific coast from East
Asian wintering grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Siberian and Alaskan tundra. Winters
on Australian and East Asian coasts. Rare
vagrant to western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eurasian Curlew similar but has white rump. Far
Eastern has dark rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous size and very long curved bill. Dark
brownish rump in flight. Rare vagrant
on Pacific coast.
