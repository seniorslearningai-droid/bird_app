Ferruginous Hawk (Buteo regalis)
==================================

Description
-----------
Largest North American buteo. Rusty-orange back and legs form
dark V against white belly in flight. Pale form common.

Behavior
--------
Hunts prairie dogs and ground squirrels. Soars on long wings
with wrist patches. Nests on the ground or in trees.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands and prairies of the western Great Plains and
intermountain basins. Winters in southwestern grasslands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-tailed Hawk lacks the rusty leg feathering. Rough-legged
Hawk has dark belly band and black wrist patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty leg feathers forming V shape against white belly in
flight. Largest buteo with very long broad wings.
