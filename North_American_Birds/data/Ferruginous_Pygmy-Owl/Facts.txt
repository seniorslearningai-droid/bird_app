Ferruginous Pygmy-Owl (Glaucidium brasilianum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny owl, rufous-brown streaked below. False eyespots
on nape, streaked crown. Rufous
tail with dark bars. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Diurnal and crepuscular. Hunts birds and lizards. Rare
resident in southern Texas and
southern Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Saguaro desert and mesquite in southern Arizona; riparian
thickets in southern Texas.
Rare resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Pygmy-Owl at higher elevation. Ferruginous
Pygmy-Owl in lowland desert and thorn
forest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny rufous-brown owl active by day. Rufous tail.
Southern Texas and Arizona lowlands.
False nape eyespots.
