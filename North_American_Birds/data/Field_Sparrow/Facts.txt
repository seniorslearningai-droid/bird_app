Field Sparrow (Spizella pusilla)
=================================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow with plain face, pink bill, rufous cap, rufous-gray back, and
buffy-white underparts. White eye ring, rufous eye line. About 5.75 inches.
No breast streaking. Clean, plain face is distinctive.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds and insects. Males sing a distinctive song of
accelerating notes ending in a trill — a "bouncing ball" pattern. Pairs
are territorial in open brushy areas.

Habitat
-------
Brushy fields, shrubby grasslands, forest edges, and overgrown pastures in
eastern North America. Prefers areas with shrubs for singing perches.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Tree Sparrow has central breast spot and bicolored bill. Chipping
Sparrow has white eye stripe over black line. Field Sparrow's pink bill and
plain face are key.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pink bill, rufous cap, plain unstreaked face, and white eye ring. Bouncing-
ball accelerating song in shrubby fields. Eastern brushy habitats.
