Fieldfare (Turdus pilaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large thrush, gray head and rump, chestnut back,
spotted orange-buff breast, white
underwings. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in flocks on open ground. Rare vagrant
to northeastern North America and
Greenland from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland, farmland, and berry-bearing hedges
in Europe. Rare vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mistle Thrush lacks gray head and chestnut back.
American Robin lacks spotted
pattern with chestnut back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray head and rump contrasting with chestnut back.
Bold spotted breast. Rare
European vagrant. Flocks in open.
