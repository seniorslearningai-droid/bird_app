Fish Crow (Corvus ossifragus)
==============================

Description
-----------
All black; nearly identical to American Crow but slightly
smaller. Best identified by voice.

Behavior
--------
Coastal and riparian scavenger. Voice is a nasal uh-uh or
ca-ha instead of the American Crow's caw.

Habitat
-------
Coastal areas, riverbanks, and tidal wetlands in the eastern
United States. Increasingly common inland along rivers.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Crow is nearly identical. Separating them requires
hearing the voice - Fish Crow gives a nasal two-note call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nasal two-note uh-uh call is the only reliable field mark.
Size difference too subtle to use in the field alone.
