Five-striped Sparrow (Amphispizopsis quinquestriata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Distinctive sparrow, gray above, white below with
black breast spot, five white
stripes on face. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense steep canyon vegetation. Difficult
to observe. Rare bird in
extreme southeast Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Dense brushy slopes in canyon systems of extreme
southeast Arizona. Very localized.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other sparrow has the distinctive five-stripe
face pattern. Unique and
unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Five white facial stripes distinctive. Black breast
spot. Gray above, white below.
Steep canyon scrub Arizona.
