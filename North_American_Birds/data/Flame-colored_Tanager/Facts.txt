Flame-colored Tanager (Piranga bidentata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tanager. Male flame-orange with black-streaked
back, white wing bars. Female
olive with wing bars. About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in pine-oak forest canopy. Rare vagrant to
Arizona mountains from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forests of Mexico and Central America. Rare
vagrant to southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Tanager has black back without streaks. Hepatic
Tanager darker red-orange.
Streaked back distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Flame-orange with streaked black back. White wing
bars. Rare Arizona vagrant
from Mexico.
