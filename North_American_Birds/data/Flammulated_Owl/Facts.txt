Flammulated Owl (Psiloscops flammeolus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny owl with cryptic gray-brown mottled plumage and rusty facial disc. Small size and dark eyes.

Behavior
--------
Strictly nocturnal, feeds almost entirely on insects. Migrates to Central America in winter.

Habitat
-------
Dry ponderosa pine forests in western mountains at mid to high elevations.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Much smaller than other owls. Western Screech-Owl has yellow eyes and streaked underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, dark eyes, moth-like flight, and insect-focused diet in pine forests.
