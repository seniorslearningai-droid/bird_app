Flesh-footed Shearwater (Ardenna carneipes)
===========================

Description
-----------
All dark brown shearwater with distinctive pale pink-flesh
colored bill and feet. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in Australia and New Zealand. Uncommon visitor
to Pacific coast of North America
in summer and fall.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean. Uncommon offshore Pacific coast.
Breeds in Southern Hemisphere.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sooty Shearwater similar but has dark bill. Flesh-footed's
pale pink bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-dark body with pale pink bill and feet diagnostic.
No other dark shearwater combines
all-dark plumage with pale bill.
