Florida Scrub-Jay (Aphelocoma coerulescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Blue and white jay with no crest. Blue wings, tail, and head collar; gray back and underparts.

Behavior
--------
Cooperative breeder living in family groups. Caches acorns and forages on the ground.

Habitat
-------
Florida scrub habitat — open sandy areas with low oaks and saw palmetto.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Similar to other scrub-jays but range is unique; Florida is the only state it lives in.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only jay in Florida scrub habitat. Lacks crest. Blue head, white forehead stripe.
