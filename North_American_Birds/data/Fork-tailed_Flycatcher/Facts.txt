Fork-tailed Flycatcher (Tyrannus savana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unmistakable flycatcher with enormously long forked
black tail. Black above, white below,
gray head. About 15 inches with tail.

Behavior
--------
Migratory South American tyrant flycatcher. Rare but
regular vagrant along Atlantic coast
during fall migration.

Habitat
-------
Open grassland and savanna of South America. Regular
vagrant to Atlantic coast particularly
in fall.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American flycatcher has such long
forked tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormously long forked black tail. Black cap, white
below. Regular fall vagrant to
Atlantic coast.
