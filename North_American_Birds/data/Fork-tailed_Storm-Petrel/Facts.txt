Fork-tailed Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates furcatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Pale gray storm-petrel, gray above and below, darker
eye patch, forked tail. Only gray
storm-petrel in North America. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on North Pacific islands off California, Oregon,
Washington, and Alaska. Common offshore
North Pacific seabird.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean. Breeds on offshore islands;
forages over North Pacific, particularly
common in Pacific Northwest.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
All other North American storm-petrels are dark. Gray
color unique among storm-petrels.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray plumage unique among storm-petrels. Forked tail,
darker gray eye patch. North
Pacific coast seabird.
