Forster's Tern (Sterna forsteri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern with white plumage, pale gray back, and deeply forked tail. Orange-red bill with black tip.

Behavior
--------
Hovers and plunge-dives for fish. Often seen over marshes and coastal waters.

Habitat
-------
Coastal bays, marshes, and inland lakes throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Tern very similar; Forster's has frosted wingtips and in winter has eye patch not cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange bill, frosted wingtips, isolated black eye patch in winter — not a dark cap.
