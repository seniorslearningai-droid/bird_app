Fox Sparrow (Passerella iliaca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large chunky sparrow with rusty-red streaked plumage. Heavy bill and large size distinguish it.

Behavior
--------
Scratches vigorously in leaf litter with both feet to uncover seeds and invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Dense thickets and shrubby areas; breeds in boreal and mountain forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Song Sparrow is smaller and less rusty. Fox Sparrow is largest of the streaked sparrows.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, heavy reddish streaking, and vigorous leaf-scratching behavior.
