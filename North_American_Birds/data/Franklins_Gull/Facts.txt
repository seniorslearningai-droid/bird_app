Franklin's Gull (Leucophaeus pipixcan)
========================================

Description
-----------
Medium gull with black hood in summer, partial hood in winter. Red bill, red
legs. Gray back, white underparts. White eye crescents. Black wingtips with
white spots. About 14.5 inches. Rosy flush on breast in breeding.

Behavior
--------
Follows farm equipment to catch insects. Breeds in large colonies on prairie
marshes. Migrates in spectacular flocks. Plunge-dives for fish occasionally.

Habitat
-------
Prairie marshes for breeding; migrates through Great Plains. Winters on Pacific
coast of South America. Rare on East Coast and Gulf Coast in fall.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Laughing Gull has longer wings with more black and no white in wingtips in
flight. Bonaparte's Gull is smaller. Franklin's hood is more complete in summer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black hood with white eye crescents. Red bill and legs. White spots in black
wingtips. Prairie migrant following plows. Smaller and rounder than Laughing Gull.
