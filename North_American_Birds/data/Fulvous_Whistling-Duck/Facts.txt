Fulvous Whistling-Duck (Dendrocygna bicolor)
=============================================

Description
-----------
Tawny-buff duck with dark brown back, white flank streaks, and white rump band.
Long dark legs trailing in flight. Upright posture. About 20 inches.
Whistling calls in flight. No wing patch.

Behavior
--------
Wades and dives for seeds and aquatic plants in shallow water. Highly nocturnal.
Forms flocks, often with other whistling ducks. Perches readily in trees.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, flooded rice fields, and shallow lakes in southern Texas,
Louisiana, California, and Florida. Widespread in tropics. Uncommon and local.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-bellied Whistling-Duck has black belly, orange bill, and white wing patch.
Fulvous is uniformly tawny with no wing patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Uniform tawny-buff plumage with white flank streaks and white rump band. No
wing patch in flight. Trailing legs. Whistling calls. Southern U.S. marshes.
