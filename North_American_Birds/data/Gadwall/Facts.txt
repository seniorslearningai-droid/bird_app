Gadwall (Mareca strepera)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dabbling duck. Male has intricate gray-brown vermiculated pattern with black rump; female resembles female Mallard.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles in shallow water for aquatic vegetation. Often found with other diving ducks.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, ponds, and lakes across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Female similar to female Mallard but slimmer with orange-sided bill and white speculum.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's gray-brown body with black rump and white speculum patch in wing.
