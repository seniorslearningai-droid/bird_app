Gambel's Quail (Callipepla gambelii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plump quail with bold black-and-white face pattern, chestnut flanks, and teardrop-shaped black head plume.

Behavior
--------
Runs rapidly on the ground in coveys. Rarely flies, preferring to run from danger.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub and brushy areas of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
California Quail has scaly belly; Gambel's has chestnut flanks and plain belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Teardrop head plume, chestnut sides, and plain cream belly in dry desert habitat.
