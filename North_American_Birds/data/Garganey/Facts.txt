Garganey (Spatula querquedula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dabbling duck. Male has brown head with bold
white supercilium, blue-gray wing patch. Female
mottled brown with striped face. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds by dabbling in shallow water. Long-distance
migrant wintering in Africa. Rare vagrant to
North America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in European and Asian freshwater wetlands.
Winters in sub-Saharan Africa. Seen in North
American marshes as a rare vagrant.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-winged Teal very similar. Female also resembles
other small dabbling duck females.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's bold white head stripe from eye to nape
diagnostic. Female separated from Blue-winged
Teal by face pattern and bill shape.
