Gila Woodpecker (Melanerpes uropygialis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium woodpecker with barred black-and-white back. Male has red cap; both sexes have plain tan face and underparts.

Behavior
--------
Excavates nest cavities in saguaro cacti. Eats cactus fruit, insects, and small lizards.

Habitat
-------
Sonoran Desert with saguaro cacti in Arizona and northwestern Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Similar to Golden-fronted Woodpecker but ranges don't overlap; Gila lacks yellow nape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Barred back, plain tan face, red cap on male, in saguaro cactus habitat.
