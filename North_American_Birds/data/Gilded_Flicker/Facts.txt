Gilded Flicker (Colaptes chrysoides)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large woodpecker with bright golden-yellow wing and tail linings, barred brown back, and spotted underparts.

Behavior
--------
Excavates nest holes in saguaro cacti. Feeds on ants and other ground insects.

Habitat
-------
Sonoran Desert with saguaro cacti in Arizona and California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Flicker overlaps but has red or yellow shaft color; Gilded has yellow only.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow wing linings, spotted breast, red mustache stripe on male, saguaro desert.
