Glaucous-winged Gull (Larus glaucescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull with gray wingtips matching mantle color, pink bill, and pale eye. Wingtips are gray not black.

Behavior
--------
Omnivorous scavenger along Pacific coast. Nests colonially on rocky islands.

Habitat
-------
Pacific Northwest coast from Alaska to California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Gull has dark gray back and black wingtips; Glaucous-winged has pale gray wingtips.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray wingtips matching mantle color on the Pacific coast — unique among large gulls.
