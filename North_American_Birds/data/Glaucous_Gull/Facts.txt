Glaucous Gull (Larus hyperboreus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large pale gull with white wingtips, pale gray mantle, and pink bill with yellow tip. No black in wings.

Behavior
--------
Scavenges along coastlines and at landfills. Aggressive and dominant over other gulls.

Habitat
-------
Arctic coasts in breeding season; winters on coasts and large lakes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Iceland Gull is smaller; Herring Gull has black wingtips. Glaucous has all-white wingtips.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest pale gull with completely white wingtips and massive pink bill.
