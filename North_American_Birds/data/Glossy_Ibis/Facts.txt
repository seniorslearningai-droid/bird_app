Glossy Ibis (Plegadis falcinellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium wading ibis, dark chestnut and glossy green-purple
plumage, downturned bill, no facial
skin in non-breeding. About 23 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in marshes and rice fields. Expanding breeding
range northward along Atlantic coast.
Flocks in migration.

Habitat
-------
Coastal and inland wetlands. Breeds Atlantic coast
marshes; expanding range northward.
Winters in Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-faced Ibis very similar. Glossy Ibis lacks
white feathering bordering facial skin.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark chestnut-green ibis without white face feather
border. Compare to White-faced Ibis.
Atlantic coast species.
