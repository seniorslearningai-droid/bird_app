Golden-cheeked Warbler (Setophaga chrysoparia)
===============================================

Description
-----------
Male has black crown and back, bright yellow cheeks and forehead, black eye
line, black throat, and white underparts with black streaks. Female is duller
with olive-green back. About 4.75 inches. Endangered species.

Behavior
--------
Forages in Ashe juniper and oaks, gleaning insects from bark. Males sing a
distinctive buzzy song. Nests made of juniper bark strips. Very site-faithful.

Habitat
-------
Ashe juniper and oak woodlands on the Edwards Plateau of central Texas — the
only place this species breeds. Winters in pine-oak highlands of Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-throated Green Warbler has green back, yellow-green cheeks. Townsend's
Warbler has yellow face with dark cheek. Golden-cheeked is endemic to Texas.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's combination of black crown, yellow cheeks, and black eye line is unique.
Only in central Texas cedar-oak hills during breeding. Buzzy, ascending song.
