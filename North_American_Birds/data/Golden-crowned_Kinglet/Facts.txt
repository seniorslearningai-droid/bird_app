Golden-crowned Kinglet (Regulus satrapa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny bird with olive-green plumage, white eyebrow, and brilliant golden-orange crown patch.

Behavior
--------
Constantly flicks wings while foraging in coniferous foliage for tiny insects and eggs.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests; breeds in boreal and mountain forests, winters widely.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruby-crowned Kinglet has bold white eye-ring and red crown; Golden-crowned has striped face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Striped black-and-white head with gold-orange crown; constant wing-flicking.
