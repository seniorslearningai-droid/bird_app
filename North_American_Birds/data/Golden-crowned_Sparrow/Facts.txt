Golden-crowned Sparrow (Zonotrichia atricapilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sparrow with bold yellow patch on forehead bordered by black stripes. Brownish body.

Behavior
--------
Scratches on the ground for seeds and berries. Forms flocks with White-crowned Sparrows.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in subalpine shrubs; winters in chaparral and brushy areas of Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-crowned Sparrow has white crown stripes; Golden-crowned has a yellow forehead patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow forehead patch with black border, large size, Pacific coast in winter.
