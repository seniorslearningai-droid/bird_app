Golden-crowned Warbler (Basileuterus culicivorus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, olive-green above, yellow below, golden-yellow
crown stripe bordered by
black. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages low in forest understory. Rare vagrant to
Texas from Mexico and Central
America.

Habitat
-------
Forest understory of Mexico and Central America. Rare
vagrant to southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rufous-capped Warbler has rufous cap. Nashville Warbler
yellow below without crown
stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Golden-yellow crown stripe bordered by black stripes.
Olive above, yellow below.
Rare Texas vagrant.
