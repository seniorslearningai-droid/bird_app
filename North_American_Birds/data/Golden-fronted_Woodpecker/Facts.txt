Golden-fronted Woodpecker (Melanerpes aurifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium woodpecker with barred black-and-white back. Male has yellow-orange nape and red cap; female lacks red.

Behavior
--------
Forages on cactus, trees, and ground for insects, acorns, and cactus fruit.

Habitat
-------
Dry woodlands and mesquite brush in Texas and Oklahoma.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gila Woodpecker has plain face with no yellow nape. Red-bellied has red extending to forehead.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow nape, barred back, in Texas woodlands — combination unique to this species.
