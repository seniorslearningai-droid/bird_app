Golden-winged Warbler (Vermivora chrysoptera)
===========================

Description
-----------
Distinctive warbler with golden wing patch, gray body, and bold black-and-white head pattern.

Behavior
--------
Forages in shrubby forest edges, probing dead leaves and bark for insects.

Habitat
-------
Breeding in early successional habitats in northeastern and Appalachian regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-winged Warbler has white wing bars and yellow body; hybrids are common.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body, golden wing patch, black throat, and bold black mask.
