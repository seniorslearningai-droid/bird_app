Golden Eagle (Aquila chrysaetos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark eagle with golden-brown nape and upper wing coverts. Long tail with narrow bands.

Behavior
--------
Hunts rabbits, ground squirrels, and other mammals. Soars on flat wings.

Habitat
-------
Open and mountainous terrain across western North America; uncommon in east.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Immature Bald Eagle has white mottling; Golden Eagle shows golden nape and uniform dark wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Golden nape, dark brown body, long banded tail, and soaring flat-winged profile.
