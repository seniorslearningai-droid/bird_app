Golden Pheasant (Chrysolophus pictus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large pheasant. Male spectacular with golden crown,
orange ruff, red underparts,
long barred tail. About 40 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dense woodland. Small introduced populations
in British Columbia and possibly
elsewhere. Shy despite bright colors.

Habitat
-------
Dense woodland and scrub. Small introduced populations
in parts of North America.
Native to China.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird approaches male Golden Pheasant's
combination of gold, orange, and
red. Unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male unmistakable with golden crown and red underparts.
Brilliantly colorful. Introduced
North American populations.
