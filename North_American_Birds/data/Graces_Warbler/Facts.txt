Grace's Warbler (Setophaga graciae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, gray above, yellow throat and breast,
white belly, two white wing
bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages high in pine canopy. Gleans insects from
pine needles. Mountain pine
forest specialist.

Habitat
-------
Yellow pine forests in mountains of Arizona, New
Mexico, and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-throated Warbler has black-and-white face, different
habitat. Virginia's Warbler
lacks yellow throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray above, yellow throat and breast, white belly.
Ponderosa pine forest in
Arizona mountains.
