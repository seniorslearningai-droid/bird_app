Grasshopper Sparrow (Ammodramus savannarum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flat-headed sparrow with buffy orange wash on face and breast, streaked back, and short tail.

Behavior
--------
Sings a high insect-like buzz. Walks or runs through grass rather than flushing.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, meadows, and hay fields across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Henslow's Sparrow has larger head and olive nape; Grasshopper has orange face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Flat-headed appearance, orange-buff face, insect-like buzzy song in grasslands.
