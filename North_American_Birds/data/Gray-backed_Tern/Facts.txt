Gray-backed Tern (Onychoprion lunatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern. Gray back, black cap with white forehead,
white underparts, forked tail. Similar to Bridled
Tern. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Picks fish from ocean surface. Breeds on Hawaiian
and Pacific island atolls. Forages over warm
tropical waters.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific islands and atolls. Breeds in Hawaii
(Midway Atoll) and other central Pacific
islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bridled Tern has darker back and different head
pattern. Gray-backed paler gray.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale gray back with black cap and white forehead.
White collar separating cap from back.
Tropical Pacific species.
