Gray-breasted Martin (Progne chalybea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large swallow, glossy blue above, gray throat and
breast, white belly. No purple
gloss of Purple Martin. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Colonial nester in gourds and buildings in Latin
America. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, towns, and forest edges from Mexico
to Argentina. Rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Martin female more streaked below. Female
distinction complex. Check
breast pattern carefully.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray throat and breast contrasting with white belly.
Blue gloss above. Larger than
Bank Swallow. Rare Texas vagrant.
