Gray-cheeked Thrush (Catharus minimus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Olive-brown thrush with spotted breast, gray cheeks, and faint eye ring. Very plain-faced.

Behavior
--------
Forages quietly on the ground for insects and berries during migration.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in boreal forests; migrates through eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Swainson's Thrush has buffy eye ring and cheek; Veery is more rusty; Hermit has rufous tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain gray-cheeked face with no distinct eye ring — subtler look than other Catharus thrushes.
