Gray-collared Becard (Pachyramphus major)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher-like bird, male gray with black cap
and gray collar, female brown with
pale underparts. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gleans insects from forest canopy. Very rare vagrant
to southern Arizona from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forest of Mexico and Central America. Very
rare vagrant to mountain canyons
of southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rose-throated Becard has rose-pink throat on male.
Gray-collared has gray collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: gray with black cap, gray collar. No pink on
throat. Very rare Arizona vagrant
from Mexican mountains.
