Gray-crowned Rosy-Finch (Leucosticte tephrocotis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Dark brown finch with gray crown, pink-washed wings and belly, and black forehead.

Behavior
--------
Forages on snowfields for wind-blown seeds and insects. Forms large winter flocks.

Habitat
-------
High mountain summits and alpine tundra; descends to lower elevations in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Rosy-Finch has black body; Brown-capped has brown crown without gray.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray crown patch, pink-washed wings, dark brown body at high elevations.
