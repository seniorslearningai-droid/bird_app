Gray-crowned Yellowthroat (Geothlypis poliocephala)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler. Male has gray crown and face, broken
black loral mask, bright yellow
underparts. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in tall dense grass. Rare vagrant to extreme
southern Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense tall grass, weedy fields, and rank vegetation
of Mexico. Rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Yellowthroat has solid black mask. Yellow-breasted
Chat larger. Gray crown
with broken mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray crown with broken black loral area. Bright yellow
below. Rare Texas vagrant
from Mexico.
