Gray-faced Petrel (Pterodroma macroptera)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark gadfly petrel with gray face and forehead.
Dark brown overall with pale
gray face patch. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on New Zealand islands; forages over Southern
and South Pacific Oceans. Very
rare vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Southern Pacific Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other large dark Pterodroma petrels similar. Gray face
patch distinguishes this species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray face patch on otherwise dark brown petrel. Large
gadfly petrel from Southern
Pacific Ocean.
