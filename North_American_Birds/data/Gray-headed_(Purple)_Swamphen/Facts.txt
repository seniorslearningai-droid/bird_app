Gray-headed (Purple) Swamphen (Porphyrio poliocephalus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large rail-like bird. Brilliant iridescent purple-
blue body, red bill and frontal shield, white
undertail coverts. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks through marsh vegetation. Highly visible and
bold. Established feral population in southern
Florida from escaped cage birds.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes with dense vegetation in Florida.
Native to southern Asia. Feral breeding
population established in South Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Gallinule similar but smaller with yellow-green
legs. Swamphen much larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large size, brilliant purple-blue plumage, and
red bill unmistakable. White undertail
conspicuous when walking.
