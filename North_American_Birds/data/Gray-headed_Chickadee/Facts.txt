Gray-headed Chickadee (Poecile cinctus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium chickadee with gray-brown cap (not black),
dirty white cheeks, pale brownish
sides. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Inhabits dense boreal willow and alder thickets.
One of least-known birds in
North America. Rarely observed.

Habitat
-------
Dense willow and alder scrub along rivers in
Arctic Alaska and Yukon.
Very difficult to access habitat.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Boreal Chickadee has chestnut sides and brown cap.
Black-capped Chickadee has blacker
cap and cleaner white cheeks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dull brown-gray cap, dirty white cheeks, pale
sides. Far northern willow scrub.
Very rare and seldom observed.
