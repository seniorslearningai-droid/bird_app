Gray-hooded Gull (Chroicocephalus cirrocephalus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull. Breeding adult has pale gray hood,
deep red bill and legs, gray back, white
underparts. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Found on coasts and inland waters. Rare vagrant
to eastern North America from South America
and Africa.

Habitat
-------
Coastal and inland waters of South America and Africa.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Laughing Gull similar size. Gray-hooded has pale
gray (not black) hood.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale gray hood (not dark) with red bill and legs.
Rare vagrant from South America
or Africa.
