Gray-streaked Flycatcher (Muscicapa griseisticta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, gray-brown above, white below with
distinct gray-brown streaking on
breast. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from perch to catch flying insects. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska from
East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges and woodland in East Asia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Asian Brown Flycatcher lacks streaking below. Dark-sided
Flycatcher has dark sides
without streaking.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinct streaking on white underparts. Gray-brown
above. Rare Alaska vagrant from
East Asia.
