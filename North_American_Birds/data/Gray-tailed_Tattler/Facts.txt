Gray-tailed Tattler (Tringa brevipes)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper. Uniform gray above, white below
with gray breast. Barred underparts in breeding.
Yellow legs. Short nasal groove. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on rocky shorelines bobbing tail. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast from Asian breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberian and Alaskan mountain streams.
Winters on Pacific island coasts. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wandering Tattler nearly identical. Gray-tailed has
shorter nasal groove and different call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Wandering Tattler. Shorter nasal
groove (tricky field mark). Call
different from Wandering Tattler.
