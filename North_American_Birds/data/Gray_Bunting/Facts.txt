Gray Bunting (Emberiza variabilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male slaty gray throughout. Female
streaked brown with rusty rump.
About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense undergrowth. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska and Aleutian Islands
from Japan.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest undergrowth of Japan. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other all-gray bunting in range. Dark-eyed Junco
has white outer tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male all slaty gray. Female streaked brown with rusty
rump. Rare Alaska vagrant
from Japan.
