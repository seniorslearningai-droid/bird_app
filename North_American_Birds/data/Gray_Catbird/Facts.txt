Gray Catbird (Dumetella carolinensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slate-gray bird with black cap and rusty undertail. Slender build with long tail.

Behavior
--------
Hides in dense thickets and mimics other birds. Named for mewing cat-like call.

Habitat
-------
Dense shrubs and thickets at forest edges across eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Mockingbird is larger with white wing patches; Catbird is plain gray with rusty vent.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Solid gray with black cap and rusty undertail coverts; mewing call.
