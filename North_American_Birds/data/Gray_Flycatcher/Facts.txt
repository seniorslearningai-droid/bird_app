Gray Flycatcher (Empidonax wrightii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain gray Empidonax with long bill, white eye ring, and distinctive downward tail dip.

Behavior
--------
Unique among Empidonax for slowly dipping tail downward rather than flicking it up.

Habitat
-------
Sagebrush and pinyon-juniper desert scrub in the Great Basin.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dusky Flycatcher very similar but shorter bill; Gray Flycatcher dips tail down distinctively.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slow downward tail dip — the key behavior distinguishing it from other Empidonax.
