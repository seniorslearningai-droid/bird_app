Gray Francolin (Ortygornis pondicerianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized partridge. Brown and gray streaked
plumage, buff face with chestnut-streaked throat
outlined in black. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Runs rather than flies when alarmed. Noisy with
loud cackling calls. Forages for seeds, berries,
and insects on ground. Introduced to Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Dry grasslands, scrubland, and agricultural areas.
Established on Hawaiian Islands, particularly
on Lanai and Maui, after introduction.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Francolin in same Hawaiian range. Gray
Francolin paler overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Buff-orange face with black-bordered throat. Streaked
gray-brown plumage. Loud rattling call
distinctive in Hawaiian dry grasslands.
