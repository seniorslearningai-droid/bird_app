Gray Gull (Leucophaeus modestus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull. Uniform gray plumage with white head
in breeding, dark tips on wings, yellow bill.
About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in Atacama Desert of South America; commutes
to Pacific coast to feed on fish. Rare vagrant
to California.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Atacama Desert, Chile/Peru. Forages along
Pacific coast of South America. Rare vagrant
to California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Heermann's Gull similar gray coloring. Gray Gull
paler with white head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Uniform pale gray with white head. Breeding in
Atacama Desert unique among gulls. Rare
vagrant to California.
