Gray Hawk (Buteo plagiatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small buteo, plain gray above and finely barred gray
and white below. Two white tail bands.
About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts lizards in riparian forest. Regular summer resident
in southeastern Arizona and
southwestern New Mexico canyons.

Habitat
-------
Riparian cottonwood-willow forest in southeastern Arizona
and southwestern New Mexico.
Winters in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-winged Hawk has brown streaking below. Gray Hawk
all-gray with fine barring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Fine gray barring below, plain gray above. Two white
tail bands. Riparian specialist
in southeastern Arizona.
