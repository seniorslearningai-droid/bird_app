Gray Heron (Ardea cinerea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gray heron, white head with black stripe and
crest, yellow bill, gray body.
About 38 inches.

Behavior
--------
Stands motionless waiting for fish. Common across
Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast and Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, rivers, and coasts of Europe and Asia.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast and
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Blue Heron very similar. Gray Heron has white
neck without rusty tones.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White neck without rusty color; black-and-white head.
Old World counterpart to Great Blue
Heron. Rare vagrant.
