Gray Kingbird (Tyrannus dominicensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gray-and-white flycatcher with large heavy bill and notched gray tail. No yellow in plumage.

Behavior
--------
Perches conspicuously and sallies after large insects; also eats lizards and small fruit.

Habitat
-------
Coastal Florida, Caribbean islands, and mangroves; rare vagrant northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Kingbird has black-and-white pattern with white tail tip; Gray Kingbird is all gray.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-gray and white with large bill, notched tail, and coastal Florida habitat.
