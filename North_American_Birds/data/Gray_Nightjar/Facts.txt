Gray Nightjar (Caprimulgus indicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium nightjar. Gray-brown cryptic plumage with
intricate patterning. White spots in wings and
tail. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal insect catcher. Roosts on forest floor
or branches during day. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska from East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in South and East Asian forests. Winters
in Southeast Asia. Very rare vagrant to
western Aleutian Islands and Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other nightjars similar. Gray Nightjar has distinctive
call and Asian origin distinguishes it.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size among nightjars with distinctive gray-brown
plumage. Very rare vagrant in western Alaska.
Wing and tail spots visible in flight.
