Gray Partridge (Perdix perdix)
===========================

Description
-----------
Rotund ground bird with orange-buff face, gray breast, and chestnut bars on flanks. Short rufous tail.

Behavior
--------
Coveys walk through agricultural fields foraging for seeds and grain.

Habitat
-------
Open farmland, grasslands, and grain fields of the northern United States and Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chukar has bold black-and-white face stripes; Gray Partridge has plain orange face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange face, gray breast with horseshoe mark on belly, chestnut flank bars in farmland.
