Gray Silky-flycatcher (Ptiliogonys cinereus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sleek bird with long tail. Male gray with
yellow undertail coverts and crest.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches prominently in fruiting trees like mistletoe.
Very rare vagrant to southwestern
United States from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forests and high-elevation scrub in Mexico.
Very rare vagrant to California,
Arizona, and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Phainopepla similar silky-flycatcher but all black
or dark gray with red eye
and white wing patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray plumage with yellow undertail coverts, long
tail, upright crest. Rare vagrant
from Mexico. Phainopepla relative.
