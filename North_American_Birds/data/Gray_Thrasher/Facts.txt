Gray Thrasher (Toxostoma cinereum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrasher, gray above, white below with bold
dark spots, long decurved bill,
yellow eye. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in desert scrub. Rare vagrant to
California from Baja California
peninsula.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub of Baja California peninsula. Very rare
vagrant to southern California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Curve-billed Thrasher more brownish. Le Conte's Thrasher
plain without spots.
Bold spots with gray above.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray above with bold dark spots below. Long decurved
bill, yellow eye. Very
rare California vagrant from Baja.
