Gray Vireo (Vireo vicinior)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain gray vireo with single thin white wing bar, faint eye ring, and long tail frequently pumped.

Behavior
--------
Actively pumps tail while foraging in dense shrubs. Song repeated frequently.

Habitat
-------
Arid scrub, pinyon-juniper, and chaparral in the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Plumbeous Vireo has two bold wing bars and white spectacles; Gray Vireo is plainer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain gray with single wing bar, tail pumping, and arid southwestern habitat.
