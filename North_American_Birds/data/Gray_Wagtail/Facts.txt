Gray Wagtail (Motacilla cinerea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender wagtail, gray above, yellow rump and undertail,
white supercilium. Very long
tail. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Wagtail behavior with especially long tail constantly
wagging. Frequents rocky streams.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Fast-flowing rocky streams in Europe and Asia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Yellow Wagtail yellow throughout below. White
Wagtail black-and-white pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray back with yellow rump and undertail. Extremely
long tail. Rocky stream
association. Rare Alaska vagrant.
