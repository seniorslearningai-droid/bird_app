Graylag Goose (Anser anser)
===========================

Description
-----------
A large, bulky grey-brown goose with a heavy orange-pink bill and pale grey head and neck. Adults weigh 2.5-4 kg.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grass, roots, and grains. Highly social, flying in V-formations. Forms strong pair bonds and is very vocal.

Habitat
-------
Breeds near lakes, marshes, and rivers across Europe and Asia. Winters in open farmland and estuaries.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Often confused with other grey geese like Bean Goose and Pink-footed Goose, which share similar plumage patterns.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for the distinctive large orange-pink bill, pale grey forewing in flight, and pink legs to distinguish from similar species.
