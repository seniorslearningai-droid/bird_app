Great-tailed Grackle (Quiscalus mexicanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large grackle. Male iridescent purple-black with huge
keel-shaped tail, yellow eye.
Female brown. About 18 inches (male).

Behavior
--------
Extremely common and expanding range. Roosts in huge
numbers in urban areas. Very
loud, varied vocalizations.

Habitat
-------
Agricultural areas, urban and suburban areas, marshes
across the southwest and increasingly
spreading eastward and northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Boat-tailed Grackle very similar. Great-tailed has
yellow eye (in both sexes).

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow eye distinctive. Huge keel-shaped tail. Expanding
inland urban and agricultural
range. Very noisy.
