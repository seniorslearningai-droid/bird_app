Great Auk (Pinguinus impennis)
===========================

Description
-----------
EXTINCT large flightless alcid. Black above, white
below, large grooved bill, white facial patch
in breeding. About 30 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swam expertly; dove for fish. Flightless. Hunted
to extinction by 1844. Last pair killed June 3,
1844 in Iceland.

Habitat
-------
Rocky North Atlantic islands. Bred on Funk Island,
Newfoundland, and other islands. Extinct since
1844.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Razorbill is closest living relative but much smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only known from museum specimens. Extinct since
June 3, 1844. Large flightless
alcid of North Atlantic.
