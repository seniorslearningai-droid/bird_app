Great Black-backed Gull (Larus marinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Enormous gull with jet-black back and wings, white body, pink legs, and yellow bill with red spot.

Behavior
--------
Dominant scavenger that pirates food from other birds and eats almost anything.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic coast of North America; also Great Lakes in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Black-backed Gull has yellow legs and smaller size; Great Black-backed is massive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest gull in North America — jet-black back, massive size, and pink legs.
