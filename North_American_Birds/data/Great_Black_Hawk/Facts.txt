Great Black Hawk (Buteogallus urubitinga)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-black hawk with one broad white tail band.
Yellow cere and legs.
About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts reptiles and amphibians near water. Rare vagrant
to southern Texas from Mexico and
Central America.

Habitat
-------
Riparian forest near water of Mexico and Central
America. Rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Black Hawk similar but has two tail bands.
Great Black Hawk has one wide tail band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large all-black hawk with single broad white tail band.
Yellow cere. Larger than Common
Black Hawk.
