Great Blue Heron (Ardea herodias)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large blue-gray heron with white face, black plumes above eye, and rusty thighs.

Behavior
--------
Stands motionless in water waiting to spear fish and frogs with long bill.

Habitat
-------
Almost any wetland — marshes, ponds, rivers, and coasts across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Little Blue Heron is much smaller; Sandhill Crane flies with neck extended not folded.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest heron — flies with neck folded in S-shape, blue-gray body, and massive wingspan.
