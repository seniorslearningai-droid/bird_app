Great Cormorant (Phalacrocorax carbo)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large cormorant, dark black-green plumage, white cheek
patch, orange-yellow throat pouch.
About 36 inches.

Behavior
--------
Large diving cormorant. Breeds in northeastern North
America; winters on rocky Atlantic
coasts and inland.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic coast rocky shores, harbors, and coastal
freshwater. Winters south to mid-Atlantic
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Double-crested Cormorant has orange throat without
white cheek patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size with white cheek patch and orange-yellow
throat. Larger than Double-crested.
Atlantic coast cormorant.
