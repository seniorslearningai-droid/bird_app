Great Crested Flycatcher (Myiarchus crinitus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large flycatcher with bright yellow belly, rufous tail, gray breast, and brown crest.

Behavior
--------
Nests in tree cavities; often incorporates shed snake skin into nest.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests and forest edges across eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ash-throated Flycatcher is paler with less yellow; Brown-crested is larger but western.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow belly, rufous tail, gray throat, crest, and loud wheep call.
