Great Crested Tern (Thalasseus bergii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large tern. White with gray back, black crest,
yellow bill. Large size and shaggy crest.
About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish. Breeds on tropical Pacific islands.
Rare vagrant to Hawaii and occasionally
Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Indian and Pacific ocean islands and coasts.
Rare in Hawaii; extremely rare on
mainland Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Elegant Tern smaller. Royal Tern has orange bill.
Great Crested has yellow bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size with yellow bill and black shaggy crest.
Gray back. Rare in Hawaii
and Pacific coast.
