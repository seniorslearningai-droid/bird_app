Great Egret (Ardea alba)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-white heron with yellow-orange bill, black legs and feet, and long white plumes in breeding.

Behavior
--------
Wades slowly in water to spear fish. Nests colonially in trees with other herons.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, marshes, ponds, and coastal areas across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snowy Egret has black bill and yellow feet; Great Egret has yellow bill and black feet.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large all-white with yellow-orange bill, black legs — no yellow feet like Snowy Egret.
