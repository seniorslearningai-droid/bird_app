Great Frigatebird (Fregata minor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large black frigatebird, male with red throat pouch,
female with white throat and breast.
Long forked tail. About 37 inches.

Behavior
--------
Kleptoparasite like other frigatebirds. Breeds on
Hawaiian Northwestern Islands; forages
widely in Pacific.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific and Indian Oceans. Breeds on Hawaiian
Northwestern Islands; forages in
Hawaiian waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Magnificent Frigatebird nearly identical. Great Frigatebird
female has red eye ring and white throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Female has red eye ring, white throat and breast.
Male indistinguishable in field from
Magnificent Frigatebird without range context.
