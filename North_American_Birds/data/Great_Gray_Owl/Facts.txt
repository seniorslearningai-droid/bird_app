Great Gray Owl (Strix nebulosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Huge owl with enormous round facial disc, yellow eyes, and no ear tufts. Gray-brown mottled plumage.

Behavior
--------
Hunts voles by sound through deep snow, plunging feet-first to catch prey.

Habitat
-------
Dense boreal and mountain coniferous forests of the northern and western regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Horned Owl has prominent ear tufts; Barred Owl is smaller with dark eyes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest owl by length — enormous facial disc, yellow eyes, and no ear tufts.
