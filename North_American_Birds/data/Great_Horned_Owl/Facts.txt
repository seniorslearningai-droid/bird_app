Great Horned Owl (Bubo virginianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large owl with prominent ear tufts, bright yellow eyes, white throat bib, and barred underparts.

Behavior
--------
Apex predator that hunts everything from mice to skunks to other raptors.

Habitat
-------
Almost every habitat across North America — forests, deserts, suburbs, and cities.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long-eared Owl has longer ear tufts closer together; Great Horned has wide-set tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Prominent wide-set ear tufts, yellow eyes, white throat bib, and deep hooting call.
