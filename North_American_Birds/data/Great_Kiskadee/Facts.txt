Great Kiskadee (Pitangus sulphuratus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large flycatcher with bold black-and-white striped head, yellow underparts, and rufous wings.

Behavior
--------
Dives into water for fish like a kingfisher. Eats insects, fruit, lizards, and fish.

Habitat
-------
Woodland edges, stream corridors, and parks in southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Boat-billed Flycatcher similar but more restricted to tropics; range barely overlaps.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Striped head, bright yellow belly, rufous wings — loud kis-ka-dee call in southern Texas.
