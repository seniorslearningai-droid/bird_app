Great Knot (Calidris tenuirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sandpiper. Breeding adult has chestnut-streaked
back, heavily spotted breast. Non-breeding gray
above and white below. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats for mollusks. Rare vagrant to
Pacific coast from Asian wintering areas. Migrates
in large flocks in Asia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberia. Winters on Australian and East
Asian coasts. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red Knot similar but smaller. Great Knot larger
with spotted breast in breeding.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger than Red Knot with spotted breast in
breeding plumage. Non-breeding gray back
with streaked breast.
