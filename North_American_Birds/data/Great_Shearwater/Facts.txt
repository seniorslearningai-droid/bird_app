Great Shearwater (Ardenna gravis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shearwater, brown back, black cap, white collar,
white below with dark belly patch,
pink bill with black tip. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Tristan da Cunha and Falkland Islands.
Very common in North Atlantic in
summer on migration.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean in summer migration. Very common
offshore Atlantic coast from spring
through fall.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cory's Shearwater lacks dark cap. Sooty Shearwater
darker without black cap and collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black cap with white collar, dark belly patch, pink
bill. Common Atlantic shearwater.
Summer migrant from South Atlantic.
