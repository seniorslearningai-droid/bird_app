Great Skua (Stercorarius skua)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large powerful seabird. All dark brown with white
wing flash, broad wings, short tail. Heavily
built. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aggressively pirates food from other seabirds. Breeds
in North Atlantic. Seen offshore during
migration on Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on North Atlantic islands (Iceland, Scotland,
Norway). Pelagic outside breeding. Seen offshore
on Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
South Polar Skua similar. Great Skua browner and
heavier built.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large dark seabird with white wing flash.
Heavy-bodied compared to jaegers.
Aggressive food pirating behavior.
