Great Spotted Woodpecker (Dendrocopos major)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium woodpecker, black and white with large white
shoulder patches, red nape on male,
red undertail. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common woodpecker across Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant
to Alaska. Drums loudly on
trees.

Habitat
-------
Woodland and forest of Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hairy and Downy Woodpeckers similar but lack large
shoulder patches and red undertail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large white shoulder patches, red undertail coverts.
Common European species, rare
Alaska vagrant.
