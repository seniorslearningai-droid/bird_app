Great Tit (Parus major)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tit, black head with white cheeks, green back,
yellow underparts with black
stripe. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and intelligent, solves complex problems. Regular
feeder visitor. Very rare
vagrant to Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Woodland, gardens, and parks across Europe and Asia.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Chickadee has different pattern. Tufted
Titmouse lacks yellow underparts.
No similar bird in Alaska.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head, white cheeks, yellow underparts with black
stripe. Bold and active.
Very rare Alaska vagrant.
