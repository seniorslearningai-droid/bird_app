Great White Pelican (Pelecanus onocrotalus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large pelican, white with black flight feathers,
pink bill pouch, yellow facial
skin. About 63 inches.

Behavior
--------
Cooperative feeder, herds fish in groups. Soars on
thermals. Very rare vagrant to
North America, usually escaped birds.

Habitat
-------
Large lakes and wetlands of Europe, Asia, and Africa.
Extremely rare vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American White Pelican very similar. Yellow pouch
vs. orange in American White.
Essentially identical in field.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous white pelican with pink bill and yellow
facial skin. Very rare; likely
escaped from captivity.
