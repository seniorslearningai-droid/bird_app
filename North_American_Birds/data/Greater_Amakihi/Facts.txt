Greater Amakihi (Viridonia sagittirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green throughout,
larger size than other amakihi,
straight bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on insects in native forest. Extinct since
approximately 1901 from the Big
Island of Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Big Island. Extinct due to deforestation
and introduced avian diseases.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other amakihi smaller. Hawaii Creeper similar habits.
No similar large olive
honeycreeper survives.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Large olive-green honeycreeper with straight
bill. Big Island. Last recorded
approximately 1901.
