Greater Koa-Finch (Rhodacanthis palmeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Hawaiian honeycreeper, male with orange head
and breast, olive-green back,
massive koa-seed bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on hard koa pods. Extinct since approximately
1896 on Big Island.

Habitat
-------
Native koa forests of the Big Island of Hawaii.
Extinct due to deforestation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Koa-Finch smaller. Palila has yellow head.
No similar birds
survive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species. Largest koa-finch. Orange head, massive
bill. No longer
exists in Hawaiian koa forest.
