Greater Necklaced Laughingthrush (Garrulax pectoralis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large thrush-like bird, brown above, white below
with bold black necklace across
breast. Black facial markings. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in noisy flocks through dense undergrowth.
Introduced to Hawaii. Loud, raucous
calls.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest undergrowth. Introduced to Oahu, Hawaii.
Native to forests of India
and Southeast Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Necklaced Laughingthrush similar but smaller.
No other large thrush-like bird
in Hawaiian forest undergrowth.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black necklace across white breast. Brown
above. Large size. Noisy flocks
in Oahu forest.
