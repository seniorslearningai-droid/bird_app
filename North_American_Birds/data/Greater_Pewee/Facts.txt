Greater Pewee (Contopus pertinax)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large pewee, olive-gray above, pale below with darker
sides. Prominent crest. Orange-yellow
lower mandible. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from high exposed perch; loud whistle song.
Summer resident in mountain pine
forest of southeastern Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Pine and pine-oak forest in mountain ranges of southeastern
Arizona and southwestern New Mexico.
Summer resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern and Western Wood-Pewees smaller without crest.
Greater Pewee's size and crest distinct.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large pewee with prominent crest, orange lower mandible.
Loud "Jose Maria" whistle. Southeastern
Arizona mountain pine forest.
