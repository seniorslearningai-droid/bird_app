Greater Prairie-Chicken (Tympanuchus cupido)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky grouse with heavily barred brown plumage, orange eye combs, and pinnate feathers on neck.

Behavior
--------
Males gather on leks to perform elaborate booming displays with inflated orange neck sacs.

Habitat
-------
Tallgrass prairie remnants in the central United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Prairie-Chicken is smaller and paler; Sharp-tailed Grouse has pointed tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Booming lek displays, orange neck sacs, and pinnate neck feathers in tallgrass prairie.
