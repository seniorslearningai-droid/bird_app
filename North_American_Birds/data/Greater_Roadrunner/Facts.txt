Greater Roadrunner (Geococcyx californianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large ground-dwelling cuckoo with long tail, streaked brown plumage, and blue-red eye patch.

Behavior
--------
Runs rapidly on the ground to catch lizards, snakes, and large insects.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub, chaparral, and dry open areas of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Roadrunner is smaller and restricted to Mexico; Greater Roadrunner is larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long tail, runs on ground, blue-red eye patch, and streaked brown plumage.
