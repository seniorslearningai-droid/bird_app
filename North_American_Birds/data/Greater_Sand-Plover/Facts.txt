Greater Sand-Plover (Anarhynchus leschenaultii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium plover with heavy bill. Breeding adult has
rufous breast band, gray-brown back. Similar to
Siberian Sand-Plover but larger. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats and sandy beaches. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast from Asian breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in central Asian steppes and deserts. Winters
on Asian and African coasts. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Siberian Sand-Plover smaller with thinner bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger size and heavier bill than Siberian Sand-
Plover. Rufous breast band in breeding.
Rare vagrant on Pacific coast.
