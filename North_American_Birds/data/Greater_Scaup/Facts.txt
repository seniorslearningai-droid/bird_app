Greater Scaup (Aythya marila)
===========================

Description
-----------
Diving duck with rounded head, blue-gray bill, and black-and-white body on males. Greenish gloss on head.

Behavior
--------
Dives deeply for mollusks, crustaceans, and aquatic plants in open water.

Habitat
-------
Coastal bays and large lakes; breeds in Arctic and boreal regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Scaup has peaked head and purple gloss; Greater has rounder head and green gloss.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rounded head with green gloss, and white wing stripe extends further into primaries.
