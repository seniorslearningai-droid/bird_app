Greater White-fronted Goose (Anser albifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium goose with orange bill, white forehead patch, pinkish-orange legs, and black belly bars.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grasses and grains in flocks. Loud laughing call given in flight.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Arctic tundra; winters in central and western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canada Goose is larger with black neck; White-fronted has orange bill and black belly blotches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White forehead patch, orange bill, and black bars on belly — laughing call in flight.
