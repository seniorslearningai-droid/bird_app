Greater Yellowlegs (Tringa melanoleuca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shorebird with bright yellow legs, long gray-and-white barred bill, and white-spotted upperparts.

Behavior
--------
Wades actively in water chasing fish and invertebrates. Loud three-note call.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, mudflats, and marshes across North America during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lesser Yellowlegs is smaller with shorter bill and softer two-note call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, yellow legs, long upturned bill, and loud three-note tu-tu-tu call.
