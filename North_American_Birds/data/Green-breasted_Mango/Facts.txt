Green-breasted Mango (Anthracothorax prevostii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large hummingbird. Male dark metallic green with
black throat and purple-bordered tail. Female green
above, white below with dark central stripe. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar from large flowers. Flies with
direct, fast flight. Rare vagrant to Texas and
southern US from Mexico and Central America.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest edges, gardens, and plantations in
Mexico and Central America. Rare visitor to
Texas and occasionally other southern states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-throated Mango in Caribbean. Female similar
to other large female hummingbirds.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's entirely green body with black throat and
dark purple-edged tail. Female's white underparts
with bold dark central stripe.
