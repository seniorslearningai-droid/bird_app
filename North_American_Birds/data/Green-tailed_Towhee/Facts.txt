Green-tailed Towhee (Pipilo chlorurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Ground-dwelling sparrow with olive-green tail and wings, gray body, white throat, and rufous cap.

Behavior
--------
Scratches vigorously in leaf litter under dense shrubs. Rarely seen in open.

Habitat
-------
Mountain shrublands and sagebrush of the western United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Towhee has rufous sides and spotted wings; Green-tailed is plain gray with green tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous cap, green tail, white throat patch in mountain shrubland habitat.
