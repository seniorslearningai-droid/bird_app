Green-winged Teal (Anas crecca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest dabbling duck. Male has chestnut head with green ear patch; both sexes have green wing speculum.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on seeds and invertebrates in shallow water. Forms large migrating flocks.

Habitat
-------
Shallow marshes, ponds, and wetlands across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-winged Teal is slightly larger with blue forewing patch; Green-winged has green speculum.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest dabbling duck — male's chestnut head with green stripe is distinctive.
