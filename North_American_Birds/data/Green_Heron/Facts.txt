Green Heron (Butorides virescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small stocky heron with dark green back, chestnut neck, orange-yellow legs, and dark cap.

Behavior
--------
Perches and waits motionlessly, sometimes using bait to lure fish to the surface.

Habitat
-------
Wooded wetlands, ponds, and stream edges across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-crowned Night-Heron is heavier with gray wings; Green Heron is slender with dark back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size, dark greenish back, chestnut neck, and habit of using lures to catch fish.
