Green Jay (Cyanocorax yncas)
===========================

Description
-----------
Colorful jay with green body, blue head, yellow
outer tail feathers, black bib.
Vivid pattern. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Moves through thorny scrub in family groups.
Uses tools to extract insects.
Raucous with varied vocalizations.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub, woodland, and forest edge in
southern Texas and from Mexico
to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Unmistakable within its range. No other jay
combines green body with blue
head and yellow tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Green and blue combination unmistakable. Yellow
outer tail feathers. Black bib.
Rio Grande Valley, Texas resident.
