Green Kingfisher (Chloroceryle americana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small kingfisher, dark green above with white spots,
male has rufous breast band, female
has green breast bands. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches low over streams, plunges for small fish.
Rare resident along clear streams in
southern Texas and Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Clear rocky streams in southern Texas and southeastern
Arizona. Uncommon resident of
Rio Grande region.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Belted Kingfisher larger with gray-blue plumage. Green
Kingfisher tiny and dark green.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny kingfisher, dark green above. Male rufous breast
band. Perches low over clear
streams in southern Texas.
