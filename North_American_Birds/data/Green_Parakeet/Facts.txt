Green Parakeet (Psittacara holochlorus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-green parakeet, long pointed tail, orange
bill. Some scattered red feathers on
head. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral population in the Rio Grande Valley
of Texas. Loud flocks in
urban areas.

Habitat
-------
Established feral flocks in southernmost Texas, particularly
McAllen and Brownsville area of
Rio Grande Valley.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other green parakeets in Texas. Green Parakeet largest
of the feral Texas parakeets.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large all-green parakeet. Orange bill. Feral in Rio
Grande Valley, Texas. Scattered
red feathers on head.
