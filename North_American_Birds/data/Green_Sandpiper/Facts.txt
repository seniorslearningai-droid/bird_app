Green Sandpiper (Tringa ochropus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper. Very dark greenish-brown back,
white underparts with streaked breast, white rump,
black and white barred tail. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bobs rear end up and down. Flies with erratic
twisting flight. Rare vagrant to North America
from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in European and Asian boreal forests near
water. Winters in African wetlands. Rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Solitary Sandpiper nearly identical. Green has darker
back and less distinct eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very dark back contrasting with white rump in
flight. Black and white barred tail.
Nearly identical to Solitary Sandpiper.
