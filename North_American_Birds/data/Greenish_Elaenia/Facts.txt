Greenish Elaenia (Myiopagis viridicata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, olive-green above with whitish eye
ring, pale yellow underparts, white
wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gleans insects in forest. Very rare vagrant from
Mexico to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland from Mexico to South America.
Very rare vagrant to southern
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Empidonax flycatchers similar. Greenish Elaenia more
green-olive without eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-green flycatcher with pale yellow underparts.
White wing bars. Very rare
Texas vagrant from Mexico.
