Groove-billed Ani (Crotophaga sulcirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
All-black bird with grooved heavy bill, long floppy tail, and glossy blue-black plumage.

Behavior
--------
Lives in groups that share nesting duties. Walks on the ground eating insects stirred up by cattle.

Habitat
-------
Open country, forest edges, and brush in southern Texas and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Smooth-billed Ani has smooth bill without grooves; range barely overlaps in Florida.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black with deeply grooved bill, floppy tail flight, and social family groups.
