Guadalupe Murrelet (Synthliboramphus hypoleucus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small alcid. Black above, white below. Less white
on face than Scripps's Murrelet; dark extends
more around eye. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests only on Guadalupe Island, Mexico. Critically
endangered; population under 1,000 birds. Dives
for fish offshore.

Habitat
-------
Breeds only on Guadalupe Island, Baja California.
Forages in nearshore Pacific waters. Critically
endangered species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scripps's Murrelet nearly identical. Guadalupe has
less white on face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Less white on face than Scripps's Murrelet; dark
cap extends further down. Only breeds on
Guadalupe Island.
