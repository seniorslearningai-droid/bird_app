Gull-billed Tern (Gelochelidon nilotica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern with thick black bill, pale gray upperparts, black cap, and white underparts.

Behavior
--------
Hawks insects over marshes rather than plunge-diving for fish like other terns.

Habitat
-------
Coastal marshes, beaches, and agricultural fields; breeds colonially.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other terns have thinner red or orange bills; Gull-billed Tern has a thick all-black bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Thick all-black bill — hunts insects over land, unusual for a tern.
