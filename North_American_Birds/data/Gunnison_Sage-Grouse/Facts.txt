Gunnison Sage-Grouse (Centrocercus minimus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smaller sage-grouse found only in Gunnison Basin,
Colorado. Male has white chest tufts, long spiky
tail feathers, yellow air sacs. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Males gather at leks to perform elaborate strutting
displays. Feeds almost exclusively on sagebrush.
Critically endangered species.

Habitat
-------
Sagebrush steppe only in Gunnison Basin, Colorado,
and a few isolated Utah populations.
Critically dependent on big sagebrush.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Sage-Grouse nearly identical but noticeably
larger. Only reliably separated by range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smaller than Greater Sage-Grouse; only found in
Gunnison Basin, CO. Filoplumes on neck
more prominent during display.
