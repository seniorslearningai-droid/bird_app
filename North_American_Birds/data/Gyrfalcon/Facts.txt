Gyrfalcon (Falco rusticolus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest falcon. Variable plumage from all-white to
dark gray. Massive size, broad-based
wings. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts ptarmigan and waterfowl in Arctic. Rare winter
visitor to northern US from
Arctic breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra breeding grounds; winters south irregularly.
Open areas, airports, and
coastal regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Peregrine Falcon much smaller. Gyrfalcon's massive size
and broad wings distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous falcon, broader wings than Peregrine. Variable
white to dark morphs. Rare Arctic
visitor in winter.
