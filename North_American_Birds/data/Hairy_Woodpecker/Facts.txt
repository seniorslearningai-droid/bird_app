Hairy Woodpecker (Dryobates villosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium black-and-white woodpecker with long chisel-like bill, white back stripe, and red nape on males.

Behavior
--------
Excavates deeply into wood for bark beetles and wood-boring insects.

Habitat
-------
Forests and woodlands across North America from Alaska to Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Downy Woodpecker is smaller with short bill and black spots on outer tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger than Downy with a much longer bill relative to head size.
