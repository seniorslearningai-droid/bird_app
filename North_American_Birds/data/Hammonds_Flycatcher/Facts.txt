Hammond's Flycatcher (Empidonax hammondii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Empidonax with short bill, large head, bold eye ring, and gray-olive plumage. Long primary projection.

Behavior
--------
Flicks tail and wings frequently. Breeds in tall coniferous forests.

Habitat
-------
Mature coniferous forests of western mountains during breeding; various habitats in migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dusky Flycatcher has longer bill and shorter primary projection; habitat differs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Short bill, bold eye ring, long primary projection, and tall coniferous forest breeding habitat.
