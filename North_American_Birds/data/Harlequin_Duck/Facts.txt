Harlequin Duck (Histrionicus histrionicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Striking small duck. Male has blue-gray plumage with white crescents, spots, and chestnut flanks.

Behavior
--------
Breeds along fast-flowing mountain streams; winters on rocky surf-pounded coasts.

Habitat
-------
Rocky coastlines in winter; swift mountain streams in summer.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Surf Scoter is all black with colorful bill; Harlequin Duck has unique white markings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's unique blue-gray plumage with white geometric markings is unmistakable.
