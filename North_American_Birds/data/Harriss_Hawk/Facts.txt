Harris's Hawk (Parabuteo unicinctus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark hawk with chestnut shoulders, thighs, and wing lining. Bold white rump and tail base.

Behavior
--------
Hunts cooperatively in family groups — unique among North American raptors.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub, cactus flats, and mesquite in the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dark phase Red-tailed lacks chestnut wing patches and white rump. Harris's is unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut shoulders and thighs, white rump patch, and cooperative hunting in groups.
