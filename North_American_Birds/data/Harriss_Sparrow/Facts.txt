Harris's Sparrow (Zonotrichia querula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sparrow with black crown, face, and bib surrounding a pink bill. Brown streaked back.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in boreal forest-tundra transition; winters in central North American grasslands.

Habitat
-------
Winters in brushy grasslands and hedgerows of the Great Plains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-crowned and White-throated Sparrows lack black face and bib of Harris's Sparrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest native sparrow — black crown, face, and bib with a distinctive pink bill.
