Hawaii Akepa (Loxops coccineus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, male bright orange-red,
female olive-green, slightly crossed
bill tips. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Uses crossed bill to twist open ohia buds for
nectar and insects. Endangered;
declining on Big Island.

Habitat
-------
Native ohia forests of the Big Island above 4,000
feet.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Apapane brighter red with curved bill. No other
orange-red small bird with
crossed bill on Big Island.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male bright orange-red, crossed bill tips. Big Island
ohia forest above 4,000
feet. Endangered endemic.
