Hawaii Amakihi (Chlorodrepanis virens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green above, yellow-green
below, slightly curved bill.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager for nectar and insects. More disease-resistant
than many Hawaiian birds.
Most common surviving honeycreeper on Big Island.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Big Island from near sea
level to treeline. More tolerant
of lower elevations than most species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oahu and Kauai Amakihi island-specific. Apapane red.
Anianiau smaller and brighter
yellow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-green with slightly curved bill on Big Island.
One of most common native
birds in Hawaii forests.
