Hawaii Creeper (Loxops mana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green above, pale
below with short straight bill.
Creeps along bark. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Creeps along branches and bark searching for insects.
Critically endangered; found
in scattered forest remnants.

Habitat
-------
Native ohia and koa forests of the Big Island
above 5,000 feet.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Maui Alauahio similar behavior on Maui. Oahu Alauahio
extinct. Bark-creeping
behavior distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Creeping bark behavior on Big Island. Small olive-green
bird. Short straight bill.
Critically endangered endemic.
