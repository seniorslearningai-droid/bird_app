Hawaii Elepaio (Chasiempis sandwichensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small monarch flycatcher endemic to Big Island. Variable
plumage with white, brown, and dark
morphs. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly active and inquisitive, often follows hikers.
Cocks tail frequently. Ancient
Hawaiians considered it sacred.

Habitat
-------
Native forests from sea level to treeline on
Hawaii. Found in ohia, koa,
and mixed forest habitats.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Elepaio species on Kauai and Oahu but
island-specific. Variable plumage
makes identification complex.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Upward-cocked tail on Big Island of Hawaii.
Variable plumage. Curious and
bold behavior near humans.
