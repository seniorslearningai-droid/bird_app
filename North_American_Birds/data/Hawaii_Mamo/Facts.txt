Hawaii Mamo (Drepanis pacifica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Hawaiian honeycreeper, mostly black with yellow
rump, undertail, and thighs.
Very long curved bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on nectar of lobelias. Hunted to extinction
for its yellow feathers used in
royal Hawaiian cloaks. Extinct ~1898.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Big Island of Hawaii. Extinct
due to feather trade and
habitat destruction.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Mamo all black. Iiwi red. No other
black-and-yellow nectar feeder survives
in Hawaii.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Black with yellow rump and thighs. Very
long curved bill. Big Island.
No longer exists.
