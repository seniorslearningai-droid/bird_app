Hawaii Oo (Moho nobilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Hawaiian honeyeater, glossy black with yellow
thigh and wing patches. Long
curved bill. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for nectar. Prized yellow feathers used
in Hawaiian royal cloaks. Driven
extinct by feather trade and habitat loss.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Big Island of Hawaii.
Extinct since approximately 1934.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Oo species on different islands. All Hawaiian
Oo now extinct.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species from Big Island. Largest Oo species.
Black with yellow patches.
No longer exists.
