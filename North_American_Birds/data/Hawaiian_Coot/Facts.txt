Hawaiian Coot (Fulica alai)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium waterbird. Sooty black body with white bill
and large white frontal shield. Some individuals
have red frontal shield knob. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims and dives for aquatic plants. Found year-round
on Hawaiian ponds and wetlands. Endemic to
Hawaiian Islands.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater ponds, wetlands, and coastal lagoons
throughout Hawaiian Islands. Endemic; found nowhere
else in the world.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Coot nearly identical but not found in
Hawaii. Hawaiian Coot endemic to Hawaiian Islands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black body with white bill and frontal shield.
Found only in Hawaiian Islands. Some
individuals show red knob on shield.
