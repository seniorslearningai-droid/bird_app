Hawaiian Crow (Corvus hawaiiensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium crow endemic to Hawaii. All black with
brownish tinge to body feathers.
Stout bill. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extinct in wild since 2002; captive breeding program
ongoing. Omnivorous forager in
native forest. Uses tools.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Big Island of Hawaii.
All remaining individuals in
captive breeding program.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other crow in Hawaii. Uniquely Hawaiian
species now extinct in
the wild.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only crow in Hawaii. Larger than American Crow.
Brownish-tinged black plumage.
Currently only in captivity.
