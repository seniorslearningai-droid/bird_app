Hawaiian Duck (Anas wyvilliana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dabbling duck, Mallard relative. Male has
greenish head, mottled brown body, less colorful
than Mallard. Female mottled brown. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds in freshwater ponds, streams, and wetlands.
Dabbles for aquatic insects, plants, and seeds.
Endemic to Hawaiian Islands; endangered.

Habitat
-------
Found only in Hawaii. Lives in freshwater marshes,
rivers, taro fields, and coastal ponds on
Kauai and other islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mallard; hybridization is a major conservation concern.
Koloa is darker with less colorful plumage
than Mallard.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Darker and smaller than Mallard with mottled brown
plumage year-round. Orange-speckled
bill. Found only in Hawaii.
