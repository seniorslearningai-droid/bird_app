Hawaiian Goose (Branta sandvicensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
A medium-sized goose with black face and crown, buff-colored cheeks, and heavily furrowed neck. Reduced webbing between toes for rocky terrain.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on native Hawaiian vegetation, grasses, and berries. Lives in pairs or small groups. Largely terrestrial adapted to lava fields.

Habitat
-------
Found only in Hawaii on volcanic slopes, grasslands, and shrublands on Maui, Kauai, and the Big Island. Hawaii's state bird.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Distantly resembles Canada Goose due to black head, but the buff cheeks and furrowed neck are unique to the Nene.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive cream-buff cheeks contrasting with black face, deeply furrowed neck, reduced toe webbing, and restricted Hawaiian habitat are definitive.
