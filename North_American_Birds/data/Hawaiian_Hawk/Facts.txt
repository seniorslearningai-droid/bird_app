Hawaiian Hawk (Buteo solitarius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small to medium buteo, polymorphic. Dark morph all
dark brown; light morph brown above
and pale below. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Endemic Hawaiian hawk; only raptor breeding in Hawaii.
Hunts birds, lizards, and insects.
Common on Big Island.

Habitat
-------
Big Island of Hawaii from sea level to tree line.
Most common in forest and
forested agricultural areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Only hawk breeding in Hawaii. Dark morph can suggest
various buteos. Endemic species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only hawk on Big Island of Hawaii. Polymorphic but
location is definitive. Crying
call, soars over Big Island forest.
