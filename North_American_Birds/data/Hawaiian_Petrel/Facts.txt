Hawaiian Petrel (Pterodroma sandwichensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gadfly petrel, dark gray-brown back, black
cap, white forehead, white underparts.
About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian mountain slopes; forages far offshore
in Pacific. Endangered; declining
due to predators and habitat loss.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Hawaiian high mountains (Haleakala, Mauna
Loa). Forages over eastern North
Pacific.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Petrel similar. Hawaiian Petrel has narrower
white collar and different face pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark cap with white forehead, dark back and wings.
White underparts. Hawaiian mountain
breeder, offshore Pacific forager.
