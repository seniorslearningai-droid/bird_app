Hawaiian Rail (Zapornia sandwichensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
EXTINCT small flightless rail endemic to Hawaii.
Brown with gray underparts, short bill, vestigial
wings. Very small at about 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Ran through dense grass on Laysan and other Hawaiian
islands. Fed on insects and plant material.
Extinct by 1884 due to introduced predators.

Habitat
-------
Dense lowland grasslands of Hawaiian Islands. Last
recorded on Hawaii (Big Island) in 1884.
Extinct due to rats, cats, and mongoose.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small flightless rails of Pacific islands.
Unique to Hawaiian Islands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only known from museum specimens. Extinct since
1884. Small flightless brown rail
of Hawaiian lowland grasslands.
