Hawfinch (Coccothraustes coccothraustes)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large finch with enormous powerful bill, orange-brown
body, blue-black wings, white
wing patches. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Crushes cherry and olive pits with powerful bill.
Shy and difficult to observe.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous woodland of Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Evening Grosbeak has yellow and black pattern.
No similar massive-billed finch
in Alaska range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive bill, orange-brown body, white wing patches.
Large size for a finch.
Rare Alaska vagrant from Asia.
