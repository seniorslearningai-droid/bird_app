Heermann's Gull (Larus heermanni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Distinctive gull with dark gray body, red bill, and white head (in breeding). Black tail with white tip.

Behavior
--------
Kleptoparasitizes Brown Pelicans, stealing fish as they surface. Nests in Baja California.

Habitat
-------
Pacific coast from Alaska to Mexico; rarely inland.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other Pacific gull has this dark gray body with red bill and white head combination.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray body, red bill, white head in breeding — reverse of most gull color patterns.
