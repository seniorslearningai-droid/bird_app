Hen Harrier (Circus cyaneus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium harrier, white rump. Male pale gray; female
brown streaked. Same species as North
American Northern Harrier. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts small mammals and birds low over open fields.
Breeds in Europe and Asia;
Old World population of Northern Harrier.

Habitat
-------
Open moors, grasslands, and marshes of Europe and
Asia. Considered same species as
Northern Harrier by some authorities.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Harrier is the same species or very similar.
Both have distinctive white rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump, low quartering flight over open fields.
Old World counterpart to North
American Northern Harrier.
