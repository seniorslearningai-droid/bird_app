Henslow's Sparrow (Centronyx henslowii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow with large flat olive-green head, streaked
chestnut back, streaked breast.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive and hard to flush. Gives insect-like hiccup
song. Nest in wet
grass fields.

Habitat
-------
Wet dense grasslands and old fields in eastern United
States. Declining rapidly.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Baird's Sparrow has orange head. Grasshopper Sparrow
has flat head but plain belly.
Olive head distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large flat olive-green head. Streaked chestnut back.
Wet dense grass. Short insect-like
hiccup song.
