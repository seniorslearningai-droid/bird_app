Hepatic Tanager (Piranga flava)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tanager. Male brick-red with dark gray bill
and cheek. Female olive-yellow
with gray bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in pine and pine-oak canopy for insects.
Mountain pine forest specialist.
Less common than Summer Tanager.

Habitat
-------
Pine and pine-oak forests in mountains of southwestern
United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Summer Tanager male brighter red with lighter bill.
Scarlet Tanager male has black
wings. Gray bill helpful.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brick-red male with gray bill and gray cheek patch.
Mountain pine forest. Darker
than Summer Tanager.
