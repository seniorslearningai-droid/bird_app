Herald Petrel (Pterodroma heraldica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Polymorphic gadfly petrel; light, intermediate, and
dark morphs. White primary flash below.
About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Tropical Pacific species. Rare visitor to North American
Pacific coast and occasionally
Atlantic via confused records.

Habitat
-------
Tropical South Pacific Ocean. Rare visitor to North
American Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kermadec Petrel very similar. Herald has different
underwing pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Polymorphic gadfly petrel with white primary flash.
Difficult to distinguish from
Kermadec Petrel. Tropical Pacific.
