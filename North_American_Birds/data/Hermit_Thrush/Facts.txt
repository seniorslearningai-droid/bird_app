Hermit Thrush (Catharus guttatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Olive-brown thrush with rufous tail contrasting with brown back, spotted breast, and red-tinged tail.

Behavior
--------
Raises and slowly lowers tail repeatedly. Song is the most musical of any North American bird.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests; the only spotted thrush that regularly winters in the US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Swainson's Thrush has buffy eye ring; Veery is rusty with faint spots; Hermit has rufous tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Reddish tail contrasting with brown back, and habit of slowly raising and lowering tail.
