Hermit Warbler (Setophaga occidentalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Bright yellow head with gray nape, gray-streaked back, white underparts with no streaking on sides.

Behavior
--------
Forages high in coniferous trees. Hybridizes with Townsend's Warbler.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests of the Pacific Coast mountains, breeding in tall Douglas firs.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Townsend's Warbler has black cheek patch and streaked sides; Hermit Warbler has plain sides.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant yellow head with gray body and no streaking on sides — coniferous forest.
