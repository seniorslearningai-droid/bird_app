Herring Gull (Larus argentatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large familiar gull with gray mantle, white body, pink legs, and yellow bill with red spot.

Behavior
--------
Omnivorous scavenger at landfills, fishing ports, and beaches. Drops shellfish to crack them.

Habitat
-------
Coasts, large lakes, and landfills across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-billed Gull is smaller with black ring on bill; Glaucous has white wingtips.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Classic large gull with gray back, pink legs, and yellow bill with red spot.
