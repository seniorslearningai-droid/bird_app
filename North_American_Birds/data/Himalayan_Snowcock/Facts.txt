Himalayan Snowcock (Tetraogallus himalayensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large partridge. Gray-brown with chestnut streaks,
white face and throat with rust-colored stripes,
white primary feathers visible in flight. About 28 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on roots, berries, and alpine plants. Difficult
to approach; runs uphill when disturbed.
Only introduced to Nevada's Ruby Mountains.

Habitat
-------
Rocky alpine slopes above treeline. In US found
only in Ruby Mountains, Nevada, at
10,000+ feet elevation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chukar at lower elevations. Snowcock much
larger than other partridges.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large size and white wing patches visible in
flight. Alpine habitat in Nevada's
Ruby Mountains only.
