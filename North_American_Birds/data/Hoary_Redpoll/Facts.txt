Hoary Redpoll (Acanthis hornemanni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch with red forehead, pinkish breast on male, white rump, and frosty pale plumage overall.

Behavior
--------
Irruptive migrant that moves south in winter when birch and alder seed crops fail.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra; winters in open brushy areas and weedy fields.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Redpoll is darker and more streaked; Hoary is frostier and paler with smaller bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Frosty pale plumage, small bill, and unstreaked white rump distinguishing it from Common Redpoll.
