Hooded Crane (Grus monacha)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large crane. Mostly dark gray-black body with white
head and neck contrasting sharply, red forehead.
About 37 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migratory; breeds in Siberia, winters in East Asia.
Extremely rare vagrant to North America; a few
records from Pacific states.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberian boreal wetlands. Winters in
Japanese and Korean paddies and marshes.
Extremely rare vagrant to western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sandhill Crane much larger. White head and dark
body combination unique among North American cranes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray-black body with contrasting white head
and neck. Red forehead patch.
Extremely rare vagrant from Asia.
