Hooded Crow (Corvus cornix)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium crow, gray body with black hood, wings, and
tail. Two-toned gray-and-black
pattern. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Omnivorous scavenger and forager. Solves complex problems.
Very rare vagrant to northeastern
North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Open country, farmland, and coasts in Europe. Very
rare vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Crow all black. Eurasian Jackdaw smaller.
No other crow has gray
body with black hood.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body with black hood, wings, and tail. Two-tone
pattern distinctive. Very
rare European vagrant.
