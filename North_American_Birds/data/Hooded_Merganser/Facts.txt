Hooded Merganser (Lophodytes cucullatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small diving duck with fan-shaped crest. Male has striking black-and-white crest, rufous sides.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish in clear shallow water. Raises and lowers its large fan-shaped crest.

Habitat
-------
Wooded ponds, rivers, and swamps across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common and Red-breasted Mergansers are larger with red bills; Hooded has yellow-green bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Fan-shaped black-and-white crest on male is unmistakable among ducks.
