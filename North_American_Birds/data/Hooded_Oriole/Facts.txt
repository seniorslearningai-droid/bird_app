Hooded Oriole (Icterus cucullatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium oriole. Male is bright orange-yellow with black face and throat, black back and wings with white bars.

Behavior
--------
Often nests in hanging fibers of palm fronds. Feeds on nectar and insects.

Habitat
-------
Desert riparian areas, palms, and urban gardens of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bullock's Oriole male has more orange on face; Hooded has black hood extending to back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-yellow with black hood, back, and wings — frequently nests in palm trees.
