Hooded Warbler (Setophaga citrina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Bright yellow face encircled by full black hood on male. Female has yellow-green with faint hood traces.

Behavior
--------
Stays low in forest understory, frequently fanning tail to flash white outer feathers.

Habitat
-------
Rich deciduous forest understory in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wilson's Warbler has black cap but no full hood; Yellow Warbler lacks all dark on head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Full black hood framing yellow face; tail-flashing behavior in forest understory.
