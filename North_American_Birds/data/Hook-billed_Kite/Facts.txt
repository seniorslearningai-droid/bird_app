Hook-billed Kite (Chondrohierax uncinatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hawk with distinctive large hooked bill. Variable
plumage: gray-barred below, or dark
morph. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Specializes in eating snails. Rare resident of Rio
Grande Valley, Texas; casual along
southern border.

Habitat
-------
Riparian woodland and tropical forest. Rare resident
in extreme southern Texas, particularly
Santa Ana NWR area.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other kites lack the distinctive large hooked bill.
Hook-billed Kite's bill unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormously large hooked bill. Gray barring below.
Rare resident southern Texas riparian
forest near Mexican border.
