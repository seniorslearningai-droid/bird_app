Horned Grebe (Podiceps auritus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small grebe. Breeding adult has black head, golden-buff ear tufts, rufous neck, and red eye.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish and aquatic invertebrates. Carries young on back after hatching.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on freshwater ponds; winters on coastal bays and open water.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eared Grebe has peaked head and black neck in breeding; Horned has flat head and rufous neck.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Golden ear tufts, flat head profile, and rufous neck in breeding plumage.
