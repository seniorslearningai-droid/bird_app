Horned Lark (Eremophila alpestris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Sandy-brown lark with black breast band, yellow face, black mask, and tiny black horn feathers.

Behavior
--------
Walks on the ground in open areas foraging for seeds. Often forms large winter flocks.

Habitat
-------
Open habitats — beaches, fields, tundra, and deserts across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
The only true lark native to North America. No other bird has tiny horn feathers on head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny horn feathers, black mask and breast band, yellow face on open ground.
