Horned Puffin (Fratercula corniculata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Seabird with large orange-red and yellow bill, white face, black back, and fleshy horn above eye.

Behavior
--------
Dives to catch fish, carrying multiple fish crosswise in bill back to burrow nest.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean; nests on rocky sea cliffs in Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Atlantic Puffin has more colorful bill without the fleshy horn; Tufted Puffin has tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White face with fleshy horn above eye and massive colorful bill in the North Pacific.
