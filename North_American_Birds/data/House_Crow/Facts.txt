House Crow (Corvus splendens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium crow, black with gray nape, breast, and belly.
Glossy black crown, bill,
wings, and tail. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Urban scavenger. Highly invasive where introduced.
Extremely rare vagrant to North
America; likely ship-assisted.

Habitat
-------
Urban areas and ports. Rare vagrant to North America.
Native to South Asia, invasive
worldwide.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Crow all black without gray areas. Eurasian
Jackdaw similar pattern but
smaller with pale eye.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black crow with contrasting gray nape and breast.
Rare ship-assisted vagrant. Very
unusual occurrence in North America.
