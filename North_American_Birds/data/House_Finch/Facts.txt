House Finch (Haemorhous mexicanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch with heavy bill. Male has red head, breast, and rump; female is brown with blurry streaks.

Behavior
--------
Abundant at backyard feeders. Nests in a wide variety of structures including cacti and ornaments.

Habitat
-------
Nearly every habitat including cities, suburbs, deserts, and forests across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Finch male is raspberry-colored overall; House Finch male has more orange-red.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red restricted to head and breast on male; female is plain brown with blurry streaking.
