House Sparrow (Passer domesticus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Introduced sparrow. Male has gray cap, chestnut nape, black bib, and white cheek. Female is plain buffy-brown.

Behavior
--------
Lives in close association with humans. Nests in building cavities and nest boxes.

Habitat
-------
Cities, suburbs, farms, and rural areas throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other sparrows are native; House Sparrow male has bold black bib and white cheek.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black bib, chestnut nape, and white cheek on male; found everywhere humans live.
