House Wren (Troglodytes aedon)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small brown wren with faintly barred wings and tail, thin bill, and cocked tail. No bold facial markings.

Behavior
--------
Aggressively fills cavities with sticks, then chooses one nest. Very loud bubbly song.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges, open woods, shrubby areas, and gardens across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Winter Wren is smaller and darker with shorter tail; Bewick's Wren has white supercilium.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain brown with faint barring, no facial stripe, and exuberant bubbling song.
