Hudsonian Godwit (Limosa haemastica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large godwit. Breeding adult has dark reddish-barred
underparts, dark back, white wing stripe, black
and white tail. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migrates along narrow corridor through Great Plains.
Winters in South America. Forages on mudflats
and wet fields for invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in subarctic Alaska and Canada. Migrates
through Great Plains. Winters on South American
coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bar-tailed and Black-tailed Godwits similar. Hudsonian
has distinctive black and white tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black and white tail with white wing stripe visible
in flight. Upturned bill. Migrates
through central North America.
