Hutton's Vireo (Vireo huttoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small stocky vireo, olive-gray above with two white
wing bars and bold white eye ring.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Year-round resident in oak woodland. Sounds and looks
like Ruby-crowned Kinglet but larger
and heavier.

Habitat
-------
Oak woodland in western foothills and mountains from
British Columbia to Nicaragua.
Year-round resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruby-crowned Kinglet very similar! Hutton's Vireo larger,
heavier bill, different wing pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white eye ring, two wing bars. Looks like kinglet
but larger. Oak woodland resident.
Year-round western species.
