Iceland Gull (Larus glaucoides)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-large pale gull with white wingtips, no dark
primary tips. Pale gray back, pink legs,
small bill. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Winter visitor from Arctic Canada. Frequents harbors,
dumps, and beaches. Softer expression
than Herring Gull.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on cliffs in Arctic Canada and Greenland.
Winters on Atlantic coast; some reach
Great Lakes region.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Glaucous Gull larger with more robust bill. Iceland
Gull smaller with rounder head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wingtips with no dark markings. Small rounded
head, small bill. Winter visitor
to Atlantic coast harbors.
