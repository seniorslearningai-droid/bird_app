Icterine Warbler (Hippolais icterina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, greenish-gray above, bright yellow
below. Peaked crown, long bill, bluish-gray
legs. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in tree canopy. Loud, varied song
with mimicry. Very rare vagrant
to northeastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests, parks, and gardens in Europe
and western Asia. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow Warbler much smaller. Melodious Warbler very
similar but different primary
projection.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow-green plumage, peaked crown, blue-gray legs.
Pale panel on folded wing.
Very rare European vagrant.
