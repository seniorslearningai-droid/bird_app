Iiwi (Drepanis coccinea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian honeycreeper, brilliant scarlet-red
with long decurved salmon-pink
bill, black wings. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on tubular ohia and lobelia flowers. Long
bill perfectly adapted. Declining
due to avian malaria moving upslope.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of main Hawaiian Islands above 4,000
feet where mosquitoes are
less prevalent.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Apapane smaller without long curved bill. No other
bird with long salmon-pink
bill in Hawaii.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long salmon-pink decurved bill, brilliant red body.
Black wings and tail. One
of most beautiful Hawaiian birds.
