Inca Dove (Columbina inca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small dove with scaly-looking plumage from dark feather edges, reddish-brown wings, and long tail.

Behavior
--------
Common in urban and suburban areas, foraging on the ground for seeds.

Habitat
-------
Urban and suburban areas of the southwestern United States and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Ground-Dove is even smaller with spotted wings; Inca Dove has scaled pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Scaly feather pattern, long tail with white edges, and reddish-brown wing flash in flight.
