Inca Tern (Larosterna inca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unmistakable tern. All dark gray with white mustache
plumes, red bill and legs, white trailing wing
edge. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages along South American Pacific coast. Rare vagrant
to California. Catches fish near surface and
follows fishing boats.

Habitat
-------
Humboldt Current coastline of Peru and Chile. Rare
vagrant to California. Associated with cold Pacific
current waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar tern in range. Absolutely unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All gray with white mustache plumes and red bill
absolutely unmistakable. Rare vagrant
on California coast.
