Indian Peafowl (Pavo cristatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Enormous pheasant family bird. Male (peacock) has
iridescent blue-green body, famous long ornamental
train with eyespots. Female brown. Male up to 90 inches.

Behavior
--------
Roosts in trees. Males fan out train in courtship
display. Forages for seeds, insects, and small
animals. Introduced feral populations in Florida.

Habitat
-------
Native to South Asian forests and scrubland. Feral
populations in Florida and other warm US states.
Often seen near farms and parks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird approaches the peacock's size and
ornamental tail train.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Unmistakable; male's iridescent blue neck and enormous
fan train with eyespots. Female large
brown bird with green neck.
