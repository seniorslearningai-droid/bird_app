Indigo Bunting (Passerina cyanea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is entirely brilliant iridescent blue; female is plain brown with faint streaking and bluish tinge.

Behavior
--------
Males sing persistently from exposed perches all summer. Migrates at night using stars.

Habitat
-------
Brushy fields, forest edges, and roadsides across eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue Grosbeak is larger with wing bars and rusty shoulder patch; Bunting is small.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-over brilliant iridescent blue on male — no wing bars or other markings.
