Island Canary (Serinus canaria)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, olive-green with yellow-green underparts,
streaked above. Wild-type ancestor
of domestic canary. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds and insects. Beautiful melodious
song. Introduced to Hawaii and
Puerto Rico.

Habitat
-------
Scrub and forest edges. Introduced populations in
Hawaii and Puerto Rico. Native
to Canary Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-fronted Canary brighter yellow below. American
Goldfinch brighter yellow.
Wild birds more olive than domestic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-green finch with streaked back. Introduced to
Hawaii. Wild ancestor of pet
canary. Melodious song.
