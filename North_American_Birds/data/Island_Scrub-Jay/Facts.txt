Island Scrub-Jay (Aphelocoma insularis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large scrub-jay endemic to Santa Cruz Island.
Deep blue above, white below, blue
breast band. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and curious, largest scrub-jay species. Forages
on ground and in oaks for
acorns and invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Oak woodland and chaparral on Santa Cruz Island,
California. Found nowhere else
on Earth. Vulnerable species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
California Scrub-Jay on mainland similar but smaller.
No other jay on Santa
Cruz Island.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Location is definitive: Santa Cruz Island only.
Larger and deeper blue than
mainland California Scrub-Jay.
