Ivory-billed Woodpecker (Campephilus principalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest North American woodpecker. Black and white
with large ivory-white bill, red crest
on male. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Stripped bark from large dead trees for beetle larvae.
Possibly extinct; last confirmed
sighting debated (1944 or 1987).

Habitat
-------
Old-growth bottomland hardwood forest. Possibly extinct.
Former range southeastern US;
any recent sightings unconfirmed.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pileated Woodpecker similar but smaller with dark bill.
Ivory-billed larger with white bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous size, ivory-white bill. Male has red crest.
Possibly extinct; any sighting would
be extraordinary.
