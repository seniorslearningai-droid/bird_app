Ivory Gull (Pagophila eburnea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Entirely pure white plumage with black eyes and legs; medium-sized gull with rounded wings and stout yellow-tipped bill.

Behavior
--------
Scavenges carrion near polar bears and seals; gives harsh, tern-like calls; follows ships in Arctic waters.

Habitat
-------
High Arctic sea ice, glaciers, and rocky coastal cliffs; rarely seen south of Arctic Circle outside winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snowy Owl lacks webbed feet; albino gulls may appear similar but show pink wingtips and skin tones.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Completely white plumage at all ages; black legs; yellow bill tip; compact rounded body shape on sea ice.
