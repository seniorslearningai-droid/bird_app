Jabiru (Jabiru mycteria)
===========================

Description
-----------
Enormous stork; tallest flying bird in Americas. Mostly
white with bare black head and neck
and red neck band. About 55 inches.

Behavior
--------
Wades in marshes and wetlands for fish and frogs.
Rare vagrant from Mexico and Central
America to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Tropical wetlands and marshes. Very rare vagrant to
southern Texas from Mexico and
Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wood Stork smaller with black flight feathers. Jabiru
much larger and all-white with bare
black head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous size; tallest flying North American bird.
All-white with bare black head, red
neck band. Unmistakable if seen.
