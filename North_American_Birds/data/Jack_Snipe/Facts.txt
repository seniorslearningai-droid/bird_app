Jack Snipe (Lymnocryptes minimus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small snipe. Cryptically patterned brown with broad
buff stripes on back, short bill, no white
on tail. About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely secretive; crouches rather than flushing.
Bobs body while feeding. Rare vagrant to North
America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in northern Eurasian bogs. Winters in European
and African wetlands. Rare vagrant to
Atlantic coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Snipe similar but larger with long bill.
Jack Snipe smaller with short bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smaller than Common Snipe with shorter bill. No
white tail edge. Bouncing feeding
motion. Rarely flushes.
