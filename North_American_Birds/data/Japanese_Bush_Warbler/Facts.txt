Japanese Bush Warbler (Horornis diphone)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, olive-brown above, pale buff below
with pale supercilium. Loud distinctive
call. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense vegetation. Famous in Japan for
its beautiful spring song. Introduced
to Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub, forest edges, and bamboo in Japan
and East Asia. Introduced population
throughout Hawaiian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar skulking brown warbler in Hawaii.
Distinctive loud call helpful.
Rarely seen in open.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Loud, distinctive song from dense vegetation in
Hawaii. Plain olive-brown with
pale buff underparts. Very skulking.
