Japanese Quail (Coturnix japonica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small rotund quail, streaked brown, black-and-white
face stripes, dark chestnut throat
on male. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Runs through dense grass. Rarely flies unless flushed.
Escaped agricultural birds
occur occasionally.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands and croplands. Occasionally escapes from
farms and aviaries. Hypothetical
record for North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Stubble Quail similar. Northern Bobwhite has different
facial pattern. Smaller
than most quail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very small quail, heavily streaked brown. Chestnut
throat on male. Occasional
escaped birds in North America.
