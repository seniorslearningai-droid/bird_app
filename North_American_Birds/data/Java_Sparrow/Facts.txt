Java Sparrow (Lonchura oryzivora)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch, gray body with black head, white cheek
patch, red bill, pink
legs. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious, forms large flocks. Forages for
seeds in open areas. Invasive
in Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, rice fields, and urban areas. Introduced
to Hawaii; now common in
open areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird in Hawaii has combination of black
head, white cheeks, and
red bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head with white cheek patch, red bill, gray
body. Plump shape. Common
Hawaiian introduced species.
