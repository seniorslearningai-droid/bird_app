Jouanin's Petrel (Bulweria fallax)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-dark petrel with long wedge tail. Larger
than Bulwer's Petrel, very similar
in appearance. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Poorly known petrel breeding in western Indian Ocean.
Very rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Western Indian Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bulwer's Petrel very similar but smaller. Jouanin's
distinguished mainly by larger size.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger than Bulwer's Petrel but virtually identical.
All-dark with long wedge tail.
Extremely rare vagrant from Indian Ocean.
