Juan Fernandez Petrel (Pterodroma externa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gadfly petrel, pale gray above, white underparts
and face, dark cap and M-pattern on
upperwing. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Juan Fernandez Islands, Chile. Migrates
north into North Pacific; occasional
vagrant off California.

Habitat
-------
Eastern North Pacific in non-breeding season. Rare
vagrant off California coast.
Breeds on Chilean islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-necked Petrel very similar. Juan Fernandez has
more extensive dark cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large pale gadfly petrel with dark cap and M-pattern
above. White underparts. Rare
vagrant off California.
