Juniper Titmouse (Baeolophus ridgwayi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain gray titmouse with small crest, small dark bill, and no wing bars or facial markings.

Behavior
--------
Forages actively in junipers, caching seeds and insects under bark.

Habitat
-------
Pinyon-juniper woodlands of the Great Basin and southwestern plateaus.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oak Titmouse is nearly identical but found in oak woodland; ranges barely overlap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain gray titmouse with crest — nearly identical to Oak Titmouse but in juniper habitat.
