Kakawahie (Paroreomyza flammea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian creeper. Male brilliant scarlet-red.
Female brown above, buff below.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Crept along branches searching bark for insects.
Extinct since approximately 1963.
Last recorded on Molokai.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Molokai. Extinct due to deforestation
and introduced avian diseases.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Iiwi larger and more curved bill. Apapane darker
crimson. No creeper-like red
bird survives in Hawaii.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct Hawaiian creeper from Molokai. Male brilliant
red. No longer exists
in the wild.
