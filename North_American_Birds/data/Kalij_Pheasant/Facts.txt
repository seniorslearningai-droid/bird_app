Kalij Pheasant (Lophura leucomelanos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large pheasant introduced to Hawaii. Male has blue-black
plumage with red facial wattles, white rump.
Female brown with scaly pattern. About 28 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on forest floor for seeds, berries, and
insects. Introduced to Hawaii; now established on
Big Island in forest understory.

Habitat
-------
Dense montane forest and woodland in Hawaii, particularly
Big Island slopes. Native to South
Asian Himalayan forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-necked Pheasant; male Kalij darker with
different face pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's glossy blue-black plumage with red facial
skin and white lower back. Long tail.
Found only in Hawaii in dense forest.
