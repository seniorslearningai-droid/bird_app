Kamao (Myadestes myadestinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian thrush, brown above, gray below
with slight spotting on breast.
Orange-based bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for fruit in wet Hawaiian forest. Beautiful
melodic song. Extinct since
approximately 2002.

Habitat
-------
Wet native forests of Kauai. Extinct due to
introduced avian diseases, rats,
and habitat loss.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Omao similar but on Big Island. All Hawaiian
thrushes now rare or extinct.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct Hawaiian thrush from Kauai. Brown above,
gray below. No longer exists
in the wild.
