Kamchatka Leaf Warbler (Phylloscopus examinandus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium leaf warbler, olive-green above, whitish below
with single weak wing bar.
Very similar to Arctic Warbler.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in forests of Kamchatka. Occasional vagrant
to western Alaska. Separated
from Arctic Warbler by call.

Habitat
-------
Forests of Kamchatka and nearby regions of Russia.
Occasional vagrant to western
Alaska and Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Arctic Warbler nearly identical; best separated
by call and genetic analysis.
Willow Warbler lacks wing bar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely similar to Arctic Warbler. Best identified
by unique call. Western Alaska
vagrant in fall migration.
