Kauai Akialoa (Hemignathus stejnegeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, extremely long strongly
decurved bill, yellow-olive body.
Extinct since approximately 1969.

Behavior
--------
Probed flowers and bark with extremely long curved
bill. Last seen in Kauai
forest in 1969.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Kauai. Extinct due to avian
disease and habitat destruction.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other akialoa on different islands, all extinct.
Kauai Nukupuu slightly shorter
bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Extremely long decurved bill. From Kauai.
No longer exists in any
Hawaiian forest.
