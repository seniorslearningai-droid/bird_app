Kauai Amakihi (Chlorodrepanis stejnegeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green above, yellow-green
below, longer curved bill
than other amakihi. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager for nectar and insects. Longer bill
used to probe deeper flowers.
Declining but still present.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Kauai from sea level to higher
elevations. Threatened by
avian disease.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Anianiau smaller and brighter. Other amakihi on different
islands. Longer bill
distinguishes from relatives.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Longer curved bill compared to other amakihi. Kauai
native forest. Olive-green
with yellow-green underparts.
