Kauai Elepaio (Chasiempis sclateri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small monarch flycatcher endemic to Kauai. Brown
above with white wing bars, whitish
below with streaked breast. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active and curious, often approaches observers.
Cocks tail upward characteristically.
Forages for insects in forest understory.

Habitat
-------
Native wet forests of Kauai, Hawaii. Found in
ohia-lehua and koa forests from
sea level to high elevations.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oahu and Hawaii Elepaio similar but on different
islands. No other flycatcher in
its Kauai forest habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small flycatcher with upward-cocked tail on Kauai.
Brown-streaked plumage, white
wing bars. Curious behavior.
