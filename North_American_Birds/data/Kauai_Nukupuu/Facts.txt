Kauai Nukupuu (Hemignathus hanapepe)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper with very long, strongly
decurved upper bill. Male yellow,
female greenish. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Used long bill to probe bark for insects. Possibly
extinct; last confirmed record
in 1996 on Kauai.

Habitat
-------
Native wet forests of Kauai. Possibly extinct due
to avian disease and
habitat degradation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Akiapolaau has different bill with shorter lower
mandible. Kauai Amakihi smaller
with shorter bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long decurved bill. Yellow male. Possibly extinct
on Kauai. Last confirmed
record 1996.
