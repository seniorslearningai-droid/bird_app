Kauai Oo (Moho braccatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small black Hawaiian honeyeater with yellow thighs,
white-streaked breast, long bill.
Last recorded 1987. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for nectar in ohia flowers. Last confirmed
recording was a lone male
singing in 1987. Extinct.

Habitat
-------
Native ohia-lehua forests of Kauai. Extinct due
to habitat destruction, introduced
predators, and avian disease.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Hawaiian Oo species similar but on different
islands. No similar birds
survive in Hawaiian forests.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species. Last records from Kauai forest.
Black with yellow thigh patches.
Last Hawaii Oo to survive.
