Kelp Gull (Larus dominicanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, jet black back, white body, yellow bill
with red spot, yellow-green legs.
About 23 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dominant gull of southern hemisphere. Rare vagrant
to Gulf Coast of North America; occasional
hybrid with Herring Gull at Chandeleur Islands.

Habitat
-------
Southern hemisphere coastlines. Rare vagrant to Gulf
Coast; Louisiana coast records include
possible hybrids.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Black-backed Gull similar but has pink legs.
Kelp Gull's yellow-green legs distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black back with yellow-green legs. Southern hemisphere
species, rare Gulf Coast vagrant.
Largest all-black-backed gull in range.
