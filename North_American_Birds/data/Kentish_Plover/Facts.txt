Kentish Plover (Charadrius alexandrinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pale plover. Sandy-brown above, white below
with incomplete breast band (dark side patches).
Black or brown lateral breast patches. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on sandy beaches and mudflats for invertebrates.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic and Pacific coasts
from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Sandy beaches, salt marshes, and lakeshores across
Eurasia and Africa. Rare vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snowy Plover nearly identical and closely related.
Kentish larger with different leg color.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Incomplete breast band with side patches. Sandy-
brown plumage. Very similar to Snowy Plover;
identified by leg color and subtle size.
