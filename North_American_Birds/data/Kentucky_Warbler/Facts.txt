Kentucky Warbler (Geothlypis formosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Olive-green warbler with bright yellow underparts, black sideburns extending below eye, and yellow eye ring.

Behavior
--------
Stays low in dense forest understory. Loud rolling churry-churry-churry song.

Habitat
-------
Rich deciduous forest floor and understory in the eastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Yellowthroat has black mask; Kentucky Warbler has yellow spectacles and sideburns.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow spectacles, black sideburns on otherwise yellow-and-olive bird in forest understory.
