Kermadec Petrel (Pterodroma neglecta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Polymorphic gadfly petrel; light, intermediate, and
dark morphs. White primary flash below.
About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Kermadec and Lord Howe Islands. Forages
tropical Pacific; rare visitor to
Hawaiian and Pacific coast waters.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific Ocean. Rare visitor to Hawaiian
waters and North American Pacific
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Herald Petrel similar polymorphic species. Kermadec
has larger white primary flash below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large white primary flash visible in flight. Polymorphic
plumage from white to dark.
Tropical Pacific visitor.
