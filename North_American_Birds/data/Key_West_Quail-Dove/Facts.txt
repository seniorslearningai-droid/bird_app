Key West Quail-Dove (Geotrygon chrysia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dove with iridescent reddish-purple back,
bold white facial stripe, white belly, and green
neck iridescence. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on forest floor foraging for seeds and
fruit. Secretive. Historically bred in Florida Keys;
now rare vagrant from Bahamas.

Habitat
-------
Dense dry forest and scrub of Caribbean islands,
especially Bahamas and Cuba. Rare
visitor to Florida Keys.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruddy Quail-Dove smaller and darker without
the bold white facial stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white stripe from bill to behind eye on
rusty-brown head. Iridescent neck patch.
Walks on dense forest floor.
