Killdeer (Charadrius vociferus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plover with two black breast bands, orange-red rump, and brown back. Long tail.

Behavior
--------
Famous for broken-wing display to lure predators away from nest on open ground.

Habitat
-------
Open areas including fields, parking lots, and gravel rooftops across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated and other plovers have only one breast band; Killdeer has two.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Two black breast bands and orange rump — broken-wing distraction display near nest.
