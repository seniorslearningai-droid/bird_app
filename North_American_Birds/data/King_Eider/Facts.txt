King Eider (Somateria spectabilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Sea duck. Male has orange frontal shield, blue-gray head, peach breast, and black-and-white body.

Behavior
--------
Dives deeply in Arctic seas for mollusks and crustaceans.

Habitat
-------
Arctic Ocean and coasts; rare winter visitor on Atlantic and Pacific coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Eider male has white back; King Eider has black back with white chest patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's orange frontal shield and blue-gray head are unmistakable.
