King Rail (Rallus elegans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large rail with rich cinnamon-orange breast, brown streaked back, and long slightly downcurved reddish bill.

Behavior
--------
Walks stealthily through marsh vegetation; grunts and cackles loudly; rarely flies; forages for invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, tidal wetlands, and rice fields of eastern North America; declining due to habitat loss.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Clapper Rail is very similar but lives in saltwater marshes; Virginia Rail is much smaller with gray cheeks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size; bright rufous breast; long reddish bill; bold white barring on flanks; freshwater marsh habitat.
