Kioea (Chaetoptila angustipluma)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large extinct Hawaiian bird, brown-streaked plumage
with pale underparts, long decurved
bill. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for nectar and possibly fruit in native
Hawaiian forest. Extinct by approximately
1859.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Big Island of Hawaii.
Extinct due to deforestation following
Western contact.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hawaiian honeyeaters similar in habits but Kioea
was one of a kind.
Related to Oo birds.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species. No surviving individuals. Large
streaked brown bird with long
curved bill from Big Island.
