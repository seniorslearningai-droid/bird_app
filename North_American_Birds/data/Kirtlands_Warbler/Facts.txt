Kirtland's Warbler (Setophaga kirtlandii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Blue-gray warbler with black streaks above, yellow underparts with black streaks on sides. Bobs tail.

Behavior
--------
Nests only in young jack pines in Michigan; requires specific wildfire succession habitat.

Habitat
-------
Jack pine barrens of Michigan for breeding; winters in Bahamas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Prairie Warbler has facial markings; Kirtland's is plain-faced with tail-bobbing habit.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tail-bobbing, yellow belly with black streaks, and restricted to Michigan jack pines.
