Kittlitz's Murrelet (Brachyramphus brevirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small alcid. Breeding adult mottled brown-gold. Winter
plumage has extensive white on face. Very short
bill. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Found near glaciers and tidewater ice. Dives for
fish. Endangered; population declining with glacier
retreat in Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Glacial fjords and inshore coastal waters of Alaska.
Nests on rocky ground near glaciers. Declining
with climate change.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Marbled Murrelet similar. Kittlitz's has shorter bill
and more white on face in winter.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely short stubby bill. Winter bird has extensive
white face. Found near glaciers
and sea ice.
