Kona Grosbeak (Chloridops kona)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green body with
massive rounded grosbeak bill
for seed-crushing. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for seeds in native forests of Kona, Hawaii.
Extinct since approximately 1894.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Kona district, Big Island of
Hawaii. Extinct due to deforestation
and introduced species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Palila has yellow head. No other surviving bird
has such a massive rounded
bill in Hawaii.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species. Massive rounded grosbeak bill, olive-green.
Big Island Kona district.
No longer exists.
