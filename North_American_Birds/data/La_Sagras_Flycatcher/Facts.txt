La Sagra's Flycatcher (Myiarchus sagrae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Myiarchus flycatcher, brown above, whitish below,
pale yellow belly. Cuban and Bahaman
species. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Caribbean island Myiarchus. Rare vagrant to southern
Florida from Cuba and Bahamas.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland of Cuba and Bahamas. Rare vagrant
to southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ash-throated Flycatcher similar but from different
range. La Sagra's whiter below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Whitish below with pale yellow belly. Caribbean Myiarchus.
Rare vagrant to
southern Florida.
