Labrador Duck (Camptorhynchus labradorius)
===========================

Description
-----------
EXTINCT sea duck, last seen 1878. Male was black
and white; female brownish gray. Had unusual broad
flat bill adapted for filter-feeding shellfish. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on mollusks and invertebrates along Atlantic
shores. Apparently never very common. Went extinct
before detailed study was possible.

Habitat
-------
Wintered along Atlantic coast from Nova Scotia
to Chesapeake Bay. Breeding range unknown,
possibly Labrador coast. Extinct since 1878.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common and White-winged Scoters have similar dark
and white male plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only known from museum specimens; extinct since 1878.
Broad flat bill distinctive among
North American sea ducks.
