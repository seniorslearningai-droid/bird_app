Ladder-backed Woodpecker (Dryobates scalaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small woodpecker with black-and-white barred back, spotted underparts, and red cap on males.

Behavior
--------
Forages on cacti, mesquite, and desert trees for insects and cactus fruit.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub, cactus flats, and dry woodlands of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nuttall's Woodpecker is nearly identical but found only in California oaks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Barred back, spotted underparts in desert habitat — nearly identical to Nuttall's.
