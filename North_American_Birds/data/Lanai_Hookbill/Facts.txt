Lanai Hookbill (Dysmorodrepanis munroi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green with strongly
hooked bill for extracting snails
from shells. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Specialized for extracting snails. Extinct since approximately
1918; known from only two
museum specimens.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Lanai. Extinct due to deforestation
following pineapple plantation
development.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar snail-eating honeycreeper survives. Extremely
unusual hooked bill unlike
any other honeycreeper.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species known from two specimens. Hooked
bill for snail-eating. Lanai
native forest. No longer exists.
