Lanceolated Warbler (Locustella lanceolata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small warbler, heavily streaked above and below.
Brown with dark streaks throughout.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely secretive, creeps through dense grass.
Rarely seen in open. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows, sedge, and tall grass in Asia.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska and Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Grasshopper Warbler larger with less streaking below.
Pallas's Grasshopper Warbler larger
with different pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny heavily streaked warbler throughout. Mouse-like
creeping behavior. Very rare
western Alaska vagrant.
