Lapland Longspur (Calcarius lapponicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Arctic breeding ground bird. Breeding male has black face and breast, chestnut nape, and white belly.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in enormous flocks over open fields and grasslands in winter.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra; winters in open fields and prairies of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chestnut-collared Longspur has chestnut collar; Smith's has yellowish face. Lapland has rufous nape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut nape, black face and bib in breeding, and enormous winter flocks in fields.
