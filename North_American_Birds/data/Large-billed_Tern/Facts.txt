Large-billed Tern (Phaetusa simplex)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large tern. Black cap, gray back, white underparts,
very large yellow bill, bold black and white
wing pattern in flight. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on large rivers for fish. Occasional vagrant
to North America from South America. Found in
interior river habitats.

Habitat
-------
Large rivers and lakes of South America. Rare vagrant
to eastern North America from South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other tern has similar large yellow bill and
wing pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous yellow bill. Striking black and white wing
pattern visible in flight. Rare
vagrant from South America.
