Lark Bunting (Calamospiza melanocorys)
===========================

Description
-----------
Breeding male is all-black with large white wing patch. Female is brown-streaked with white wing patch.

Behavior
--------
Males perform spiraling display flights over prairie grass singing a melodious song.

Habitat
-------
Shortgrass prairie and grasslands of the Great Plains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bobolink male has yellow nape and white back patches; Lark Bunting male is all black.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black breeding male with large white wing patch — gregarious in Great Plains prairies.
