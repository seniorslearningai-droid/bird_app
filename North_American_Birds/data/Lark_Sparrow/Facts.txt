Lark Sparrow (Chondestes grammacus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sparrow with bold chestnut-and-white head pattern, white underparts with central breast spot.

Behavior
--------
Often runs on the ground. Male courts female by strutting with spread tail.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, roadsides, and fields with scattered trees across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other sparrow has the bold chestnut-and-white striped head pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold chestnut, black, and white striped head with central black breast spot.
