Laughing Gull (Leucophaeus atricilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull with black hood, dark gray mantle, red bill, and dark wingtips. White eye crescents.

Behavior
--------
Common at beaches and fishing piers. Laughing ha-ha-ha call gives it its name.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic and Gulf coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Franklin's Gull is smaller and has white band across wingtip; Laughing Gull is larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black hood, dark gray back, red bill, and laughing call at Atlantic beaches.
