Lavender Waxbill (Estrilda caerulescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch, pale lavender-gray body, crimson rump
and tail, red bill. About 4
inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious in small flocks. Forages for small seeds.
Introduced to Hawaii; present
on several islands.

Habitat
-------
Dry grasslands and scrub. Introduced to Hawaii,
found particularly on
Big Island.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Orange-cheeked Waxbill has orange cheek. Common Waxbill
has red face mask.
Lavender gray color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale lavender-gray with crimson rump and tail. Tiny
size. Red bill. Hawaiian
introduced species.
