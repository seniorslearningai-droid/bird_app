Lawrence's Goldfinch (Spinus lawrencei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has black face, gray body with yellow wing bars and yellow on breast. Female is grayish-yellow.

Behavior
--------
Highly nomadic, following seed crops. Often forages on the ground for seeds.

Habitat
-------
Dry chaparral and open woodlands of California and Baja California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American and Lesser Goldfinches have different patterns; Lawrence's has gray body with yellow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body with black face and yellow wing bars — nomadic and unpredictable in California.
