Laysan Albatross (Phoebastria immutabilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large albatross, white body and head, dark brown-black
back and wings, pink bill. Dark smudge
around eye. About 32 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern Islands; forages across
North Pacific. Regular winter visitor
offshore Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean. Breeds primarily on Midway and
other Hawaii Northwestern Islands.
Offshore Pacific coast in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-footed Albatross is all dark. Laysan has white
body and head with dark back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White head and body with dark back and wings. Pink
bill with dark smudge around eye.
Common North Pacific albatross.
