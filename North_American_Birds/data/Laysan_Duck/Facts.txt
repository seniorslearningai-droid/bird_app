Laysan Duck (Anas laysanensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small brown dabbling duck, Mallard relative.
Males and females similar mottled brown. White
eye ring distinctive. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on brine flies, shrimp, and plants.
Highly terrestrial. One of world's rarest ducks;
only about 1,000 remain on Hawaiian atolls.

Habitat
-------
Endemic to low-lying Hawaiian atolls. Lives near
hypersaline lagoons and freshwater seeps on
Laysan Island and Midway Atoll.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hawaiian Duck very similar; both are Mallard
relatives. Distinguished by range and white eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Mottled brown plumage with distinctive white eye ring.
Found only on Laysan Island
and Midway Atoll.
