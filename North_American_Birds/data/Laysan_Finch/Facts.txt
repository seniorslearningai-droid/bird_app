Laysan Finch (Telespiza cantans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large Hawaiian honeycreeper, yellow head and breast,
streaked brown back, massive
finch bill. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Omnivorous, eating seeds, insects, eggs, and carrion.
Bold and inquisitive. Found only
on Laysan Island.

Habitat
-------
Laysan Island in the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands.
Open grassland, shrubs, and
beach strand vegetation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nihoa Finch similar but island-specific. No other
large yellow finch on Laysan
Island.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large yellow-headed finch with massive bill on
Laysan Island. Bold behavior.
No similar bird on its island.
