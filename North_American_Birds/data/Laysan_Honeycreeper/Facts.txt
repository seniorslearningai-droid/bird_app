Laysan Honeycreeper (Himatione fraithii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, crimson-red with dark
wings and tail, curved bill.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on flowers for nectar on Laysan Island. Extinct
in 1923 when last three
individuals died in a sandstorm.

Habitat
-------
Laysan Island in the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands.
Extinct.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Apapane nearly identical but survives on main Hawaiian
Islands. Iiwi larger with
longer curved bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Nearly identical to Apapane but from Laysan
Island only. No longer exists.
Last died 1923.
