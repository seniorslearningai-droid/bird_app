Laysan Rail (Zapornia palmeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
EXTINCT tiny flightless rail. Brown with gray head,
short red bill, and streaked upperparts. Flightless
and very small. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Ran rapidly through low vegetation on Laysan Island.
Omnivorous; ate insects, carrion, and bird eggs.
Extinct due to rabbit habitat destruction and WWII.

Habitat
-------
Endemic to Laysan Island in Hawaiian archipelago.
Last recorded 1944. Extinct due to introduced
rabbits that destroyed its habitat.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small rails. Flightless behavior and Laysan
Island range were unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only known from museum specimens and historical photos.
Extinct since 1944. Flightless small
rail of Laysan Island, Hawaii.
