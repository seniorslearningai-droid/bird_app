Lazuli Bunting (Passerina amoena)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has turquoise-blue head and back, white wing bars, orange-buff breast, and white belly.

Behavior
--------
Males sing from exposed perches in brushy areas. Migrates in flocks.

Habitat
-------
Brushy hillsides, chaparral, and riparian shrubs in western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting lacks wing bars; Lazuli Bunting has two white wing bars and orange breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Turquoise-blue with white wing bars and orange breast — western counterpart to Indigo Bunting.
