LeConte's Sparrow (Ammospiza leconteii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small marsh sparrow with orange-buff face and breast, purple-gray nape, and streaked back.

Behavior
--------
Runs mouse-like through grass rather than flushing. Very secretive.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows and grassy marshes of the central and eastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nelson's Sparrow has orange face but duller; Grasshopper Sparrow lacks purple nape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange face and breast with purple-gray nape patch in wet grass.
