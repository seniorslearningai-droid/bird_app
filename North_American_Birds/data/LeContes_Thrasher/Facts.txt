LeConte's Thrasher (Toxostoma lecontei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Pale sandy-gray thrasher with pale underparts, downcurved bill, and rufous undertail coverts.

Behavior
--------
Runs rapidly on the ground through sparse desert vegetation. Rarely flies.

Habitat
-------
Very sparse desert with saltbush and cholla in southwestern California and Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Curve-billed and Sage Thrashers are darker; LeConte's is palest of all thrashers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Palest thrasher — sandy gray with rufous undertail in very sparse desert habitat.
