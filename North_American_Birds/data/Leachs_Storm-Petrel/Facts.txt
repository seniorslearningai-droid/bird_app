Leach's Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates leucorhous)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dark storm-petrel with white rump divided
by dark central stripe, forked tail, erratic
bounding flight. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on North Atlantic islands; common offshore.
Erratic bounding flight distinctive.
Comes to land only at night.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic and North Pacific. Breeds on offshore
islands; forages over
open ocean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wilson's Storm-Petrel has square tail and more direct
flight. Leach's has forked tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Erratic bounding flight with white rump split by
dark stripe. Forked tail.
Common North Atlantic seabird.
