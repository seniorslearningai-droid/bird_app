Least Auklet (Aethia pusilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny alcid, smallest in North America. Black above
with white scapular streaks, variable white below,
stubby reddish bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in enormous colonies on Alaskan islands.
Dives for copepods. Forms massive flocks at
sea. Joins mixed auklet flocks.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in huge colonies on rocky Alaskan and Siberian
islands. Winters in Bering Sea and
North Pacific.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other auklets much larger. Least Auklet tiny size
unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely small size among alcids. Stubby red bill.
White scapular streaks. Breeds in vast
colonies on Aleutian Islands.
