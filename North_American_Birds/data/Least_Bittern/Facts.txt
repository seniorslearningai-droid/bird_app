Least Bittern (Ixobrychus exilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny heron with tan and black back, chestnut sides of neck, and buffy wing patches showing in flight.

Behavior
--------
Climbs through cattail reeds rather than wading. Very secretive and rarely seen.

Habitat
-------
Dense freshwater marshes with tall cattails and reeds across eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Bittern is much larger and brown-streaked; Least Bittern is tiny.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest heron — tiny size, buffy wing patches, and climbing through cattails.
