Least Flycatcher (Empidonax minimus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Empidonax with bold white eye ring, two white wing bars, and short primary projection.

Behavior
--------
Sings emphatic che-BEK song from perch. Often in loose colonies in deciduous forests.

Habitat
-------
Open deciduous forests and forest edges across northern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Similar Empidonax species best separated by song — che-BEK is distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold eye ring, short primary projection, and loud emphatic che-BEK song.
