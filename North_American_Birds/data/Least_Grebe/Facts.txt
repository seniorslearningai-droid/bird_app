Least Grebe (Tachybaptus dominicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest North American grebe. Dark sooty gray
with bright yellow eye and dark bill. White
wing patch in flight. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives from surface to catch small fish and
aquatic invertebrates. Quickly dives when alarmed.
Found in Texas, Florida, and Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Small ponds, lakes, and slow rivers with emergent
vegetation in south Texas, southern Florida,
and throughout the Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pied-billed Grebe larger with striped bill. Least
Grebe much smaller and darker overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, bright yellow eye, and dark sooty
plumage. White wing patches visible
in flight.
