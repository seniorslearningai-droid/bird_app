Least Sandpiper (Calidris minutilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiniest sandpiper with yellow-green legs, brown-streaked back, and streaked buff-brown breast.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats and marsh edges for tiny invertebrates, often crouching.

Habitat
-------
Mudflats, marshes, and shorelines throughout North America during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated Sandpiper has black legs and gray back; Least has yellow legs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest sandpiper — yellowish legs and brown overall coloring distinguish it.
