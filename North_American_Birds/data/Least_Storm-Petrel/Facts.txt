Least Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates microsoma)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest storm-petrel in North America. All dark
brown-black, rounded wedge tail, rapid
direct flight. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on islands off Baja California. Very small
size and rapid direct flight distinguish
it from other Pacific storm-petrels.

Habitat
-------
Eastern Pacific off California and Baja California.
Breeds on San Benito and other
Baja California islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Storm-Petrel much larger. Least Storm-Petrel
very tiny with rapid wingbeats.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest storm-petrel. Very tiny all-dark bird with
rapid direct flight. Wedge-shaped
tail. Eastern Pacific.
