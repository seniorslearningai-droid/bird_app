Least Tern (Sternula antillarum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest tern with yellow bill with black tip, yellow feet, white forehead patch, and gray-and-white plumage.

Behavior
--------
Nests on sandy beaches and gravel bars. Hovers and dives for small fish.

Habitat
-------
Coastal beaches and river sandbars across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small terns have orange or red bills; Least Tern has yellow bill with black tip.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiniest tern — yellow bill with black tip and yellow feet on sandy beaches.
