Lesser Akialoa (Hemignathus obscurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, very long strongly curved
bill, yellow-olive body.
Extinct since approximately 1940.

Behavior
--------
Probed bark and flowers with long curved bill.
Extinct from Oahu due to
deforestation and avian disease.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu. Extinct.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other akialoa species on different islands. No similar
birds survive on Oahu.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Very long curved bill, yellow-olive. Oahu
native forest. No longer exists.
