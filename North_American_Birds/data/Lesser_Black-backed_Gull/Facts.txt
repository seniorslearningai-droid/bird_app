Lesser Black-backed Gull (Larus fuscus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, dark gray back (nearly as dark as Great
Black-backed), yellow legs, yellow bill.
About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
European species; increasingly common winter visitor
to Atlantic coast North America.
Frequents coastlines and inland areas.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic coast; regular and increasing winter
visitor from Europe. Atlantic coast,
Great Lakes, and inland reservoirs.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Black-backed Gull larger and has pink legs.
Lesser has yellow legs and smaller size.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow legs with dark gray back. Medium-large size.
Increasing winter visitor to Atlantic
coast from its European range.
