Lesser Frigatebird (Fregata ariel)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small frigatebird. Male black with white axillary
spurs under wings; female has white belly
and breast band. Long forked tail. About 30 inches.

Behavior
--------
Kleptoparasite that steals food from other seabirds.
Rare vagrant from tropical Pacific
to Hawaiian and Pacific waters.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific and Indian Oceans. Rare vagrant to
Hawaiian waters and North American
Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Magnificent Frigatebird larger. Lesser Frigatebird's
white axillary spurs diagnostic in male.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest frigatebird. Male has white axillary spurs.
Female has white belly and breast.
Rare Pacific vagrant.
