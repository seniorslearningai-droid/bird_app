Lesser Goldfinch (Spinus psaltria)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has black back (in eastern range), yellow underparts, and white wing patches. Female is dull olive-yellow.

Behavior
--------
Forages in weedy fields and scrub for seeds. Often hangs upside down on seed heads.

Habitat
-------
Weedy fields, gardens, and open scrub of the western United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Goldfinch male has yellow back and black wings; Lesser has black back and yellow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black back, bright yellow underparts, and white wing patches in male.
