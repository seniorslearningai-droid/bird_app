Lesser Koa-Finch (Rhodacanthis flaviceps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian honeycreeper, male with orange-yellow
head, olive-green body, large
bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on koa seeds and pods in native forest.
Extinct since approximately 1891
on Big Island.

Habitat
-------
Native koa forests of the Big Island of Hawaii.
Extinct due to deforestation
and introduced species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Koa-Finch similar but larger. No similar
birds survive in Hawaiian
koa forest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct Hawaiian species from Big Island koa forest.
Orange-yellow head, olive body,
large bill. No longer exists.
