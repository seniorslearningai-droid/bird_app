Lesser Nighthawk (Chordeiles acutipennis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Nightjar with cryptic brown mottled plumage, long wings with white bar, and small bill.

Behavior
--------
Active at dawn and dusk rather than fully nocturnally like Common Nighthawk.

Habitat
-------
Dry open country, desert scrub, and arid lowlands of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Nighthawk has white bar further from wingtip; Lesser has bar close to wingtip.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing bar close to wingtip, active at dusk in desert lowlands — crepuscular not nocturnal.
