Lesser Prairie-Chicken (Tympanuchus pallidicinctus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smaller and paler than Greater Prairie-Chicken; heavily barred brown and buff with orange-red air sacs on neck.

Behavior
--------
Males boom and dance on leks in spring; flocks forage for seeds and insects in shrubby grasslands.

Habitat
-------
Shortgrass and mixed-grass prairies with shinnery oak and sand sagebrush in southern Great Plains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Prairie-Chicken is darker and larger; Sharp-tailed Grouse has pointed tail and less barring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale barred pattern; rose-red neck sacs inflated in display; pinnae feathers on neck; short rounded tail.
