Lesser Scaup (Aythya affinis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Diving duck with peaked head, blue-gray bill, and white-and-black pattern. Purple gloss on head.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mollusks and aquatic plants. Forms large flocks on lakes and bays.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater lakes, reservoirs, and coastal bays across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Scaup has rounded head and green gloss; Lesser has peaked head and purple gloss.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Peaked head with purple gloss and shorter white wing stripe than Greater Scaup.
