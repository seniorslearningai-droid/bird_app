Lesser White-fronted Goose (Anser erythropus)
===========================

Description
-----------
A small, compact goose with a white forehead patch, yellow eye-ring, and orange legs. Smaller than the Greater White-fronted Goose.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grasses and sedges. Highly migratory, forming mixed flocks with other goose species during migration and winter.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in shrubby tundra and alpine areas of northern Eurasia. Winters in wetlands, grasslands, and agricultural fields.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Easily confused with Greater White-fronted Goose. The Bean Goose is also similar but larger with no white forehead.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive yellow eye-ring visible at close range, smaller size, and white forehead extending higher on crown than in Greater White-fronted Goose.
