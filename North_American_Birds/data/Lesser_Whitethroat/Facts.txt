Lesser Whitethroat (Curruca curruca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, gray-brown above with darker mask,
white throat, pale gray-white
underparts. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulking in dense scrub but sings from exposed
perch. Rare vagrant to western
and eastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub, hedgerows, and thickets in Europe
and Asia. Rare vagrant to
both coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Whitethroat larger with rusty wing panel.
Garden Warbler rounder and
lacks mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray mask, white throat, plain gray-brown back.
Small skulking warbler.
Very rare vagrant from Eurasia.
