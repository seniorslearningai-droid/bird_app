Lesser Yellowlegs (Tringa flavipes)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shorebird with bright yellow legs, straight thin bill, and white-spotted gray upperparts.

Behavior
--------
Forages gracefully in shallow water bobbing head as it walks. Two-note call.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, mudflats, and marshes across North America during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Yellowlegs is larger with longer uptilted bill and three-note call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smaller than Greater with straight shorter bill and soft two-note tu-tu call.
