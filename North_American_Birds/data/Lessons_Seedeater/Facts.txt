Lesson's Seedeater (Sporophila bouvronides)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch. Male black above with white below and
white wing spot. Female plain
brown. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for grass seeds in open areas. Rare vagrant
to Trinidad and occasionally
farther north.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands and open areas of Trinidad and northeastern
South America. Rare vagrant
to southern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Morelet's Seedeater similar black-and-white pattern.
Variable Seedeater similar.
Small black-and-white finch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male tiny black-and-white with white wing spot. Very
rare vagrant from Caribbean
and South America.
