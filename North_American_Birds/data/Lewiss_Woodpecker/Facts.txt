Lewis's Woodpecker (Melanerpes lewis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unique woodpecker with dark greenish back, red face, pink belly, and gray collar. Crow-like flight.

Behavior
--------
Catches insects in aerial sorties like a flycatcher. Stores acorns in bark crevices.

Habitat
-------
Open ponderosa pine forests and burned areas of western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other woodpecker has this combination of greenish back and pink belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark greenish back, pink belly, red face, and crow-like flapping flight style.
