Light-mantled Albatross (Phoebetria palpebrata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant sooty albatross with pale silvery-gray mantle
contrasting with dark head and wings.
Long narrow wings. About 35 inches.

Behavior
--------
Graceful flier with very narrow wings. Breeds on
Southern Ocean subantarctic islands.
Very rare vagrant.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North Pacific
and Atlantic coasts of North
America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sooty Albatross very similar. Light-mantled has pale
gray mantle contrasting with dark head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale silvery-gray back contrasting with dark sooty
head and wings. Very long narrow
wings. Southern Ocean species.
