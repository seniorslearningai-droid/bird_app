Lilac-crowned Amazon (Amazona finschi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium parrot, green with lilac-blue crown, red lores,
green cheeks. Long rounded tail.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Endangered in Mexico; established feral population
in Southern California. Loud calls.

Habitat
-------
Feral in Los Angeles and San Diego area of Southern
California. Endangered in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-crowned Amazon has brighter red lores. Lilac-crowned
has lilac-blue crown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Lilac-blue crown, red lores. Green parrot. Feral in
Southern California. Endangered
Mexican species.
