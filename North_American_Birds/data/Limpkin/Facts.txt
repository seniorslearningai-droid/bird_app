Limpkin (Aramus guarauna)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large brownish wading bird densely spotted white; long neck and legs; long slightly downcurved bill; heron-like appearance.

Behavior
--------
Specializes in extracting apple snails from shells; gives loud wailing screams; walks with limping gait.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, swamps, and river edges in Florida and expanding into southeastern US coastal states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Immature Night-Herons lack white spots; ibises have curved bills and different body proportions.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown plumage with distinctive white spots and streaks; long bill; loud screaming call; swampy habitat.
