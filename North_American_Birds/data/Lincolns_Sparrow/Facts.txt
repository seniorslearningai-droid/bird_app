Lincoln's Sparrow (Melospiza lincolnii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Neat sparrow with buffy breast band finely streaked with black, gray face, and bold brown head stripes.

Behavior
--------
Secretive skulker in dense vegetation. Song is rich bubbly wren-like warble.

Habitat
-------
Moist shrubby areas, mountain meadow edges; breeds in boreal and mountain west.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Song Sparrow has heavier streaking with central breast spot; Lincoln's has fine streaking.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Buffy breast with fine black streaks, gray face, and bold brown crown stripes.
