Little Blue Heron (Egretta caerulea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slate-blue heron with maroon-purple neck, blue-gray bill with black tip, and greenish legs.

Behavior
--------
Immatures are entirely white, creating confusion; they gradually acquire blue-gray plumage.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater and coastal marshes of the southeastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Blue Heron is much larger; Tricolored Heron has white belly stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small slate-blue heron with maroon neck and blue-tipped bill — immatures are all white.
