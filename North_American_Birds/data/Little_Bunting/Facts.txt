Little Bunting (Emberiza pusilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bunting, streaked above, buff-white below with
streaking, chestnut-red face
sides and crown. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in dense scrub. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Scrub and woodland edges of northern Siberia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Reed Bunting has black head on male. Rustic Bunting
has chestnut cap. Little
Bunting smallest of genus.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size, chestnut face, streaked. Rare Alaska
vagrant. Bold pale supercilium
and eye ring.
