Little Curlew (Numenius minutus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small curlew with slightly downcurved short bill.
Brown streaked plumage, striped crown, buffy wash
on face and breast. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in grasslands and fields rather than mudflats.
Rare vagrant to Pacific coast from Asian
breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Siberian and Alaskan tundra. Migrates
through Australia. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whimbrel much larger. Upland Sandpiper similar
but has shorter bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size for a curlew with short slightly curved
bill. Grassland habitat preference. Rare
vagrant on Pacific coast.
