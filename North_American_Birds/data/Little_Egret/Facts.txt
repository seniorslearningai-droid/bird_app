Little Egret (Egretta garzetta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small white egret, black bill, black legs, yellow
feet. Two long white plumes on nape
in breeding. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Wades actively for fish using yellow feet to disturb
prey. Rare but increasing vagrant
to Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Coastal marshes and estuaries of Europe and Asia.
Increasingly rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snowy Egret has yellow feet and black bill. Little
Egret larger with distinctive nape plumes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow feet on black legs. Black bill. Rare
European vagrant. Nearly identical to
Snowy Egret but slightly larger.
