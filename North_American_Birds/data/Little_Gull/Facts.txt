Little Gull (Hydrocoloeus minutus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny gull, world's smallest. Breeding adult has
black hood, dark red bill, rounded wings with
dark underwing. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dips gracefully to water surface for food. Regular
but uncommon visitor to Great Lakes and Atlantic
coast from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Eurasian marshes. Winters on European
and North American coasts. Regular visitor
to Great Lakes and Atlantic.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bonaparte's Gull larger. Little Gull tiny with round
wings and dark underwing.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size and round wings. Dark underwing coverts
distinctive in flight. Black hood
in breeding.
