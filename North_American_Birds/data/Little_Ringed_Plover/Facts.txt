Little Ringed Plover (Charadrius dubius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plover. Brown above, white below, black breast
band and head pattern, yellow eye ring, dull
flesh-pink legs. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on invertebrates along freshwater margins. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and Atlantic coast
from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds along freshwater rivers, streams, and gravel
pits across Eurasia. Rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Ringed Plover similar. Little Ringed has
yellow eye ring and lacks wing bar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow orbital eye ring diagnostic. Lacks wing bar
visible in flight. Fresh water habitat
preference. Rare vagrant.
