Little Stint (Calidris minuta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small sandpiper. Breeding adult has rufous
face and breast with white V on back. Non-
breeding gray-brown. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages actively on mudflats picking small invertebrates.
Rare vagrant to both coasts from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic Eurasian tundra. Winters on African
and South Asian coasts. Rare vagrant to
both coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated and Least Sandpipers nearly identical
in non-breeding. Rufous tones in breeding.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous-orange face and back in breeding plumage
with white V. Very small. Rare
vagrant from Eurasia.
