Little Tern (Sternula albifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny tern. White with gray back, black cap with
white forehead, yellow bill with black tip. Small
and fast-flying. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hovers and dives for small fish. Breeds on sandy
beaches. Rare vagrant to North America from
Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Sandy beaches and coastal areas of Eurasia and Africa.
Rare vagrant to Pacific coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Least Tern nearly identical. Little Tern very similar;
different range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size. Yellow bill with black tip. White
forehead. Rare vagrant; nearly identical
to Least Tern.
