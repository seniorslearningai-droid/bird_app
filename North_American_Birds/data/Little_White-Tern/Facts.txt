Little White-Tern (Gygis microrhyncha)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pure white tern, smaller than White Tern.
All white with black-tipped bill, large black
eye. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Lays single egg on bare branch. Picks fish from
surface. Found on Pacific tropical islands
and atolls.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific islands including Hawaiian Northwestern
chain and other atolls.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White (Fairy) Tern similar but larger. Little White-
Tern smaller with different bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white plumage. Smaller than Fairy Tern. Found
on tropical Pacific islands and
Hawaiian atolls.
