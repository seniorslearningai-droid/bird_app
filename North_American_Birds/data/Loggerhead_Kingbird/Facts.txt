Loggerhead Kingbird (Tyrannus caudifasciatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large kingbird, black and white head with large head
and bill, white below, brown-black
above. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Caribbean island kingbird. Rare vagrant to southern
Florida from Cuba and Bahamas.

Habitat
-------
Forest and open areas of Caribbean islands. Rare vagrant
to southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Kingbird similar but smaller-headed. Loggerhead
has noticeably large head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large head and heavy bill on kingbird. Black above,
white below. Caribbean species,
rare Florida vagrant.
