Loggerhead Shrike (Lanius ludovicianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray bird with black mask, hooked bill, and black-and-white wings. Impales prey on thorns.

Behavior
--------
Perches on wires and fence posts. Impales insects, lizards, and small birds on thorns.

Habitat
-------
Open fields, shrubby grasslands, and agricultural areas across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Mockingbird is larger without black mask; Northern Shrike has barred breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black mask, hooked bill, and habit of impaling prey on thorns or barbed wire.
