Long-billed Curlew (Numenius americanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shorebird with extremely long downcurved bill, cinnamon-brown plumage, and barred underparts.

Behavior
--------
Probes deeply in mud for crabs and invertebrates with its remarkably long bill.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands in breeding season; coastal mudflats and marshes in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whimbrel is similar but smaller with shorter bill and striped head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest North American shorebird — extraordinarily long downcurved bill is unmistakable.
