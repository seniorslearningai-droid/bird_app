Long-billed Dowitcher (Limnodromus scolopaceus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-large shorebird with long bill, rusty-red breeding plumage, and white wedge up the back.

Behavior
--------
Probes in rapid sewing-machine motion in shallow water. High keek call.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes and mudflats during migration across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Short-billed Dowitcher prefers saltwater; Long-billed prefers freshwater; calls differ.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
High keek call and freshwater preference separates it from Short-billed Dowitcher.
