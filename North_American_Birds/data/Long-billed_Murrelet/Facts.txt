Long-billed Murrelet (Brachyramphus perdix)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium alcid. Brown streaked breeding plumage, black
and white winter plumage. Longer bill than Marbled
Murrelet. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in old-growth forests, sometimes far from sea.
Rare vagrant to North America from Asia. Dives
for fish.

Habitat
-------
Old-growth forests and coastal waters of East Asia
(Russia, Japan). Rare vagrant to Pacific coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Marbled Murrelet nearly identical. Long-billed has
longer bill and less distinct collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Longer bill than Marbled Murrelet. Different white
collar pattern in winter. Rare Asian
vagrant on Pacific coast.
