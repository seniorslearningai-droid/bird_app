Long-billed Thrasher (Toxostoma longirostre)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large thrasher, brown above with heavily streaked
white breast, orange eye, long
decurved bill. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground by tossing leaves and debris.
Loud, rich song. Resident in
dense Texas brush country.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub and brushy woodland of the
Rio Grande Valley and south
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Thrasher very similar but has yellow eye
and two wing bars. Overlaps
rarely.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange eye, long decurved bill, heavily streaked
below. Rio Grande Valley brush
country. Similar to Brown Thrasher.
