Long-eared Owl (Asio otus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium owl with long ear tufts close together, orange facial disc, and streaked underparts.

Behavior
--------
Roosts communally in dense conifers during winter. Silent hunter over open fields.

Habitat
-------
Open country near woodland edges; uses abandoned hawk nests for breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Horned Owl is larger with wider-set ear tufts; Short-eared has shorter ear tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long ear tufts set close together on an orange facial disc; communal winter roosts.
