Long-legged Buzzard (Buteo rufinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large buteo, pale morph tawny-brown with pale head,
dark carpal patches; dark morph all
dark. About 24 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts small mammals over open steppe. Old World counterpart
to Ferruginous Hawk. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Open steppe and desert of Europe and Asia. Very rare
vagrant to Atlantic coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ferruginous Hawk similar. Long-legged Buzzard has pale
tail and different underwing pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large pale buteo with pale tail and dark carpal patches.
Old World species, very rare
vagrant.
