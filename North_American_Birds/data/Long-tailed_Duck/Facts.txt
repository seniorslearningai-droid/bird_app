Long-tailed Duck (Clangula hyemalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Sea duck. Male has long tail pins, black-and-white winter plumage, and bubbly ow-owdle-ow call.

Behavior
--------
Dives deeply — to 200 feet — for mollusks in Arctic seas. Very vocal.

Habitat
-------
Arctic and subarctic seas; winters on open ocean and large lakes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other sea duck has long tail pins and bold black-and-white winter plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long tail pins on male, black-and-white winter plumage, and yodeling call.
