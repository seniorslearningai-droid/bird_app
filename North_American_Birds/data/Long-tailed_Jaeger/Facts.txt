Long-tailed Jaeger (Stercorarius longicaudus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest and most elegant jaeger. Very long central
tail feathers, gray back, dark wings without
white flash. About 22 inches including tail.

Behavior
--------
Graceful buoyant flight. Less piratical than other
jaegers; forages more independently. Regular offshore
migrant on both coasts.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Pelagic outside breeding.
Migrates offshore on both coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Parasitic Jaeger shorter tailed. Long-tailed has very
long streamers and paler gray back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long elegant tail streamers. Pale gray back
without white wing flash. Buoyant graceful
flight.
