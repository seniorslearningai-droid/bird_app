Long-toed Stint (Calidris subminuta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small sandpiper. Brown streaked upperparts,
white underparts with streaked breast, long toes,
yellow legs. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on floating vegetation using long toes. Forages
in freshwater marshes. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberian bogs and wet meadows. Winters
in Southeast Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Least Sandpiper similar. Long-toed has longer toes,
yellow legs, and different head pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long toes visible. Yellow legs. Bolder head pattern
with white supercilium. Rare vagrant
in western Alaska.
