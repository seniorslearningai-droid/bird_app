Louisiana Waterthrush (Parkesia motacilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Warbler with white supercilium, brown back, streaked underparts, and buff wash on flanks. Bobs tail.

Behavior
--------
Walks along fast-moving streams bobbing tail continuously. Loud ringing song.

Habitat
-------
Fast-flowing forested streams in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Waterthrush prefers still water and has yellowish supercilium; Louisiana has white.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pure white supercilium, buffy flanks, and bobbing tail along fast-moving streams.
