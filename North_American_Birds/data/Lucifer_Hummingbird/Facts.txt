Lucifer Hummingbird (Calothorax lucifer)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small hummingbird. Male has elongated iridescent
purple gorget that flares to sides. Long curved
bill. Female streaked below. About 3.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on agave and desert flowers. Males display
with pendulum swings. Found in desert canyons
and scrub of Big Bend area, Texas.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub with agave, rocky canyon slopes in
Big Bend area of Texas, and adjacent
Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Costa's Hummingbird similar purple gorget. Lucifer's
curved bill and elongated gorget extensions distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's dramatically elongated purple gorget extending
on sides. Long curved bill. Female's long
curved bill distinctive.
