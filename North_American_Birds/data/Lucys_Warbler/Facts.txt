Lucy's Warbler (Leiothlypis luciae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny plain gray warbler with white eye ring, chestnut rump patch, and chestnut crown patch.

Behavior
--------
Only warbler that regularly nests in cavities, using abandoned woodpecker holes.

Habitat
-------
Desert riparian areas with mesquite trees in the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Virginia's Warbler has yellow on breast; Lucy's is plain gray with chestnut rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny plain gray warbler with chestnut rump and crown — nests in tree cavities.
