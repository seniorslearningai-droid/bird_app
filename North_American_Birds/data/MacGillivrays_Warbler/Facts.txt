MacGillivray's Warbler (Geothlypis tolmiei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has olive back, yellow underparts, gray hood with broken white eye crescents, and black bib.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense low vegetation, hard to see. Loud rolling song.

Habitat
-------
Dense brushy areas and riparian thickets in western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mourning Warbler is nearly identical but has complete hood; MacGillivray's has eye crescents.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Broken white eye crescents separate it from Mourning Warbler.
