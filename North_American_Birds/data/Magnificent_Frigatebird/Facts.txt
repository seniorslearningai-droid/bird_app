Magnificent Frigatebird (Fregata magnificens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large black seabird with enormously long forked tail.
Male has brilliant red inflatable throat
pouch. About 40 inches.

Behavior
--------
Kleptoparasite; chases other seabirds and steals food.
Soars effortlessly on thermals.
Breeds on Florida Keys and Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Atlantic and Pacific coasts. Regular along
Florida and Gulf coasts; breeds on
Dry Tortugas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Frigatebird similar but found in Pacific/Indian
Ocean. Magnificent is Atlantic species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous long forked tail, all-black plumage. Male's
red throat pouch inflated at colony.
Common along Gulf and Florida coasts.
