Magnolia Warbler (Setophaga magnolia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has black back, yellow underparts with black streaks, white wing patch, and yellow rump.

Behavior
--------
Active forager at mid-level in conifers. Bold white tail band visible from below.

Habitat
-------
Young coniferous and mixed forests; breeds in boreal forest.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Prairie Warbler has facial streaking; Magnolia has white wing patch and streaked yellow breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing patch, yellow rump, and yellow breast with black streaking.
