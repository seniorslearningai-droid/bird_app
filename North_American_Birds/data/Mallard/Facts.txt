Mallard (Anas platyrhynchos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Most familiar duck. Male has iridescent green head, yellow bill, white neck ring, and chestnut breast.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles for seeds and aquatic vegetation. Ancestor of most domestic duck breeds.

Habitat
-------
Almost any freshwater habitat across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Green head and yellow bill of male are distinctive; female resembles other female dabbling ducks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's iridescent green head and yellow bill — the most recognized duck in the world.
