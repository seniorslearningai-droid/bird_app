Mandarin Duck (Aix galericulata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium duck. Male extraordinarily colorful with orange
sail feathers, purple breast,
green crown, red bill. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches in trees, nests in tree cavities. Small
introduced populations exist in
California and other areas.

Habitat
-------
Woodland near ponds and rivers. Escaped individuals
and small introduced populations
in North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wood Duck male similar flamboyance but different pattern.
No other duck looks
like breeding male Mandarin.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male unmistakable with orange sail feathers and
multicolored head. Most ornate duck
in world.
