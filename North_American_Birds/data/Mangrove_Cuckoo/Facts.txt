Mangrove Cuckoo (Coccyzus minor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slim cuckoo with gray-brown upperparts, buffy-yellow underparts, black mask, and long white-tipped tail.

Behavior
--------
Secretive and hard to see. Slow deliberate movements in dense mangrove vegetation.

Habitat
-------
Mangrove swamps and tropical hammocks of southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-billed Cuckoo lacks black mask and buffy underparts; Mangrove is distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black mask, buffy-yellow underparts, and mangrove habitat in southern Florida.
