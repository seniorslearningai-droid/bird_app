Mangrove Swallow (Tachycineta albilinea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small swallow, iridescent green above with white
rump, white below. White loral
spot. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages over water near mangroves and rivers.
Nests in tree cavities. Rare
vagrant to southern Texas and Florida.

Habitat
-------
Mangroves and coastal rivers from Mexico to Panama.
Very rare vagrant to southern
Texas and Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tree Swallow lacks white rump. Violet-green Swallow
has white patches on face
and white rump in different range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump distinctive. Iridescent green above,
white below. White loral spot.
Coastal Mexico/Central America.
