Mangrove Yellow (Yellow) Warbler (Setophaga petechia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, bright yellow with chestnut head on
male in Caribbean subspecies.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in mangroves for insects. Resident in mangrove
forests of Caribbean and
Gulf Coast.

Habitat
-------
Mangrove forests of Florida Keys, Gulf Coast, and
Caribbean. Non-migratory resident
subspecies group.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Yellow Warbler lacks chestnut head. Wilson's
Warbler has black cap.
Chestnut head on male distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male with chestnut head in Caribbean form. Mangrove
habitat. Yellow overall.
Resident non-migratory subspecies.
