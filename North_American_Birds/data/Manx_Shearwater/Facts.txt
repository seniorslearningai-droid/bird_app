Manx Shearwater (Puffinus puffinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shearwater, jet black above, bright white below,
sharp contrasting pattern, black bill.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on British and Irish islands. Common in North
Atlantic; migrates to South America.
Regular offshore Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean. Regular offshore Atlantic coast
in summer. Breeds in European
islands, winters off South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Audubon's Shearwater similar but smaller with brown
(not black) back. Manx has sharp
black-and-white contrast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sharp black-and-white contrast with jet-black upperparts.
Medium shearwater. Common
Atlantic coast seabird.
