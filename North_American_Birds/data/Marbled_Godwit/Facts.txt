Marbled Godwit (Limosa fedoa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shorebird with long upturned bill, cinnamon-brown plumage, and barred underparts.

Behavior
--------
Probes deeply in mud for invertebrates. Flies long distances in V-shaped flocks.

Habitat
-------
Coastal mudflats in winter; northern Great Plains grasslands for breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long-billed Curlew has downcurved bill; Godwit's bill is straight with slight upward bend.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large cinnamon-brown shorebird with long pinkish-based slightly uptilted bill.
