Marbled Murrelet (Brachyramphus marmoratus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Seabird in breeding has marbled brown plumage; in winter has black-and-white with dark cap.

Behavior
--------
Uniquely nests on bare branches of old-growth trees far inland, not in burrows.

Habitat
-------
Old-growth coastal forests near the Pacific; forages at sea.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other murrelets nest underground; Marbled Murrelet nests in old-growth trees.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Uses old-growth tree branches for nesting — unusual for a seabird. Marbled brown in summer.
