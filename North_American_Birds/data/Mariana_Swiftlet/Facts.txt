Mariana Swiftlet (Aerodramus bartschi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small swift. Dark sooty gray-brown overall with paler
gray rump. Small size and forked tail.
About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Uses echolocation to navigate in dark caves where
it nests. Aerial insect feeder. Endangered; found
only in Mariana Islands and Guam.

Habitat
-------
Found only on Guam and northern Mariana Islands
(Saipan, Tinian, Aguijan). Nests in limestone
caves. Endangered species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other small swiftlets. Mariana is only swiftlet
in its range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only swiftlet in the Mariana Islands region. Small
size, dark plumage, and gray rump.
Nests in limestone caves.
