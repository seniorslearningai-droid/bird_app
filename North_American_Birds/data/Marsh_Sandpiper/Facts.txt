Marsh Sandpiper (Tringa stagnatilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small elegant sandpiper. Gray-brown above, white
below, very thin straight bill, long pale legs.
Like a miniature Greenshank. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in shallow freshwater with delicate picking
movements. Rare vagrant to Pacific coast from
Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Eurasian steppe wetlands. Winters in
Africa and Asia. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Greenshank much larger. Wilson's Phalarope has
needle-thin bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very thin straight bill. Long pale legs. Delicate
Greenshank-like appearance. White rump
in flight.
