Marsh Wren (Cistothorus palustris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small wren with bold white supercilium, black-and-white streaked crown, and unstreaked rufous back.

Behavior
--------
Sings day and night from cattail marshes. Males build many dummy nests.

Habitat
-------
Dense cattail and bulrush marshes across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sedge Wren has streaked crown and back; Marsh Wren has plain rufous back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white eyebrow, streaked crown, and unstreaked rufous back in cattail marshes.
