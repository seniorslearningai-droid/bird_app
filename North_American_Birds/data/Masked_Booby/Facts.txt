Masked Booby (Sula dactylatra)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large booby. White body and head with black flight
feathers and tail, yellow bill,
black face mask. About 32 inches.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives from height for fish. Breeds on tropical
islands; forages offshore in Atlantic
and Pacific.

Habitat
-------
Tropical oceans. Breeds on Dry Tortugas, Florida and
other Gulf/Caribbean islands. Offshore
Atlantic and Gulf.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nazca Booby nearly identical. Masked has yellow bill;
Nazca has orange bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White plumage with black flight feathers, black mask.
Yellow bill; compare to Nazca Booby's
orange bill.
