Masked Duck (Nomonyx dominicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small stiff-tailed duck. Male has rufous body and
bold black mask with blue bill. Female and non-breeding
male streaked brown with two facial stripes. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives in dense aquatic vegetation. Secretive and
hard to spot. Rare visitor to Texas and Florida
from Mexico and Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater ponds and marshes with dense floating
vegetation. South Texas and south Florida;
wanders from Mexico and Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ruddy Duck same family. Female could be confused
with female Ruddy but facial stripe
pattern differs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's black face mask and reddish body with white
wing patch in flight. Female's two dark
facial stripes on pale face.
