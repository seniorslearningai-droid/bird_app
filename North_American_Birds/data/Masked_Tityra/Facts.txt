Masked Tityra (Tityra semifasciata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky bird, male pale gray with black mask, wings,
and tail tip, red bare facial skin.
Female brown and gray. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Eats fruit and some insects. Perches conspicuously at
forest canopy. Very rare vagrant
to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Forest edge and open woodland from Mexico to South
America. Very rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-and-white plumage pattern unique among cotingas.
Red facial skin distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: gray with black mask and red bare facial skin.
Cotinga family. Very rare
Texas vagrant from Mexico.
