Maui-nui Akialoa (Hemignathus lanaiensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, long decurved bill,
yellow-olive body. Extinct from
Lanai and Maui.

Behavior
--------
Probed bark and flowers with long bill. Extinct
since approximately early 20th century.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Lanai and possibly Maui. Extinct
due to deforestation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other akialoa species island-specific, all extinct.
No similar birds survive in
Maui-nui island group.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Long decurved bill, yellow-olive. Lanai and
Maui group. No longer exists.
