Maui Akepa (Loxops ochraceus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, male orange-yellow with
slightly crossed bill. Female
olive-green. Extinct early 1900s.

Behavior
--------
Used crossed bill to open flower buds. Extinct from
Maui in early twentieth
century.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Maui. Extinct due to deforestation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hawaii Akepa on Big Island. Akekee on Kauai. No
similar birds survive on Maui.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Orange-yellow male, crossed bill. Maui.
No longer exists.
