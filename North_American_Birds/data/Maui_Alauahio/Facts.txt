Maui Alauahio (Paroreomyza montana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian creeper. Male yellowish-green with
olive back. Female olive-green.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Actively creeps along branches and bark searching
for insects. Still present in
native forests of Maui.

Habitat
-------
Native ohia and koa forests of east Maui above
4,000 feet. Uncommon but persistent.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Maui Amakihi has curved bill. Maui Warbler larger
with different behavior.
Only creeper remaining on Maui.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small yellowish-green bird creeping along branches
on Maui. Active bark searcher.
Native forest above 4,000 feet.
