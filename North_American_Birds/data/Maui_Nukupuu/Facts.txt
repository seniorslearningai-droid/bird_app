Maui Nukupuu (Hemignathus affinis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, long strongly decurved
bill, yellow male with greenish
back. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Used long bill to probe bark for insects. Possibly
extinct; last confirmed record
in the 1990s on Maui.

Habitat
-------
Native wet forests of east Maui. Possibly extinct
due to avian malaria and
habitat degradation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Maui Alauahio has short bill. Iiwi much larger
and red. Long bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long strongly decurved bill, yellow male. East
Maui wet forest. Possibly
extinct. Last records 1990s.
