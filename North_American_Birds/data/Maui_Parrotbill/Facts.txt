Maui Parrotbill (Pseudonestor xanthophrys)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, olive-green above, yellow
below, massive hooked parrot-like
bill. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Uses massive bill to tear bark and branches for
insects. Critically endangered; fewer
than 500 individuals remain.

Habitat
-------
Native wet forest of east Maui above 4,500 feet.
Very restricted range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Maui Alauahio creeps on bark but smaller bill.
Ou has yellow head.
No other hookbill on Maui.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive hooked bill, olive-green above, yellow below.
High Maui native forest.
Critically endangered endemic.
