McCown's Longspur (Rhynchophanes mccownii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky sparrow-like bird; breeding male has black cap, breast patch, and gray back; white tail with black T-shape.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds and insects; male sings in flight display; flocks in winter on bare fields.

Habitat
-------
Short-grass prairies, plowed fields, and sparse rangeland of Great Plains; winters in Texas and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chestnut-collared Longspur has chestnut nape; Thick-billed Longspur has heavier bill and different tail pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White tail with inverted T-shaped black tip; heavy bill; breeding male's black chest patch; short primary projection.
