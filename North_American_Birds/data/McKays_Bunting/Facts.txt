McKay's Bunting (Plectrophenax hyperboreus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting, almost entirely white with small
black wing tips. Female with
some brown. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds and insects. Breeds
only on St. Matthew and Hall
Islands, Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Tundra of St. Matthew and Hall Islands in Bering
Sea. Winters on mainland
Alaska and occasionally coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snow Bunting has more extensive black in wings
and back. Nearly all-white
pattern distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Almost entirely white with minimal black wing tips.
St. Matthew Island breeder.
More white than Snow Bunting.
