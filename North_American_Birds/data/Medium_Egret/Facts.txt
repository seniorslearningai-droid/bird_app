Medium Egret (Ardea intermedia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized white egret, yellow bill, black legs
and feet. Similar to Great Egret but
smaller. About 26 inches.

Behavior
--------
Wades in shallow water for fish. Asian and African
species; very rare vagrant to
western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands and rice paddies of Asia and Africa. Very
rare vagrant to western Alaska
from East Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Egret larger with longer neck. Snowy Egret
has yellow feet. Medium Egret intermediate.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white egret with yellow bill and black legs and
feet. Smaller than Great Egret.
Rare Alaska vagrant.
