Merlin (Falco columbarius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small aggressive falcon with blue-gray or brown upperparts, heavily streaked underparts, and banded tail.

Behavior
--------
Pursues songbirds in fast dashing flight. Bold and aggressive for its small size.

Habitat
-------
Open country, forest edges, and cities across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Kestrel has rusty back; Merlin is darker and lacks kestrel's black facial marks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small compact falcon, heavily streaked below, dashing fast flight after small birds.
