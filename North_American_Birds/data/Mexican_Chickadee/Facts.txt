Mexican Chickadee (Poecile sclateri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium chickadee, black cap and bib, gray back
and wings, gray sides extending
far onto flanks. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages actively for insects and seeds in pine
and pine-oak forests. Less gregarious
than other chickadees.

Habitat
-------
Pine and pine-oak forests of the Sierra Madre
Occidental, with small U.S. population
in Chiricahua Mountains, Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mountain Chickadee has white supercilium. Black-capped
Chickadee has white cheeks
and buff sides.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extensive dark gray wash on flanks and sides.
Black cap and bib. Chiricahua
Mountains, Arizona or Mexico.
