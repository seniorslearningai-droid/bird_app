Mexican Duck (Anas diazi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dabbling duck, closely related to Mallard.
Both sexes mottled brown similar to female Mallard.
Males have yellow-green bill. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles in freshwater for aquatic plants and
invertebrates. Forms mixed flocks with Mallards;
hybridization common in southwestern US.

Habitat
-------
Desert rivers, ponds, and wetlands in New Mexico,
Texas, and Mexico. Prefers rivers with
cottonwood and willow vegetation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Female Mallard nearly identical. Hybridization
makes identification of pure individuals difficult.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Mottled brown plumage throughout. Male has yellower
bill than female Mallard. Best identified
by range and darker overall tone.
