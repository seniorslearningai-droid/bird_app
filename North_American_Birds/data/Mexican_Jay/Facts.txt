Mexican Jay (Aphelocoma wollweberi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium jay, blue-gray above, gray below, no streaking
on throat. No crest. Blue wings
and tail. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly social, lives in permanent flocks of related
birds. Cooperative breeding common.
Loud and conspicuous.

Habitat
-------
Oak and pine-oak woodlands of the Madrean sky
islands in Arizona, New Mexico,
and Texas into Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Woodhouse's Scrub-Jay has white throat streaking.
Steller's Jay has crest and
is darker.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain gray-blue jay with no white throat streaks
and no crest. Sky island
oak woodland specialist.
