Mexican Violetear (Colibri thalassinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Brilliant metallic green body,
iridescent blue-violet ear patch on sides of
head. Blue-green tail band. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Males sing persistently from prominent perches. Feeds
on nectar and small insects. Rare vagrant to
Texas and southern US from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Mountain forests of Mexico and Central America.
Rare visitor to southern Texas; occasionally wanders
north to other US states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-billed Hummingbird. Mexican Violetear's violet
ear patches diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Metallic green plumage with distinctive blue-violet
ear patches on sides of face.
Persistent singing from treetops.
