Mexican Whip-poor-will (Antrostomus arizonae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium nightjar with cryptic brown, black, and gray
patterned plumage. Recently split from Eastern
Whip-poor-will. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal; catches flying insects at night. Roosts
on forest floor by day. Found in mountain
forests of southwest US and Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak and mixed mountain forests of Arizona, New
Mexico, and west Texas. Winters in
Mexico and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Whip-poor-will nearly identical; recently split.
Separated by range in western mountains.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Eastern Whip-poor-will. Range in
southwestern mountain forests key. Call
slightly different from eastern counterpart.
