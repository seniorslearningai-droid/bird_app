Middendorff's Grasshopper Warbler (Locustella ochotensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium grasshopper warbler, brown above with streaked
crown, whitish below, pale supercilium.
Rounded tail. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense grass and scrub. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands.

Habitat
-------
Dense grass, scrub, and sedge meadows in northeastern
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lanceolated Warbler smaller and more heavily streaked.
Pallas's Grasshopper Warbler has
spotted breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger grasshopper warbler with plain white underparts.
Western Alaska vagrant. Skulking
in dense grass or scrub.
