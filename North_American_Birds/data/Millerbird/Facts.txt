Millerbird (Acrocephalus familiaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small reed warbler endemic to Northwestern Hawaiian
Islands. Brown above, buff-white
below. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulking and hard to see. Moves through dense
vegetation searching for insects.
Species was relocated to save it.

Habitat
-------
Dense low vegetation on Nihoa and now Laysan
Islands in the Northwestern Hawaiian
Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar species in its isolated island habitat.
Unique Hawaiian endemic
reed warbler.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Location is definitive: Northwestern Hawaiian Islands.
Small brown warbler, skulking
behavior. No look-alikes present.
