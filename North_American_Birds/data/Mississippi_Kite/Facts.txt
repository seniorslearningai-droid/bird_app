Mississippi Kite (Ictinia mississippiensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant gray raptor with pale head, dark wings, red eyes, and square black tail.

Behavior
--------
Catches and eats large insects in flight. Often soars in groups on thermals.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, riparian areas, and towns in the south-central United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-tailed Kite has white body and tail; Mississippi Kite is uniformly gray.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale gray head, dark gray body, red eye, and square black tail — graceful insect-eating hawk.
