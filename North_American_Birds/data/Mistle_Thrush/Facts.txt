Mistle Thrush (Turdus viscivorus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large thrush, gray-brown above with heavily spotted
white underparts, white underwings.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and fearless, known as stormcock for singing
in harsh weather. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland, parks, and farmland in Europe and
Asia. Very rare vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Song Thrush smaller with buffy wash below. American
Robin has orange-red
breast, not spotted.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, heavily spotted white underparts, gray-brown
above. White underwings.
Very rare European vagrant.
