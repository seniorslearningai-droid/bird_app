Mitred Parakeet (Psittacara mitratus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large green parakeet with variable red patches on
face and head. Long pointed tail,
orange bill. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral population in Los Angeles area.
Loud flocking behavior. Mixes with
other feral parakeets.

Habitat
-------
Established in suburban Southern California, particularly
Los Angeles area.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-masked Parakeet has more extensive red on head.
Mitred has variable scattered red.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large green parakeet with variable red scattered on
head and face. Los Angeles area
feral population.
