Monk Parakeet (Myiopsitta monachus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium green parrot with gray breast and face, long
pointed tail. Builds large stick nests.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Only parrot that builds stick nests. Forms large colonies.
Established in many US cities;
expanding range.

Habitat
-------
Established in cities and suburbs in Florida, Texas,
Illinois, and other states. Lives
year-round outdoors.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other green parakeets lack gray breast. Monk Parakeet's
gray face and breast distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Green with gray breast and face. Builds large communal
stick nests on power poles.
Found in many US cities.
