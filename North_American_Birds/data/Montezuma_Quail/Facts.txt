Montezuma Quail (Cyrtonyx montezumae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Chunky round quail. Male has bold black and white
face pattern, chestnut sides with white spots, blue-gray
breast. Female cryptically brown-streaked. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Probes soil with strong claws for bulbs and tubers.
Freezes when disturbed rather than flushing.
Found in montane pine-oak woodlands.

Habitat
-------
Rocky pine-oak and oak woodlands in mountain
grasslands of southeastern Arizona, New Mexico,
and Texas into Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other quail species in range. Male's harlequin
face pattern unmistakable; female more difficult.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's harlequin black and white face pattern unique.
Round body shape and spotted sides distinctive
even in female.
