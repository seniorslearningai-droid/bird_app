Morelet's Seedeater (Sporophila morelleti)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch. Male black above with white collar, white
below. Female plain brown.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for grass seeds in tall grass. Small population
resident in Rio Grande Valley,
Texas.

Habitat
-------
Tall native grasses along the Rio Grande and adjacent
areas in south Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-collared Seedeater very similar. Black-and-white
pattern on tiny finch
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male tiny black-and-white with white collar. Tall
grass along Rio Grande, Texas.
Resident in specific grass areas.
