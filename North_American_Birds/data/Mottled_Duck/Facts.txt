Mottled Duck (Anas fulvigula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized dabbling duck with mottled brown plumage; buffy face and throat; blue-purple speculum with black borders.

Behavior
--------
Resident non-migratory duck; pairs year-round; dabbles in shallow water for aquatic vegetation and invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Coastal marshes, estuaries, and freshwater wetlands along Gulf Coast from Florida to Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Female Mallard has orange-sided bill and white tail; American Black Duck has darker body and different speculum.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Clean buffy unmarked throat and face; blue speculum with narrow black borders; no white in tail; Gulf Coast.
