Mottled Owl (Ciccaba virgata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium owl, brown mottled plumage, round head without
ear tufts, dark eyes.
About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal forest hunter. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas from Mexico. Calls with
hooting series.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest of Mexico and Central America. Rare
vagrant to southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Barred Owl similar but larger. Mottled Owl more
uniformly brown and mottled.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Medium round-headed owl, mottled brown, dark eyes.
Very rare Texas vagrant from
Mexican tropical forest.
