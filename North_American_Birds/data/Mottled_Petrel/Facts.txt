Mottled Petrel (Pterodroma inexpectata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gadfly petrel with distinctive dark gray belly
patch, white face and underparts,
dark diagonal bar on underwing. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on New Zealand islands; migrates into North
Pacific in winter. Occasional off Pacific
coast.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean in winter. Occasional visitor
to Pacific coast; breeds on New
Zealand islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other gadfly petrels lack dark belly patch. Mottled
Petrel's dark belly unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray belly patch contrasting with white face
and throat. Dark diagonal bar on
underwing. North Pacific visitor.
