Mountain Bluebird (Sialia currucoides)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is brilliant sky-blue overall — no rusty color anywhere. Female is gray with blue tinges.

Behavior
--------
Hovers over meadows to spot insects on the ground. Nests in tree cavities and boxes.

Habitat
-------
Open mountain meadows, sagebrush, and grasslands of western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Bluebird has rusty breast; Western Bluebird has rusty back; Mountain is all blue.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All sky-blue male with no rust — hovers over open mountain meadows.
