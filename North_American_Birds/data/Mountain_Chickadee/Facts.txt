Mountain Chickadee (Poecile gambeli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Chickadee with white supercilium stripe through black cap, gray back, and white cheeks.

Behavior
--------
Caches seeds for winter. Highly cold-tolerant resident of high-elevation forests.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests at middle and upper elevations in western mountains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-capped Chickadee lacks the white stripe through the black cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White stripe through black cap is the key field mark distinguishing it from Black-capped.
