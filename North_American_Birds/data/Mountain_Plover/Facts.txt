Mountain Plover (Charadrius montanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain sandy-brown plover with no wing stripe, thin white supercilium, and black forecrown in breeding.

Behavior
--------
Runs on the ground in dry open areas rather than near water. Very hard to spot.

Habitat
-------
Dry shortgrass prairie and bare ground in the western Great Plains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Killdeer has two breast bands; other plovers have white breast band; Mountain Plover is plain.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain tan plover with no breast bands — drier habitat than all other North American plovers.
