Mountain Quail (Oreortyx pictus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plump quail with long straight head plume, chestnut throat, bold white barring on chestnut flanks.

Behavior
--------
Walks along mountain slopes foraging for seeds and bulbs. Hard to observe.

Habitat
-------
Dense chaparral and mountain brush of the Pacific Coast ranges.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
California Quail has teardrop-shaped plume and lacks chestnut flanks; Mountain is larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long straight head plume, chestnut throat, and bold white-barred flanks.
