Mourning Dove (Zenaida macroura)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender dove with long pointed tail, pinkish body, black spots on wing, and iridescent neck.

Behavior
--------
One of the most abundant birds in North America. Mournful cooing call.

Habitat
-------
Open fields, roadsides, gardens, and woodlands across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-winged Dove has white wing patch; Mourning Dove has long pointed tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long pointed tail and mournful cooing call — most abundant game bird in North America.
