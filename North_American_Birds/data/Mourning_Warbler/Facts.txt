Mourning Warbler (Geothlypis philadelphia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has olive back, yellow underparts, gray hood with complete black bib. Female has pale throat.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense low shrubs. Song a rolling churry-churry-churry.

Habitat
-------
Dense shrubby habitats and forest edges in northern and eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
MacGillivray's Warbler has broken white eye crescents; Mourning has complete hood.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Complete gray hood without eye crescents — western counterpart is MacGillivray's Warbler.
