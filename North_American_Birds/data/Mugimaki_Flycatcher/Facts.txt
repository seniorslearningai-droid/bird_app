Mugimaki Flycatcher (Ficedula mugimaki)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher. Male black above with orange breast
and white wing patch. Female
brown. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from perch for flying insects in forest.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska from East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forest of East Asia and Siberia.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Narcissus Flycatcher more yellow. American Redstart
has white belly. Different
pattern from other Ficedula.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male black above with orange breast and white wing
patch. Very rare Alaska
vagrant from Siberia/Japan.
