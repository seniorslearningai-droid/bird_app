Murphy's Petrel (Pterodroma ultima)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dark gadfly petrel, gray-brown overall, pale
primary bases forming white flash,
pale gray face. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on central South Pacific islands. Migrates
north into North Pacific; regular
rare visitor off California.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific in non-breeding season. Regular but
rare offshore California in
spring migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Providence Petrel larger and darker. Cook's Petrel
smaller and paler.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Medium gray-brown gadfly petrel in North Pacific.
Pale face, white primary flash
from below. Spring migrant off California.
