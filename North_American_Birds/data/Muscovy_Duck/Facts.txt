Muscovy Duck (Cairina moschata)
===========================

Description
-----------
A large, heavy duck with dark iridescent plumage and distinctive red facial caruncles (fleshy knobs). Males much larger than females.

Behavior
--------
Forages for plants, seeds, fish, and insects. Perches readily in trees. Largely silent compared to other ducks; males produce a low hiss.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forests near rivers and lakes in Central and South America. Widely domesticated; feral populations established worldwide.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Domestic Muscovy ducks vary widely in color. Wild birds could be confused with large dark ducks but red facial caruncles are unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Prominent red or red-and-black fleshy caruncles around the face and bill base are diagnostic. Large size and dark iridescent plumage confirm identity.
