Mute Swan (Cygnus olor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Introduced very large white swan with orange bill, black knob at base, and graceful S-curved neck.

Behavior
--------
Holds wings arched over back in defensive posture. Less vocal than native swans.

Habitat
-------
Lakes, ponds, and coastal bays; introduced from Europe to eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tundra Swan has yellow spot at bill base and black bill; Mute Swan has orange bill with black knob.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange bill with black knob and habit of arching wings over back when threatened.
