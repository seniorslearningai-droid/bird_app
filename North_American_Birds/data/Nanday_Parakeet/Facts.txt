Nanday Parakeet (Aratinga nenday)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium green parrot with black head, blue breast band,
red thighs, long pointed tail.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral populations in Florida and California.
Loud and conspicuous; roosts
communally.

Habitat
-------
Established in Florida (St. Petersburg area) and Southern
California in suburban and urban
habitats.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other green parakeets lack black head. Nanday's black
head distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Green with black head. Blue breast wash, red thighs.
Long tail. Feral populations in
Florida and Southern California.
