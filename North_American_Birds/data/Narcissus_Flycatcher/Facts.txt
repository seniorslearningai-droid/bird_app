Narcissus Flycatcher (Ficedula narcissina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher. Male brilliant orange-yellow below
with black above and white wing
patch. Female brown. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from perch for insects. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska and Aleutian Islands
from East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Mixed and deciduous forest of Japan and East Asia.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Redstart has different pattern. No other
bright orange-yellow small flycatcher
in Alaska.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male brilliant orange-yellow with black above, white
wing patch. Rare Alaska vagrant
from Japan.
