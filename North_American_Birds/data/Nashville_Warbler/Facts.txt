Nashville Warbler (Leiothlypis ruficapilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Compact warbler with gray head, white eye ring, yellow underparts, olive back, and hidden rusty crown.

Behavior
--------
Actively gleans insects from shrubs and trees at low to mid levels.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in brushy openings and bogs in northeastern and western forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Connecticut Warbler has complete eye ring and larger size; Nashville has plain gray head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray head, complete white eye ring, and yellow underparts with no streaking.
