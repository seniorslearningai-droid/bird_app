Naumann's Thrush (Turdus naumanni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, brown above with rufous-orange tail
and underparts patterning, white
supercilium. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for berries and invertebrates.
Very rare vagrant to Alaska
and western North America from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland, scrub, and farmland in Siberia and
East Asia. Very rare vagrant
to Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dusky Thrush darker overall. Eyebrowed Thrush
lacks rufous tail. Fieldfare
has gray head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous-orange tail and chest patches on brown thrush.
Very rare Alaska and west
coast vagrant from Asia.
