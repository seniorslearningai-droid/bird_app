Nazca Booby (Sula granti)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large booby, white body with black flight feathers
and tail, orange bill, black face mask.
About 32 inches.

Behavior
--------
Replaced Masked Booby in Galapagos region. Plunge-dives
for fish. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America.

Habitat
-------
Eastern tropical Pacific. Breeds Galapagos Islands;
rare vagrant to Pacific coast of
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Masked Booby nearly identical but has yellow bill.
Nazca Booby's orange bill diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White booby with black flight feathers. Orange bill
distinguishes from Masked Booby's
yellow bill. Rare Pacific vagrant.
