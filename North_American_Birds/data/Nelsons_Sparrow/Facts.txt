Nelson's Sparrow (Ammospiza nelsoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow with orange-buff face and breast, gray nape and cheek, streaked gray and white sides.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in salt marshes and wet grass. Very secretive and hard to flush.

Habitat
-------
Coastal salt marshes and interior wet meadows in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
LeConte's Sparrow is brighter orange; Saltmarsh Sparrow has more contrast; similar habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-buff face, gray cheeks, and blurry streaking on sides in salt marshes.
