Neotropic Cormorant (Nannopterum brasilianum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dark cormorant with long tail, small yellow
throat pouch bordered by white.
About 26 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish in freshwater and coastal areas. Common
in Texas and southwestern US; expanding
northward and eastward.

Habitat
-------
Inland fresh water, coastal marshes and ponds. Common
in Texas, Louisiana, and expanding
north and east.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Double-crested Cormorant larger with orange throat.
Neotropic smaller with white-bordered
yellow throat pouch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size, long tail, white-bordered yellow throat
pouch. Common at Texas freshwater
ponds and coastal areas.
