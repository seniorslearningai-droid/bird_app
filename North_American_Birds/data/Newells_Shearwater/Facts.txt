Newell's Shearwater (Puffinus newelli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shearwater, black above, white below, white
undertail coverts. Similar to Manx but
with white extending up sides. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian mountains. Endangered; declining
from predators and light attraction.
Forages offshore Hawaiian waters.

Habitat
-------
Hawaiian offshore waters. Breeds on high volcanic
slopes of Hawaiian Islands.
Endangered species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Manx Shearwater nearly identical. Newell's has more
white on sides and undertail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black above with white below extending more onto flanks.
Hawaiian species. Nearly identical
to Manx Shearwater.
