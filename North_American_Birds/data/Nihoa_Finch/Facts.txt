Nihoa Finch (Telespiza ultima)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian honeycreeper, yellow head and underparts,
streaked brown back, large
finch bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Omnivorous forager, bold and active. Found only on
tiny Nihoa Island in
Northwestern Hawaiian Islands.

Habitat
-------
Nihoa Island in the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands.
Rocky slopes with low vegetation
and beach strand.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Laysan Finch similar but on different island. No
other large finch on tiny
Nihoa Island.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow finch with large bill on Nihoa Island only.
No other similar bird
present. Bold and inquisitive.
