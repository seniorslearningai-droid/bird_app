Northern Beardless-Tyrannulet (Camptostoma imberbe)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny flycatcher, olive-gray above, whitish below with
two wing bars, small bill, slight
crest. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gleans insects from foliage. Found in riparian and
dry scrub in southern Arizona
and Texas.

Habitat
-------
Riparian thickets and dry thorn scrub in southern
Arizona and southern Texas.
Year-round resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Empidonax flycatchers similar but larger. Beardless-Tyrannulet
tiny with small bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny olive-gray flycatcher with small bill. Two wing
bars. Whistle call. Southern
Arizona and Texas resident.
