Northern Bobwhite (Colinus virginianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small quail with bold black-and-white head pattern on male, mottled brown plumage, and short tail.

Behavior
--------
Lives in coveys that explode upward when flushed. Familiar bob-WHITE whistle call.

Habitat
-------
Brushy fields, grasslands, and open pine forests of eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
California Quail has topknot; other quails lack the bob-white striped head pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black-and-white facial stripes on male and whistled bob-WHITE call.
