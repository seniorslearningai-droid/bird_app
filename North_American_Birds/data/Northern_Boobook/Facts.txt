Northern Boobook (Ninox japonica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium owl, brown with white-spotted plumage, round
head, large yellow eyes. No ear tufts.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migratory owl from East Asia. Rare vagrant to Aleutian
Islands and western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland of East Asia. Rare vagrant to
Aleutian Islands and western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Owl similar but much larger. Northern Boobook
smaller with round head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Medium round-headed owl with yellow eyes. Brown with
white spots. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Asia.
