Northern Cardinal (Cardinalis cardinalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is entirely brilliant red with black mask and prominent crest. Female is brown with red tinges.

Behavior
--------
Year-round resident at bird feeders. Male feeds female during courtship.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges, gardens, and shrubby areas across eastern and central North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pyrrhuloxia has gray body with red tinges and parrot-like bill in the desert southwest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male is entirely red with crest and black mask — unmistakable and beloved backyard bird.
