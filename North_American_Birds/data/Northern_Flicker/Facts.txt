Northern Flicker (Colaptes auratus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large woodpecker with barred brown back, spotted underparts, and yellow or red under wings and tail.

Behavior
--------
Often forages on the ground for ants. Loud wicker-wicker-wicker call.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands and forest edges across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gilded Flicker is all yellow-shafted; Northern has yellow (east) or red (west) under wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown with spotted underparts, red or yellow flash under wings in flight, ground-foraging.
