Northern Fulmar (Fulmarus glacialis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky seabird, gray and white (light morph) or all
dark (dark morph), thick yellow tube-nose
bill, stiff-winged gliding flight. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common North Atlantic and Pacific seabird. Stiff-winged
gliding flight over waves. Follows
fishing boats.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. Breeds on Arctic
cliffs; winters offshore. Common
from Alaska to California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gulls lack tube-nose and have more flexible wing
beats. Fulmars glide stiffly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Stiff-winged gliding flight on flat wings. Thick yellow
tube-nose bill. Gray and white
or all-dark plumage.
