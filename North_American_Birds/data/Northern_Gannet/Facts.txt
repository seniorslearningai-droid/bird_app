Northern Gannet (Morus bassanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large seabird, brilliant white with golden-buff head,
black wingtips, blue-ringed eye, pointed
tail. About 37 inches.

Behavior
--------
Spectacular plunge-diver; dives from height to catch
fish. Common in North Atlantic.
Breeds on rocky Atlantic islands.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean. Breeds on rocky islands; common
offshore Atlantic coast in winter
and migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other large white seabird combines golden head
with black wingtips in North Atlantic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant white with golden head and black wingtips.
Spectacular plunge-diving. Very
common North Atlantic seabird.
