Northern Giant-Petrel (Macronectes halli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large petrel. Dark brown overall, massive
pale pink bill with reddish-brown tip.
About 35 inches.

Behavior
--------
Carrion scavenger and predator. Breeds on subantarctic
islands. Very rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast. Resembles a small
albatross in flight.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Southern Giant-Petrel very similar but bill tip color
differs. Both are very rare in North America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Massive pale bill, huge dark petrel. Hunched posture.
Very rare southern vagrant.
Resembles small dark albatross.
