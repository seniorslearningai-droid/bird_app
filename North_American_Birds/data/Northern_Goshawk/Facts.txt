Northern Goshawk (Accipiter gentilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large accipiter with blue-gray back, finely barred gray underparts, bold white supercilium, and red eye.

Behavior
--------
Aggressive nester that attacks humans near its nest. Hunts birds and mammals in forest.

Habitat
-------
Mature boreal and mountain coniferous forests across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cooper's Hawk is smaller; Sharp-shinned is much smaller; Goshawk is crow-sized.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Crow-sized accipiter with bold white supercilium, finely barred gray underparts, and red eye.
