Northern Harrier (Circus hudsonius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender hawk with long tail, white rump patch, flat facial disc, and low quartering flight.

Behavior
--------
Flies low over marshes and fields tilting side to side listening for prey.

Habitat
-------
Open marshes, fields, and prairie across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other hawk has white rump patch combined with flat facial disc and low quartering flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump patch and low quartering flight over open marshes — facial disc unique among hawks.
