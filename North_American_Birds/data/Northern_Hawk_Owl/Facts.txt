Northern Hawk Owl (Surnia ulula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium owl with long tail, hawk-like posture. Brown
barred below, white-spotted above,
black facial border. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Diurnal hunter perching at treetops. Irruptive visitor
to northern US from boreal forest.
Conspicuous and unafraid of humans.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forest. Irruptive visitor from Canada to northern
tier states and New England.
Perches prominently.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other owls lack hawk-like long tail and diurnal habits.
Northern Hawk Owl's shape unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long tail, hawk-like shape. Perches conspicuously at
treetop. Diurnal. Boreal irruptive
visitor to northern US.
