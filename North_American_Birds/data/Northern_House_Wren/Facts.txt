Northern House Wren (Troglodytes aedon)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small brown wren, plain brown above with barred
wings and tail, pale supercilium.
Very common. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Energetic and vocal, sings a loud bubbling song.
Nests in cavities including nest
boxes. Cocks tail upward.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges, gardens, thickets, and suburbs across
North America. One of commonest
cavity-nesting birds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Winter Wren smaller, darker, shorter tail. Pacific
Wren similar to Winter.
Bewick's Wren has long flat tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain brown wren without bold face markings. Energetic
with cocked tail. Loud
bubbling song. Very common.
