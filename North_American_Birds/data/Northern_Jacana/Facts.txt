Northern Jacana (Jacana spinosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unusual wader. Dark chestnut body, black head and
neck, yellow frontal shield and bill, extremely
long toes. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on floating vegetation using enormously long
toes. Polyandrous; female defends territory with
multiple males. Found in Texas and Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater ponds and marshes with floating vegetation
in southern Texas, Caribbean, and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in range. Long toes and
chestnut coloring unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut body with black head and yellow bill/shield.
Walks on lily pads on enormous
toes. Unmistakable.
