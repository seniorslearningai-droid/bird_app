Northern Lapwing (Vanellus vanellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large plover with long wispy black crest, iridescent
green-black upperparts, white underparts, orange
undertail. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Tumbling display flights in spring. Forages for
earthworms and insects. Rare vagrant to northeastern
North America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Farmland, meadows, and wetland edges in Eurasia.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast of North
America, especially after Atlantic storms.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other lapwing in North America. Crest and
iridescent plumage distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long wispy crest, iridescent green back, and black
and white plumage distinctive. Broad
rounded wings in flight.
