Northern Mockingbird (Mimus polyglottos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray bird with white wing patches, white outer tail feathers, and long tail. Mimics dozens of species.

Behavior
--------
Sings at night during breeding season. Aggressively defends territory year-round.

Habitat
-------
Open areas with shrubs, gardens, and forest edges across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray Catbird is darker gray without white wing patches; Loggerhead Shrike has black mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing patches flashed in display, long tail, and extraordinary mimicry of other birds.
