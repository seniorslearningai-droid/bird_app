Northern Parula (Setophaga americana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny warbler with blue-gray back, yellow-green patch on back, yellow throat and breast with dark breast bands.

Behavior
--------
Hangs upside down from branch tips foraging for insects. Nests in hanging lichens.

Habitat
-------
Mature forests with hanging lichens or Spanish moss in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tropical Parula lacks the dark breast bands and is more uniformly yellow below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny blue-gray warbler with yellow-green back patch and dark breast bands.
