Northern Pintail (Anas acuta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant dabbling duck. Male has chocolate-brown head, white neck stripe, gray body, and long pin tail.

Behavior
--------
Very long-distance migrant. Dabbles in shallow water for seeds and invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, flooded fields, and lakes across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Male's long tail pin and white neck stripe are unmistakable. Female is elegant and slim.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's long tail pin and white neck stripe — most elegant and slender of dabbling ducks.
