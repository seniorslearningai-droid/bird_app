Northern Pygmy-Owl (Glaucidium gnoma)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny owl without ear tufts. Brown spotted plumage,
streaked below, false eyespots on nape.
About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Diurnal; hunts birds and insects. Presence revealed
by scolding mobbing birds. Western
mountain forest resident.

Habitat
-------
Mountain conifer and mixed forest of western North
America. Year-round resident from
Alaska to Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ferruginous Pygmy-Owl found at lower elevations. Northern
Pygmy-Owl is mountain forest species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny spotted owl, active by day. Long tail, false
eyespots on nape. Western mountain
forests.
