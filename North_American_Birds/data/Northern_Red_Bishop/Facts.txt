Northern Red Bishop (Euplectes franciscanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bird. Breeding male brilliant red-orange with
black face, belly, and wings.
Female streaked brown. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Males display from grass stems. Breeds in weedy fields
and marshes. Introduced and
established in California and Texas.

Habitat
-------
Weedy fields, marshes, and grasslands. Established
feral populations in California,
Texas, and Hawaii.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Orange Bishop similar. No other small bird in range
has all-orange and black
combination.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male brilliant orange-red with black belly and face.
Breeding plumage unmistakable.
Established feral in California.
