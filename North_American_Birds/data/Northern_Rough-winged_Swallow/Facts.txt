Northern Rough-winged Swallow (Stelgidopteryx serripennis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain brown-backed swallow with dingy gray-brown throat and white belly. No white rump.

Behavior
--------
Nests in burrows in earthen banks, under bridges, and in pipes. Usually solitary.

Habitat
-------
Near streams, rivers, and lakes with exposed earthen banks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bank Swallow has sharp brown breast band; Rough-winged has graduated dingy throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain brown back, no white rump, and gradually dingy throat shading to white belly.
