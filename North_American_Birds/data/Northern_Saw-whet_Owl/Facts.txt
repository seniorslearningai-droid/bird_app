Northern Saw-whet Owl (Aegolius acadicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny owl with round head, no ear tufts, bright yellow eyes, and streaked brown-and-white underparts.

Behavior
--------
Completely nocturnal. Calls persistently with a repeated whistled note in spring.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Boreal Owl is slightly larger with spotted forehead; Saw-whet has streaked forehead.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny owl — rounded head, yellow eyes, and streaked forehead, not spotted like Boreal.
