Northern Shoveler (Spatula clypeata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Distinctive duck with huge spatula-shaped bill, green head, chestnut sides, and white breast.

Behavior
--------
Swings bill side to side to filter water for crustaceans and seeds.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes and lakes across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mallard has smaller bill; no other duck has such an enormous spatula-shaped bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Giant spatula-shaped bill used to filter-feed — unmistakable identification feature.
