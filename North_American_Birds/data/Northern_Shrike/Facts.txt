Northern Shrike (Lanius borealis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gray-and-white shrike with black mask, hooked bill, and black wings with white patches.

Behavior
--------
Impales prey on thorns and barbed wire. Irrupts south from boreal forest in winter.

Habitat
-------
Open country and forest edges; irruptive winter visitor from boreal regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Loggerhead Shrike is smaller with complete black mask; Northern Shrike has barred breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Larger than Loggerhead, with barred breast and incomplete mask — winter visitor from north.
