Northern Waterthrush (Parkesia noveboracensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Warbler with yellowish supercilium, heavily streaked yellow-buff underparts, and tail-bobbing behavior.

Behavior
--------
Walks along still water edges and flooded forest floors bobbing tail.

Habitat
-------
Still or slow-moving water in boreal forests; breeds in northern areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Louisiana Waterthrush has white supercilium and buffy flanks; Northern has yellowish.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellowish supercilium, streaked yellowish underparts, and preference for still water.
