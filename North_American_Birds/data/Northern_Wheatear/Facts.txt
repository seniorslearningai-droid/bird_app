Northern Wheatear (Oenanthe oenanthe)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small thrush-like bird, gray above, buff-white below,
bold black mask on male, white
rump. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches upright on rocks, bobs and bows. Breeds
in rocky tundra in Alaska and
Canada. Long-distance migrant to Africa.

Habitat
-------
Rocky tundra and open ground in Alaska and northern
Canada. Migrates across oceans
to Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other wheatears rare in North America. No other
bird has white rump combined
with gray-backed pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump conspicuous in flight. Gray above, black
mask. Bobs on rocky
tundra. Arctic breeder.
