Northern Yellow (Yellow) Warbler (Setophaga petechia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, bright yellow throughout, male with
chestnut streaks on breast, yellow
tail spots. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in willows and shrubs. Sings sweet-sweet-sweet
song. Common summer breeder.

Habitat
-------
Willow thickets, streamside shrubs, and woodland
edges across North America. Common
migratory warbler.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wilson's Warbler has black cap. Prothonotary Warbler
has gray wings. Yellow Warbler
has yellow tail spots.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow throughout with chestnut streaks on
breast. Yellow spots in tail.
Willows and streamside shrubs.
