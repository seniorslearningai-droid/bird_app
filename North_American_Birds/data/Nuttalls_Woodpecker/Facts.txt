Nuttall's Woodpecker (Dryobates nuttallii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small woodpecker with barred black-and-white back, white-spotted wings, and red nape on males.

Behavior
--------
Forages on oaks for insects. Nearly identical to Ladder-backed Woodpecker.

Habitat
-------
Oak woodlands and chaparral of California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ladder-backed Woodpecker is nearly identical but found in desert; ranges barely overlap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
California oak woodlands — nearly identical to Ladder-backed but habitat separates them.
