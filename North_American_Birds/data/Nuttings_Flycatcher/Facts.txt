Nutting's Flycatcher (Myiarchus nuttingi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Myiarchus flycatcher, very similar to Ash-throated.
Orange mouth lining and subtle
differences in tail rufous. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Mexican species occasionally recorded in southern
Arizona. Very rare vagrant; best identified
by call.

Habitat
-------
Dry deciduous and thorn forest of Mexico. Very rare
vagrant to southern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ash-throated Flycatcher nearly identical. Best distinguished
by call; orange vs pale mouth lining.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Ash-throated Flycatcher. Orange
mouth lining visible when calling.
Very rare Arizona vagrant.
