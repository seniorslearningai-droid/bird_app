Oahu Akepa (Loxops wolstenholmei)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, male orange-red with
slightly crossed bill tips.
Female olive. Extinct approximately 1900.

Behavior
--------
Used crossed bill to open flower buds for nectar.
Extinct from Oahu due to
deforestation and disease.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu. Extinct.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hawaii Akepa on Big Island. Akekee on Kauai. No
similar birds survive on Oahu.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Orange-red male, crossed bill. Oahu.
No longer exists.
