Oahu Akialoa (Hemignathus ellisianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, very long decurved bill,
yellow-olive body. Extinct
since approximately 1837.

Behavior
--------
Probed bark for insects and flowers for nectar.
Among earliest Hawaiian birds
documented after Western contact.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu. Extinct due to early
deforestation after Western
contact.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other akialoa on different islands. No similar long-billed
birds survive on Oahu.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Long decurved bill, yellow-olive. Oahu.
One of earliest recorded extinctions
in Hawaii.
