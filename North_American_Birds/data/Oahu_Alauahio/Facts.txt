Oahu Alauahio (Paroreomyza maculata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian creeper. Male yellow with greenish
back. Female olive-green above,
yellowish below. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Crept along branches searching bark for insects.
Extinct since approximately 1985.
Last confirmed records from Oahu.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu. Extinct due to deforestation,
introduced predators, and avian
diseases.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oahu Amakihi similar but has curved bill. Other
alauahio species on different
Hawaiian islands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct creeper from Oahu. Male yellow with green
back. No longer exists in
native forest.
