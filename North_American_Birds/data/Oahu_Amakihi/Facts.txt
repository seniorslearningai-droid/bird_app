Oahu Amakihi (Chlorodrepanis flava)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, bright yellow-green below,
olive-green above, curved bill.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager for nectar and insects. Shows some
resistance to avian malaria.
Oahu's most common native forest bird.

Habitat
-------
Native and suburban forests of Oahu. Tolerates lower
elevations and some introduced
tree species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Warbling White-eye has white eye ring. Hawaii Amakihi
on different island. Brighter
yellow than other amakihi.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow-green underparts, curved bill on Oahu.
Often in native and suburban
trees. Common endemic.
