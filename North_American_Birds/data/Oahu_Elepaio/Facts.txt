Oahu Elepaio (Chasiempis ibidis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small monarch flycatcher endemic to Oahu. Rusty-brown
above, white rump, whitish below.
Cocks tail upward. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and inquisitive, will approach humans closely.
Forages actively for insects.
Important species in Hawaiian forest.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Koolau and Waianae mountain
ranges on Oahu. Endangered due
to habitat loss and introduced predators.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kauai and Hawaii Elepaio similar but island-specific.
No other small flycatcher in
Oahu native forest habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Upward-cocked tail, rusty-brown tones on Oahu.
Distinctive white rump patch.
Endemic and endangered forest bird.
