Oahu Nukupuu (Hemignathus lucidus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, long strongly decurved
bill, yellow male. Extinct since
approximately 1860.

Behavior
--------
Used long bill to probe bark for insects and nectar.
Extinct shortly after Western
contact with Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu. Extinct due to early deforestation
and introduced species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Oahu Amakihi smaller with shorter bill. No similar
birds survive on Oahu.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct. Long decurved bill, yellow male. From Oahu.
No longer exists.
