Oahu Oo (Moho apicalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small black Hawaiian honeyeater with yellow shoulder
patches and undertail coverts.
Long bill. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for nectar in native Hawaiian forest.
Extinct since approximately 1837
due to early human impacts.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Oahu. Extinct due to habitat
loss following Polynesian and
later Western settlement.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Oo species similar but island-specific. All
Hawaiian Oo now extinct.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species from Oahu. Black with yellow
shoulder and undertail patches.
No longer exists.
