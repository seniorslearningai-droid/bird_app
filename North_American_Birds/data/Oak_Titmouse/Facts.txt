Oak Titmouse (Baeolophus inornatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain gray titmouse with small crest and no distinctive markings. Short dark bill.

Behavior
--------
Forages actively in oaks. California counterpart to Juniper Titmouse in the Great Basin.

Habitat
-------
Oak woodlands of California and the Pacific slope.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Juniper Titmouse is nearly identical but found in juniper woodland; ranges barely overlap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain gray with crest — nearly identical to Juniper Titmouse but in California oak woodland.
