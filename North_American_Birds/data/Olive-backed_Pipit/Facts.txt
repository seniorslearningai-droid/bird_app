Olive-backed Pipit (Anthus hodgsoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pipit, olive-green back with little streaking,
white underparts with dark streaks,
bold supercilium. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in forest and woodland edges.
Regular vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands.

Habitat
-------
Forests and forest edges of Siberia and East Asia.
Regular vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pechora Pipit more heavily streaked above. Tree Pipit
less olive above. Red-throated
has brighter pink throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-green back with little streaking. Bold supercilium.
Regular Alaska vagrant in
fall migration.
