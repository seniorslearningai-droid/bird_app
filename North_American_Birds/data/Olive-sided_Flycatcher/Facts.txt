Olive-sided Flycatcher (Contopus cooperi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large flycatcher with dark olive vest on sides, white central belly stripe, and white tufts on rump.

Behavior
--------
Perches at treetops singing loud quick-THREE-beers. Long-distance migrant.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests and burned areas with tall snags across western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wood-Pewees lack the dark vest; Olive-sided has distinctive vest and rump tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark olive vest framing white belly, white rump tufts, and quick-THREE-beers song.
