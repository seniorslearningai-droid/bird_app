Olive Sparrow (Arremonops rufivirgatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, olive-green above, pale below, bold
brown crown stripes, no wing
bars. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground under dense brush by scratching
with both feet. Secretive.
Resident in south Texas.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub and brushy woodland of south
Texas and eastern Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Green-tailed Towhee has rufous crown. Lincoln's Sparrow
streaked below. Olive color
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-green above, plain below, bold crown stripes.
South Texas thorn scrub.
Ground-scratching forager.
