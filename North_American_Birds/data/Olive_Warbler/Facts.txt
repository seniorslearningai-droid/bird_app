Olive Warbler (Peucedramus taeniatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium wood warbler. Male has orange head with black
mask, olive-gray back, white
wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages high in pine canopy for insects. Mountain
specialist in Arizona and New
Mexico pine forests.

Habitat
-------
High-elevation pine and mixed pine-oak forests of
Arizona, New Mexico, and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hermit Warbler has yellow head but no black mask.
Virginia's Warbler lacks orange
and mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange head with black mask unique. White wing bars.
High-elevation pine forest.
Arizona and New Mexico.
