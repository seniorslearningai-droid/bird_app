Olomao (Myadestes lanaiensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian thrush, brown above with gray
underparts. Island endemic of
Lanai and Molokai. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for fruit in native forest. Very few
if any individuals likely remain.
Song melodious.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of Lanai and Molokai. Lanai population
extinct; Molokai population
possibly persists.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Omao similar but on Big Island. Kamao similar
but Kauai only.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Possibly extinct Hawaiian thrush. Brown above, gray
below. Lanai and Molokai
native forest habitat.
