Omao (Myadestes obscurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian thrush, dark brown above, gray
below, white eye ring. Upright
posture. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for fruit and insects in native forest.
The most common surviving Hawaiian
thrush. Melodious song.

Habitat
-------
Native forests of the Big Island of Hawaii.
Found primarily above 4,000 feet
in ohia and koa forest.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Puaiohi smaller with pink legs. Other Hawaiian
thrushes on different islands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eye ring on dark brown thrush. Big Island
of Hawaii native forest.
Most common surviving Hawaiian thrush.
