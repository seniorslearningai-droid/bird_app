Orange-billed Nightingale-Thrush (Catharus aurantiirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small thrush, gray-brown above, gray below with
orange bill and orange eye ring.
About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in dense forest. Melodious song.
Rare vagrant to extreme southern
Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense humid forest understory from Mexico to South
America. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Russet Nightingale-Thrush more russet. Spotted thrushes
have spotted breasts.
Orange bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange bill and orange eye ring on gray-brown
thrush. Rare Texas vagrant from
Mexico. Melodious song.
