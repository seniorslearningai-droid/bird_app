Orange-cheeked Waxbill (Estrilda melpoda)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch, brown above, gray below, bright orange
cheek patches, red bill.
About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious in small flocks. Forages for small
seeds. Introduced to Puerto Rico
and possibly Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands and scrub. Introduced to Puerto Rico.
Native to West Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Waxbill has red face mask. Lavender Waxbill
gray-lavender. Orange cheeks
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright orange cheek patches distinctive. Gray below.
Red bill. Tiny size. Puerto
Rico introduced.
