Orange-crowned Warbler (Leiothlypis celata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Dull olive-green warbler with obscure streaking, yellow undertail, and rarely seen orange crown patch.

Behavior
--------
One of the earliest spring migrants. Forages low in brushy vegetation.

Habitat
-------
Dense brush, willows, and chaparral across western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nashville Warbler has white eye ring and gray head; Orange-crowned is plain dull olive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dull olive with no wing bars, no eye ring, and faint streaking — often identified by elimination.
