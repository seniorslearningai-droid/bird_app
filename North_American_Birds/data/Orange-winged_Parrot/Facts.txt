Orange-winged Parrot (Amazona amazonica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large parrot, green with blue and yellow forehead
stripes, orange wing patch in
flight. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious in large flocks. Very noisy. Small feral
populations in Florida.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest and woodland of South America. Feral
populations in Florida. Native
to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other Amazon parrots lack orange wing patch. Wing
patch visible only in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange wing patch visible in flight distinctive. Blue
and yellow forehead stripes.
Green parrot. Florida feral.
