Orchard Oriole (Icterus spurius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest oriole. Male has chestnut and black body; female is greenish-yellow with white wing bars.

Behavior
--------
Builds hanging nest in orchards and open woodlands. Eats nectar, insects, and fruit.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, orchards, and suburban areas of eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Baltimore Oriole male is bright orange; Orchard Oriole male is darker chestnut.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Chestnut and black male — darkest of the North American orioles.
