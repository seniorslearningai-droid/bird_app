Oriental Cuckoo (Cuculus optatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium slender cuckoo. Gray above, barred below,
yellow orbital ring. Nearly identical to Common
Cuckoo. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Brood parasite like Common Cuckoo. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Siberia and East Asia.
Best identified by call.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberian and East Asian forests. Winters
in Southeast Asia and Australia. Rare vagrant
to western Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Cuckoo nearly identical. Field separation
requires careful attention to call and
slight size differences.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Common Cuckoo; best separated
by call (lower, less clear "cuck-oo").
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.
