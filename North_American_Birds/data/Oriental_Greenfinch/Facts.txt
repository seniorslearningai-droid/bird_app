Oriental Greenfinch (Chloris sinica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch, olive-green with yellow-green in wings,
yellow-green rump, short pale
bill. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds in trees and on ground. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutian Islands from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Woodland, scrub, and gardens in East Asia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eurasian Siskin has black crown and streaking. Pine
Siskin heavily streaked. Yellow-green
color distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive-green with yellow-green wing flashes. Short
pale bill. Rare Alaska vagrant
from Japan and Siberia.
