Oriental Honey-buzzard (Pernis ptilorhynchus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hawk, long tail, small pigeon-like head, variable
brown plumage. Long neck and small
head distinctive. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Raids wasp and bee nests for larvae. Variable plumage
with many color morphs. Rare vagrant
to Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland of East and Southeast Asia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-winged and other buteos similar. Oriental Honey-
buzzard's small head and long neck distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small pigeon-like head with long neck. Variable brown
plumage. Rare Alaska vagrant
from East Asia.
