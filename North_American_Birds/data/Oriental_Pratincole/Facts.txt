Oriental Pratincole (Glareola maldivarum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Swallow-like wader. Brown above, buff throat outlined
in black, red bill base, deeply forked tail,
rusty underwings. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Catches insects in flight like a swallow but walks
on ground too. Rare vagrant to Pacific coast
from Asian breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Asian grasslands and fields. Winters in
Africa and Australia. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in range. Swallow-like flight and
forked tail distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Swallow-like flight with forked tail. Brown above,
buff throat with black border. Rusty
underwing in flight.
