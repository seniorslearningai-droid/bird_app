Oriental Scops-Owl (Otus sunia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small scops owl with ear tufts. Brown streaked plumage,
yellow eyes. Similar to other
scops owls. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal insect hunter. Migratory; rare vagrant to
western Alaska from East Asia.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland of East and South Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Screech-Owl similar. Oriental Scops-Owl found
only in western Alaska as vagrant.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small ear-tufted owl in western Alaska. Brown streaked
plumage, yellow eyes. Very rare
vagrant from Asia.
