Oriental Turtle-Dove (Streptopelia orientalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dove. Reddish-brown upperparts with dark scaly
pattern, pinkish-gray underparts, and black and
white striped neck patch. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds and berries. Migrates
long distances through East Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in forests and woodland of East Asia and
Siberia. Winters in South and Southeast Asia.
Rare vagrant to Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
European Turtle-Dove similar. Oriental larger with
more heavily scaled upperparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Scaly reddish-brown upperparts with striped neck patch.
Larger and darker than European Turtle-Dove.
Rare vagrant in western Alaska.
