Osprey (Pandion haliaetus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large raptor with white underparts, dark brown back, dark eye stripe, and reversible outer toe for gripping fish.

Behavior
--------
Dives feet-first into water to catch fish. Often hovers before diving.

Habitat
-------
Near water — lakes, rivers, coasts, and reservoirs across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bald Eagle is much larger and all dark; Osprey has distinctive white belly and dark wrist patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White belly, dark wrist patches, and feet-first dives into water for fish.
