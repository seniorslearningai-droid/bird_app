Ou (Psittirostra psittacea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian honeycreeper, male yellow-green head
and breast, olive-green back,
hooked parrot-like bill. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on fruit, seeds, and buds. Possibly extinct;
no confirmed records since
the 1980s.

Habitat
-------
Native forests throughout Hawaiian Islands. Possibly
extinct due to avian disease
and introduced species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Palila has yellow head and heavy bill. No other
bird in Hawaii has
a hooked parrot-like bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Hooked parrot-like bill distinctive. Yellow-green head,
olive back. Possibly extinct.
No confirmed modern records.
