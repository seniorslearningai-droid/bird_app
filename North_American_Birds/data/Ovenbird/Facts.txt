Ovenbird (Seiurus aurocapilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Warbler with orange crown stripe bordered by black, olive back, heavily streaked white underparts.

Behavior
--------
Walks on the forest floor. Loud tea-CHER tea-CHER song. Builds domed oven-shaped nest.

Habitat
-------
Mature deciduous forests with open understory in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Waterthrushes bob tail; Ovenbird walks slowly with tail cocked. Orange crown is unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-striped crown, walks on forest floor, and loud ringing tea-CHER song.
