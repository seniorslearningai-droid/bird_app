Pacific-slope Flycatcher (Empidonax difficilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Olive-green Empidonax with teardrop-shaped eye ring, yellow-green wash on underparts, and almond eye ring.

Behavior
--------
Gives distinctive upslurred pseet call. Breeds in moist coniferous forests.

Habitat
-------
Moist forests and canyons of the Pacific Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cordilleran Flycatcher is nearly identical but found inland; best separated by call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Almond-shaped eye ring, yellow-green wash, and upslurred pseet call in Pacific Coast forests.
