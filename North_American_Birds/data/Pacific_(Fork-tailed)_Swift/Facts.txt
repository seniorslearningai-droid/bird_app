Pacific (Fork-tailed) Swift (Apus pacificus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large swift. Dark sooty brown with distinct white
rump patch and deeply forked tail. White throat
patch. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aerial insect feeder. Forms large flocks. Migrates
long distances. Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Pacific coast from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds across Siberia and East Asia. Migrates to
Australia. Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Swift lacks forked tail and white rump.
Fork-tailed Swift's white rump distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive white rump patch and deeply forked tail
unique. Dark body and white throat.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.
