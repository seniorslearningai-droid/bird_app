Pacific Golden-Plover (Pluvialis fulva)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium plover. Golden-speckled brown upperparts, black
face and underparts in breeding plumage. Smaller
and slimmer than American Golden-Plover. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on beaches, mudflats, and grasslands for
invertebrates. Migrates between Arctic Alaska and
Pacific islands to wintering areas.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra of Alaska and Siberia.
Winters on Pacific islands, Hawaii, and
Southeast Asian coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Golden-Plover similar; Pacific is smaller
and slightly yellower overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smaller and slimmer than American Golden-Plover.
Pacific migrants through Hawaii distinctive in
grassland habitat.
