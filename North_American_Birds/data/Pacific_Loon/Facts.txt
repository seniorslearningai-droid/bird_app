Pacific Loon (Gavia pacifica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium loon with gray nape, black throat patch, and white-checkered back in breeding plumage.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in long lines over the Pacific coast. Dives for fish.

Habitat
-------
Pacific Ocean and large lakes; breeds on Arctic lakes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Loon is larger with heavier bill; Red-throated has upturned bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray nape and black throat with fine white striping in breeding — Common Loon has heavier bill.
