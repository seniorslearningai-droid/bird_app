Pacific Wren (Troglodytes pacificus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny dark brown wren, heavily barred throughout,
very short stubby tail, pale
buff supercilium. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Creeps through dense forest undergrowth. Extraordinarily
loud song for its tiny size.
Typically keeps very low.

Habitat
-------
Dense moist forests of Pacific Coast, primarily
in old-growth coniferous forest.
Prefers fallen logs and root tangles.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Winter Wren nearly identical but different range.
House Wren larger, less
barred, longer tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny dark wren with very short tail. Heavy barring
throughout. Pacific Coast
old-growth forest habitat.
