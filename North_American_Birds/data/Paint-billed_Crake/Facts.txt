Paint-billed Crake (Mustelirallus erythrops)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small secretive rail. Brown and gray plumage, short
bright red and yellow bill. Gray face, brownish
back. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Very secretive; rarely seen in dense marsh grass.
Rare vagrant to North America from
South America.

Habitat
-------
Dense freshwater marshes and wet grasslands in South
America. Very rare vagrant to southern US,
mainly Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-breasted Crake similar. Paint-billed has colorful
red and yellow bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Short red and yellow bill diagnostic. Secretive brown
and gray plumage. Dense wet
marsh habitat.
