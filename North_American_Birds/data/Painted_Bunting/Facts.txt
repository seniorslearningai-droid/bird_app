Painted Bunting (Passerina ciris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is the most colorful North American bird: blue head, red underparts, and green back.

Behavior
--------
Males are highly territorial. Often secretive despite brilliant colors.

Habitat
-------
Brushy areas and forest edges; breeds in south-central and southeastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American bird has the combination of blue, red, and green.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's combination of blue head, red body, and green back — most colorful NA bird.
