Painted Redstart (Myioborus pictus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Striking black warbler with red breast, white wing patch, and white outer tail feathers.

Behavior
--------
Fans tail and droops wings to flush insects. Active and acrobatic forager.

Habitat
-------
Montane pine-oak forests of southeastern Arizona and New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Redstart is orange-and-black; Painted Redstart is red-and-black with white patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black warbler with brilliant red breast and white wing and tail patches.
