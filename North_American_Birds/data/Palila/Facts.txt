Palila (Loxioides bailleui)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium Hawaiian finch, yellow head and breast, gray
back, white belly, heavy
seed-cracking bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds almost exclusively on unripe mamane seeds.
Critically endangered. Found only
on high slopes of Mauna Kea.

Habitat
-------
Subalpine mamane-naio woodland on Mauna Kea, Hawaii.
Range restricted to small area
above 6,500 feet.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow head and heavy bill distinctive. Hawaii Amakihi
smaller with curved bill.
No similar finch in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow head and breast with heavy finch bill. Mauna
Kea mamane forest only.
Critically endangered endemic.
