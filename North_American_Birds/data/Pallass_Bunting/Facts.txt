Pallas's Bunting (Emberiza pallasi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bunting. Male has black head and throat, white
collar, brown back, white
outer tail feathers. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in open areas and wet meadows.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows and marshy areas of Siberia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Reed Bunting similar but larger with different bill.
Lapland Longspur has chestnut
nape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small bunting with black head, white collar. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
Pale lesser wing coverts.
