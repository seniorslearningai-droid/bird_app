Pallas's Grasshopper Warbler (Locustella certhiola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, heavily streaked brown above, whitish
below with spotted breast band.
Rounded tail. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense grass and sedge. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska. Creeps
mouse-like through vegetation.

Habitat
-------
Wet grasslands, sedge meadows, and marshes in
Asia. Rare vagrant to Aleutian
and Pribilof Islands, Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lanceolated Warbler similar but smaller and more
heavily streaked below. Grasshopper
Warbler has unspotted rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked above with spotted breast, rufous-tinged rump.
Western Alaska vagrant. Skulks
in dense wet vegetation.
