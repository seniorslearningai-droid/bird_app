Pallas's Gull (Ichthyaetus ichthyaetus)

Description
-----------
Very large gull. Black hood, heavy yellow bill with
red and black markings, pale gray back, large
size. About 27 inches.

Behavior
--------
Largest hooded gull. Feeds on fish and other water
life. Very rare vagrant to western Alaska and
Pacific coast from Central Asia.

Habitat
-------
Central Asian lakes and Caspian Sea region. Winters
on Indian Ocean coast. Very rare vagrant
to western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Black-backed Gull similar size. Pallas's has
black hood and different bill pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large size with black hood. Heavy yellow bill
with red and black marks. Rare
vagrant from Central Asia.
