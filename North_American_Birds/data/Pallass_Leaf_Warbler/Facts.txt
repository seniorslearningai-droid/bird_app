Pallas's Leaf Warbler (Phylloscopus proregulus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny leaf warbler, green above with yellow crown
stripe, two yellow wing bars,
yellow rump. About 3.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely active, hovers to pick insects from leaves.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.
Often hangs upside-down like tit.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests in Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-browed Warbler lacks crown stripe and rump
patch. Goldcrest has orange
crown but different shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow crown stripe, yellow rump, and two yellow
wing bars. Tiny size. Very
active hovering forager.
