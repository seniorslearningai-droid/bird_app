Pallas's Rosefinch (Carpodacus roseus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch. Male pale pink with white wing bars,
white spots on wing coverts.
Female streaked brown. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds and berries in forest and scrub.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Forests and scrub of Siberia and East Asia. Very
rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Rosefinch less pale, more rosy-red. Long-tailed
Rosefinch has very long tail.
Other rosefinches rare in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale pink male with white wing spots. Larger than
Common Rosefinch. Very rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
