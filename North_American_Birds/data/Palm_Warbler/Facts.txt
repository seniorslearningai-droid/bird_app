Palm Warbler (Setophaga palmarum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brown-and-yellow warbler with rufous cap, yellow undertail, and constant tail-bobbing habit.

Behavior
--------
Constantly bobs tail while walking on ground. One of earliest fall migrants.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in boreal bogs; winters in southeastern states near beaches and palms.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Prairie Warbler bobs tail but has facial markings; Palm Warbler has rufous cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous cap, yellow undertail, and incessant tail-bobbing on the ground.
