Parakeet Auklet (Aethia psittacula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium auklet. Black above, white below, distinctive
round orange bill, white eye plume. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for jellyfish and copepods. Breeds on Alaskan
and Siberian cliff islands. Winters in North
Pacific offshore.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on rocky islands in Bering Sea and Gulf
of Alaska. Winters offshore in North Pacific.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other auklets. Parakeet Auklet's round orange bill
and white eye plume distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Round orange bill unique among auklets. White eye
plume. Black above, white below.
Aleutian Islands species.
