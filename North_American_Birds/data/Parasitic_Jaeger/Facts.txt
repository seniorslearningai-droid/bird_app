Parasitic Jaeger (Stercorarius parasiticus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium jaeger. Dark and light morphs. Pointed central
tail projection in breeding. Slender and falcon-
like. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aggressively pursues terns and gulls to steal food.
Most commonly seen jaeger from shore. Regular
migrant on both coasts.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Pelagic outside breeding.
Regular migrant on both coasts; most
often seen from shore.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pomarine Jaeger larger with different tail projection.
Long-tailed Jaeger slimmer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Medium size and pointed (not blunt) tail projection.
Most commonly seen jaeger from
coastal promontories.
