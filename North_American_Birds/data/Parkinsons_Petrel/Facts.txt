Parkinson's Petrel (Procellaria parkinsoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large black petrel with pale ivory-yellow bill with
dark culmen. All black otherwise.
About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Great Barrier Island, New Zealand. Migrates
into North Pacific; rare visitor off
California and Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Eastern North Pacific in non-breeding season. Rare
visitor off California; breeds on
New Zealand islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-chinned Petrel similar but may show white chin.
Parkinson's all black with pale bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black body with pale bill with dark culmen. Large
petrel in eastern North Pacific.
New Zealand breeder, Pacific migrant.
