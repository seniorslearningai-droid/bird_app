Passenger Pigeon (Ectopistes migratorius)
===========================

Description
-----------
EXTINCT North American pigeon. Male blue-gray above,
reddish below, long pointed tail. Female duller.
Billions once existed. Last died September 1, 1914.

Behavior
--------
Formed enormous flocks that darkened the sky for
hours. Nested in massive colonies. Hunted to
extinction by 1914 due to commercial hunting.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests of eastern North America, particularly
beech and oak forests. Migratory;
moved with food sources. Now extinct.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mourning Dove similar in shape; smaller and
lighter in color.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Only known from museum specimens and historical
photos of last captive bird "Martha" (1914).
Extinct since September 1, 1914.
