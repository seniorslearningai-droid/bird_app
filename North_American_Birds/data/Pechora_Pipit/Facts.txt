Pechora Pipit (Anthus gustavi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pipit, heavily streaked brown above with white
stripes on back, buff-white
below with dark streaks. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense grass, difficult to see. Short
flight when flushed. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows and scrub in Siberia. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Olive-backed Pipit more olive above. Red-throated
Pipit has reddish throat. Two
white stripes on back helpful.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Two white stripes visible on back. Heavily streaked
above. Skulks in grass.
Rare Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
