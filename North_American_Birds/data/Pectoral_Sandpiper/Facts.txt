Pectoral Sandpiper (Calidris melanotos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper with abrupt contrast between heavily streaked brown breast and white belly.

Behavior
--------
Often found in grassy or flooded fields. Long-distance migrant from Arctic to South America.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows, marshes, and mudflats during migration across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-rumped Sandpiper has white rump; Pectoral has sharp breast-belly contrast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sharply demarcated streaked breast and white belly — like wearing a bib.
