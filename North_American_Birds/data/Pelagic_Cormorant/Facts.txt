Pelagic Cormorant (Urile pelagicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small cormorant with iridescent green-and-purple plumage, slender neck, thin bill, and red facial skin.

Behavior
--------
Nests on narrow cliff ledges. Dives for fish in rocky inshore waters.

Habitat
-------
Rocky Pacific coast from Alaska to California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brandt's Cormorant is larger with blue throat pouch; Pelagic is slender with red face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest Pacific cormorant — iridescent plumage, slender neck, and thin bill on rocky cliffs.
