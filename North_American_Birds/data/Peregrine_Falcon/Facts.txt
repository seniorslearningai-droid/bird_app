Peregrine Falcon (Falco peregrinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Powerful falcon with slate-blue back, dark hood with thick mustache stripe, and barred underparts.

Behavior
--------
Stoops at 200+ mph to strike birds in flight — fastest animal on Earth.

Habitat
-------
Coastal cliffs, skyscrapers, and open country across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Prairie Falcon is sandy-brown with black axillaries; Peregrine is slate-blue with dark hood.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slate-blue back, heavy mustache stripe, and fastest stoop of any animal.
