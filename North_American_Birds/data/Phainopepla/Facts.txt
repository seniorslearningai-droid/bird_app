Phainopepla (Phainopepla nitens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Silky flycatcher. Male is glossy black with red eyes and white wing patches; female is gray.

Behavior
--------
Eats mistletoe berries and disperses seeds. Flies with moth-like wing flicks.

Habitat
-------
Desert mistletoe groves, riparian areas, and chaparral of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird has the male's glossy black plumage with white wing patches and red eyes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Glossy black male with red eyes and white wing patches — eats mistletoe berries.
