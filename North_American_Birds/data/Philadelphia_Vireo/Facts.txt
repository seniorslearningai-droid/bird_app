Philadelphia Vireo (Vireo philadelphicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small vireo with pale yellow underparts, gray cap, dark eye line, and whitish supercilium.

Behavior
--------
Often found high in trees with other vireos and warblers during migration.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in young deciduous and mixed forests; migrates through eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Warbling Vireo is paler with no yellow; Red-eyed Vireo is larger with gray cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow wash on underparts and dark eye line — plainer than other vireos.
