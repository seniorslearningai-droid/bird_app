Pied-billed Grebe (Podilymbus podiceps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky grebe with chicken-like bill banded with black in breeding, brown plumage, and white rump.

Behavior
--------
Can adjust buoyancy to sink slowly out of sight rather than diving. Rarely flies.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes and ponds across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other grebes have pointed bills; Pied-billed has thick chicken-like bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Stout chicken-like bill with black band in breeding — sinks slowly rather than diving.
