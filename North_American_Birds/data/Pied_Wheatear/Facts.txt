Pied Wheatear (Oenanthe pleschanka)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat. Male black throat and back, white crown
and rump, white below. Female
brown. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches upright on rocks and boulders. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska from
Siberia and Central Asia.

Habitat
-------
Open rocky and semi-arid country of Siberia and
Central Asia. Very rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Wheatear gray above without black back.
No similar black-backed chat
in Alaska range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male black throat and back with white crown and
rump. Very rare Alaska vagrant
from Siberia.
