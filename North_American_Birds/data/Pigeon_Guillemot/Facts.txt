Pigeon Guillemot (Cepphus columba)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium alcid. Breeding adult black with white wing
patch bisected by black bar, red mouth and legs.
Winter mostly white. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish and invertebrates near shore. Less
pelagic than other alcids. Found year-round along
rocky Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Rocky Pacific coastlines from Alaska to California.
Nests in rock crevices and sea caves. Year-round
resident along Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Guillemot on Atlantic coast is nearly identical.
Pigeon Guillemot has wing patch bisected by bar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing patch with black wedge through middle
on otherwise black breeding bird. Red
legs and mouth.
