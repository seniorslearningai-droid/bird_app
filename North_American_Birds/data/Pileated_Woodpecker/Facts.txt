Pileated Woodpecker (Dryocopus pileatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Crow-sized woodpecker with brilliant red crest, black body, white wing patches, and white stripes on neck.

Behavior
--------
Excavates large rectangular holes in dead trees for carpenter ants. Loud wuk-wuk-wuk call.

Habitat
-------
Mature forests with large trees across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other woodpecker approaches its large size and brilliant red crest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest North American woodpecker — crow-sized with spectacular red crest.
