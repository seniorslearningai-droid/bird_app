Pin-tailed Snipe (Gallinago stenura)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium snipe. Brown cryptically patterned plumage
with narrow outer tail feathers (pin-like). Bill
moderately long. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive; rises with slow wingbeats when flushed.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska from Asian
breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Asian boreal and subarctic wetlands. Winters
in South Asia. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Snipe nearly identical. Pin-tailed Snipe has
narrow pin-like outer tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Narrow outer tail feathers diagnostic but very hard
to see in field. Slow wingbeats
when flushed.
