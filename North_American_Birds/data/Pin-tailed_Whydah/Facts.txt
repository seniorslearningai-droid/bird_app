Pin-tailed Whydah (Vidua macroura)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bird. Breeding male black-and-white with extraordinary
long black pin-tail. Female
streaked brown. About 5 inches (plus 8-inch tail).

Behavior
--------
Brood parasite of waxbills. Males display with pin-tail
in flight. Established in
California.

Habitat
-------
Open areas and gardens. Established feral population
in California. Native to
sub-Saharan Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird has black-and-white pattern plus extremely
long pin-tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male extraordinary long black tail, black-and-white
body. Unmistakable. California
feral population.
