Pine Bunting (Emberiza leucocephalos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male has white head with brown
streaking, chestnut breast, chestnut
back. Female streaked brown. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds. Very rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland, scrub, and farmland of Siberia and
Central Asia. Very rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-breasted Bunting has yellow below. Yellowhammer
has yellow head. No similar
white-headed bunting in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male white head and chestnut breast. Very rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
Female streaked brown.
