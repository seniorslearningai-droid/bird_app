Pine Flycatcher (Empidonax affinis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Empidonax flycatcher, olive-green above, pale
below with two wing bars. Very similar
to other Empidonax. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Very rare vagrant from Mexico. Best identified by
call which differs from similar species.

Habitat
-------
Pine forest of Mexico and Central America. Very rare
vagrant to southern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cordilleran and other Empidonax nearly identical.
Best identified by voice.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to other Empidonax flycatchers. Best
identified by call. Very rare
vagrant from Mexico.
