Pine Grosbeak (Pinicola enucleator)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large finch with rosy-red plumage on male, two white wing bars, and large finch bill. Female is olive-gray.

Behavior
--------
Irruptive migrant that moves south when northern food crops fail. Very tame.

Habitat
-------
Boreal coniferous forests; irrupts to northern states in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-winged Crossbill has crossed bill; Purple Finch is smaller; Pine Grosbeak is largest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest North American finch — rosy-red male with white wing bars and plump body.
