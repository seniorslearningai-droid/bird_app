Pine Siskin (Spinus pinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small streaked finch with yellow in wings and at base of tail, pointed bill, and heavily streaked body.

Behavior
--------
Irruptive nomad that follows conifer seed crops. Aggressive at feeders.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests and weedy fields across North America; highly irruptive.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Goldfinch is brighter yellow with less streaking; Pine Siskin is heavily streaked.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Heavily streaked with yellow wing and tail flashes — often visits feeders in irruption years.
