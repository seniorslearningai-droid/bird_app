Pine Warbler (Setophaga pinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Yellow-olive warbler with unstreaked back, white wing bars, and yellow breast with faint streaking.

Behavior
--------
Year-round resident in pine forests. Probes pine bark and eats pine seeds in winter.

Habitat
-------
Pine forests of eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Prairie and Palm Warblers have distinctive marks; Pine Warbler is plain with unstreaked back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain olive-yellow with unstreaked back, white wing bars, and pine forest year-round residency.
