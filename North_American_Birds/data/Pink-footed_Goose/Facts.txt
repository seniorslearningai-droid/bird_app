Pink-footed Goose (Anser brachyrhynchus)
===========================

Description
-----------
A medium-sized goose with a short pink-and-black bill, distinctive pink legs and feet, and a rounded brown head with dark neck.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on crops, grass, and root vegetables. Highly gregarious, forming enormous flocks. Known for spectacular mass flights at dusk.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Greenland, Iceland, and Svalbard in remote mountain valleys. Winters on agricultural land and estuaries in western Europe.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Similar to Bean Goose but has pink (not orange) legs. Greylag Goose is larger with all-orange bill and paler plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pink legs and feet are the primary field mark. Short pink-and-black bill, small rounded head, and dark chocolate-brown neck are key identifiers.
