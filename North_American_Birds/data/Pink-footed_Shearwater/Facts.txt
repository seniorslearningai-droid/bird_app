Pink-footed Shearwater (Ardenna creatopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shearwater, grayish-brown above, white below
with mottled sides, pink bill and feet.
About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Juan Fernandez Islands; common offshore
Pacific coast. One of the most regular
Pacific shearwaters off California.

Habitat
-------
Eastern North Pacific. Common offshore California
and Pacific coast. Breeds on
Chilean islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Buller's Shearwater paler with bold M-pattern. Flesh-footed
all-dark with pink bill and feet.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large grayish-brown shearwater with pink bill and
feet. Mottled belly. Very common
offshore California.
