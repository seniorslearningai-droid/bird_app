Pinyon Jay (Gymnorhinus cyanocephalus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky blue jay with no crest, short tail, long
bill. Dull blue throughout with
pale streaks on throat. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly nomadic, travels in large flocks through
pinyon-juniper woodlands. Caches pine
seeds extensively. Loud, crowlike calls.

Habitat
-------
Pinyon-juniper woodlands of western United States.
Nomadic, moving in response to
pine cone crop abundance.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Scrub-Jay and Steller's Jay both crested.
Clark's Nutcracker grayish
with white wing patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-blue jay with no crest. Long sharp bill
adapted for pine seeds. Travels
in large noisy flocks.
