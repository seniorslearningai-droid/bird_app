Piping Plover (Charadrius melodus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Pale sandy plover with orange legs, black chest band, orange bill base, and white forehead.

Behavior
--------
Nests on open sand beaches. Runs rapidly and freezes, blending with sand perfectly.

Habitat
-------
Sandy ocean beaches and Great Lakes shores during breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated Plover is darker brown; Piping Plover is pale sand-colored.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale as dry sand, orange legs, and partial black breast band — endangered beach nester.
