Piratic Flycatcher (Legatus leucophaius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, brown above streaked, whitish below
with brown streaking, dark mask,
yellow crown patch. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pirates nest cavities from other species. Migratory;
rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic and Gulf coasts.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and forest edge of tropical Americas.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic and Gulf
coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Variegated Flycatcher very similar. Piratic Flycatcher
has different streaking pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked brown flycatcher with white supercilium, yellow
crown. Rare vagrant to Atlantic
and Gulf coasts.
