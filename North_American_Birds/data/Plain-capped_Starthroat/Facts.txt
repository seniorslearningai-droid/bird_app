Plain-capped Starthroat (Heliomaster constantii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large hummingbird. Long straight bill. Gray-brown
above and below, crimson throat, broad white facial
stripes, white rump patch. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar from various flowers. Often perches
prominently. Rare vagrant to Arizona from
Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dry thorn scrub, tropical forest edges, and arid
areas of Mexico and Central America. Rare
visitor to southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long bill distinguishes it from other hummingbirds.
White facial stripes and rump patch distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long straight bill, red throat patch, bold white
facial stripes, and white rump patch.
Rare vagrant in southeastern Arizona.
