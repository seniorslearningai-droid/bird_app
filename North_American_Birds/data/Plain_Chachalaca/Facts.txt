Plain Chachalaca (Ortalis vetula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Chicken-like bird with long tail and small head.
Olive-brown overall with bare red throat skin.
About 22 inches including long tail.

Behavior
--------
Groups call loudly at dawn with raucous chorus.
Forages in trees and shrubs for fruit, berries,
and leaves in dense South Texas brush.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorny scrub, riparian forests, and brushy
areas of southern Texas and Central America.
Common in Lower Rio Grande Valley.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in US range. Large dove or small
pheasant in shape but behavior is distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long tail, small head, olive-brown coloring with
bare red throat skin. Loud "cha-cha-lac"
calls given in groups at dawn.
