Plumbeous Vireo (Vireo plumbeus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium vireo with gray back, white underparts, white spectacles, and two white wing bars; no yellow tones.

Behavior
--------
Sings persistently from treetops; gleans insects from foliage methodically; builds cup nest in fork of branch.

Habitat
-------
Montane pine-oak forests and juniper woodlands of Rocky Mountains and southwestern US highlands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cassin's Vireo has greenish or yellowish tones; Blue-headed Vireo has blue-gray head contrasting with white throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All gray and white; bold white spectacles; strong white wing bars; no yellow or olive tones; mountain forests.
