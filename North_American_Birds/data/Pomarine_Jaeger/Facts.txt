Pomarine Jaeger (Stercorarius pomarinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large jaeger with twisted spoon-shaped central tail
feathers (breeding). Dark and light morphs.
Broad wings, heavy chest. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pursues other seabirds to steal food. Follows ships.
Seen offshore on both coasts during
migration. Most common large jaeger.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Pelagic outside breeding
season. Regular offshore both coasts
during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Parasitic Jaeger smaller and slimmer. Spoon-shaped
tail projection distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Twisted blunt-tipped central tail feathers in breeding.
Heavy-chested compared to Parasitic Jaeger.
Yellow neck on light morph.
