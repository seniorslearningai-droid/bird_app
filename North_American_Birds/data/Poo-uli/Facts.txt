Poo-uli (Melamprosops phaeosoma)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian honeycreeper, dark brown above with
black mask, pale buff-white
below. Short thick bill. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Foraged for snails in dense native forest. Last
individual died in captivity in 2004.
Fastest extinction of a modern bird.

Habitat
-------
Dense wet native forest on the slopes of Haleakala,
Maui. Extinct due to habitat
loss and avian disease.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird. Unique among Hawaiian honeycreepers
with its snail-eating habits
and black mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct species. Black mask, brown above, pale below.
Short thick bill. Last seen
in wild 2004 on Maui.
