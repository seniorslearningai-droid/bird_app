Prairie Falcon (Falco mexicanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Sandy-brown falcon with white cheeks, thin dark mustache, dark axillaries, and dark wing linings.

Behavior
--------
Hunts ground squirrels and birds by flying low and fast over open terrain.

Habitat
-------
Open prairies, grasslands, and desert of western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Peregrine Falcon has slate-blue back and heavy mustache; Prairie is sandy with thin mustache.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sandy-brown with narrow mustache and distinctive black axillaries visible in flight.
