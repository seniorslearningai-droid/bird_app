Prairie Warbler (Setophaga discolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Yellow warbler with chestnut streaks on back, black streaks on sides, and black-and-yellow face pattern.

Behavior
--------
Bobs tail constantly. Sings ascending buzzy zee-zee-zee-zee-zee.

Habitat
-------
Shrubby fields, young pines, and mangrove edges in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Palm Warbler has rufous cap; Prairie has face pattern and back streaks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tail-bobbing, face pattern with black streaks, and chestnut back streaks.
