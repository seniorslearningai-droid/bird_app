Prothonotary Warbler (Protonotaria citrea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brilliant golden-yellow head and breast, blue-gray wings, and white underparts. Cavity nester.

Behavior
--------
Only eastern warbler that nests in cavities over water. Loud sweet-sweet song.

Habitat
-------
Wooded swamps and flooded bottomlands in the southeastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow Warbler lacks blue-gray wings; Blue-winged has yellow with black eye line.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Golden-yellow head and breast with blue-gray wings in flooded swamp forests.
