Providence Petrel (Pterodroma solandri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark brown gadfly petrel with white primary
patch on underwing. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Lord Howe and Norfolk Islands. Forages
North Pacific in non-breeding season;
occasional vagrant to Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific in non-breeding season; breeds on Australian
subantarctic islands. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Murphy's Petrel similar but smaller and paler. Providence
Petrel larger and darker overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large dark petrel with white primary bases visible
from below. North Pacific in
non-breeding season.
