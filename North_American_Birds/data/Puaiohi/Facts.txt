Puaiohi (Myadestes palmeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Hawaiian thrush, brown above, gray below,
pink legs, white eye ring.
About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Critically endangered. Forages for fruit and insects
along steep stream banks. Captive
breeding program underway.

Habitat
-------
Steep stream valleys in native wet forest of
Kauai. Very few individuals remain
in small area.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kamao larger, now extinct. Omao on Big Island.
Pink legs distinctive among
Hawaiian thrushes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pink legs, white eye ring, small size. Kauai
native wet forest stream
valleys only. Critically endangered.
