Purple Finch (Haemorhous purpureus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is raspberry-red washed over head, back, and underparts. Female is heavily streaked brown.

Behavior
--------
Males give rich warbling song. Irruptive migrant following seed crop abundance.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests; irrupts to more southern states in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
House Finch male has red chest only; Purple Finch male is raspberry-washed overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male is dipped in raspberry juice — uniformly wine-red rather than orange-red like House Finch.
