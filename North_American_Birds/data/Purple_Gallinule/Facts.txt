Purple Gallinule (Porphyrio martinica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stunning bird with purple-blue underparts, green back, red-and-yellow bill, and yellow-green legs.

Behavior
--------
Walks on floating vegetation and lily pads. Climbs into shrubs to roost.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes with dense floating vegetation in southeastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Gallinule has red frontal shield and gray body; Purple Gallinule is blue-purple.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Purple-blue underparts, green back, and rainbow-colored bill and legs — unmistakable.
