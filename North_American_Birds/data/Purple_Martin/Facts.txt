Purple Martin (Progne subis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest swallow. Male is all-dark glossy blue-black. Female has whitish belly and gray collar.

Behavior
--------
Highly colonial, nesting almost exclusively in martin houses in eastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Open areas near water; nests in multi-unit martin houses across eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tree Swallow is green-and-white; Purple Martin male is the only all-dark large swallow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest swallow — male is entirely glossy blue-black.
