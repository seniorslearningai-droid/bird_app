Purple Sandpiper (Calidris maritima)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky sandpiper with slate-gray plumage, orange-yellow bill base and legs, and purple gloss in fresh plumage.

Behavior
--------
Forages on wave-washed rocky coasts and jetties in winter. Very tame.

Habitat
-------
Rocky seacoasts and jetties along the Atlantic coast in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rock Sandpiper is the Pacific counterpart; Purple Sandpiper stays on Atlantic rocky shores.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray with yellowish bill base, found only on rocky Atlantic shorelines in winter.
