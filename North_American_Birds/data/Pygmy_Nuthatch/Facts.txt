Pygmy Nuthatch (Sitta pygmaea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny nuthatch with gray-brown cap, blue-gray back, pale underparts, and white spot on nape.

Behavior
--------
Highly social; roosts communally in cavities. Forages in flocks at treetops.

Habitat
-------
Ponderosa pine forests of western mountains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-breasted Nuthatch has rust-colored underparts; Pygmy is tiny with brown cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiniest nuthatch — brown cap, communal roosting, and acrobatic foraging at pine treetops.
