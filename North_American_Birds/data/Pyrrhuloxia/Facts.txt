Pyrrhuloxia (Cardinalis sinuatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Desert cardinal; male gray with red tinges on crest, wings, and tail; stubby curved yellow parrot-like bill.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds and insects in desert scrub; sings rich whistled phrases; often in pairs or small flocks.

Habitat
-------
Thorny desert scrub, mesquite thickets, and brushy desert washes of southwestern US and northern Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Cardinal male is all red; female Cardinal has orange bill; both have less curved bills than Pyrrhuloxia.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Curved yellow bill; gray body with red accents on crest and wings; male's red face mask; desert habitat.
