Razorbill (Alca torda)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium alcid. Black above, white below, distinctive
deep laterally compressed bill with white line.
About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish. Swims underwater using wings. Breeds
in North Atlantic; winters offshore. Regular winter
visitor on Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on rocky Atlantic islands and cliffs. Winters
offshore in North Atlantic. Regular winter
visitor to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Murre similar size but has thinner bill.
Razorbill's deep compressed bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Deep blade-like bill with white cross-stripe distinctive.
Thicker bill compared to murres.
Atlantic species.
