Red-backed Shrike (Lanius collurio)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shrike. Male has gray head, chestnut back,
black mask. Female brown with
barred underparts. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts from exposed perches, diving on insects,
lizards, and small birds. Impales
prey on thorns. Rare vagrant.

Habitat
-------
Open country with scattered bushes and thorns in
Europe and Asia. Very rare
vagrant to northeastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Shrike lacks gray head in male. Loggerhead
Shrike much larger, gray
above with heavy hooked bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: gray head plus chestnut back distinctive.
Female: brown with scalloped pattern.
Very rare vagrant from Eurasia.
