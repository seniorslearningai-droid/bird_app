Red-bellied Woodpecker (Melanerpes carolinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium woodpecker with red cap and nape on male, black-and-white barred back, and pale belly with faint red tinge.

Behavior
--------
Loud churr-churr call. Caches food in bark crevices.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests and suburbs of eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-headed Woodpecker has all-red head; Red-bellied has red only on cap and nape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Barred black-and-white back, red cap and nape on male, and faint reddish belly wash.
