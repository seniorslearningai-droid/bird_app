Red-billed Leiothrix (Leiothrix lutea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small colorful bird, olive-green back, yellow-orange
throat and breast, red bill,
red and yellow in wing. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active and noisy in dense undergrowth. Beautiful
melodious song. Introduced to Hawaii
and present in forests.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest understory and scrub. Introduced to
Hawaiian Islands, found on several
islands in native forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in Hawaii. Colorful combination
of yellow, orange, and red
with olive-green back unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red bill, yellow-orange underparts, colorful wing
pattern. Olive-green back. Melodious
song. Hawaiian introduced species.
