Red-billed Pigeon (Patagioenas flavirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium pigeon. Dark maroon-red head, neck and breast,
darker brown back, pale gray belly. Bright red bill
with yellow tip. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on berries, seeds, and fruit in tree canopy.
Usually found in pairs or small flocks. Year-round
resident in southern Texas Rio Grande Valley.

Habitat
-------
Dense riparian forest and woodland edges along
lower Rio Grande in Texas. Common in Mexico
and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Band-tailed Pigeon larger with different bill. No
other large red-billed pigeon in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-dark maroon-red body with distinctly bright red
bill. Slightly larger than Mourning Dove. Found
in dense riverside forest in South Texas.
