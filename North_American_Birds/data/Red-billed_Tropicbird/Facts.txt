Red-billed Tropicbird (Phaethon aethereus)
===========================

Description
-----------
White seabird with brilliant red bill, black-barred
back, very long white tail streamers,
black eye stripe. About 40 inches with tail.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives for fish and squid. Nests in cliff
crevices on tropical islands. Rare
offshore in Atlantic and Pacific.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Breeds on
Caribbean and eastern Pacific islands.
Occasional offshore Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-tailed Tropicbird similar but has yellow or
orange bill. Red bill of this species diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant red bill most distinctive feature. White
tail streamers, barred back.
Rare but striking offshore seabird.
