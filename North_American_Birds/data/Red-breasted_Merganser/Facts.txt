Red-breasted Merganser (Mergus serrator)
===========================

Description
-----------
Diving duck with shaggy double crest, red serrated bill, and white neck ring on male.

Behavior
--------
Dives in coastal waters for fish. Fast flier in low lines over water.

Habitat
-------
Coastal bays and large lakes; breeds near water in boreal regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Merganser male has smooth green head; Red-breasted has shaggy crest and streaked breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Shaggy double crest, red serrated bill, and rusty streaked breast on male.
