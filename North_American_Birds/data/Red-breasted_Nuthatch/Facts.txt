Red-breasted Nuthatch (Sitta canadensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small nuthatch with black cap, white supercilium, blue-gray back, and rusty-orange underparts.

Behavior
--------
Irruptive migrant; moves south in large numbers when conifer cone crops fail.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests; irruptive visitor to parks and feeders in irruption years.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-breasted Nuthatch is larger with white underparts; Red-breasted has rusty belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty-orange underparts and bold white eyebrow on blue-gray nuthatch.
