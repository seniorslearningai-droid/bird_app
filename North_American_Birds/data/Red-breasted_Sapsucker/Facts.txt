Red-breasted Sapsucker (Sphyrapicus ruber)
===========================

Description
-----------
Woodpecker with entirely red head and breast, black
and white back pattern. White wing
patch in flight. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Drills rows of sap wells in trees. Common in Pacific
coast forests from Alaska to
Baja California.

Habitat
-------
Conifer and mixed forest along Pacific coast. Breeds
in mountains; moves to lower
elevations in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-bellied and Red-naped Sapsuckers have partial
red. Red-breasted has entirely red head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entirely red head and breast. White wing patch.
Pacific coast and western mountain
forest sapsucker.
