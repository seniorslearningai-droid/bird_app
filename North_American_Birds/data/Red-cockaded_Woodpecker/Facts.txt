Red-cockaded Woodpecker (Dryobates borealis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small woodpecker with large white cheek patch, black cap and nape, barred back, and tiny red cockade on males.

Behavior
--------
Excavates nest cavities only in living longleaf pines. Lives in cooperative family groups.

Habitat
-------
Old-growth longleaf pine savannas of the southeastern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hairy and Downy have unbarred black-and-white backs; Red-cockaded has barred back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large white cheek patch on barred-back woodpecker in old longleaf pine forest.
