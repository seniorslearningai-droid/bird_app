Red-crested Cardinal (Paroaria coronata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, brilliant red crest and face, gray above,
white below. Bold and distinctive.
About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and conspicuous in open areas. Forages on ground
for seeds. Introduced to
Hawaii; very common.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, gardens, and parks. Introduced to Hawaii;
very common on several
islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Cardinal has all-red body on male. Yellow-billed
Cardinal lacks gray back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant red crest and face contrasting with gray
back and white belly. Very
common in Hawaii.
