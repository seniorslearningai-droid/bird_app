Red-crowned Amazon (Amazona viridigenalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium parrot, green with red crown and lores, blue
behind eye, yellowish face.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Endangered in Mexico; established feral populations
in California and southern Texas.
Loud flocking calls.

Habitat
-------
Feral in Los Angeles, San Diego, and Rio Grande Valley,
Texas. Endangered in native
Mexican range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lilac-crowned Amazon has lilac on crown. Red-crowned
has brighter red crown patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red crown patch, blue behind eye. Green parrot. Established
feral populations in California
and Texas.
