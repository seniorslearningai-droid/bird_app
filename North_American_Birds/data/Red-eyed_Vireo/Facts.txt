Red-eyed Vireo (Vireo olivaceus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium vireo with red eye, gray cap, white supercilium, dark eye line, olive back, and white underparts.

Behavior
--------
Sings persistently all summer long — one of the most tireless singers. Stays in tree canopy.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests across eastern North America; one of the most common forest birds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-headed Vireo has bold white spectacles and wing bars; Red-eyed is plainer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red eye, gray cap, and white supercilium with dark eye line — sings endlessly from treetops.
