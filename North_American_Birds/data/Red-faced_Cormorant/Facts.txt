Red-faced Cormorant (Urile urile)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium cormorant, dark glossy green-black plumage
with bright red-orange facial skin.
About 28 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish. Breeds on Aleutian Islands and Alaskan
rocky coasts. Common in Alaskan
waters.

Habitat
-------
Alaskan waters; breeds on rocky Aleutian Islands.
Western Alaska, not found in
lower 48 states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pelagic Cormorant in same range. Red-faced has much
broader red facial skin.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Broad red-orange facial skin around eye and base of
bill. Dark glossy plumage. Western
Alaska species.
