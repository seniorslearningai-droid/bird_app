Red-faced Warbler (Cardellina rubrifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray warbler with brilliant red-and-black head pattern and white nape patch.

Behavior
--------
Forages actively in pine-oak forest, often hanging from branch tips.

Habitat
-------
Mountain pine-oak forests of southeastern Arizona and New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Painted Redstart has red breast; Red-faced Warbler has red face with black borders.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red face bordered by black with white nape patch — brilliant in its mountain forest.
