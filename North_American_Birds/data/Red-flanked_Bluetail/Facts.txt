Red-flanked Bluetail (Tarsiger cyanurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat. Male blue above with orange-red flanks,
white belly. Female brown with
blue tail. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense vegetation but perches openly.
Flicks tail. Increasingly regular
vagrant to western Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forest of Siberia and East Asia. Regular
vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bluethroat has blue throat, breeds in Alaska.
No other bird with orange
flanks and blue tail in Alaska.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-red flanks contrasting with blue tail. Male
blue above. Increasingly regular
Alaska fall vagrant.
