Red-footed Booby (Sula sula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small booby with bright red feet. Polymorphic: white
morph white with black wings; brown
morph all brown. About 28 inches.

Behavior
--------
Only tree-nesting booby; breeds in shrubs. Plunge-dives
for fish and squid. Breeds on Hawaiian
islands; forages offshore.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific and Atlantic. Breeds on Hawaiian
Northwestern Islands; forages in
tropical Pacific.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown Booby has yellow feet. Red-footed Booby's bright
red feet diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright red feet and legs distinctive. White morph
most common; brown morph also occurs.
Only booby that nests in trees.
