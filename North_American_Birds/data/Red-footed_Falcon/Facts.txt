Red-footed Falcon (Falco vespertinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small falcon. Male dark gray with red feet and vent;
female rufous with gray back and
barred underparts. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly migratory; feeds on insects. Very rare vagrant
from Europe/Asia to Atlantic coast
of North America.

Habitat
-------
Open farmland and steppe of Europe and Central Asia.
Very rare vagrant to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Merlin similar size. Red-footed Falcon male's all-gray
body with red feet distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: dark gray with red feet. Female: rufous with
barred underparts. Very rare
Atlantic vagrant from Europe.
