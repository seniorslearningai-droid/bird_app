Red-headed Woodpecker (Melanerpes erythrocephalus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Striking woodpecker with entirely crimson-red head, pure white body, and large white wing patches.

Behavior
--------
Catches insects in flight like a flycatcher. Stores food under bark.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands with large trees, orchards, and snags in eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-bellied has red only on cap; Red-headed has entire head red with white body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entire head is brilliant red — only North American woodpecker with this pattern.
