Red-legged Honeycreeper (Cyanerpes cyaneus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bird. Male brilliant blue with black back and
wings, red legs, yellow underwing.
Female greenish. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Probes flowers for nectar with curved bill. Forages
in fruiting trees. Rare vagrant
to Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest and woodland from Mexico to South
America. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting has different bill. Shining Honeycreeper
similar. Red legs
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male brilliant blue with black wings and red legs.
Yellow underwing in flight.
Rare Texas vagrant.
