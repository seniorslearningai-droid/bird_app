Red-legged Kittiwake (Rissa brevirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull. White with gray back, black wingtips,
red legs (not yellow). Short yellow bill.
About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pelagic; breeds on steep Alaskan cliff islands.
Forages over Bering Sea. Less common than
Black-legged Kittiwake.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Pribilof and Aleutian Islands in Bering
Sea. Winters offshore in North Pacific.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-legged Kittiwake nearly identical but has yellow
legs and is far more common.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red legs diagnostic; Black-legged Kittiwake has yellow
legs. Shorter bill. Breeds mainly
on Pribilof Islands.
