Red-legged Thrush (Turdus plumbeus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, blue-gray above, white throat with
black streaks, orange-red bill
and legs. About 10.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for fruit and invertebrates.
Bold in parks and gardens. Rare
vagrant to Florida from Bahamas.

Habitat
-------
Forest, gardens, and parks of the Bahamas and
Caribbean. Rare vagrant to
southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin has red breast. Blue-gray plumage
distinctive. Red legs
unique among thrushes in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red-orange legs distinctive. Blue-gray overall with
white throat. Florida vagrant
from the Bahamas.
