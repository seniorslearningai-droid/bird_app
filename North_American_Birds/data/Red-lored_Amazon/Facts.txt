Red-lored Amazon (Amazona autumnalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large parrot, green with red forehead and lores,
yellow cheeks, blue crown.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious, noisy in flocks. Forages for
fruit and seeds. Feral populations
in California and Florida.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest and urban areas. Feral populations
in southern California and
Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-headed Amazon has yellow head. Red-crowned
Amazon has red crown only.
Red lores distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red forehead and lores with yellow cheeks. Green
parrot. Feral in California
and Florida suburbs.
