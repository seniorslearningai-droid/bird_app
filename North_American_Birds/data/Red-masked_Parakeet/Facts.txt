Red-masked Parakeet (Psittacara erythrogenys)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large green parakeet with extensive red on head extending
behind eye. Long tail.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral populations in San Francisco and
San Diego. Often mixes with other
feral parakeets.

Habitat
-------
Feral in San Francisco Bay Area and San Diego in
suburban habitats. Also feral in
Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mitred Parakeet similar but less red on head. Red-masked
has extensive red face mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extensive red mask on face and head. Green body.
Feral in San Francisco Bay Area
and San Diego.
