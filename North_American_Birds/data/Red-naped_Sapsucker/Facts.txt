Red-naped Sapsucker (Sphyrapicus nuchalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Black-and-white woodpecker with red crown, nape, and chin on male; red crown and nape on female.

Behavior
--------
Drills rows of sap wells in trees and returns to feed on sap and trapped insects.

Habitat
-------
Aspen and riparian forests of the Rocky Mountains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-bellied Sapsucker has white nasal tufts and no red nape; Red-naped has red nape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red crown and nape, sap well rows in aspen trees of the Rocky Mountain region.
