Red-necked Grebe (Podiceps grisegena)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium grebe with rich rusty-red neck, black cap, white cheeks, and yellow-and-black bill.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish and invertebrates. Often seen in large flocks on open water in winter.

Habitat
-------
Large lakes and coastal bays; breeds on boreal lakes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Horned Grebe is smaller with golden ear tufts; Red-necked is larger with rusty neck.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty-red neck, white cheek, and yellow-based bill — larger than other grebes.
