Red-necked Phalarope (Phalaropus lobatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small shorebird. Breeding female has rusty neck patches and gray back. Spins on water surface.

Behavior
--------
Females are more colorful; males incubate eggs and raise young. Spins to stir up plankton.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra; winters on ocean. Migrates through inland lakes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red Phalarope is larger with brick-red neck; Red-necked has thinner neck patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Spins on water surface, rusty neck patches, and reversed sex roles in breeding.
