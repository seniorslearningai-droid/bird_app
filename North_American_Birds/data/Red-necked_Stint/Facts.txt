Red-necked Stint (Calidris ruficollis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small sandpiper. Breeding adult has reddish-orange
face and upper breast. Non-breeding gray above,
white below. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on mudflats picking small invertebrates. Regular
migrant through western Alaska; rare on Pacific
coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic Siberian tundra. Migrates through
western Alaska. Winters on Southeast Asian and
Australian coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated Sandpiper similar in non-breeding. Red-
necked has rufous face and breast in summer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous-orange face and breast in breeding plumage.
Non-breeding very similar to Semipalmated. Rare
on Pacific coast.
