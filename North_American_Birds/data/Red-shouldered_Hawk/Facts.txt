Red-shouldered Hawk (Buteo lineatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hawk with rusty-orange barred underparts, reddish shoulders, and checkered black-and-white wings.

Behavior
--------
Calls loudly kee-ahh in forest. Often seen near wetlands and streams.

Habitat
-------
Forests near streams and wet areas in eastern North America and California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-tailed Hawk has solid belly band; Red-shouldered has barred rusty underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty barred underparts, checkered wings, and loud kee-ahh call in forested wetlands.
