Red-tailed Hawk (Buteo jamaicensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Most common and widespread hawk. Adults have a distinctive brick-red tail, dark back, and belly band.

Behavior
--------
Soars over open country. Perches on utility poles and fence posts.

Habitat
-------
Almost every habitat from forests to deserts across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-shouldered has barred underparts; Ferruginous has all-white belly; Red-tailed has belly band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brick-red tail on adults visible from below — the most common soaring hawk in North America.
