Red-tailed Tropicbird (Phaethon rubricauda)
===========================

Description
-----------
White seabird with red tail streamers, red bill,
very clean white plumage with limited
black markings. About 37 inches.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives for fish. Breeds on Hawaiian and Pacific
islands. Nests in burrows or
cliff crevices.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific Ocean. Breeds on Hawaiian Northwest
Islands and other Pacific tropical islands.
Offshore Pacific waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-tailed Tropicbird has white tail and more black.
Red-tailed has clean white body and red streamers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red tail streamers on very white body. Minimal black
markings. Pacific species.
Cleanest-looking of the tropicbirds.
