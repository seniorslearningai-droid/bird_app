Red-throated Loon (Gavia stellata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest loon with thin upturned bill and rusty-red throat patch in breeding. Gray head with striped nape.

Behavior
--------
Can take flight from land without a running start, unlike other loons.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra lakes in summer; winters along coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pacific and Common Loons have heavier bills; Red-throated has slim upturned bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slim upturned bill, smallest loon, and rusty throat patch in breeding plumage.
