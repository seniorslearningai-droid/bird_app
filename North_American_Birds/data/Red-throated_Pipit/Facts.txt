Red-throated Pipit (Anthus cervinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pipit, heavily streaked above, pinkish-red
throat and breast in breeding.
About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on open ground and tundra. Breeds in arctic
tundra of Alaska.
Migrates to Asia in fall.

Habitat
-------
Open tundra, wet meadows, and arctic grassland in
Alaska. Winters in
Southeast Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Olive-backed Pipit lacks reddish throat. American Pipit
more uniform below in fall.
Red throat distinctive in spring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Reddish throat and breast in breeding. Heavily streaked
in fall. Alaska tundra
breeder. Long-distance migrant to Asia.
