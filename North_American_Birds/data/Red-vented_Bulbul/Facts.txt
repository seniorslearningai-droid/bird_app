Red-vented Bulbul (Pycnonotus cafer)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium songbird, brown-black with scaly pattern,
white rump, red undertail coverts.
Pointed crest. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Noisy and bold, often near human habitation.
Invasive species in Hawaii, eating
fruit and damaging crops.

Habitat
-------
Introduced to Oahu and Maui, Hawaii. Scrub, forest
edges, and urban areas. Invasive,
spreading from original native range in South Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-whiskered Bulbul has red cheek patch and longer
crest. Common in same
Hawaii habitats.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red undertail coverts, white rump, scaly brown-black
pattern, pointed crest. Invasive
in Hawaii. Very noisy.
