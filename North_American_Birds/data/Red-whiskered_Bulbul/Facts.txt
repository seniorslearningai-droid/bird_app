Red-whiskered Bulbul (Pycnonotus jocosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium songbird with tall pointed black crest, white
face with red cheek spot,
red undertail coverts. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Noisy and conspicuous in suburbs. Eats fruit
and insects. Invasive in Florida
and Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Suburban gardens, parks, and forest edges. Introduced
populations in southern Florida
and Hawaii.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-vented Bulbul lacks red cheek patch and has
more uniform brown-black plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red cheek spot and tall black crest unique. Red
undertail coverts. Invasive in
south Florida and Hawaii.
