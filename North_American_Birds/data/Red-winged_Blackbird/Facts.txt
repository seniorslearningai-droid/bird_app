Red-winged Blackbird (Agelaius phoeniceus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is all black with brilliant red-and-yellow shoulder patches. Female is brown and streaked.

Behavior
--------
Males display shoulder patches prominently. One of the most abundant birds in North America.

Habitat
-------
Marshes, wet fields, and roadsides across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other blackbird has red-and-yellow shoulder patches on all-black body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black with red-and-yellow shoulder patches on male — among the most common birds.
