Red Avadavat (Amandava amandava)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch. Male brilliant red with white spots
throughout, red bill. Female brown
with red bill and rump. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious in flocks. Forages for grass seeds.
Introduced to Hawaii and
Puerto Rico.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands and open areas. Introduced to Hawaii and
Puerto Rico. Native to India
and Southeast Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other tiny red bird with white spots in Hawaii.
Unique pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant red with white spots and red bill. Tiny
size. Gregarious. Introduced
to Hawaii.
