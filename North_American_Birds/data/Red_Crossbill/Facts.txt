Red Crossbill (Loxia curvirostra)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch. Male brick-red with dark wings. Female
olive-green. Bill tips visibly
crossed. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Uses crossed bill to pry open conifer cones. Nomadic,
following cone crops across
North America.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests across North America and Eurasia.
Nomadic following cone crop
availability.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-winged Crossbill has white wing bars. Cassia
Crossbill similar but restricted
to Cassia County, Idaho.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Crossed bill tips visible in field. Male brick-red.
Female olive-green. Nomadic
in conifer forests.
