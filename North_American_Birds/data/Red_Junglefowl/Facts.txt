Red Junglefowl (Gallus gallus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Ancestor of domestic chicken. Male has brilliant
red and orange plumage, red comb and wattles, arched
iridescent green-black tail. Female brown. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on forest floor for seeds, insects, and
fruit. Male crows loudly. Feral populations
established in Hawaii from escaped domestic birds.

Habitat
-------
South and Southeast Asian forests. In US found
in Hawaii as feral birds, particularly on
Kauai where they roam freely.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Domestic chickens are direct descendants; identical
to wild type males.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's red comb, orange-red hackles, dark iridescent
tail. Female similar to domestic hen.
Wild birds slimmer than domestic chickens.
