Red Knot (Calidris canutus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium chunky sandpiper. Breeding adult has rusty-red underparts and mottled reddish-gray back.

Behavior
--------
Makes one of the longest migrations — from Arctic to Tierra del Fuego and back.

Habitat
-------
Coastal beaches and mudflats during migration; Arctic tundra for breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dunlin has drooping bill tip; Dowitcher is larger; Red Knot is stocky with straight bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Stocky medium sandpiper with rusty-red underparts in breeding — one of longest migrations.
