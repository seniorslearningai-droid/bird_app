Red Phalarope (Phalaropus fulicarius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium phalarope. Breeding female has brick-red body,
white face, yellow bill. Non-breeding gray above,
white below. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims in offshore waters spinning to stir up plankton.
Roles reversed; female more colorful. Common
offshore in fall migration.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on High Arctic tundra. Pelagic in non-breeding
season. Seen offshore during migration; rarely
near shore.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-necked Phalarope thinner-billed. Breeding Red
Phalarope is red below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding female unmistakable red below with white
face. Non-breeding gray above with
thick yellow-based bill.
