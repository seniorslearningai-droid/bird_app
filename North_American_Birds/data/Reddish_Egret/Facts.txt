Reddish Egret (Egretta rufescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium egret. Dark morph: slate gray with rufous
head and neck. White morph: all white.
Both have pink-based dark bill. About 30 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active and agile forager; rushes and dances while
hunting fish. Coastal specialist.
Gulf Coast and Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Coastal lagoons, mangroves, and tidal flats of Gulf
Coast and Florida. Also Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dark morph: distinctive. White morph: similar to
other white egrets but has pink-based
dark bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pink-based dark bill diagnostic. Dark morph uniquely
slate-gray with rufous neck. Active
dancing feeding behavior.
