Redhead (Aythya americana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Diving duck. Male has chestnut-red head, gray body, and blue-gray bill. Female is brown with blue-gray bill.

Behavior
--------
Often parasitizes other duck nests by laying eggs for others to raise.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater lakes and coastal bays across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canvasback has rusty-red head but also has sloping forehead and black bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Round red head and blue-gray bill on male — contrasts with Canvasback's sloping profile.
