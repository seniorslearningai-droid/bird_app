Redpoll (Acanthis flammea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, streaked brown with red forehead patch,
pinkish-red breast in male,
small yellow bill. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Irruptive species, invades southern areas when birch
seed crop fails. Forages in
birch and alder trees.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forest and arctic scrub across North America
and Eurasia. Irruptive winter
visitor to northern states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common and Hoary Redpolls nearly identical. Pine Siskin
has yellow wing stripe.
Red forehead spot helpful.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red forehead, pink breast on male. Streaked brown.
Irruptive visitor to birch
trees in winter.
