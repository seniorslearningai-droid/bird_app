Redwing (Turdus iliacus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, brown above with bold creamy supercilium,
heavily streaked breast, red-orange
underwing and flank patch. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Travels in flocks, foraging for berries in winter.
Rare but annual vagrant to
northeastern North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland, parks, and berry-bearing scrub in
Europe. Rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Song Thrush has spots not streaks below, lacks
red flank patch. Fieldfare
has gray head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red-orange flank patch and underwing distinctive.
Bold creamy supercilium. Streaked
breast. Rare European vagrant.
