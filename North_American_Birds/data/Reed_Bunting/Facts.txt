Reed Bunting (Emberiza schoeniclus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male has black head and throat with
white collar. Female streaked
brown. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Inhabits wetlands and reed beds. Sings from reeds.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, reed beds, and marshes across Europe and
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pallas's Bunting smaller. Lapland Longspur has chestnut
nape. Male pattern distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male black head and throat with white collar. Wetland
habitat. Rare Alaska vagrant
from Siberia.
