Rhinoceros Auklet (Cerorhinca monocerata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large alcid. Dark gray-brown above, lighter below.
Breeding adult has two white facial plumes and
horn-like bill projection. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in burrows. Brings fish to colony at night.
Pelagic outside breeding; found along Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Nests on Pacific coast islands from Alaska to
California. Winters offshore along Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tufted Puffin similar size but has orange bill.
Rhinoceros Auklet brownish with white plumes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding adult's white facial plumes and bill horn.
Non-breeding dark grayish. Large alcid
of Pacific coast.
