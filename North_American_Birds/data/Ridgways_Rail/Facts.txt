Ridgway's Rail (Rallus obsoletus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large rail. Brown streaked back, orange-brown breast,
gray cheeks, long orange-red bill. Striped flanks.
About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive; walks through salt marsh grass. Calls
with loud "kek-kek-kek." Found in Pacific coast
salt marshes; endangered.

Habitat
-------
Coastal salt marshes of California and Baja California.
Endangered; habitat loss from coastal
development is primary threat.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Clapper Rail nearly identical; separated by range on
Pacific coast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large secretive marsh bird with long orange bill.
Orange-brown breast and gray cheeks.
Pacific coast salt marshes only.
