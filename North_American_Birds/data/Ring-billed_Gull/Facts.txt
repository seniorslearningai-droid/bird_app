Ring-billed Gull (Larus delawarensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull with yellow bill with complete black ring, yellow legs, gray mantle, and black wingtips.

Behavior
--------
Very common urban gull at parking lots, fast food restaurants, and landfills.

Habitat
-------
Inland lakes, coasts, and urban areas across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Herring Gull is larger with red spot on bill; Ring-billed has complete black ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow bill with complete black ring — the most common gull at shopping centers.
