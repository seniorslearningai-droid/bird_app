Ring-necked Duck (Aythya collaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Diving duck with black back, gray sides, peaked head, and bold white ring near bill tip.

Behavior
--------
Dives for aquatic vegetation and invertebrates in fresh water.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater lakes and marshes; winters on coastal bays.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scaup have white backs; Ring-necked Duck has black back with white ring near bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black back and white ring on bill — despite its name, the neck ring is nearly invisible.
