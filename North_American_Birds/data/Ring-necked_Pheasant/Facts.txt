Ring-necked Pheasant (Phasianus colchicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large introduced game bird. Male has iridescent green head, red face wattles, white neck ring, and long tail.

Behavior
--------
Introduced from Asia. Lives in agricultural areas and brushy fields.

Habitat
-------
Farmland, grasslands, and brushy areas across northern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No native North American bird has this combination of colorful plumage and long tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Introduced game bird — iridescent green head, white neck ring, and very long tail.
