Ringed Kingfisher (Megaceryle torquata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest kingfisher in North America with shaggy crest, blue-gray back, and rufous belly on male.

Behavior
--------
Dives from perch or hovers to catch large fish in rivers.

Habitat
-------
Rivers and large water bodies in southern Texas and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Belted Kingfisher is smaller with white belly; Ringed is larger with rufous belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Largest kingfisher — rufous belly on male, and shaggy crest on Texas rivers.
