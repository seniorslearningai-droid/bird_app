Ringed Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates hornbyi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Distinctive storm-petrel with black cap, gray back,
white collar and underparts, black breast
band. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in Atacama Desert. Very rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast; rare and
poorly known species.

Habitat
-------
Humboldt Current off South American Pacific coast.
Very rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other storm-petrel has black cap with white collar
and breast band combination.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black cap with white collar, gray back, white underparts
with black breast band.
Distinctive and very rare vagrant.
