River Warbler (Locustella fluviatilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, olive-brown above, whitish below
with spotted breast and olive-brown
streaks on flanks. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense streamside vegetation. Distinctive
mechanical reeling song. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Dense riparian vegetation, forest edges, and scrub
in Europe and Asia. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Savi's Warbler similar but unstreaked below. Marsh
Warbler and Reed Warbler
lack streaked flanks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Spotted breast, streaked olive-brown flanks, plain
brown back. Skulking behavior.
Very rare vagrant from Europe.
