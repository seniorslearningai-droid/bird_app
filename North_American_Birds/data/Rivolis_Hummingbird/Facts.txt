Rivoli's Hummingbird (Eugenes fulgens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large hummingbird. Male has brilliant iridescent purple
crown, emerald-green throat, dark body. Female
dull green above, spotted gray below. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar; defends feeding territories aggressively.
Hovers with slow wingbeats for large size.
Found in mountain canyons of Southwest.

Habitat
-------
Mountain pine-oak and riparian canyons of Arizona,
New Mexico, and west Texas. Also Mexico
and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue-throated Mountain-gem shares similar habitat.
Male Rivoli's purple crown and green throat unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's purple crown and emerald-green throat iridescent
in good light. Large size among
hummingbirds. Mountain canyon habitat in Southwest.
