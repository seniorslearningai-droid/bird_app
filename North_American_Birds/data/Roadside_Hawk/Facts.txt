Roadside Hawk (Rupornis magnirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small hawk, gray-brown above with rufous barring below,
gray head, yellow eyes.
About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common hawk of roadsides and forest edges in Mexico
and Central America. Rare vagrant
to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Forest edges, roadsides, and open areas from Mexico
to Argentina. Rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-winged Hawk similar but larger with different
tail pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small hawk with rufous barring below, gray head.
Commonly perches on roadside wires
and posts. Rare Texas vagrant.
