Rock Dove (Columba livia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Familiar pigeon with blue-gray plumage, iridescent neck, two black wing bars, and white rump; variable plumage.

Behavior
--------
Highly adaptable; eats seeds and scraps; nests on building ledges; coos softly; flocks in urban areas.

Habitat
-------
Cities, towns, farms, and cliff faces worldwide; originally cliff-dwelling species from Eurasia and North Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Band-tailed Pigeon is larger with yellow bill tip and band across tail; Eurasian Collared-Dove has collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump in flight; two black wing bars; iridescent neck; feral urban pigeon; variable color morphs common.
