Rock Ptarmigan (Lagopus muta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Grouse with all-white winter plumage, black eye stripe through eye, and feathered feet.

Behavior
--------
Turns white in winter for camouflage in snow. Males retain black eye stripe.

Habitat
-------
Arctic and alpine tundra across northern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Willow Ptarmigan is larger and lacks black eye stripe in winter; Rock has black stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white winter plumage with black eye stripe — camouflaged in alpine and Arctic tundra.
