Rock Sandpiper (Calidris ptilocnemis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shorebird; breeding plumage has rufous back, gray head, and black belly patch; dull gray in winter.

Behavior
--------
Forages on wave-washed rocky shores and kelp beds; tame and approachable; winters on rocky Pacific coasts.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on tundra and rocky coasts of Alaska; winters on rocky shorelines of Pacific Coast south to California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Sandpiper is Atlantic counterpart; Dunlin is smaller without black belly patch in breeding plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rocky shoreline habitat; black belly patch in summer; yellowish legs; drooped bill tip; stocky chunky build.
