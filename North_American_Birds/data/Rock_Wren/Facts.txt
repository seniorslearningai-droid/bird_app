Rock Wren (Salpinctes obsoletus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Sandy-gray wren with finely spotted back, buffy rump, and rufous tail corners. Bobs body constantly.

Behavior
--------
Bobs its body up and down constantly. Paves nest entrance with small flat stones.

Habitat
-------
Rocky slopes, cliffs, and dry canyon walls across western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Canyon Wren has white throat and rufous body; Rock Wren is sandy-gray throughout.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Sandy-gray with spotted back, constant body bobbing, and rocky habitat in the west.
