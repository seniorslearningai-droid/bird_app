Rose-breasted Grosbeak (Pheucticus ludovicianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has black head, white underparts, brilliant rose-red triangular breast patch, and large white bill.

Behavior
--------
Rich warbling song. Males share incubation duties. Visits backyard feeders.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous forests and forest edges of eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-headed Grosbeak male has orange breast; Rose-breasted male has rose-red triangle.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's rose-red triangle on white breast is unmistakable — visits backyard feeders.
