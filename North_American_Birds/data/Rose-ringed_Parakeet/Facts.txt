Rose-ringed Parakeet (Psittacula krameri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender green parakeet, long pointed tail. Male has
black and rose-pink collar ring.
About 16 inches including tail.

Behavior
--------
Established feral populations worldwide. Flocks in
parks and suburbs. Noisy. Feral in
Florida and California.

Habitat
-------
Feral in Florida, California, and many worldwide cities.
Native to Africa and
South Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Monk Parakeet shorter tail and gray breast. Rose-ringed
has very long tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long pointed tail. Male has rose-pink neck ring.
Green. Feral in Florida and
California cities.
