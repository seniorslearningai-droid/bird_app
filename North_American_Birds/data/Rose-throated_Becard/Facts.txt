Rose-throated Becard (Pachyramphus aglaiae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, male gray with black cap and rose-pink
throat. Female brown with gray cap
and buff underparts. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Builds large globular hanging nest. Regular rare summer
visitor to southeastern Arizona
riparian canyons.

Habitat
-------
Riparian sycamore and cottonwood canyon forest in
southeastern Arizona. Summer visitor;
nests along Sonoita Creek.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray-collared Becard lacks pink throat. Rose-throated
Becard male's pink throat distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: rose-pink throat patch. Female: brown with buff
underparts. Builds hanging nest.
Arizona riparian canyons.
