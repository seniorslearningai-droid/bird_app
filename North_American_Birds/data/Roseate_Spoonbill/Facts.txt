Roseate Spoonbill (Platalea ajaja)
===========================

Description
-----------
Pink bird with spatula-shaped bill, naked yellow-green head, and red shoulder bar.

Behavior
--------
Swings spoon-shaped bill side to side in water to capture small fish and invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Coastal marshes and mangroves of the Gulf Coast and Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Flamingo is deeper pink and taller; Spoonbill has spatula bill and shorter stature.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pink plumage and spatula-shaped bill — unmistakable wading bird of Gulf Coast marshes.
