Roseate Tern (Sterna dougallii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender white tern with pale gray back, black cap, deeply forked tail with long outer streamers, and dark bill.

Behavior
--------
Dives for small fish; colonial nester; gives harsh grating calls; associates with Common and Arctic Terns.

Habitat
-------
Coastal islands and beaches; breeds in small colonies on rocky or sandy islands in northeastern US and tropics.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Tern has orange-red bill base; Arctic Tern has all-red bill; both have shorter tail streamers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely long tail streamers beyond wings at rest; mostly dark bill; pale whitish underparts with rosy flush.
