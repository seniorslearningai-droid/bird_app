Ross's Goose (Anser rossii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small stocky white goose with black wingtips; tiny stubby pink bill with bluish-gray warty base; round head.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in large flocks with Snow Geese; grazes on grain fields; breeds on tundra in Arctic Canada.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra for breeding; winters in Central Valley of California, Gulf Coast, and interior agricultural fields.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snow Goose is larger with longer bill showing grinning patch; blue morph Snow Goose is similar to blue morph Ross.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny bill without grinning patch; small rounded head; smaller overall than Snow Goose; stubby pink bill.
