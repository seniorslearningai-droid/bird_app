Ross's Gull (Rhodostethia rosea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Arctic gull. Breeding adult has rosy-pink
flush on white plumage, thin black collar, wedge-
shaped tail. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds at high Arctic latitudes; rarely seen south.
Irregular winter visitor to northern Atlantic and
Pacific coasts.

Habitat
-------
High Arctic tundra and sea ice. Irregular winter
visitor to northeastern North America. Very rare
south of Arctic.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bonaparte's Gull similar size. Ross's has wedge-
shaped tail and pink flush.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Wedge-shaped tail distinctive. Pink flush on white
plumage. Thin black collar.
Rare Arctic visitor.
