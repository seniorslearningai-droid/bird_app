Rosy-faced Lovebird (Agapornis roseicollis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small stocky parrot, bright green with rosy-pink face
and throat, blue rump.
About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral population in Phoenix area, Arizona.
Nests in cactus and buildings.
Popular cage bird.

Habitat
-------
Established feral population in Phoenix, Arizona suburban
areas. Native to southwestern
Africa.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other parrot in Arizona has rosy-pink face. Unique
in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rosy-pink face, green body, blue rump. Small stocky
parrot. Feral in Phoenix, Arizona.
Unique in North America.
