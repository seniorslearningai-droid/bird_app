Rough-legged Hawk (Buteo lagopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large buteo with feathered legs, dark belly band, and pale tail with broad dark terminal band.

Behavior
--------
Hovers while hunting voles over open fields. Irrupts south from Arctic in winter.

Habitat
-------
Open tundra in summer; fields and marshes in winter across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ferruginous Hawk has rusty back; Rough-legged has feathered tarsi and distinct belly band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Feathered legs, distinctive dark belly, and hovering flight over open fields in winter.
