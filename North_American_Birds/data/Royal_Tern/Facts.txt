Royal Tern (Thalasseus maximus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large orange-billed tern with black cap (often showing white forehead in non-breeding), and deeply forked tail.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives for fish in coastal waters. Nests in dense colonies on beaches.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic and Pacific coasts and Gulf of Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Caspian Tern is larger with red bill; Elegant Tern is slimmer with longer curved bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large tern with bright orange bill — often shows white forehead outside breeding season.
