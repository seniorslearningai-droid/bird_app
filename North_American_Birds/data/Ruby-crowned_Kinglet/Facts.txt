Ruby-crowned Kinglet (Corthylio calendula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny olive-green bird with bold white eye ring, two white wing bars, and hidden red crown on male.

Behavior
--------
Constantly flicks wings while foraging. Huge song for its tiny size.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests; widespread migrant in shrubs and forests.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Golden-crowned Kinglet has striped head; Ruby-crowned has bold eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white eye ring, constant wing-flicking, and huge bubbling song from tiny bird.
