Ruby-throated Hummingbird (Archilochus colubris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has brilliant iridescent ruby-red throat, green back, and white underparts. Tiny size.

Behavior
--------
Only hummingbird regularly nesting in eastern North America. Migrates across Gulf of Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Gardens, forest edges, and flowering areas across eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-chinned Hummingbird is the western counterpart; Ruby-throated is eastern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's iridescent ruby-red throat — the only hummingbird nesting throughout eastern North America.
