Ruddy Duck (Oxyura jamaicensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small stiff-tailed duck. Breeding male has bright blue bill, rufous body, black cap, and white cheek.

Behavior
--------
Holds stiff tail cocked up when alert. Rarely walks on land.

Habitat
-------
Open water, lakes, and coastal bays across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Masked Duck is rare; Ruddy Duck male's blue bill and white cheek are distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright blue bill, white cheek, and stiff cocked tail on breeding male.
