Ruddy Ground Dove (Columbina talpacoti)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dove. Male reddish-brown with blue-gray head.
Female grayish-brown. Dark spots on wings.
About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for seeds on ground in pairs or small groups.
Common and approachable in its range.
Resident in Mexico; rare visitor to southern US.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, farms, gardens, and roadsides in
tropical lowlands. Rare visitor to southern
California, Arizona, and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Ground Dove similar. Male Ruddy is richer
reddish-brown with blue-gray head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's deep reddish-rust plumage and blue-gray head.
Dark wing spots. Smaller and
richer-colored than Common Ground Dove.
