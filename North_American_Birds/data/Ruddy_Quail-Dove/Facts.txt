Ruddy Quail-Dove (Geotrygon montana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chunky dove. Male rich reddish-brown with
purple gloss, pale face stripe, iridescent neck.
Female duller brown. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks quietly on forest floor seeking seeds and
fallen fruit. Very secretive. Rare visitor
to Florida Keys from Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Dense tropical forest understory in Caribbean and
Central America. Rarely seen in Florida
in dense subtropical growth.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Key West Quail-Dove has distinct bold white
face stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's rich rufous-brown plumage with pale facial
stripe and purplish sheen. Found
walking on dense forest floor.
