Ruddy Shelduck (Tadorna ferruginea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large duck-goose, bright orange-rufous body, pale
head, black tail and wingtips,
white wing patches. About 26 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on grasslands and wetlands. Most sightings
in North America likely escaped
birds. Rare vagrant.

Habitat
-------
Wetlands, grasslands, and river valleys of Europe
and Asia. Rare vagrant to
North America; most records questionable.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar orange-rufous duck in North America. White
wing patches distinctive in
flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright orange-rufous body with pale head. White wing
patches in flight. Likely
escaped from captivity in most cases.
