Ruddy Turnstone (Arenaria interpres)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky shorebird with bold harlequin pattern of rusty-red, black, and white. Short orange legs.

Behavior
--------
Flips pebbles and shells to find invertebrates underneath. Bold and approachable.

Habitat
-------
Rocky and sandy shores and mudflats along coasts during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Turnstone has all-dark face; Ruddy Turnstone has complex rusty-and-black pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold rusty, black, and white pattern and habit of flipping stones on rocky shores.
