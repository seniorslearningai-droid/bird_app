Ruff (Calidris pugnax)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unusual sandpiper with variable plumage. Breeding
male has elaborate neck ruff and head tufts in
variable colors. Female (reeve) smaller and plain. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Males display at leks with spectacular ruff plumage.
Rare but regular vagrant to North America
from Eurasia. Forages in fields and wetlands.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Eurasian wet meadows. Winters in Africa.
Uncommon but regular vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Buff-breasted Sandpiper similar in non-breeding. Breeding
male's elaborate ruff unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding male's colorful neck ruff unmistakable. Non-
breeding birds scaly-backed with small
head and drooping bill tip.
