Ruffed Grouse (Bonasa umbellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized grouse with brown or gray mottled
plumage, fan-shaped tail with dark terminal band,
and feathered neck ruff. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Male drums on logs by beating wings rapidly.
Explodes into flight when flushed. Feeds on
buds, leaves, berries, and insects seasonally.

Habitat
-------
Mixed deciduous and coniferous forests with young
aspen stands. Common across northern and
eastern North America's forested areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spruce Grouse in boreal forests. Sharp-tailed Grouse
in more open areas.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Fan tail with dark subterminal band. Male's neck
ruff visible when displaying. Explosive
wing thunder when flushed.
