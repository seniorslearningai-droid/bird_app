Rufous-backed Robin (Turdus rufopalliatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush with deep rufous-orange back, gray
head, white throat with streaks,
orange bill. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for fruit in forest understory. Rare but
regular winter vagrant to Arizona
and New Mexico from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Forest and scrub of western Mexico. Rare winter
visitor to Arizona and New Mexico
canyons.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin has red breast not rufous back.
Clay-colored Thrush all dull
brown without rufous tones.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright rufous-orange back contrasting with gray head.
Arizona/New Mexico winter vagrant.
Orange bill. Streaked white throat.
