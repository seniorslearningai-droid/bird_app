Rufous-capped Warbler (Basileuterus rufifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, rufous crown and cheeks, white supercilium,
olive-green above, bright
yellow underparts. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages low in dense scrub and understory. Cocks
and pumps tail. Rare vagrant to
Arizona and Texas from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub and second growth in mountains of Mexico.
Rare vagrant to Arizona
and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird with rufous cap and cheeks plus
yellow underparts in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous cap and cheeks distinctive. White supercilium.
Bright yellow below. Rare
Arizona and Texas vagrant.
