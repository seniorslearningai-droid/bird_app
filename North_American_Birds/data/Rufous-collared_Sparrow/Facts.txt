Rufous-collared Sparrow (Zonotrichia capensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, gray head with black stripes, rufous
collar, streaked brown back.
About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground. One of commonest birds in South
America. Very rare vagrant
to Texas and Florida.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, roadsides, and gardens throughout Central
and South America. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-crowned Sparrow lacks rufous collar. Chipping
Sparrow has rusty cap.
Rufous collar distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous collar distinctive. Gray-striped head. Very
rare vagrant from Central and
South America.
