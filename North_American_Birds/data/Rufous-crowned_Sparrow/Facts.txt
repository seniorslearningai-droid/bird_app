Rufous-crowned Sparrow (Aimophila ruficeps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brown sparrow with rufous crown, gray face, single black malar stripe, and plain gray underparts.

Behavior
--------
Stays close to the ground in rocky scrub. Rarely flies far when disturbed.

Habitat
-------
Rocky slopes with sparse grass and shrubs in the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chipping Sparrow has white supercilium and black eye line; Rufous-crowned has malar stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous cap, gray face, single black malar stripe on rocky southwestern slopes.
