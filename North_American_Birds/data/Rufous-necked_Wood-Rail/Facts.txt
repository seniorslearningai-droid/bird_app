Rufous-necked Wood-Rail (Aramides axillaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large rail. Rufous-orange head and neck, gray back
and wings, black and white barred flanks, red
legs and bill. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks and forages in mangroves and forest edges.
Loud calls at dusk. Very rare vagrant to
Texas from Mexico and Central America.

Habitat
-------
Mangroves and coastal forest in Mexico and Central
America. Very rare vagrant to southern
Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other wood-rails in neotropics. Rufous neck and
gray body combination distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous-orange head and neck contrasting with gray
body. Red legs and bill. Very rare
vagrant in Texas mangroves.
