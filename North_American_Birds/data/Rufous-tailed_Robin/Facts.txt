Rufous-tailed Robin (Larvivora sibilans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat-flycatcher, olive-brown above with rusty-rufous
rump and tail, scaly-spotted
breast. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense low cover. Sings loud rising whistle.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest and scrub undergrowth of East Asia.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Siberian Blue Robin male brilliant blue. Taiga
Flycatcher sits more upright.
Rufous tail distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous-orange tail contrasting with brown body.
Scaly spots on breast. Very
rare Alaska vagrant from Asia.
