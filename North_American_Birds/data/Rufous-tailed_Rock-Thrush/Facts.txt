Rufous-tailed Rock-Thrush (Monticola saxatilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush. Male blue head and back, orange-red
underparts and tail. Female brown
with scaled pattern. About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches on rocks and boulders. Sings from exposed
rocky perch. Very rare vagrant
to North America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Rocky mountain slopes and open woodland in Europe
and Asia. Very rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue Rock-Thrush male all blue without orange underparts.
No similar bird in
North America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male blue head with orange-red underparts and tail.
Female scaled brown. Very
rare European vagrant.
