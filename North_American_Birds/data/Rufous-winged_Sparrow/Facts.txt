Rufous-winged Sparrow (Peucaea carpalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow with rufous-edged wing coverts forming orange shoulder patch; gray face with two black malar stripes.

Behavior
--------
Sings from tall cactus or shrub after summer rains; forages on ground for seeds; rare and local in range.

Habitat
-------
Desert grassland with mixed grasses and thorny shrubs in southern Arizona near Mexican border.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rufous-crowned Sparrow lacks double malar stripe; Botteri's Sparrow is browner with different face pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Two black whisker stripes; rufous shoulder patch; flat gray head with rufous lateral crown stripe; desert grass.
