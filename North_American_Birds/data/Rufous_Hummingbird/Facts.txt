Rufous Hummingbird (Selasphorus rufus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male is entirely bright rufous-orange with iridescent red-orange throat and small white breast patch.

Behavior
--------
Fiercely defends feeders and flowers. Makes one of the longest migrations relative to body size.

Habitat
-------
Open forests, meadows, and gardens; breeds from the Pacific Northwest to Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Allen's Hummingbird is nearly identical but has green back; Rufous male is all rufous.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-rufous male — the most aggressive hummingbird at feeders.
