Rustic Bunting (Emberiza rustica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male has black head, white supercilium,
chestnut breast band, white
belly. Female streaked. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in woodland. Regular vagrant to
western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Moist forest and scrub of northern Siberia. Regular
vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Reed Bunting male has black throat. Yellow-throated
Bunting has yellow. White
belly with chestnut band distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male black head, white supercilium, chestnut breast
band. Regular fall vagrant
to Aleutian Islands.
