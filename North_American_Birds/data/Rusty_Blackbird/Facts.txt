Rusty Blackbird (Euphagus carolinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium blackbird; male glossy black with pale yellow eye; female grayish; both show rusty edges in fresh fall plumage.

Behavior
--------
Forages in wet woodland edges flipping leaf litter; declining significantly; migrates in mixed blackbird flocks.

Habitat
-------
Boreal bogs and wet forests for breeding; swampy woodlands, flooded fields, and wet thickets in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brewer's Blackbird has purplish gloss and rounder head; Common Grackle has longer keel-shaped tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty feather edges in fall; pale yellow eye; wet woodland habitat; short tail; creaking squeaky vocalizations.
