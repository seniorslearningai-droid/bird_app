Sabine's Gull (Xema sabini)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gull with forked tail. Breeding adult has
dark gray hood, yellow-tipped black bill, bold
black and white and gray wing triangle. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Picks food from surface like a phalarope. Migrates
offshore. Regular pelagic migrant on both coasts
in fall.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Pelagic migrant offshore.
Regular fall migrant off both coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bonaparte's Gull similar size. Sabine's has forked
tail and striking wing pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Striking black, white, and gray wing triangle in
flight. Forked tail. Pelagic migrant
visible offshore.
