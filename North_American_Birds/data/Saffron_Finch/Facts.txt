Saffron Finch (Sicalis flaveola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium finch, brilliant yellow throughout, male with
orange forehead. Female yellowish-
green. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious in flocks, forages for seeds. Bold in
gardens. Introduced to Hawaii
and Puerto Rico.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, gardens, and grasslands. Introduced to
Hawaii and Puerto Rico. Native
to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-fronted Canary smaller and streaked. American
Goldfinch has black wings.
Brilliant yellow throughout distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant yellow throughout with orange forehead on
male. Introduced to Hawaii.
Gregarious seed-eater in open areas.
