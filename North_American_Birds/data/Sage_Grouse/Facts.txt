Sage Grouse (Centrocercus urophasianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large grouse; male has black belly, white breast rosette, spiky tail, and yellow air sacs; female mottled brown.

Behavior
--------
Males display on leks with popping air sac sounds; forages almost exclusively on sagebrush leaves and buds.

Habitat
-------
Sagebrush steppe of western North America; strictly dependent on big sagebrush for food and cover.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gunnison Sage-Grouse is smaller and found only in southwest Colorado; Sharp-tailed Grouse has different tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size; spiky pointed tail; male's filoplume plumes on neck; black belly; sagebrush habitat association.
