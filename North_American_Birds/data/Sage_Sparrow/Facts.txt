Sage Sparrow (Artemisiospiza nevadensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow with gray head, white supercilium, single dark breast spot, brown streaked back, and dark malar stripe.

Behavior
--------
Runs on ground with tail cocked; sings from sagebrush tops; pumps tail while walking; reluctant to fly.

Habitat
-------
Sagebrush flats and alkali scrub of Great Basin; interior subspecies now considered separate species from coastal.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bell's Sparrow is coastal and darker; Black-throated Sparrow lacks breast spot; Brewer's Sparrow more streaked.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Single dark breast spot; gray head; white eye-ring; tail-pumping behavior; sagebrush habitat; dark malar mark.
