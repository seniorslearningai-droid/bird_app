Sage Thrasher (Oreoscoptes montanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pale thrasher with streaked underparts, short bill, white wing bars, and yellow eye.

Behavior
--------
Sings from sagebrush perches. Runs on the ground between shrubs.

Habitat
-------
Sagebrush flats of the Great Basin and Rocky Mountain foothills.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Curve-billed and Brown Thrashers have longer bills; Sage Thrasher is smallest with short bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest thrasher — streaked underparts, short bill, and white wing bars in sagebrush.
