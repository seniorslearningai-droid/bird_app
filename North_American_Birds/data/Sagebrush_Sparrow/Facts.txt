Sagebrush Sparrow (Artemisiospiza nevadensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray-headed sparrow with white eye ring, single malar stripe, and plain white underparts with breast spot.

Behavior
--------
Runs with tail cocked. Rarely flies more than a short distance.

Habitat
-------
Dense sagebrush of the Great Basin.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bell's Sparrow is almost identical but found in coastal California chaparral.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eye ring, single malar stripe, and central breast spot in Great Basin sagebrush.
