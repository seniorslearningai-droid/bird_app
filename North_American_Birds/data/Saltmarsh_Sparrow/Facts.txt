Saltmarsh Sparrow (Ammospiza caudacuta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow, orange-buff face triangle, streaked
breast and sides, flat head.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense saltmarsh grass. Nests at high tide
level, flooding nests periodically.
Extremely difficult to see.

Habitat
-------
Dense saltmarsh grass along Atlantic Coast from Maine
to Virginia. Strictly saltmarsh
specialist.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nelson's Sparrow more orange overall. Seaside Sparrow
darker gray. Orange face
triangle helpful.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-buff face triangle, streaked below. Dense saltmarsh
specialist along Atlantic Coast.
Flat head, secretive.
