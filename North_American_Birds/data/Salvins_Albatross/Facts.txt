Salvin's Albatross (Thalassarche salvini)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium mollymawk albatross. Gray head and neck, dark
back, white body, pale grayish bill with
yellow sides. About 37 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Bounty and Snares Islands, New Zealand.
Very rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Southern Pacific Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North
American Pacific coast, mostly
off California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-capped Albatross has whiter head. Salvin's has
grayish head and pale gray bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Grayish head with pale gray bill. Medium albatross.
Very rare Pacific vagrant from
New Zealand subantarctic.
