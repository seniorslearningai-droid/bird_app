Sanderling (Calidris alba)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pale sandpiper that chases wave edges on sandy beaches. Has black shoulder spot and pale gray back.

Behavior
--------
Runs back and forth following waves on sandy ocean beaches, probing wet sand.

Habitat
-------
Sandy ocean beaches and mudflats during migration; Arctic tundra for breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dunlin is darker; Sanderling is palest small sandpiper and lacks hind toe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Palest small sandpiper — chases waves on sandy beaches, no hind toe.
