Sandhill Crane (Antigone canadensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tall gray bird with red forehead patch, white cheeks, and bustle of drooping rump feathers; long legs and neck.

Behavior
--------
Bugling calls heard from great distances; dances in courtship; migrates in large flocks; omnivorous forager.

Habitat
-------
Prairies, marshes, grain fields, and tundra; huge concentrations at Platte River Nebraska during spring migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Whooping Crane is white with red and black head; Great Blue Heron folds neck in flight; cranes fly with neck extended.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray plumage; red forehead; bustle of drooping feathers over rump; neck extended in flight; bugling calls.
