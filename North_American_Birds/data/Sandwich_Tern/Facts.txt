Sandwich Tern (Thalasseus sandvicensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-large tern with black bill with yellow tip, shaggy black crest, and pale gray upperparts.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives for fish. Nests in colonies often with Royal Terns.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic and Gulf coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Royal Tern has orange bill; Elegant has long curved bill; Sandwich has black bill with yellow tip.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black bill with yellow tip and shaggy crest — combination unique among North American terns.
