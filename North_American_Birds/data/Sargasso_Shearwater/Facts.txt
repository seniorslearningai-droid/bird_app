Sargasso Shearwater (Puffinus lherminieri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small shearwater, dark brown above, white below with
some brown on sides. Tropical Atlantic
species. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Caribbean and Atlantic islands. Forages
in tropical Atlantic; rare vagrant
to North American Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Tropical North Atlantic. Rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Audubon's Shearwater nearly identical. Sargasso represents
Atlantic population previously lumped.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small dark-brown shearwater with white below. Tropical
Atlantic. Distinguished from Audubon's
by range and subtle details.
