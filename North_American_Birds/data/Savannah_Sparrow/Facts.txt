Savannah Sparrow (Passerculus sandwichensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small streaked sparrow with yellow supercilium, notched tail, and variable streaking. Short tail.

Behavior
--------
Abundant and widespread. Sings buzzy insect-like song from low perches in grass.

Habitat
-------
Open habitats including grasslands, marshes, and fields across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Song Sparrow has longer tail and central breast spot; Savannah is shorter-tailed with notch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow supercilium, notched short tail, and buzzy song — most common open-country sparrow.
