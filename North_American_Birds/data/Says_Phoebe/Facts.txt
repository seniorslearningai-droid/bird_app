Say's Phoebe (Sayornis saya)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher with washed-out brown back, pale gray-brown breast, and peach-orange belly.

Behavior
--------
Wags tail constantly. Nests on cliff ledges, buildings, and under bridges.

Habitat
-------
Open and dry habitats including deserts, ranches, and cliff faces in western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Phoebe has white belly; Black Phoebe has black head; Say's Phoebe has orange belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Peach-orange belly, washed-out brown back, and tail-wagging in dry western habitats.
