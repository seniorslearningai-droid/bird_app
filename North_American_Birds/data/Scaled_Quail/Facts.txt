Scaled Quail (Callipepla squamata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Blue-gray quail with scaly pattern on breast and belly, white-tipped crest, and rusty flanks.

Behavior
--------
Prefers running over flying. Found in large coveys in dry desert grassland.

Habitat
-------
Desert grasslands and dry brushlands of the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gambel's Quail has teardrop plume and chestnut flanks; Scaled has cotton-ball crest.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Scaly breast pattern, white-tipped cotton-ball crest, and desert grassland habitat.
