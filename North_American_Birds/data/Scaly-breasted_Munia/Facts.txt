Scaly-breasted Munia (Lonchura punctulata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, rich brown above, white below with
bold brown scalloping on breast
and flanks. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious, large flocks in open areas. Forages
for grass seeds. Common
invasive in Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, roadsides, and open areas. Very common
introduced species throughout
Hawaiian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chestnut Munia has chestnut head. Tricolored Munia
darker with black belly.
Scaly breast pattern distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown scaly pattern on white breast and flanks.
Brown above. Very common
in Hawaiian grasslands.
