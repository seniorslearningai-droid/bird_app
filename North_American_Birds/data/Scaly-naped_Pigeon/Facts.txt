Scaly-naped Pigeon (Patagioenas squamosa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark pigeon. Dark gray-brown overall with
iridescent reddish-purple on head and neck,
scaly pattern on hindneck. Red eye ring. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on fruit in forest canopy. Found in Caribbean;
rare vagrant to Florida Keys. Flies
fast with strong wingbeats.

Habitat
-------
Humid forest and wooded areas in Caribbean islands,
particularly Puerto Rico and Lesser Antilles.
Rare in Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-crowned Pigeon more common in US range.
Scaly-naped darker with purple head gloss.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large dark pigeon with reddish-purple head gloss
and scaly hindneck pattern.
Found in Caribbean forests.
