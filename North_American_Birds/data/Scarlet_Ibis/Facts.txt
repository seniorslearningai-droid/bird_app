Scarlet Ibis (Eudocimus ruber)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brilliant scarlet-red wading bird, same shape as White
Ibis. Red bill, legs, and plumage.
About 25 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in tropical wetlands. Hybridizes with White
Ibis. Very rare vagrant to southern
Florida and Gulf Coast.

Habitat
-------
Tropical South America and Caribbean. Very rare vagrant
to southern Florida and Gulf
Coast; some escapes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White Ibis closely related and hybridizes. Scarlet
Ibis brilliantly red with no white.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant all-scarlet plumage unmistakable. Ibis shape
with downturned bill. Very rare
Florida vagrant or escape.
