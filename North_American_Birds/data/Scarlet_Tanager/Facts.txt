Scarlet Tanager (Piranga olivacea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tanager. Male brilliant scarlet with jet black
wings. Female olive-green.
About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in deciduous forest canopy. One of most
striking North American birds.
Eastern forest breeder.

Habitat
-------
Mature deciduous and mixed forests of eastern United
States. Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Summer Tanager male all red without black wings.
Western Tanager male has black
back with yellow head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male brilliant scarlet with jet black wings unique.
Female olive-green with dark
wing. Eastern deciduous forest.
