Scissor-tailed Flycatcher (Tyrannus forficatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Pale gray flycatcher with salmon-pink sides and axillaries, and enormously long forked tail on adult.

Behavior
--------
Catches insects in graceful aerial sorties. Very long tail used in courtship displays.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands and ranchlands of the south-central United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Fork-tailed Flycatcher is rarer vagrant; Scissor-tailed's pink sides and range are distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely long scissor-like forked tail and salmon-pink sides — unmistakable.
