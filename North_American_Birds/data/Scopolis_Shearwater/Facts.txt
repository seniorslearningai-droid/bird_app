Scopoli's Shearwater (Calonectris diomedea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shearwater, brown above, white below, yellow
bill, pinkish legs. Very similar to
Cory's Shearwater. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Mediterranean breeding shearwater that migrates into
North Atlantic. Rare but regular
off Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean. Rare but increasing vagrant
off Atlantic coast of North America.
Mediterranean breeder.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cory's Shearwater almost identical. Scopoli's has
smaller dark mark on outer primaries.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Cory's Shearwater. Less dark on
outer primaries from below.
Mediterranean counterpart to Cory's.
