Scott's Oriole (Icterus parisorum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has jet-black head, back, and breast with bright lemon-yellow body and wings with white bars.

Behavior
--------
Nests in yucca plants. Sings rich warbling song from prominent perches.

Habitat
-------
Desert mountain slopes with yuccas and pinyon-juniper in the southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hooded Oriole male is orange with black back; Scott's male is yellow with black head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Jet-black head and breast with lemon-yellow body — nests in yucca in the desert southwest.
