Scripps's Murrelet (Synthliboramphus scrippsi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small alcid. Black above, white below. White extends
up onto face, partial white eye ring. Short bill.
About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in rock crevices on offshore California islands.
Chicks jump from nest cliff night of hatching.
Swims and dives for fish.

Habitat
-------
Nests on offshore islands of Southern California and
Baja. Forages in nearshore Pacific waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Guadalupe Murrelet is sister species. Scripps's has
more white on face.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black and white alcid of Southern California waters.
White face pattern extends above and
around eye more than Guadalupe.
