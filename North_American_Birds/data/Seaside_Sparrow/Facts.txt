Seaside Sparrow (Ammospiza maritima)
===========================

Description
-----------
Dark streaked sparrow with yellow lore spot, short rounded tail, flat head, and heavy bill adapted for salt marsh.

Behavior
--------
Sings buzzy insect-like song from marsh vegetation; forages on mud for invertebrates; rarely leaves salt marsh.

Habitat
-------
Salt marshes along Atlantic and Gulf coasts; highly resident and tied to specific marsh grass communities.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nelson's Sparrow has orange wash on face; Saltmarsh Sparrow has sharper streaking and more orange tones.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow lore spot; olive-gray streaked plumage; flat head; salt marsh habitat; dark malar stripe; short tail.
