Sedge Warbler (Acrocephalus schoenobaenus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, streaked brown above with pale supercilium,
buff below. Rusty rump.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings from dense vegetation, often at night. Very
rare vagrant to eastern North
America from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Wetland edges, reed beds, and scrub near water
in Europe and Asia. Very rare
vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Marsh Wren also streaked but has barred back.
Sedge Wren smaller with
finely streaked crown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked brown back, bold pale supercilium, rusty
rump. Rare European vagrant.
Associated with wetland vegetation.
