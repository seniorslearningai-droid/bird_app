Sedge Wren (Cistothorus platensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small wren, streaked brown above with finely streaked
crown, buff below, barred wings.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive, creeps through wet meadows. Nomadic,
may breed in different locations
year to year. Hard to observe.

Habitat
-------
Wet meadows, sedge marshes, and damp grassy areas
across eastern and central North
America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Marsh Wren has bold white supercilium and plain
crown. Sedge lacks the
bold eyebrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked crown (Marsh Wren has plain crown). Subtle
streaking throughout. Wet meadow
habitat, not cattails.
