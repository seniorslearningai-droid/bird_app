Semipalmated Plover (Charadrius semipalmatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small brown plover with single black breast band, orange-yellow legs, and small orange-based bill.

Behavior
--------
Very common migrant on mudflats and beaches. Runs and pauses like other plovers.

Habitat
-------
Mudflats, beaches, and coastal areas during migration across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Piping Plover is much paler; Killdeer has two breast bands; Semipalmated has one band.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brown back, single black breast band, and orange-yellow legs — very common migrant.
