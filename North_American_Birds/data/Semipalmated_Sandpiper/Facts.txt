Semipalmated Sandpiper (Calidris pusilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sandpiper with black legs, stubby straight bill, and gray-brown back with scaly pattern.

Behavior
--------
One of the most numerous sandpipers in migration. Forms enormous flocks.

Habitat
-------
Mudflats and beaches during migration across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Least Sandpiper has yellow legs; Western Sandpiper has longer drooping bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black legs and stubby bill — abundant in coastal mudflats during migration.
