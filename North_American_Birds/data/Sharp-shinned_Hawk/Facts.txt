Sharp-shinned Hawk (Accipiter striatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest accipiter with short rounded wings, long squared tail, and orange-barred underparts.

Behavior
--------
Pursues small birds through dense forest. Common at hawk watch sites in fall.

Habitat
-------
Forests and woodlands across North America; common at hawk migration sites.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cooper's Hawk is larger with rounded tail tip; Sharp-shinned has squared tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smallest accipiter with squared tail — compared to Cooper's Hawk which has rounded tail.
