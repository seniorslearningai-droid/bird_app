Sharp-tailed Grouse (Tympanuchus phasianellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brown and white spotted grouse with short pointed tail; purple air sacs on neck; white underwing visible in flight.

Behavior
--------
Males dance on leks doing rapid foot stomping and cooing displays; flocks in winter; forages for berries and buds.

Habitat
-------
Prairie grasslands, agricultural fields, and forest-grassland edges of northern Great Plains and Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Prairie-chickens have rounded tails and horizontal barring; Ruffed Grouse has fan-shaped tail and forest habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pointed white-edged tail; spotted not barred underparts; purple neck sacs in display; prairie and parkland habitat.
