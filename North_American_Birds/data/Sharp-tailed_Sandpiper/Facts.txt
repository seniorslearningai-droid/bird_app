Sharp-tailed Sandpiper (Calidris acuminata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper. Bright rufous cap, streaked breast
fading to spotted flanks, olive-brown back.
About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in wet grassy areas and mudflats. Migrates
through western Alaska in fall; rare on Pacific
coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Siberia. Migrates through western Alaska.
Winters in Australia. Regular migrant in
western Alaska; rare elsewhere on Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pectoral Sandpiper similar. Sharp-tailed has rufous
cap and spotted (not streaked) lower breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright rufous cap, olive-brown back, and spotted
flanks. Regular fall migrant in
western Alaska.
