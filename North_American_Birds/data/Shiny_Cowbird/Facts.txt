Shiny Cowbird (Molothrus bonariensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium cowbird. Male entirely glossy black with purple
iridescence. Female dull brown.
About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Brood parasite like other cowbirds. Spreading northward
into Florida and southeastern
United States from Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Open fields, farmland, and brushy areas. Established
in Florida and Caribbean.
Native to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Brown-headed Cowbird male has brown head. Rusty Blackbird
thinner bill. All-glossy
black male distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male all glossy black with purple sheen. Female dull
brown. Brood parasite.
Expanding range in Florida.
