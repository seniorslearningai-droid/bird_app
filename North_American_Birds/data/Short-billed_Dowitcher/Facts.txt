Short-billed Dowitcher (Limnodromus griseus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shorebird with long bill, rusty-and-white speckled breeding plumage, and white wedge on back.

Behavior
--------
Probes in sewing-machine fashion. Prefers saltwater habitats. Mellow tu-tu-tu call.

Habitat
-------
Coastal mudflats and beaches during migration; Arctic tundra for breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long-billed Dowitcher prefers freshwater; Short-billed prefers coastal salt flats and gives soft call.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Mellow tu-tu-tu call and preference for coastal salt flats versus Long-billed's preference for freshwater.
