Short-billed Gull (Larus brachyrhynchus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull, pale gray back, small stubby bill, large
dark eye. White body, yellow-green bill.
About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pacific coast counterpart to Common Gull of Europe.
Forages on tidal flats and coastlines.
Common in Pacific Northwest winter.

Habitat
-------
Pacific coast tidal flats, harbors, and estuaries.
Breeds in western Canada and Alaska;
winters on Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-billed Gull similar but larger with thicker bill.
Short-billed has distinctly small stubby bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small stubby bill and large dark eye. Pacific coast
species. Pale gray back, white body.
Winters on coastal beaches and flats.
