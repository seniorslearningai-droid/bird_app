Short-eared Owl (Asio flammeus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium owl with buffy streaked plumage, yellow eyes, black eye patches, and tiny barely visible ear tufts.

Behavior
--------
Hunts voles in low quartering flight at dawn and dusk; roosts on ground; wanders with prey availability.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, marshes, tundra, and agricultural fields; nomadic following vole population cycles.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long-eared Owl has prominent ear tufts and orange facial disc; Northern Harrier has similar buoyant flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow eyes with black mascara marks; buoyant floppy flight low over fields; daytime hunting; open country.
