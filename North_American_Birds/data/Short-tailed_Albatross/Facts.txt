Short-tailed Albatross (Phoebastria albatrus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large albatross, white body and head with golden-buff
wash, pink bill, dark wings with white
shoulder patches. About 36 inches.

Behavior
--------
Critically endangered; recovering from near extinction.
Breeds on Torishima Island, Japan.
Regular rare sighting in North Pacific.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean. Rare but regular off Alaska and
Pacific coast. Recovering endangered
species from Japan.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Laysan Albatross lacks golden head wash. Short-tailed's
large pink bill diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Golden-buff head wash and large pink bill. White
back with dark wings. Endangered
species recovering in North Pacific.
