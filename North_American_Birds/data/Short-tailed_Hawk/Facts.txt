Short-tailed Hawk (Buteo brachyurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small buteo, two morphs: light morph white below
and dark morph all dark. Clean white
underparts in light morph. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Soars high and dives on birds. Florida resident;
very local in southern Florida forest.
Rare in Texas and Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Dense hammock and cypress swamp forest in southern
Florida. Rare in Texas and
Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-winged Hawk similar light morph. Short-tailed's
very clean white underparts distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Light morph: brilliant clean white below. Dark morph:
all dark. Both show clean two-toned
pattern. Florida specialist.
