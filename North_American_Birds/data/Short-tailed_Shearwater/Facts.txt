Short-tailed Shearwater (Ardenna tenuirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dark shearwater, dark brown overall with pale
underwing and short stubby bill.
About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in Australia; migrates north through Pacific
to Bering Sea. Abundant in North
Pacific; common off Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific Ocean. Common offshore Pacific coast
particularly in winter. Migrates from
Australian breeding grounds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sooty Shearwater similar but has more silvery underwing.
Short-tailed has shorter bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark shearwater with short stubby bill. Pale grayish
underwing. Very common in North
Pacific winter, abundant off Alaska.
