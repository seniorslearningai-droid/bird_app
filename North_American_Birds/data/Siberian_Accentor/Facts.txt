Siberian Accentor (Prunella montanella)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow-like bird, streaked brown above, orange-buff
below with white throat,
bold dark facial pattern. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in dense scrub. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska and occasionally
farther inland from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Mountain scrub and taiga of Siberia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and
Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
House Sparrow lacks orange-buff underparts and bold
face. No similar accentor
in North America.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange-buff underparts with dark facial pattern. White
throat. Rare Alaska vagrant
from Siberia.
