Siberian Blue Robin (Larvivora cyane)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat-flycatcher. Male brilliant blue above,
white below with blue side band.
Female brown above. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense undergrowth near ground. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska from
Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest undergrowth of Siberia and East Asia.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting smaller and different shape. No other
bright blue robin in Alaska.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male brilliant blue above with white underparts.
Blue band on sides. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
