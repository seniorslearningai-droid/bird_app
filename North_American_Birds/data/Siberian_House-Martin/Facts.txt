Siberian House-Martin (Delichon lagopodum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small swallow, blue-black above, white rump, white
underparts. Slightly forked tail.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Colonial nester on cliffs. Forages over open areas.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutian Islands.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, cliffs, and river valleys in Siberia
and northeastern Asia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common House-Martin very similar but different range.
Cliff Swallow has rusty
rump. Tree Swallow has green back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump, blue-black above, white below. Tiny
swallow. Alaska vagrant from
Siberia. Slightly forked tail.
