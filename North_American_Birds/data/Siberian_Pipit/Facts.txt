Siberian Pipit (Anthus rubescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pipit, olive-brown streaked above, buff-white
below with dark streaking. Very
similar to American Pipit. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in open areas. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Siberia.
Recently separated from American Pipit.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, tundra, and meadows in Siberia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Pipit nearly identical; best separated by
subtle plumage and call differences.
Range overlap in Alaska.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very similar to American Pipit. Subtle differences
in streaking and buff tones.
Western Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
