Siberian Rubythroat (Calliope calliope)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat. Male has brilliant red throat bordered
by white, brown above, white
below. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense thickets. Rich melodious song. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutian Islands from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub, forest edge undergrowth in Siberia.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska
and Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bluethroat has blue throat, breeds in Alaska.
No other red-throated small bird
in Alaska habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant red throat on male distinctive. Brown above,
white below. Rare Alaska
vagrant from Siberia.
