Siberian Sand-Plover (Anarhynchus mongolus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium plover. Breeding adult has rufous-orange breast
band, gray-brown back, black mask. Non-breeding
plain gray-brown. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on sandy beaches and mudflats for invertebrates.
Rare vagrant to Pacific coast from Asian
breeding grounds.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on central Asian mountains and Siberian tundra.
Winters on Asian and African coasts.
Rare vagrant to Pacific coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Sand-Plover larger with heavier bill. Recently
split; formerly called Mongolian Plover.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous breast band in breeding plumage. Smaller
and slimmer bill than Greater Sand-Plover.
Rare vagrant on Pacific coast.
