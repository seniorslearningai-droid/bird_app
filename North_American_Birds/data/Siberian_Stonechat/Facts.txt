Siberian Stonechat (Saxicola maurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small chat. Male black head and back, white neck
patch, orange-rufous breast. Female
brown. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches upright on low vegetation, bobs tail. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutian Islands from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Open country with scattered scrub in Siberia and
Asia. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
European Stonechat very similar. American Robin much
larger with red breast. No
similar chat in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male black head with white neck patch and orange
breast. Upright perching posture.
Rare Alaska vagrant.
