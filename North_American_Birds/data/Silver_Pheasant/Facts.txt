Silver Pheasant (Lophura nycthemera)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large pheasant. Male white above with fine black
markings, blue-black below, red
facial skin. About 40 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in dense forest. Shy and secretive.
Small introduced populations
in North America.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest and woodland. Small introduced populations
in parts of North America.
Native to Southeast Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-necked Pheasant male different coloration. No
other large pheasant with white
back in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male white above with black markings and blue-black
below. Introduced populations.
Red facial skin distinctive.
