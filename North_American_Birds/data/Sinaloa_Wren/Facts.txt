Sinaloa Wren (Thryophilus sinaloa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium wren, rusty-brown above with barred wings
and tail, white supercilium,
streaked head. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulks in dense thorn scrub. Loud rich song.
Very rare vagrant to southeastern
Arizona from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub of western Mexico. Very rare
vagrant to extreme southeastern Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bewick's Wren larger with longer tail and different
habitat. Canyon Wren has white
throat and different habitat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rusty-brown wren with bold white supercilium in
Arizona thorn scrub. Very rare.
Loud melodious song.
