Slate-throated Redstart (Myioborus miniatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, slate-gray above, bright red breast,
white outer tail feathers fanned.
About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fans and droops wings and tail to flush insects.
Rare vagrant to Arizona and
New Mexico from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak mountain forests of Mexico and Central America.
Rare vagrant to Arizona
and New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Painted Redstart has white wing patch. American Redstart
different pattern. Red breast
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slate-gray with red breast, white outer tail feathers.
Fan display behavior. Rare
Arizona vagrant from Mexico.
