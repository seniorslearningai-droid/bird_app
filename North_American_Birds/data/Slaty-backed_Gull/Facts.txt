Slaty-backed Gull (Larus schistisagus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, very dark slate-gray back, pink legs,
broad white tertial crescent. "String-of-pearls"
wingtip pattern. About 25 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in Siberia; winters in Asia. Rare but regular
visitor to western Alaska; occasional
vagrant to Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Rocky Siberian coastlines. Rare visitor to western
Alaska and Aleutian Islands; occasional
on Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Gull similar but paler gray back. Slaty-backed's
very dark back and pink legs distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very dark slate back with pink legs. Broad white
tertial crescent, "string-of-pearls" wingtip.
Asian species rare on Pacific coast.
