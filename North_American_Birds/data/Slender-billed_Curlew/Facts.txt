Slender-billed Curlew (Numenius tenuirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium curlew, possibly extinct. Brown streaked plumage
with distinctive round dark spots on flanks,
slender bill. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Formerly wintered in Mediterranean; now possibly extinct.
Last confirmed sighting 1995. Listed as critically
endangered or extinct.

Habitat
-------
Bred in western Siberia. Wintered in Mediterranean
wetlands. Possibly extinct; no confirmed
sightings since 1990s.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eurasian Curlew similar but lacks round flank spots.
Slender-billed's spots diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Round dark spots on flanks diagnostic if seen.
Possibly extinct since the 1990s.
Extremely rare or extinct.
