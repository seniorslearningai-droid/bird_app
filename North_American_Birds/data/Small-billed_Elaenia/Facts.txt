Small-billed Elaenia (Elaenia parvirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, olive above, pale below, two wing
bars, small bill. Hidden white crown
patch. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migrant; breeds in South America, winters in tropics.
Very rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
South American woodlands. Very rare vagrant to Atlantic
coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other elaenias very similar. Small bill and plain
olive plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small olive flycatcher with small bill, two wing bars.
Very rare Atlantic vagrant from
South America.
