Smew (Mergellus albellus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small merganser. Male is striking white with black
eye patch, back, and wing marks. Female gray-brown
with chestnut cap and white cheeks. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish and aquatic invertebrates. Nests
in tree cavities. Rare vagrant to North
America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in northern Eurasian boreal forests near
lakes and rivers. Winters on European lakes.
Rare vagrant to North American Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bufflehead has similar small size. Male Smew's
white plumage unique among North American ducks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's nearly all-white plumage with black eye patch
and back distinctive. Female's rufous
cap and white face pattern.
