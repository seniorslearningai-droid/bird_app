Smith's Longspur (Calcarius pictus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting-like bird, streaked brown above, rich
buff-orange below with black-and-white
face pattern on male. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on ground in flocks. Polyandrous breeding system.
Winters in specific grass
fields, often hard to find.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on tundra, winters in short-grass fields
of Great Plains from Oklahoma
to Tennessee.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lapland Longspur male has rusty nape. Chestnut-collared
Longspur has chestnut collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rich buff-orange underparts, male has black-and-white
face. Specific winter grass
fields. Secretive ground bird.
