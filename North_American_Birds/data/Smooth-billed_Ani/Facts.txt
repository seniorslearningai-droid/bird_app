Smooth-billed Ani (Crotophaga ani)
===========================

Description
-----------
All-black cuckoo family bird with long floppy tail
and large laterally compressed bill with curved
ridge on top. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Lives in social groups; multiple pairs share nest.
Follows cattle to catch disturbed insects. Found in
southern Florida; declining in US range.

Habitat
-------
Open brushy areas, fields, and scrubby vegetation
in Florida, Caribbean, and South America. Found
near livestock in tropical lowlands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Groove-billed Ani very similar but smaller with
grooved bill. Grackles lack compressed bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-black with enormous smooth-ridged bill unique
among Florida birds. Long floppy tail and
clumsy flight distinctive.
