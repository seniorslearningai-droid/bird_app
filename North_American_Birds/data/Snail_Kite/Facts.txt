Snail Kite (Rostrhamus sociabilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium raptor; male slate gray with red eyes and orange cere; female brown streaked; strongly hooked bill.

Behavior
--------
Snatches apple snails from water surface; soars over marshes; endangered in Florida; highly social nester.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes and lakes in Florida, especially Lake Okeechobee and associated wetlands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Harrier has owl-like face and longer tail; Hook-billed Kite in tropics has very different plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely hooked narrow bill; white base of tail; red eye and orange cere; slow buoyant flight over marshes.
