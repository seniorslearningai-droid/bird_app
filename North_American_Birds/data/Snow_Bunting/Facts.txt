Snow Bunting (Plectrophenax nivalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Breeding male mostly white with black back and wingtips; winter birds buffy with white wings contrasting in flight.

Behavior
--------
Flocks wheel in flight showing white wing flashes; forages on ground for seeds; breeds on Arctic tundra.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra for breeding; winters in open fields, beaches, and coastal dunes of northern US and Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
McKay's Bunting is nearly all white with less black; Lapland Longspur shows less white in wings in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extensive white in wings visible in flight; whitish overall in winter; black and white breeding plumage; open fields.
