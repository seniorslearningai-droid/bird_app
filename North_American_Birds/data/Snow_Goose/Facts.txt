Snow Goose (Anser caerulescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
White goose with black wingtips and pink bill with dark grinning patch; blue morph has dark body and white head.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in enormous flocks; forages in agricultural fields; overabundant and damaging Arctic tundra habitat.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra; winters in coastal marshes, rice fields, and agricultural areas across southern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ross's Goose is smaller with stubbier bill and no grinning patch; Cackling Goose is very different darker species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White body with black wingtips; pink bill with black grinning patch; large flocks; blue morph also common.
