Snowy Egret (Egretta thula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small all-white egret with black bill, black legs, and bright yellow feet; breeding adults have wispy plumes.

Behavior
--------
Actively forages by shuffling yellow feet to stir prey; more animated than Great Egret; colonial nester.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater and saltwater marshes, mudflats, ponds, and coastal lagoons throughout Americas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Egret is much larger with yellow bill; Cattle Egret is shorter with yellow bill and buff breeding plumes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow feet contrasting with black legs; black bill; small size; wispy breeding plumes; active foraging style.
