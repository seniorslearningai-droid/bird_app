Snowy Owl (Bubo scandiacus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large white owl; adult male nearly pure white; females and young have dark barring; yellow eyes; no ear tufts.

Behavior
--------
Hunts lemmings on tundra; irrupts south in winter during low prey years; perches prominently on ground or posts.

Habitat
-------
Open Arctic tundra for breeding; open country, airports, and beaches during southern winter irruptions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Barn Owl is smaller with heart-shaped face; Short-eared Owl is buffy brown; no other large white owl exists.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large white owl; yellow eyes; diurnal activity; perches on ground or fence posts; open treeless landscapes.
