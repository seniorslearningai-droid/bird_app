Snowy Plover (Charadrius nivosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pale sandy-colored plover with dark partial breast band, dark ear patch, and thin dark bill; slender build.

Behavior
--------
Runs quickly on beaches; nests in open sand scrapes; chick precocial; threatened by beach disturbance.

Habitat
-------
Sandy beaches, alkali flats, and dry lakeshores of western US and Gulf Coast; year-round on coastal beaches.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Piping Plover has orange bill base and complete or thicker breast band; Wilson's Plover has thicker bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale sandy plumage; incomplete dark breast patches on sides; thin dark bill; dark ear patch; slim proportions.
