Social Flycatcher (Myiozetetes similis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, olive-brown above, yellow below,
gray head with white supercilium and
hidden orange crown patch. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and aggressive; mobs much larger birds. Common
resident from Mexico south. Very rare
vagrant to southern Texas.

Habitat
-------
Forest edge and open areas from Mexico to South America.
Very rare vagrant to
southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Kiskadee and other tyrannids similar. Social Flycatcher
smaller with gray head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray head with white supercilium, yellow below. Small
and bold. Very rare vagrant from
Mexico to Texas.
