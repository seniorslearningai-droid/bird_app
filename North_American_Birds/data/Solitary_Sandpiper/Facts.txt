Solitary Sandpiper (Tringa solitaria)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper with dark olive-brown back heavily spotted white, white eye-ring, and boldly barred tail.

Behavior
--------
Usually alone as name suggests; bobs body when foraging; nests in old songbird nests in boreal trees.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forest ponds for nesting; freshwater pools, streams, and flooded fields during migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Spotted Sandpiper teeters continuously; Lesser Yellowlegs has yellow legs and longer bill; Green Sandpiper similar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Boldly barred white outer tail; white eye-ring; spotted back; dark legs; bobs body; solitary freshwater habitat.
