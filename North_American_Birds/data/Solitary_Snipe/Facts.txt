Solitary Snipe (Gallinago solitaria)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large snipe. Pale grayish-brown streaked plumage
with rufous tones, long bill, rather pale and
washed out appearance. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Solitary; rarely flushes. Forages along mountain
streams in Asia. Very rare vagrant to
western Alaska from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Mountain streams and wet rocky areas in Asia.
Winters in South and East Asia. Very rare
vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Snipe similar. Solitary Snipe larger and
paler with rufous tones.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large, pale snipe. Rufous tones. Found along
mountain streams. Very rare vagrant
in western Alaska.
