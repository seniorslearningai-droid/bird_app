Song Sparrow (Melospiza melodia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium brown sparrow with heavy brown streaks converging in large central breast spot; long rounded tail; gray face.

Behavior
--------
Sings complex melodious songs from exposed perches; pumps tail in flight; common backyard species.

Habitat
-------
Thickets, gardens, marsh edges, and shrubby areas across most of North America; highly variable subspecies.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Lincoln's Sparrow is buffier with finer streaking; Savannah Sparrow has notched tail and yellow lore.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large central breast spot; heavy streaking; long rounded tail pumped in flight; variable but streaked throughout.
