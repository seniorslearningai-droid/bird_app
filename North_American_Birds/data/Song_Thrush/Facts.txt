Song Thrush (Turdus philomelos)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, warm brown above, white to buff
below with bold dark arrowhead
spots. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Famous for smashing snail shells against stones.
Repeats song phrases two or
three times. Rare vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Woodland, gardens, hedgerows across Europe. Rare
vagrant to eastern and western
coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mistle Thrush larger with gray-brown tones. Redwing
has red flank patch.
American Robin has red-orange breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark arrowhead spots on pale buff breast. Warm
brown upperparts. Rare
European vagrant. Song repetitions distinctive.
