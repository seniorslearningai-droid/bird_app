Sooty Grouse (Dendragapus fuliginosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large forest grouse. Male sooty gray-brown with
yellow-orange eye combs and bare yellow-orange neck
sacs. Female cryptically mottled brown. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Males hoot from treetops in spring. Moves upslope
in winter to feed on conifer needles. More
arboreal than other North American grouse.

Habitat
-------
Pacific coast coniferous forests from Alaska to
California. Prefers dense fir and hemlock
forests on mountain slopes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dusky Grouse nearly identical; separated by range
(west of Rockies vs. east).

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's yellow-orange neck sacs and eye combs.
Sooty gray-brown plumage. Found in
Pacific coast conifer forests.
