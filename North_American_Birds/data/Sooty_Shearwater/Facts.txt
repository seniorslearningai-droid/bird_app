Sooty Shearwater (Ardenna grisea)
===========================

Description
-----------
All dark brown shearwater with pale silvery-white wing linings contrasting with dark upperwing; long pointed wings.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in enormous flocks offshore; stiff-winged gliding flight; dives for fish and squid; transoceanic migrant.

Habitat
-------
Open ocean; one of most abundant seabirds; breeds in Southern Hemisphere; visits North Pacific and Atlantic coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Short-tailed Shearwater is very similar but has steeper forehead; dark-phase shearwaters lack white wing linings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark brown body; pale silvery underwing linings; long narrow wings; stiff shearwearing flight over open ocean.
