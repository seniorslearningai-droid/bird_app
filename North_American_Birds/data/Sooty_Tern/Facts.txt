Sooty Tern (Onychoprion fuscatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium tern with jet black upperparts, white forehead and underparts, deeply forked tail, and black bill.

Behavior
--------
Colonial breeder on tropical islands; rarely lands on water; snatches fish from surface; raucous calls.

Habitat
-------
Tropical and subtropical oceans worldwide; breeds on coral islands including Dry Tortugas in Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bridled Tern has white collar and browner back; Aleutian Tern has gray underparts; different geographic ranges.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black back contrasting with white forehead and underparts; long forked tail; tropical ocean habitat; wide white forehead.
