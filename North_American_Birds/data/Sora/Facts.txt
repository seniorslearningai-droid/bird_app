Sora (Porzana carolina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plump rail with yellow bill, black face mask, gray breast, and brown barred flanks; short upturned tail.

Behavior
--------
Gives descending whinny call; secretive in marsh vegetation; flushes reluctantly; very common but rarely seen.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes with cattails and sedges throughout North America; migrates to Central America in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Virginia Rail is larger with long reddish bill; Yellow Rail is smaller and very secretive with white wing patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow bill; black face patch; gray underparts with barred flanks; short tail cocked up; freshwater marsh calls.
