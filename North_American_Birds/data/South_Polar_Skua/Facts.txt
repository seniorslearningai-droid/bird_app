South Polar Skua (Stercorarius maccormicki)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large powerful seabird. Variable plumage from all-
dark brown to pale-headed form. White wing flash,
broad wings. About 21 inches.

Behavior
--------
Pirates food from other seabirds. Breeds in Antarctic;
migrates to northern oceans. Seen offshore
on both coasts.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Antarctic. Pelagic migrant to northern
oceans in austral winter. Seen offshore
both coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Skua very similar. South Polar Skua has
pale morph; browner Great Skua.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale-headed morph distinctive. Dark morph nearly
identical to Great Skua. White wing
flash in flight.
