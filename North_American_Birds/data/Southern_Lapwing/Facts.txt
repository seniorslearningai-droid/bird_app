Southern Lapwing (Vanellus chilensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large plover with black breast, gray-brown back,
pinkish-red facial skin, backward-pointing head crest,
carpal spurs. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Loud, aggressive defender of territory. Common
in South American grasslands. Rare vagrant to
Caribbean and southern US from South America.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, wetland edges, and agricultural
areas in South America. Rare vagrant to
Caribbean and southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Lapwing similar in shape but different
coloring. No other lapwing in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black breast band, gray back, red facial skin,
and backward-pointing crest distinctive.
Loud persistent calling.
