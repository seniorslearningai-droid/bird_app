Southern Martin (Progne elegans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large swallow. Male all glossy blue-black. Female
brown above, pale below with
streaked breast. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aerial forager over open country. Very rare vagrant
to North America from South
America.

Habitat
-------
Open country, cliffs, and buildings in South America.
Very rare vagrant to North
America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Purple Martin very similar; male indistinguishable
in field. Female Purple Martin
has less streaking below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Purple Martin. Male all dark.
Female streaked. South American
species, very rare vagrant.
