Spectacled Eider (Somateria fischeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sea duck. Male has white body, black breast,
green head with bold white eye patches rimmed in black
creating a spectacle pattern. Female brown. About 21 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for bivalves, crustaceans, and marine
invertebrates. Nests on coastal tundra. Winters
in Bering Sea polynyas. Threatened species.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on coastal tundra of western Alaska and
Siberia. Winters in large sea ice openings
in the Bering Sea.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other eiders lack the spectacle face pattern.
Male unmistakable among North American sea ducks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's bold white eye patch rimmed in black and
green gives distinctive spectacled appearance.
Female has faint pale eye ring.
