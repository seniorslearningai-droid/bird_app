Spoon-billed Sandpiper (Calidris pygmaea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Critically endangered tiny sandpiper with extraordinary
spatula-shaped bill tip. Reddish-brown breeding
plumage, white below. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sweeps bill side to side in shallow water. Critically
endangered; fewer than 400 remain. Rare vagrant
to Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Chukotka Peninsula, Russia. Winters in
Southeast Asian mudflats. Extremely rare vagrant
to Pacific coast of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-necked Stint similar size. Bill shape unmistakable
when seen.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Spatula-shaped bill tip absolutely diagnostic. Breeding
adult has reddish face. Critically endangered;
any sighting is exceptional.
