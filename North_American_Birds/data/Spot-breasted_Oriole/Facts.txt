Spot-breasted Oriole (Icterus pectoralis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large oriole, orange with black back and wings,
black spots on breast sides,
white wing bars. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in flowering trees. Established introduced
population in southeastern Florida.
Beautiful loud song.

Habitat
-------
Suburban areas, gardens, and open woodland. Introduced
to southeastern Florida.
Native to Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Baltimore Oriole lacks spots on breast. Scott's Oriole
has black head. Spots
on breast distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black spots on breast sides distinctive. Orange with
black back. Southeastern Florida
introduced population.
