Spotted Dove (Spilopelia chinensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium dove with pinkish-brown body, long tail,
and distinctive black hindneck collar with white
spots like a necklace. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds and grain. Found in
pairs or small flocks. Introduced from Asia;
established in southern California and Hawaii.

Habitat
-------
Urban parks, gardens, agricultural areas, and open
woodland. Introduced populations in Los Angeles
area and Hawaiian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eurasian Collared-Dove similar size but lacks spotted
neck collar. Mourning Dove slimmer.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black and white spotted collar on hindneck unique
among North American doves. Brown-pink plumage
and long rounded tail.
