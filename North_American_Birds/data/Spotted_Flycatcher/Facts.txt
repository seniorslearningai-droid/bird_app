Spotted Flycatcher (Muscicapa striata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, gray-brown above with streaked
crown, white below with streaked
breast. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies from upright perch returning to same spot.
Rare vagrant to North America
from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and gardens in Europe and Asia.
Very rare vagrant to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray-streaked Flycatcher has gray-brown breast streaking
without crown streaking.
European vagrant context helpful.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked crown distinguishes from other Muscicapa flycatchers.
Streaked breast. Very rare
European vagrant to East Coast.
