Spotted Owl (Strix occidentalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium owl with dark brown plumage and white spots covering breast and belly; dark eyes; no ear tufts; round head.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal hunter of flying squirrels and woodrats; hooting calls; threatened by Barred Owl competition.

Habitat
-------
Old-growth and mature forests of Pacific Coast and Rocky Mountains; needs large trees and dense canopy.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Barred Owl has streaked belly and barred breast; Barn Owl is paler with heart-shaped face; larger owls have ear tufts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark brown with white spots on belly; dark brown eyes; old-growth forest; no ear tufts; round facial disc.
