Spotted Rail (Pardirallus maculatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium rail. Dark brown with bold white spots on
breast and flanks, long red bill with green base,
red legs. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive; skulks in dense marsh vegetation. Rarely
seen. Rare vagrant to US from Caribbean
and South America.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes and wet grasslands in Caribbean,
South America, and occasionally Mexico. Rare
vagrant to Gulf Coast states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sora and other small rails. Spotted Rail's bold
white spots and red bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white spots on dark brown plumage with bright
red bill. Found in dense marsh
vegetation. Very secretive.
