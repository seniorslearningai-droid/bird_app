Spotted Redshank (Tringa erythropus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sandpiper. Breeding adult entirely sooty black
with white spots; non-breeding pale gray above,
white below. Long red legs. About 12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages by wading and swimming. Rare vagrant to
both coasts of North America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Eurasian subarctic tundra. Winters on
African and Asian coasts. Rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Redshank has white wing patches. Breeding
Spotted Redshank all-black unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding adult all-black with white spots unmistakable.
Non-breeding pale gray. Long red
legs and bill.
