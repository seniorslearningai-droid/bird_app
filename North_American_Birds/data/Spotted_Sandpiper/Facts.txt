Spotted Sandpiper (Actitis macularius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sandpiper; breeding plumage has bold black spots on white underparts; orange bill; constantly teeters body.

Behavior
--------
Teeters rear end up and down constantly while walking; low stiff-winged flight; polyandrous breeding system.

Habitat
-------
Shorelines of streams, lakes, and rivers throughout North America; very widespread migratory species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Solitary Sandpiper bobs but has spotted back and different tail pattern; Common Sandpiper is very similar Eurasian.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Constant teetering bobbing motion; spotted breast in summer; orange bill with dark tip; stiff shallow wingbeats in flight.
