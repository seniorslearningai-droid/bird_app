Spotted Towhee (Pipilo maculatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sparrow; male has black hood, white-spotted black back, rufous sides, and white belly; red eye; white corners in tail.

Behavior
--------
Scratches vigorously in leaf litter with both feet; sings loud 'drink your tea'; dense shrub thicket dweller.

Habitat
-------
Dense brushy undergrowth, chaparral, and shrubby forest edges of western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Towhee is similar but lacks white spots on back; Rufous-sided Towhee was former combined name.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White spots on black back and wings; rufous sides; black hood; red eye; white outer tail corners; ground scratching.
