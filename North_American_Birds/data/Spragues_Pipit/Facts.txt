Sprague's Pipit (Anthus spragueii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Buffy streaked pipit with pale face, buffy streaked breast, pale pinkish legs, and white outer tail feathers.

Behavior
--------
Flies up in spiraling song flight for extended periods; very secretive on ground; walks rather than hops.

Habitat
-------
Native short and mixed-grass prairies of northern Great Plains; declining due to grassland conversion.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Pipit has gray back and bobs tail; Horned Lark has black face marks; Savannah Sparrow has notched tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Buffy streaked appearance; pale pinkish legs; white outer tail feathers; extended spiraling song flight over grasslands.
