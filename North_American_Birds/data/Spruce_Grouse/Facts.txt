Spruce Grouse (Canachites canadensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Chicken-sized grouse with dark plumage. Male has
black throat and breast, red eye combs. Female
cryptically barred brown. About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly confiding; often called "fool hen." Feeds
primarily on spruce and fir needles in winter.
Year-round resident of boreal forests.

Habitat
-------
Dense boreal spruce and fir forests of Canada
and northern US. Found in wet coniferous
forests with dense understory.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sooty and Dusky Grouse in similar western habitats.
Male's black and red pattern distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's black throat and breast with red eye combs.
Female barred brown in spruce forest.
Very tame and approachable.
