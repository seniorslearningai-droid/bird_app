Stejneger's Petrel (Pterodroma longirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gadfly petrel very similar to Cook's Petrel.
Darker cap, slightly darker gray above.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Juan Fernandez Islands, Chile. Migrates into
North Pacific; rare vagrant off
California.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific in non-breeding season. Rare vagrant
off Pacific coast; breeds on
Chilean islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cook's Petrel almost identical. Stejneger's has darker
gray cap contrasting with back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very similar to Cook's Petrel; darker cap contrasting
more with back. Rare Pacific coast
vagrant requiring careful scrutiny.
