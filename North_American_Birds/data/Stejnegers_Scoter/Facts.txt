Stejneger's Scoter (Melanitta stejnegeri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sea duck. Male is all black with white wing
patch and distinctly knobbed bill with orange-yellow
sides. Female dark brown. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mollusks in coastal marine waters.
Breeds in Siberian taiga lakes. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska; Asian counterpart of White-winged Scoter.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on large Siberian lakes and rivers.
Winters along East Asian coast. Rare in
western Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-winged Scoter nearly identical. Best
distinguished by bill pattern and range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's distinctly knobbed bill with orange coloring
and white wing patches in flight. Nearly
impossible to separate from White-winged Scoter in field.
