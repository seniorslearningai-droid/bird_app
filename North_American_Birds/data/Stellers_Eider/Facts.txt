Steller's Eider (Polysticta stelleri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Smallest eider duck. Male has white head with
green face patch, black eye ring, white body
with rusty underparts. Female mottled brown. About 18 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mollusks, crustaceans, and marine
invertebrates. Forms large flocks on Alaskan
coast in winter. Threatened species.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra in Alaska and Siberia.
Winters along Alaska Peninsula and Aleutian
Islands in shallow coastal waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other eider species are larger. Male's distinctive
color pattern unlike other eiders.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small size compared to other eiders. Male's white
and rust coloring with green face patch
unmistakable. Female's blue speculum.
