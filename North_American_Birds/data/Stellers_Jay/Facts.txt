Steller's Jay (Cyanocitta stelleri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large crested jay with sooty black head and upper body and vivid blue wings, tail, and lower body; white streaks on forehead.

Behavior
--------
Bold and loud; mimics hawks and other birds; caches acorns; dominates feeders; noisy flocking in winter.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests of western North America from Alaska to Central America; common in mountains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Blue Jay has white in wings; Scrub-jays lack crest; Pinyon Jay is all blue without crest and different body shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black head with prominent crest; brilliant blue body; white forehead streaks; western mountain conifer forests.
