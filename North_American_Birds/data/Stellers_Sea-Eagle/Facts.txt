Steller's Sea-Eagle (Haliaeetus pelagicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest eagle in the world. Dark black-brown with
white shoulders, thighs, and tail. Massive
orange-yellow bill. About 42 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts salmon and other fish; powerful predator. Rare
vagrant from Siberia to western
Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Coasts and rivers of eastern Russia. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska and
Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bald Eagle lacks white shoulder patches and huge bill.
Steller's Sea-Eagle unmistakably large.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous size with white shoulder patches. Massive
orange bill. Very rare vagrant
from Russia to Alaska.
