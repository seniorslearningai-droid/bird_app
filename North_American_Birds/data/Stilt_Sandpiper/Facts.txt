Stilt Sandpiper (Calidris himantopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper with long greenish-yellow legs, drooped bill tip, and in breeding plumage rufous ear patch with barred underparts.

Behavior
--------
Wades deeply and sews bill through mud like dowitcher; often with dowitchers in shallow mudflats.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra; migrates through interior wetlands, mudflats, and shallow ponds; winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dowitchers have longer straight bills; Dunlin is smaller; Lesser Yellowlegs has bright yellow legs and straight bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long greenish legs; slightly drooped bill; sewing sewing motion feeding; rufous ear patch in breeding plumage.
