Streak-backed Oriole (Icterus pustulatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium oriole, orange-red with black-streaked back,
black throat, white wing bars.
About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in trees for insects and nectar. Rare vagrant
to California, Arizona, and
New Mexico from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Thorn forest and woodland of Mexico. Rare vagrant
to southwestern United States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hooded Oriole all-orange back. Bullock's Oriole has
bold white wing patch.
Streaked back distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked black-and-orange back unique. Orange-red body.
Rare fall and winter vagrant
from Mexico.
