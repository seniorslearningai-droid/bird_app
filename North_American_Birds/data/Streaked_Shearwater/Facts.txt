Streaked Shearwater (Calonectris leucomelas)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large shearwater, brown above, white below with brown-
streaked white head. Pink bill. Large
Calonectris shearwater. About 19 inches.

Behavior
--------
Asian Pacific shearwater. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America. Follows
fishing boats.

Habitat
-------
Northwestern Pacific Ocean. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast of North America, particularly
in late summer and fall.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cory's Shearwater similar but has plain yellow bill
without streaked head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked white head with brown markings unique among
shearwaters. Pink bill, white below.
Asian Pacific vagrant.
