Striped Sparrow (Oriturus superciliosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, streaked above, white below, bold
white supercilium, rufous cap
with white central stripe. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Secretive in tall grass. Rare vagrant to Arizona
and New Mexico from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
High-elevation grasslands of Mexico. Rare vagrant
to Arizona and New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Baird's Sparrow has orange head. No other sparrow
has bold white supercilium
plus rufous cap with central stripe.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white supercilium and rufous-striped cap. White
below. Rare high-elevation
vagrant from Mexico.
