Stygian Owl (Asio stygius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-large dark owl with long ear tufts. Dark brown
to blackish plumage, orange-red eyes,
white forehead patch. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal forest hunter. Rare vagrant to southern
Texas and Florida from Caribbean
and Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Forest of Caribbean islands and Mexico. Rare vagrant
to southern Florida and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long-eared Owl paler with more pronounced facial disk.
Stygian Owl much darker overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very dark owl with long ear tufts, orange-red eyes,
white forehead. Rare vagrant from
Caribbean and Mexico.
