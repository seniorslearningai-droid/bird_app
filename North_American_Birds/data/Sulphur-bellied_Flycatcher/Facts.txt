Sulphur-bellied Flycatcher (Myiodynastes luteiventris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large flycatcher with heavily streaked body, rufous tail, yellow belly, and bold white supercilium over black mask.

Behavior
--------
Gives loud squeaky rubber duck calls; catches insects by sallying; nests in sycamore tree cavities in canyons.

Habitat
-------
Riparian sycamore canyons of southeastern Arizona; migrant through Mexico; winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Streaked Flycatcher is very similar but found in tropics; other large flycatchers lack bold streaked pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Heavily streaked breast; bright yellow belly; rufous tail; loud squeaky call; sycamore canyon habitat in Arizona.
