Summer Tanager (Piranga rubra)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male entirely rose-red with large pale bill; female olive-yellow; both larger and heavier-billed than other tanagers.

Behavior
--------
Catches bees and wasps in flight; rubs stingers off before eating; sings robin-like phrases; canopy species.

Habitat
-------
Open oak and pine-oak woodlands and riparian forests across southern US; winters in Central and South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scarlet Tanager male has black wings; Northern Cardinal has crest; male House Finch has streaked back and sides.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Entirely red male with no wing contrast; large pale bill; female olive-yellow; bee-eating behavior; southern woods.
