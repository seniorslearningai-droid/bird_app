Sungrebe (Heliornis fulica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender waterbird. Brown and black striped head and
neck with black and white facial stripes, brown
body. Lobed toes. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Swims and dives in tropical rivers. Female carries
chicks in wing pouches during flight. Rare vagrant
to Texas from Mexico and Central America.

Habitat
-------
Tropical rivers and streams with dense overhanging
vegetation in Mexico and Central America.
Very rare vagrant to Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Grebes have similar body shape. Sungrebe's striped
head and neck unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black and white striped face and neck on brown
body. Lobed toes. Swims low in
tropical rivers.
