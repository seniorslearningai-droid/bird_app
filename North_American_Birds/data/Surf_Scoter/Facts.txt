Surf Scoter (Melanitta perspicillata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sea duck; male black with white patches on forehead and nape; gaudy orange-red bill with white and black pattern.

Behavior
--------
Dives through surf to eat mussels and clams; large rafts on coastal waters; nests on northern lakes.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on boreal lakes; winters in coastal bays, estuaries, and surf zones along both North American coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Scoter male is all black with yellow bill; White-winged Scoter has white wing patch; female scoters all brown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's white head patches; multicolored orange bill; black body; white in wing absent; coastal ocean winter habitat.
