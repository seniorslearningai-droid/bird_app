Surfbird (Calidris virgata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky shorebird with short yellow legs, short bill, white tail base with black band, and gray overall plumage.

Behavior
--------
Forages on wave-washed rocks feeding on barnacles and mussels; tame; migrates along Pacific Coast.

Habitat
-------
Rocky coastlines of Pacific Coast in winter; breeds on alpine tundra of Alaska; associated with wave-washed rocks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rock Sandpiper is similar on same rocky habitat; Wandering Tattler has longer bill and barred breeding plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump with black terminal band on tail; yellow legs; stocky build; wave-washed rocky intertidal habitat.
