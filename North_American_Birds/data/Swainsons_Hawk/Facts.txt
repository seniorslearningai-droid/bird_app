Swainson's Hawk (Buteo swainsoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large hawk; typical light morph has white belly with dark chest band and brown back; two-tone wings from below.

Behavior
--------
Long-distance migrant to Argentina; follows army ants; soars in large kettles called whirlpools during migration.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, agricultural fields, and prairies of western North America; breeds in isolated trees on plains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-tailed Hawk has dark belly band not chest band; Ferruginous Hawk has leg feathering and different pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark bib contrasting with white belly; dark flight feathers contrasting with white wing linings from below; long pointed wings.
