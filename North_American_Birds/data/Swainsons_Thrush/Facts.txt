Swainson's Thrush (Catharus ustulatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium olive-brown thrush with buffy eye-ring and buffy wash on spotted breast; uniform olive-brown upperparts.

Behavior
--------
Gives high peep call at night during migration; sings upward spiraling fluty phrases; forages on ground.

Habitat
-------
Moist coniferous and mixed forests of boreal zone and mountains; migrates through eastern and western US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hermit Thrush has rufous tail contrasting with brown back; Veery is richer brown with fainter spots.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Buffy eye-ring and face; buffy spotted breast; olive-brown back without rufous tail; upward spiraling flute song.
