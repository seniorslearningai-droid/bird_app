Swainson's Warbler (Limnothlypis swainsonii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, plain olive-brown above, plain pale
below, rusty-brown crown, bold
supercilium. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages secretively on ground in dense vegetation.
Loud clear song belies its
secretive nature.

Habitat
-------
Dense lowland swamps and rhododendron thickets in
mountains of southeastern United
States.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Worm-eating Warbler has striped head. Ovenbird has
orange crown. Plain brown above
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain olive-brown above, pale below, rusty crown.
Very secretive. Loud clear song
from dense swamp vegetation.
