Swallow-tailed Gull (Creagrus furcatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gull with distinctive deeply forked tail.
Gray back, white head with red eye ring, dark
hood in breeding. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Only fully nocturnal gull; feeds at night on squid.
Breeds on Galapagos. Rare vagrant to Pacific
coast from South America.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Galapagos Islands. Pelagic in Pacific
Ocean. Rare vagrant to Pacific coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other gull has deeply forked tail. Unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Deeply forked tail unique among gulls. Red eye ring.
Gray back with dark hood. Rare
vagrant on Pacific coast.
