Swallow-tailed Kite (Elanoides forficatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unmistakable black-and-white kite with very long deeply
forked tail. White head and body,
black wings. About 23 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aerial acrobat; catches insects and lizards in flight.
Breeds in southeastern US; winters
in South America.

Habitat
-------
Bottomland hardwood forest and cypress swamps of
southeastern US. Rare but regular
in Florida and along Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American raptor has long forked tail
with black and white pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long deeply forked tail on black-and-white bird.
Unmistakable. Southeastern US breeding
species, winter migrant to South America.
