Swamp Sparrow (Melospiza georgiana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky sparrow with rufous crown, gray face, white throat, and reddish-brown streaked back; dull breast with no spots.

Behavior
--------
Secretive in marsh vegetation; chips sharply; sings slow sweet musical trills; forages on mud for invertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, bogs, and shrubby wetland edges throughout eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Song Sparrow has streaked breast and central spot; Lincoln's Sparrow has buffy wash and fine streaking on breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous crown; gray face; white throat; plain gray-brown breast without streaking; freshwater marsh habitat.
