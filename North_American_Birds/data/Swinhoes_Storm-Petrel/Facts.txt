Swinhoe's Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates monorhis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small all-dark storm-petrel without white rump. Dark
brown plumage, pale upperwing bar.
About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Asian Pacific storm-petrel. Rare vagrant to North
American Atlantic coast; occasional in
North Pacific offshore waters.

Habitat
-------
Northwestern Pacific Ocean. Rare vagrant to North
American Atlantic coast; very
rare elsewhere in North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ashy Storm-Petrel similar but smaller and grayer.
Swinhoe's lacks white rump.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-dark storm-petrel without white rump. Pale upperwing
bar. Asian Pacific species,
rare North American vagrant.
