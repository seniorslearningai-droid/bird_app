Swinhoe's White-eye (Zosterops simplex)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny bird, olive-green above, yellow throat, white
eye ring, whitish below.
About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly gregarious in flocks. Forages for insects
and nectar. Introduced to California.

Habitat
-------
Gardens, woodland edges, and parks. Introduced to
Los Angeles area California.
Native to China and Japan.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Warbling White-eye (Japanese White-eye) nearly identical;
same group. No other bird
has white eye ring in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eye ring on olive-green bird. Tiny size.
California introduced population.
Gregarious in flocks.
