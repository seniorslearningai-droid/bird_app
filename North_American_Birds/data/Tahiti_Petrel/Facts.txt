Tahiti Petrel (Pseudobulweria rostrata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark petrel with contrasting white belly and
undertail. Dark brown-black above and
on throat. Heavy dark bill. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on South Pacific islands. Rare visitor to
tropical Pacific waters; occasional
vagrant to Hawaii and Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Tropical South Pacific. Rare vagrant to Hawaiian waters
and North American Pacific
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Large Bulweria petrels similar but all dark. Tahiti
has contrasting white belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark above and on breast, contrasting white belly
and undertail. Heavy dark bill.
Large tropical Pacific petrel.
