Taiga Bean-Goose (Anser fabalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
A medium-large brown goose with an orange and black bill pattern and orange legs. Has a long neck and relatively small head.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on grass, crops, and berries. Migrates in family groups. Tends to be shy and wary compared to other geese.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in taiga forests and bogs of Scandinavia and Russia. Winters in agricultural fields and wetlands in western Europe.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Very similar to Tundra Bean-Goose and Pink-footed Goose. Greater White-fronted Goose is similar but has white forehead patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for the orange-banded black bill, longer neck, and larger size compared to Tundra Bean-Goose. Orange (not pink) legs are key.
