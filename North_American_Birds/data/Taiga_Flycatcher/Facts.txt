Taiga Flycatcher (Ficedula albicilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, gray-brown above, white below with
orange-red throat on male. Black
tail with white base. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches upright on exposed branches. Fans tail showing
white patches. Rare vagrant
to western Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Taiga forests of Siberia and East Asia. Rare
vagrant to western Alaska and
Aleutian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-flanked Bluetail has blue tail. Bluethroat
has blue throat. Siberian Rubythroat
has red throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small brown flycatcher with orange-red throat on
male. White tail base. Rare
Alaska vagrant from Siberia.
