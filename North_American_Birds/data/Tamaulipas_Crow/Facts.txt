Tamaulipas Crow (Corvus imparatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small glossy crow, entirely black with blue-green
gloss. Short bill, small size
compared to other crows. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Formerly regular in small numbers at Brownsville,
Texas landfill. Social in flocks.
Now very rare north of Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Arid scrub and agricultural areas of northeastern
Mexico. Rarely wanders into
extreme southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Crow much larger. Chihuahuan Raven larger
with different range. Fish
Crow similar but different range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very small glossy crow in far southern Texas.
Strong blue-green gloss.
Short bill. Rare vagrant.
