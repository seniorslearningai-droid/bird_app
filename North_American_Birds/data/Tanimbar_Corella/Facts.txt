Tanimbar Corella (Cacatua goffiniana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium white cockatoo with salmon-pink face and lores,
small erectile crest. About
12 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly intelligent, uses tools. Gregarious in flocks.
Small feral populations in
California and Florida.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and agricultural areas. Feral populations
in parts of California
and Florida. Native to Indonesia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sulphur-crested Cockatoo larger with yellow crest.
Other white cockatoos different
crest color.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White cockatoo with salmon-pink face. Small size
among cockatoos. Feral in
California and Florida.
