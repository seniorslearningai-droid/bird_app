Tawny-shouldered Blackbird (Agelaius humeralis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium blackbird. Male black with tawny-buff shoulder
patch. Female dusky brown.
About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in flocks in open agricultural areas. Rare
vagrant to Florida from Cuba
and Hispaniola.

Habitat
-------
Open agricultural land of Cuba and Haiti. Rare vagrant
to southern Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-winged Blackbird has red-and-yellow epaulet.
Tawny-buff shoulder patch
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tawny-buff shoulder on black body. Very rare Florida
vagrant from Cuba. Similar
shape to Red-winged Blackbird.
