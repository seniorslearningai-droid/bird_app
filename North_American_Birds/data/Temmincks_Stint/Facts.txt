Temminck's Stint (Calidris temminckii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small sandpiper. Uniform gray-brown above, white
below with gray-brown breast band. Short legs,
creeping behavior. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Creeps mouse-like through vegetation. Prefers freshwater
to coastal habitats. Rare vagrant to North
America from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on subarctic tundra and mountain slopes. Winters
on African and Asian wetlands. Rare vagrant
to North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Little Stint similar. Temminck's has white outer
tail feathers and uniform gray-brown back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White outer tail feathers visible in flight. Uniform
gray-brown above without white supercilium.
Mouse-like creeping behavior.
