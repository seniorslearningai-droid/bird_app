Tennessee Warbler (Leiothlypis peregrina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler; breeding male gray-green with white eyebrow, green back, and white underparts; female greener and yellower.

Behavior
--------
Sings staccato three-part accelerating song; forages high in trees; pierces flower bases for nectar in tropics.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forests for breeding; migrates through eastern and central US; winters in Central American shade coffee.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Orange-crowned Warbler is yellowish; Nashville Warbler has yellow underparts; Philadelphia Vireo is larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eyebrow; clean white underparts; green back; gray head in male; flat forehead; thin pointed bill.
