Terek Sandpiper (Xenus cinereus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper with distinctive long upturned bill.
Gray-brown above, white below, orange-yellow legs.
About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Runs rapidly along mudflats with body tilted forward.
Rare vagrant to both coasts from Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds along Eurasian rivers. Winters on African
and Asian coasts. Rare vagrant to Pacific
and Atlantic coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other sandpiper has upturned bill. Dunlin similar
size but bill curves down.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive long upturned bill unique among sandpipers.
Gray-brown with orange-yellow legs. Rapidly
running feeding behavior.
