Thick-billed Kingbird (Tyrannus crassirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large kingbird with noticeably thick heavy bill. Brown-
gray above, white throat, pale yellow
belly. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Perches conspicuously and makes long aerial sallies
for insects. Regular summer resident
in southeastern Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Riparian cottonwood-sycamore forest in southeastern
Arizona. Summer resident; winters
in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tropical Kingbird similar but has thinner bill. Thick-
billed has noticeably heavier bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Noticeably heavy thick bill. Brown-gray back, pale
yellow belly. Summer resident in
Arizona riparian forest.
