Thick-billed Murre (Uria lomvia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large alcid with black head and upperparts, white underparts, and short thick bill with pale gape stripe at base.

Behavior
--------
Dives to great depths for fish; dense cliff colonies; single egg laid on bare rock ledge; excellent swimmer.

Habitat
-------
Arctic and subarctic ocean cliffs for nesting; winters offshore on northern Atlantic and Pacific oceans.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Murre has thinner longer bill without gape stripe; Razorbill has deep laterally compressed bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White stripe at bill base called gape stripe; stockier shorter bill than Common Murre; black and white seabird.
