Thick-billed Parrot (Rhynchopsitta pachyrhyncha)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium parrot, mostly green with red forehead and
shoulders, thick black bill, red leg
bend. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Endangered; flocks in pine forest. Extirpated from
Arizona; recent small irruptions to
Chiricahua Mountains.

Habitat
-------
Pine forest in Sierra Madre Occidental of Mexico.
Occasional irruptive visitor to
southeastern Arizona mountains.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other parrot in range has thick black bill with
red forehead and shoulders.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red forehead and shoulders, thick black bill. Green
parrot in pine forest. Occasional
irruptive visitor to Arizona mountains.
