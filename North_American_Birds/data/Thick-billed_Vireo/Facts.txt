Thick-billed Vireo (Vireo crassirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small vireo, olive-gray above with two wing bars and
broken eye ring. Yellow-white below.
Thick bill. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Caribbean vireo. Rare vagrant to southern Florida from
Bahamas; possibly breeds on
Florida Keys.

Habitat
-------
Forest and thicket of Bahamas and Caribbean. Rare
vagrant to southern Florida; possible
rare breeder in Keys.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-eyed Vireo similar. Thick-billed Vireo has thicker
bill and different eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Thick bill, broken eye ring. Olive-gray with wing bars.
Caribbean species rare in
southern Florida.
