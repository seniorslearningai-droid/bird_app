Thick-billed Warbler (Arundinax aedon)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large plain warbler, brown above with rufous tones,
buff below. Heavy bill, rounded
tail. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Skulking in dense vegetation. Loud rich song.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Dense reed beds, scrub, and tall grass in Asia.
Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Reed Warbler similar but smaller. Blyth's
Reed Warbler thinner billed.
Generally distinctive in Alaska.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size, heavy bill, rufous-brown tones. Very
rare Alaska vagrant. Skulks
in dense vegetation.
