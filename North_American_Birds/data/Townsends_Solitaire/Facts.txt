Townsend's Solitaire (Myadestes townsendi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush-like bird with gray body, white eye-ring, buffy wing patches, and white outer tail feathers; slender build.

Behavior
--------
Sings beautiful complex warbling song year-round; guards juniper berry patches in winter; aerial flycatching.

Habitat
-------
Montane coniferous forests for breeding; winters in juniper woodlands and lower elevation shrubby areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Mockingbird has white wing patches but is larger; Townsend's Solitaire is slimmer than thrushes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold buffy wing patches in flight; white outer tail; white eye-ring; slim gray body; upright posture on perch.
