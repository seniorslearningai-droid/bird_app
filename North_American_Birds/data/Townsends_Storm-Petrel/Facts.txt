Townsend's Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates socorroensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dark storm-petrel with white rump. Very similar
to Leach's Storm-Petrel. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Guadalupe Island, Mexico. Smaller and shorter-
tailed than Leach's. Forages in
Pacific off Mexico and California.

Habitat
-------
Eastern Pacific Ocean off Baja California and Mexico.
Breeds on Guadalupe Island.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Leach's Storm-Petrel nearly identical but larger.
Townsend's best identified by smaller size.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very similar to Leach's Storm-Petrel but smaller.
Best distinguished at sea by size.
Eastern Pacific off Mexico.
