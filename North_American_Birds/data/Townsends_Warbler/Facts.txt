Townsend's Warbler (Setophaga townsendi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Bright warbler; male has black and yellow face pattern with black throat; yellow breast; greenish back with streaking.

Behavior
--------
Forages in conifer foliage gleaning insects; sings buzzy rising and falling phrases; high elevation breeder.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests of Pacific Northwest and mountains; winters along California coast and in Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hermit Warbler male has yellow face without black cheeks; Black-throated Green Warbler has yellow face and green back.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black and yellow face with black cheek patch; black throat; yellow breast with black streaking; conifer forest.
