Tree Pipit (Anthus trivialis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small pipit, streaked brown above, buff breast with
dark streaks, pale base to
bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Song flight from tree top. Perches in trees unlike
most pipits. Very rare vagrant
to western Alaska from Europe.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and scrub in Europe and Asia. Very
rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Olive-backed Pipit has olive tones. Red-throated Pipit
brighter in breeding. Very
similar to other pipits.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale base to lower mandible (pinkish). Buff breast
streaking. Perches in trees.
Very rare Alaska vagrant.
