Tree Swallow (Tachycineta bicolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Glossy iridescent blue-green upperparts contrasting with clean white underparts; slightly notched tail; compact build.

Behavior
--------
Eats flying insects caught aerially; readily uses nest boxes; flocks in large numbers in migration near water.

Habitat
-------
Open areas near water with dead trees for nesting; fields, marshes, and lakeshores throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Violet-green Swallow has white patches on sides of rump; Bank Swallow has brown chest band; Barn Swallow has long tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Iridescent blue-green above; clean white below extending to below eye; no brown chest band; slight tail notch.
