Tricolored Blackbird (Agelaius tricolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium blackbird. Male black with red and white
shoulder patch. Female dark brown
with streaking. About 8.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nests in huge colonies, sometimes hundreds of thousands.
Highly colonial and nomadic.
Rapidly declining.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, agricultural fields, and open
land in California. Critically
endangered.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-winged Blackbird has red and yellow shoulder
without white bar. White bar
on shoulder distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White bar on red shoulder patch distinctive. All-black
male. California endemic.
Massive colony nester.
