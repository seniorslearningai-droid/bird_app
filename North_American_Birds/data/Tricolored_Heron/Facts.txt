Tricolored Heron (Egretta tricolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender heron with dark slate-blue neck and upperparts, white belly, and white stripe down foreneck; slim proportions.

Behavior
--------
Actively chases fish with quick dashing movements; spreads wings to shade water while foraging; colonial nester.

Habitat
-------
Saltwater and brackish marshes, mangroves, and coastal wetlands of southeastern US and Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Little Blue Heron lacks white belly; Reddish Egret is stockier and rusty; Great Blue Heron is much larger.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive white belly contrasting with dark blue neck; white foreneck stripe; slender build; rusty neck plumes.
