Tricolored Munia (Lonchura malacca)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, chestnut-brown head and back, black
breast and belly, white sides.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious, forages in flocks for grass seeds.
Introduced to Hawaii; present on
several islands.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands, rice fields, and open areas. Introduced
to Hawaii.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chestnut Munia has chestnut belly not black. Scaly-breasted
Munia has spotted breast.
Black belly distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black belly contrasting with white sides. Chestnut-brown
head. Gregarious in Hawaiian
grasslands.
