Trindade Petrel (Pterodroma arminjoniana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Polymorphic gadfly petrel; light, intermediate, and
dark morphs. Breeds on Trindade Island,
Brazil. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Trindade Island; disperses into Atlantic
and occasionally to North American
Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
South Atlantic Ocean. Rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic coast, particularly
offshore mid-Atlantic states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Herald Petrel similar in Pacific. Trindade in Atlantic
is dark with white primary flash.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark morph most common vagrant: dark brown with white
primary flash. Rare Atlantic vagrant.
Polymorphic species.
