Tristram's Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates tristrami)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark storm-petrel with pale upperwing bar.
Larger than most Pacific storm-petrels.
About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern Islands; forages in
Hawaiian and central Pacific waters.
Nocturnal at breeding colonies.

Habitat
-------
Central North Pacific Ocean. Breeds on Hawaiian Northwestern
Islands; rare in Hawaiian offshore
waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Storm-Petrel similar but found in eastern Pacific.
Tristram's larger than most Hawaiian
storm-petrels.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large dark storm-petrel in Hawaiian waters. Pale
upperwing bar. Largest storm-petrel
in central North Pacific.
