Tropical Kingbird (Tyrannus melancholicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large flycatcher with gray head, yellow belly, olive-green back, notched brown tail, and concealed orange crown patch.

Behavior
--------
Sings twittering trilled calls; aggressive defense of territory; sallies for insects; expanding into Arizona.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland edges, riparian areas, and urban parks of Mexico and Central America; regular in southeast Arizona.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Kingbird has white outer tail edges; Couch's Kingbird is nearly identical but different call and range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Notched brown tail without white edges; large size; bright yellow belly; gray head; twittering call in Arizona.
