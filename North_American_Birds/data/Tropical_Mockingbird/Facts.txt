Tropical Mockingbird (Mimus gilvus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium mockingbird, gray above, white below, white
wing patches, white outer tail
feathers. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Mimics other species and sings persistently. Bold
in open areas. Rare vagrant
to southern Florida and Texas.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, urban environments, and forest edges from
Mexico to South America. Rare
vagrant to Florida and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Mockingbird nearly identical. Tropical slightly
more gray above with
different tail pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Northern Mockingbird. Smaller tail
pattern and subtle gray tones.
Rare tropical vagrant to Florida.
