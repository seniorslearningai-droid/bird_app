Tropical Parula (Setophaga pitiayumi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, blue-gray above, bright yellow throat
and breast, no white eye ring.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active forager in tree canopy. Breeds rarely in
extreme south Texas and more
commonly in Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Riparian woodland, Spanish moss, and forest in
south Texas and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Parula has white eye ring and dark breast
band. Tropical lacks eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Blue-gray above with yellow below but no eye ring.
Lacks breast band of Northern
Parula. South Texas.
