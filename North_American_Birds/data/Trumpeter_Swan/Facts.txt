Trumpeter Swan (Cygnus buccinator)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest North American waterfowl; entirely white with black bill and lores; no yellow on bill base; very long neck.

Behavior
--------
Gives loud trumpeting call; grazes aquatic vegetation; recovering from near extinction; family bonds strong.

Habitat
-------
Large lakes, rivers, and wetlands; reintroduced across much of northern US and Canada; wintering flocks in Pacific Northwest.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tundra Swan is smaller with yellow spot on bill base; Whooping Crane is taller with red crown and different profile.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All black bill with no yellow; very large size; all white plumage; straight neck; loud trumpeting vocalizations.
