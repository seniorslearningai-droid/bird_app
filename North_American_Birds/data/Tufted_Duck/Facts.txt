Tufted Duck (Aythya fuligula)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male black and white with distinctive drooping head tuft; yellow eye; blue-gray bill; female brown with smaller tuft.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mollusks and aquatic invertebrates; Eurasian species appearing regularly in North America in winter.

Habitat
-------
Lakes, reservoirs, and coastal bays; rare visitor to North America often associating with scaup flocks.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Ring-necked Duck lacks head tuft; Greater Scaup lacks tuft and has different head shape; Lesser Scaup similar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Drooping black head tuft; black and white male; yellow eye; blue-gray bill; Eurasian vagrant to North America.
