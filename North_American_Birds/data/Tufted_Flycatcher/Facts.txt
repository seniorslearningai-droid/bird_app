Tufted Flycatcher (Mitrephanes phaeocercus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher with prominent pointed crest. Brown-
olive above, orange-buff below with
two wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies for insects from exposed perch. Mexican species;
rare vagrant to southern Arizona
and Texas.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forest of Mexico and Central America. Rare
vagrant to southeastern Arizona
and southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------
Pewees lack the pointed crest. Tufted Flycatcher's
crest distinguishes it.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Prominent pointed crest. Buff-orange below. Small.
Rare vagrant from Mexico to
Arizona and Texas.
