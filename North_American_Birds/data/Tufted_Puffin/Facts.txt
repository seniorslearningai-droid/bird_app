Tufted Puffin (Fratercula cirrhata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large stocky alcid; black body with white face patch and drooping yellowish tufts in breeding; huge orange-red bill.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish; nests in burrows on grassy clifftops; large colorful bill used in courtship; colonial nester.

Habitat
-------
Rocky offshore islands and sea cliffs of North Pacific; winters far offshore on open ocean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Atlantic Puffin has different facial pattern without tufts; Horned Puffin lacks tufts and has different bill pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long yellow tufts behind eye in summer; white face; massive orange bill; black body; North Pacific ocean habitat.
