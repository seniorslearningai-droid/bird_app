Tufted Titmouse (Baeolophus bicolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bird with prominent gray crest, gray above,
white below with rusty-orange flanks.
Black forehead. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bold and inquisitive at feeders. Caches food
for winter. Loud and persistent
peter-peter-peter song.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests of eastern United States.
Common in suburbs and at backyard
feeders year-round.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-crested Titmouse has black crest. Carolina Chickadee
smaller with black bib
and cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray pointed crest, black forehead, rusty flanks.
Bold behavior at feeders. Loud
repetitive peter-peter song.
