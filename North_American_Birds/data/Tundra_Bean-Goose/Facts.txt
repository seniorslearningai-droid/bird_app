Tundra Bean-Goose (Anser serrirostris)
===========================

Description
-----------
A compact, dark brown goose with a shorter neck than Taiga Bean-Goose, orange-and-black bill, and orange legs. Rounder head profile.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on tundra vegetation, grasses, and agricultural crops in winter. Forms large flocks on wintering grounds in Asia and Europe.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on open Arctic tundra in Russia and eastern Siberia. Winters in coastal wetlands, rice fields, and grasslands in East Asia.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Very similar to Taiga Bean-Goose but stockier. Also resembles Greater White-fronted Goose without the white face patch.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Shorter, stubbier bill and neck than Taiga Bean-Goose. Rounder head, darker brown overall plumage, and compact body shape are key.
