Tundra Swan (Cygnus columbianus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large white swan with black bill and small yellow spot at base of bill near eye; more rounded head than Trumpeter.

Behavior
--------
Gives musical whooping calls; migrates in family groups; winters in coastal estuaries and agricultural fields.

Habitat
-------
Arctic tundra for nesting; winters on Chesapeake Bay, Central Valley California, and interior marshes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Trumpeter Swan is larger with all-black bill and no yellow; Whooping Crane is taller not a swan.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small yellow spot at bill base near eye; black bill otherwise; smaller than Trumpeter Swan; musical whooping calls.
