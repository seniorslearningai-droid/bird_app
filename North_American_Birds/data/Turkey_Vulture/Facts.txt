Turkey Vulture (Cathartes aura)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large black soaring bird with bare red head, two-toned underwings, and small head; distinctive V-shaped soaring posture.

Behavior
--------
Locates carrion by smell, unique among raptors; rocks in flight due to small wingloading; rarely flaps wings.

Habitat
-------
Open country, roadsides, and forests throughout the Americas; roosts communally; very widespread species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Vulture has white wing patches and flat soaring posture; eagles and hawks have feathered heads.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red bare head; two-toned black and silver underwing; V-shaped dihedral soaring; rocking side-to-side in thermals.
