Ula-ai-hawane (Ciridops anna)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny Hawaiian honeycreeper, male brilliant red with
gray wings and tail. Female
brown. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Fed on hawane palm fruits in native Hawaiian forest.
Extinct since approximately 1892.

Habitat
-------
Native forests with loulu palm on Big Island. Extinct
due to deforestation and
introduced species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Iiwi larger with curved bill. Apapane similar red
color. No similar tiny
red bird survives.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extinct tiny red honeycreeper from Big Island. Associated
with loulu palms. No
longer exists.
