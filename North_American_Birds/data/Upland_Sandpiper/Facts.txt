Upland Sandpiper (Bartramia longicauda)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium brown sandpiper with small pigeon-like head, long neck, long tail, and short bill; grassland species.

Behavior
--------
Perches on fence posts; gives ascending wolf-whistle call; feeds on grasshoppers; long-distance migrant.

Habitat
-------
Native grasslands and prairies of Great Plains and Great Lakes region; declining with grassland loss.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Buff-breasted Sandpiper is similar but stockier without small head appearance; Whimbrel has curved bill and different size.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small round head on long neck; pigeon-like proportions; long primary projection; perches on fence posts; grasslands.
