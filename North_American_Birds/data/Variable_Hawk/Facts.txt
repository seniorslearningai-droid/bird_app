Variable Hawk (Geranoaetus polyosoma)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium to large hawk, highly variable plumage from
gray and white to dark rufous-brown.
Broad rounded wings. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Soars over open country and hillsides. Extremely rare
vagrant to North America from
South America.

Habitat
-------
Open country, hillsides, and mountains of South America.
Extremely rare vagrant to
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-tailed Hawk has dark belly band. Highly variable
plumage makes comparison
difficult.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely rare South American vagrant. Variable plumage
from gray to rufous. Broad
rounded wings and open country.
