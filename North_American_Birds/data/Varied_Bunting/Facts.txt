Varied Bunting (Passerina versicolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bunting. Male complex purple-blue with red nape
patch and red-purple rump.
Female plain brown. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in dense thorny scrub. Male often appears
blackish in poor light. Skulks.

Habitat
-------
Dense thorn scrub along streams and brushy areas
in southeastern Arizona and
southwest Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Indigo Bunting lighter blue. Painted Bunting has green
back and red belly.
Complex purple-blue in good light.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male complex purple-blue with red nape, appears dark
in poor light. Dense thorn
scrub in Arizona and Texas.
