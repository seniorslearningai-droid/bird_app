Varied Thrush (Ixoreus naevius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Robin-like thrush; male has orange and dark gray pattern with orange supercilium and dark breast band; orange wing bars.

Behavior
--------
Solitary and shy; gives long haunting whistled note; winters irruptively at feeders; forages under dense conifers.

Habitat
-------
Moist old-growth and mature coniferous forests of Pacific Northwest; winters in California and southwest.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Robin has plain red breast without band; female Varied Thrush is brownish where male is gray.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Orange supercilium and wing bars; dark breast band; gray and orange male; haunting single-note whistle call.
