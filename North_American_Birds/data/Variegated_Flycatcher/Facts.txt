Variegated Flycatcher (Empidonomus varius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher, brown and white streaked above,
white below with brown streaks, rufous
on wing and tail. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
South American migrant; rare vagrant to eastern North
America. Perches conspicuously.

Habitat
-------
Open woodland and forest edge of South America. Rare
vagrant to Atlantic coast of
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Piratic Flycatcher similar but lacks rufous on wings
and tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Streaked flycatcher with rufous wing edging and tail.
Rare South American vagrant
to Atlantic coast.
