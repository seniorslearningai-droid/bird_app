Vaux's Swift (Chaetura vauxi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small swift. Dark sooty gray-brown overall,
slightly paler throat and rump. Tiny with
stubby tail and cigar-shaped body. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Aerial insect feeder; never perches on ground.
Roosts and nests in hollow trees. Migrates along
Pacific coast; gregarious in large flocks.

Habitat
-------
Forests of Pacific coast from Alaska to Central
America. Uses hollow conifers for nesting. Migrates
through Pacific states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chimney Swift nearly identical but found in East.
Both are tiny dark swifts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size, sooty gray plumage, and cigar-shaped body
in twinkling flight. Paler rump.
Mainly on Pacific Coast.
