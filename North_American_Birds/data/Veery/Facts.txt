Veery (Catharus fuscescens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush with rich tawny-cinnamon upperparts and lightly spotted breast; spotted breast much less distinct than congeners.

Behavior
--------
Sings descending ethereal spiraling veer-y veer-y song at dusk; forages on ground; long-distance migrant.

Habitat
-------
Moist deciduous forests and streamside thickets; breeds across northeastern and north-central US and Canada.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Swainson's Thrush has buffy eye-ring; Hermit Thrush has rufous tail only; Wood Thrush has bolder spots.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Warm tawny-cinnamon color; faint spots on upper breast; no bold eye-ring; descending spiraling ethereal song.
