Vega Gull (Larus vegae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, pale gray back, pink legs, yellow bill
with red spot. Similar to Herring Gull.
About 24 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in Siberia; winters in eastern Asia and occasionally
reaches western Alaska and Pacific coast
of North America.

Habitat
-------
Siberian coastlines and tundra. Rare winter visitor
to Alaska and occasionally Pacific coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Herring Gull very similar. Vega Gull slightly
darker gray with pinkish orbital ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Siberian counterpart to Herring Gull. Slightly darker
gray back. Rare visitor to western
Alaska and Pacific coast.
