Verdin (Auriparus flaviceps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny gray bird with yellow head and chestnut shoulder patches; small and active in desert thorny shrubs.

Behavior
--------
Builds spherical stick nests for roosting year-round in addition to breeding; gleans insects from thorny desert plants.

Habitat
-------
Thorny desert scrub, mesquite, and arid brushland of southwestern US and northern Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar tiny gray-bodied yellow-headed bird in range; Lucy's Warbler has white not chestnut shoulder.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow head and face; chestnut shoulder patch; tiny gray body; desert thorny shrub habitat; round stick nests.
