Vermilion Flycatcher (Pyrocephalus rubinus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unmistakable small flycatcher. Male brilliant red crown
and underparts, brown-black back.
Female brown with streaked belly. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sallies for insects. Males display dramatically in
flight. Common in desert riparian
areas of Southwest.

Habitat
-------
Desert and semi-arid areas near water. Common from
Texas to California; also along
Gulf Coast in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other small flycatcher has the brilliant red plumage
of male Vermilion Flycatcher.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male: brilliant red with brown-black back. Unmistakable.
Female: streaked below. Desert
Southwest riparian areas.
