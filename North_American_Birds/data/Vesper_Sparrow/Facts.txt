Vesper Sparrow (Pooecetes gramineus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sparrow, streaked brown above and below, white
outer tail feathers, chestnut
shoulder patch. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings beautiful song at dusk, giving it the name
Vesper. Walks on ground in
open grasslands.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, fields, and sagebrush across North
America. Declining in many
areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Savannah Sparrow lacks chestnut shoulder. Song Sparrow
more heavily streaked.
White outer tail in flight helpful.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White outer tail feathers in flight. Chestnut shoulder
patch. Streaked grassland
sparrow. Beautiful dusk song.
