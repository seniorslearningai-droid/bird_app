Violet-crowned Hummingbird (Leucolia violiceps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Brilliant violet-blue crown,
plain white underparts, green back, red-orange bill.
Clean white underparts distinctive. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar and insects. Rare summer visitor
to Arizona and New Mexico riparian canyons from
Mexico. Visits flowers and feeders.

Habitat
-------
Riparian woodland and canyon scrub in southeastern
Arizona, southwestern New Mexico, and adjacent
Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Berylline Hummingbird in similar range. Violet-crowned's
plain white underparts unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant violet-blue crown and entirely clean white
underparts unmistakable. Red-orange bill.
Riparian canyons in southeastern Arizona.
