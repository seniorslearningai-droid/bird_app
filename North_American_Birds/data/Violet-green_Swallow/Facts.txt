Violet-green Swallow (Tachycineta thalassina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Iridescent green and purple upperparts with white patches wrapping onto sides of rump; white extends above eye.

Behavior
--------
Forages aerially for insects; nests in tree cavities and nest boxes; colonial and often mixes with other swallows.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, mountain forests, and cliffs of western North America; common in suburban areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Tree Swallow lacks white rump patches and white above eye; White-throated Swift is black and white with longer wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White patches on sides of rump; white wraps above eye level on face; green and purple iridescent back; western US.
