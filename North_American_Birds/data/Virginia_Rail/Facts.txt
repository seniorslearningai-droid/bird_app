Virginia Rail (Rallus limicola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small rail with long slightly downcurved reddish bill, rufous breast, barred flanks, and gray cheeks; brown streaked back.

Behavior
--------
Secretive in dense marsh vegetation; gives distinctive kid-ik descending calls and oinking grunts at night.

Habitat
-------
Dense freshwater and brackish marshes with cattails and sedges throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sora has short yellow bill; King Rail is much larger with rufous cheeks; Clapper Rail is gray-brown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long reddish-orange bill; gray cheeks; rufous breast; barred flanks; small size; freshwater cattail marshes.
