Virginia's Warbler (Leiothlypis virginiae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small gray warbler with white eye-ring, yellow rump, yellow patch on breast, and chestnut crown patch; slim build.

Behavior
--------
Bobs tail constantly like Nashville Warbler; sings from dense mountain shrubs; ground nester on steep slopes.

Habitat
-------
Dense mountain brush and scrub oak on dry slopes of southern Rocky Mountains and Great Basin ranges.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Nashville Warbler has yellow throat; Colima Warbler is larger and browner; Lucy's Warbler has white underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body with yellow chest patch; yellow rump; white eye-ring; tail bobbing behavior; mountain shrubby slopes.
