Virginia's Warbler (Leiothlypis virginiae)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small warbler, gray above, white below, yellow breast
patch and undertail coverts,
rusty crown. About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages low in shrubs. Bobs tail frequently. Mountain
scrub specialist in southwestern
United States.

Habitat
-------
Dry mountain scrub and pinyon-juniper woodland in
the southern Rocky Mountains and
Great Basin.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Colima Warbler in Chisos Mountains. Nashville Warbler
more yellow below. Gray body
with yellow patches.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body with yellow breast patch and undertail.
Rusty crown. Tail bobbing behavior.
Mountain scrub in southwest.
