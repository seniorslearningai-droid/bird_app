Wandering Albatross (Diomedea exulans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest albatross; greatest wingspan of any living
bird. White body, black wingtips, pink bill.
About 11-foot wingspan.

Behavior
--------
Circumnavigates Southern Ocean on wind currents. Breeds
every other year on South Atlantic and
Indian Ocean islands.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean; rare vagrant to North Pacific and
North Atlantic. Among the most
impressive seabirds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Royal Albatross similar. Wandering Albatross distinguishable
by white back developing with age.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous wingspan; all-white body develops with age.
Pink bill. Very rare North
American vagrant from Southern Ocean.
