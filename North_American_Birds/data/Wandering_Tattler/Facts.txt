Wandering Tattler (Tringa incana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper. Uniform gray above, white below
with barred underparts in breeding. Yellow legs.
About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bobs tail while walking on rocky shores. Loud "weet"
calls. Found on rocky Pacific coasts. Long nasal
groove separates from Gray-tailed Tattler.

Habitat
-------
Breeds along mountain streams in Alaska and Yukon.
Winters on rocky Pacific shores and islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Gray-tailed Tattler nearly identical. Wandering has
longer nasal groove on bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Uniform gray plumage on rocky Pacific shores. Longer
nasal groove on bill. Loud piped
calls. Bobs tail constantly.
