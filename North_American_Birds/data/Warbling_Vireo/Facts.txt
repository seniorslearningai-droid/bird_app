Warbling Vireo (Vireo gilvus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain gray-green vireo with pale eyebrow, whitish underparts, and no wing bars; one of dullest but sweetest-singing vireos.

Behavior
--------
Sings long warbling phrases from canopy; deliberately gleans caterpillars and insects; forages high in trees.

Habitat
-------
Riparian woodlands, deciduous forests, and shade trees throughout temperate North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Philadelphia Vireo has yellow underparts; Tennessee Warbler has shorter bill; Red-eyed Vireo has bold head stripes.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain unmarked face with pale eyebrow; no wing bars; plain greenish-gray; prolonged warbling song from treetops.
