Warbling White-eye (Zosterops japonicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny bright green bird with distinctive white eye
ring, yellow throat and breast,
gray flanks. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Highly active in flocks foraging for insects, nectar,
and fruit. Invasive in Hawaii,
competing with native species.

Habitat
-------
Forests, gardens, and suburban areas. Introduced
to Hawaii; now one of commonest
birds throughout the islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other bird in Hawaii has the white eye ring.
Amakihi greener without
eye ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white eye ring on green body. Yellow throat.
Tiny size. Common in Hawaii.
Chattering calls in flocks.
