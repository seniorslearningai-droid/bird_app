Waved Albatross (Phoebastria irrorata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large albatross with fine wavy brown and white body
pattern, yellow bill, white head with
yellow-buff wash. About 35 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds almost exclusively on Espanola Island, Galapagos.
Forages off South American coast; rare
vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
Eastern Pacific off South America. Breeds Galapagos;
very rare vagrant to Pacific coast
of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other large albatrosses lack the wavy brown-white
body pattern of Waved Albatross.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Wavy brown-and-white body pattern distinctive. Yellow
bill, white head. Galapagos breeder,
rare North American vagrant.
