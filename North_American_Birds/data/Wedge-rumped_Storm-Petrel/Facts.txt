Wedge-rumped Storm-Petrel (Hydrobates tethys)
===========================

Description
-----------
Dark storm-petrel with enormous white rump patch
extending nearly to tail tip. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Galapagos; post-breeding birds move up Pacific
coast. Rare visitor to eastern
Pacific off California.

Habitat
-------
Eastern tropical Pacific. Post-breeding dispersal
brings rare visitors to California
offshore waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other storm-petrels have smaller white rump patches.
Wedge-rumped's enormous white rump diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Enormous white rump patch extending almost to tail.
Much larger white rump than other
dark storm-petrels.
