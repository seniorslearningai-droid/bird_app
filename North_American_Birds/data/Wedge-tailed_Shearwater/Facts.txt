Wedge-tailed Shearwater (Ardenna pacifica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shearwater with long wedge-shaped tail. Polymorphic:
dark morph all brown, light morph
white below. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Hawaiian and Pacific islands. Common in
Hawaiian offshore waters and tropical
Pacific.

Habitat
-------
Tropical Pacific Ocean. Breeds on Hawaiian Islands
and other Pacific tropical islands.
Common in Hawaiian offshore waters.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other dark shearwaters lack wedge-shaped tail. Light
morph similar to Buller's but has
longer wedge tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long wedge-shaped tail distinctive. Polymorphic; dark
morph all-brown, light morph has
white underparts.
