West Indian Whistling-Duck (Dendrocygna arborea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large duck, dark brown above, pale below with dark
spots, long neck, upright
posture. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal, roosts in trees. Whistling calls. Rare
visitor to southern Florida from
Caribbean.

Habitat
-------
Mangroves, swamps, and woodland near water in the
Caribbean. Rare visitor to
Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-bellied Whistling-Duck has red bill and black
belly. Fulvous Whistling-Duck
has buff body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark brown with spotted flanks, upright posture, long
neck. Rare Florida visitor
from Caribbean.
