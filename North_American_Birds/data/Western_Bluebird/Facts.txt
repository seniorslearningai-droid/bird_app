Western Bluebird (Sialia mexicana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male deep blue head and wings with rusty-orange breast; some show orange on back; female grayish with blue tones.

Behavior
--------
Drops to ground from perch to catch insects; uses nest boxes and tree cavities; flocks in winter on berries.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, pine-oak forests, and forest edges of western North America; partial migrant.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Bluebird has orange on throat; Mountain Bluebird male is all blue without orange breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Blue throat and head contrasting with orange breast; no orange on throat unlike Eastern Bluebird; open western forests.
