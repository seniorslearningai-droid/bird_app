Western Cattle-Egret (Bubulcus ibis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small white egret with orange-buff patches on head,
breast, and back in breeding. Short
yellow bill. About 20 inches.

Behavior
--------
Follows cattle and large animals to catch disturbed
insects. Highly successful colonizer;
spread from Africa to Americas.

Habitat
-------
Pastures, agricultural fields, and open areas. Common
throughout southeastern US; spreading
northward and westward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Snowy Egret all white with black bill. Cattle Egret
has yellow bill and stocky build.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Short yellow bill, stocky build, follows cattle and
farm machinery. Orange-buff patches
in breeding. Common in pastures.
