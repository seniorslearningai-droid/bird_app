Western Flycatcher (Empidonax difficilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Empidonax flycatcher, olive-green above, yellow
wash on belly, teardrop eye ring.
About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds in moist conifer and mixed forest of Pacific
states. Gives sharp upward-slurred
whistle call.

Habitat
-------
Moist forest near streams along Pacific coast from
Alaska to Baja. Common but
secretive.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cordilleran Flycatcher nearly identical. Western and
Cordilleran best separated by range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Teardrop eye ring, yellow wash on throat and belly.
Pacific coast moist forests. Sharp
whistle call.
