Western Grebe (Aechmophorus occidentalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large grebe with black and white plumage, long slender neck, long yellow-green pointed bill, and red eyes.

Behavior
--------
Performs spectacular rushing courtship display across water; dives for fish; builds floating nest in reeds.

Habitat
-------
Large lakes and reservoirs for breeding; winters on coastal bays and larger inland lakes of western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Clark's Grebe is nearly identical but has white around eye and brighter orange-yellow bill; both do rush display.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long slender neck; black cap extends below eye level; yellow-green bill; large size; lakes of western North America.
