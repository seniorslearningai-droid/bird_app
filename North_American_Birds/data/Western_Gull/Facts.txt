Western Gull (Larus occidentalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, dark gray back, pink legs, yellow bill
with red spot. White head and underparts.
About 25 inches.

Behavior
--------
Common along Pacific coast. Bold scavenger at beaches,
harbors, and fishing docks. Nests on
offshore rocky islands.

Habitat
-------
Pacific coast from Washington to Baja California.
Rocky islands for nesting; coastlines,
harbors, and beaches year-round.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Glaucous-winged Gull similar in range. Western Gull
has darker gray back and pink legs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray back combined with pink legs distinctive
on Pacific coast. Large size, yellow
bill with red spot.
