Western Kingbird (Tyrannus verticalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray-headed kingbird with yellow belly, olive back, and black tail with white outer edges; orange crown patch concealed.

Behavior
--------
Aggressive toward larger birds; sallies for insects from exposed perch; chatters constantly; common roadside bird.

Habitat
-------
Open country with scattered trees, roadsides, farms, and rangelands throughout western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Cassin's Kingbird has darker gray chest with white chin; Tropical Kingbird has notched brownish tail without white edges.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White outer tail edges on black tail; yellow belly; gray head; no chest band; common open country western bird.
