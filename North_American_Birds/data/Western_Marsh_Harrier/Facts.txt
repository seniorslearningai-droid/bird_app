Western Marsh Harrier (Circus aeruginosus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large harrier. Male brown with gray wings and tail;
female dark brown with cream crown
and throat. About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Hunts over reedbeds and marshes. Common in Europe.
Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast
of North America.

Habitat
-------
Reedbeds, marshes, and wetlands of Europe and western
Asia. Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Harrier lacks brown body coloration and cream
head of Western Marsh Harrier.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large brown harrier, cream crown and throat. Male
has gray wing patches. Rare Atlantic
vagrant.
