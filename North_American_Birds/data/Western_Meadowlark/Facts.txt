Western Meadowlark (Sturnella neglecta)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brown streaked bird with brilliant yellow underparts, V-shaped black breast band, and white outer tail feathers.

Behavior
--------
Sings beautiful flute-like bubbling song; walks through grass foraging for insects; flushes showing white outer tail.

Habitat
-------
Native grasslands, prairies, and open fields throughout western North America; state bird of several states.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Meadowlark is nearly identical visually but has different song; both have same field marks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow breast with black V; flute-like bubbling song; white outer tail feathers; brown streaked back; grasslands.
