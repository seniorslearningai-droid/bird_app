Western Reef-Heron (Egretta gularis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium heron with two morphs: dark gray or white.
Yellow bill with dark culmen, yellow-green
legs. About 23 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on rocky coasts and reefs. African and Asian
coastal species; very rare vagrant to
North American Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Rocky coasts and estuaries of Africa and Asia. Very
rare vagrant to northeastern
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dark morph similar to Little Blue Heron. White morph
similar to Little or Snowy Egret.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow bill with dark culmen on dark or white heron.
Coastal reef habitat. Very
rare Atlantic vagrant.
