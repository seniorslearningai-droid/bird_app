Western Sandpiper (Calidris mauri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sandpiper with slightly drooped bill tip; rufous back and cap in breeding; gray in winter; black legs.

Behavior
--------
Migrates in enormous flocks; probes mud in shallow water; common peep in western and central US on migration.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra of Alaska; migrates along all coasts and interior; winters on southern coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated Sandpiper has straighter shorter bill; Dunlin has more drooped bill and larger size; Least Sandpiper has yellow legs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Slightly drooped bill longer than Semipalmated; rufous back and cap in breeding; black legs; small peep shorebird.
