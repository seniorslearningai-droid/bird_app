Western Screech-Owl (Megascops kennicottii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small owl with ear tufts, yellow eyes, and gray or brownish cryptic bark-patterned plumage; compact body.

Behavior
--------
Gives descending whinny and double trill calls; nocturnal; roosts in tree cavities by day; hunts insects and small vertebrates.

Habitat
-------
Various woodlands, riparian areas, and deserts of western North America; uses nest boxes.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Screech-Owl is very similar but limited overlap in range; Whiskered Screech-Owl has finer streaking.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small ear-tufted owl; gray bark-like cryptic patterning; yellow eyes; western range; descending whinny call.
