Western Spindalis (Spindalis zena)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird. Male striking with black head, white
stripes, orange-red breast. Female
olive-green. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in fruiting trees. Rare but regular visitor
to Florida Keys from
Bahamas and Cuba.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland of the Bahamas and Cuba. Rare
but regular visitor to southern
Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar bird in Florida range. Male with black-white-orange
pattern unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male unmistakable: black head, white stripes, orange
breast. Rare Florida visitor
from Caribbean islands.
