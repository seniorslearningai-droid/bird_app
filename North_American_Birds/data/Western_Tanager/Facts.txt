Western Tanager (Piranga ludoviciana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has red head, yellow body, and black wings with yellow and white wing bars; female yellow-green with wing bars.

Behavior
--------
Forages in conifer canopy for insects; eats berries in migration; distinctive two-note burry song.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous and mixed forests of western North America; breeds in mountains; widespread in migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Summer Tanager male is all red; Scarlet Tanager male has scarlet body; no other tanager has red head with yellow body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Red head; yellow body; black wings with two white or yellow wing bars; male unmistakable; mountain conifer forests.
