Western Warbling-Vireo (Vireo swainsoni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small plain vireo, gray-green above, whitish below
with faint yellowish wash on flanks.
No wing bars. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Sings persistently from dense foliage,
a rambling warbling song. Forages slowly
for insects in tree canopy.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests, riparian woodlands,
and mountain slopes of western
North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Warbling-Vireo nearly identical. Cassin's Vireo
has white spectacles and wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain vireo with no wing bars or eye ring.
Distinctive warbling song. Western
range helpful for separation.
