Western Wood-Pewee (Contopus sordidulus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium flycatcher with dark gray-olive plumage, two white wing bars, and slightly peaked head; no eye-ring.

Behavior
--------
Sings harsh descending burry peeer call; sallies for insects from exposed dead branch; long-distance migrant.

Habitat
-------
Open woodlands, forest edges, and riparian areas throughout western North America; winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Eastern Wood-Pewee is very similar but different call; Empidonax flycatchers have eye-rings; pewees have longer wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Dark gray-olive; two wing bars; no eye-ring; flat-headed look; harsh burry call; open woodland sallying behavior.
