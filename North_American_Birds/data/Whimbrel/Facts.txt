Whimbrel (Numenius phaeopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large brownish shorebird with long downcurved bill, striped crown, and dark eyeline; mottled brown overall plumage.

Behavior
--------
Migrates along both coasts; eats fiddler crabs and invertebrates; gives rapid repetitive tititi whistles.

Habitat
-------
Tundra for breeding; coastal mudflats, wetlands, and beaches during migration; winters on tropical coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Long-billed Curlew is larger with very long bill and cinnamon underparts; Bristle-thighed Curlew similar but rare.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Long downcurved bill; striped crown with pale central stripe; brown mottled plumage; rapid whistled call on coasts.
