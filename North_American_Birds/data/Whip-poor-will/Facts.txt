Eastern Whip-poor-will (Antrostomus vociferus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Cryptically patterned brown and gray nightjar with large dark eyes, wide mouth, and rictal bristles; white throat patch.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal insect hunter; sings loud repetitive whip-poor-will at dusk and dawn; sits motionless on ground by day.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests of eastern North America; sits on leaf litter; rare and declining species.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chuck-will's-widow is larger with buff throat; Common Nighthawk has white wing bar and pointed wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Cryptic leaf-litter camouflage; repetitive whip-poor-will song at night; white tail corners in male; wide gaping mouth.
