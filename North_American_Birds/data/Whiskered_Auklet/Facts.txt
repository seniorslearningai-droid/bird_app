Whiskered Auklet (Aethia pygmaea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dark auklet with elaborate white facial plumes
(whiskers). Dark gray with small red bill with
white basal plate. About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on zooplankton in tidal rips. Found mainly
in central Aleutian Islands. Dives and forages
in strong currents.

Habitat
-------
Central Aleutian Islands and adjacent Pacific; strongly
tied to tidal rips with strong currents.
Rarely seen far from Aleutians.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Crested Auklet has forward crest. Whiskered Auklet's
multiple white facial plumes distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Multiple white facial whisker plumes. Small size.
Found in Aleutian Island tidal rips.
Rarely far from the Aleutians.
