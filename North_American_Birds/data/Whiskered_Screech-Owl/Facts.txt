Whiskered Screech-Owl (Megascops trichopsis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small owl with ear tufts. Gray-brown streaked plumage,
yellow eyes. Bold facial disk
whisker marks. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Nocturnal insect hunter. Regular resident in oak-pine
forest of southeastern Arizona and
southwestern New Mexico mountains.

Habitat
-------
Oak-pine woodland in mountain canyons of southeastern
Arizona and southwestern New Mexico.
Year-round resident.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Screech-Owl similar but larger with different
call. Whiskered has bolder whisker marks.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold whisker marks on facial disk. Small ear-tufted
owl. Southeastern Arizona mountain
oak woodland resident.
