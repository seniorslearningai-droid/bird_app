Whiskered Tern (Chlidonias hybrida)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small marsh tern. Breeding adult has gray body,
black cap, white cheeks, red bill. Non-breeding
gray and white. About 10 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages over freshwater marshes for insects and
small fish. Rare vagrant to Atlantic coast from
Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on freshwater marshes in Eurasia and Africa.
Rare vagrant to eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Tern in non-breeding. Breeding Whiskered has
gray belly, not black.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray belly (not black like Black Tern) with white
cheeks in breeding. Non-breeding pale
gray overall.
