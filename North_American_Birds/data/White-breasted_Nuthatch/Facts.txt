White-breasted Nuthatch (Sitta carolinensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Black crown contrasting white face and underparts; blue-gray back; rusty vent area; compact neckless body.

Behavior
--------
Creeps headfirst down tree trunks; caches seeds; nasal yank-yank calls; pairs defend territory year-round.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests, oak woodlands, and wooded suburbs throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-breasted Nuthatch has rusty underparts and white eyebrow; Pygmy Nuthatch is tiny and brown-capped.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black cap; white face; creeping headfirst down trees; nasal calls; blue-gray back; compact stocky body.
