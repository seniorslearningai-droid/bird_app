White-capped Albatross (Thalassarche steadi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium mollymawk albatross. White head and body, dark
gray-black back and wings, yellow bill.
About 36 inches wingspan.

Behavior
--------
Pelagic wanderer from New Zealand subantarctic islands.
Very rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean, particularly waters off New Zealand.
Very rare vagrant to North American
Pacific coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Salvin's Albatross has grayish head. White-capped
has cleaner white head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very white head and forehead contrasting with dark
back. Rare vagrant albatross.
Southern Ocean species rare in North America.
