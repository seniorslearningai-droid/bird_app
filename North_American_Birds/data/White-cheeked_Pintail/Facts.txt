White-cheeked Pintail (Anas bahamensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender dabbling duck with brown streaked body,
white cheek and throat, red-based gray bill.
Long pointed tail. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dabbles in shallow saltwater and brackish lagoons.
Less wary than other ducks. Found in Caribbean
and South America; vagrant to Florida.

Habitat
-------
Caribbean wetlands, mangroves, coastal lagoons,
and brackish ponds. Florida records mostly in
South Florida lagoons and ponds.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Northern Pintail similar shape but has white
neck stripe and different overall coloring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright white cheek patch and throat contrasting
with brown body unmistakable. Red bill
base visible at close range.
