White-chinned Petrel (Procellaria aequinoctialis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large all-dark petrel with pale ivory bill, white
chin patch (variable, sometimes absent).
About 22 inches.

Behavior
--------
Large and aggressive. Follows ships. Breeds on South
Atlantic and Indian Ocean islands.
Very rare vagrant to North Atlantic.

Habitat
-------
Southern Ocean. Very rare vagrant to North American
Atlantic and Pacific coasts.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Parkinson's Petrel similar but all black with pale
bill. White chin patch of White-chinned
variable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large dark petrel with pale bill. White chin patch
variable. Follows ships. Very rare
southern vagrant.
