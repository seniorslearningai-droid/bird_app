White-collared Swift (Streptoprocne zonaris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large swift. Entirely black with distinctive
white collar around neck. Large size and collar
unmistakable. About 8 inches with 18-inch wingspan.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on flying insects in fast, powerful flight.
Forms huge flocks. Rare vagrant to southern US
from Mexico and Central America.

Habitat
-------
Tropical and subtropical mountain forests from Mexico
to South America. Rare vagrant to
Texas and Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chimney and Vaux's Swifts much smaller. White collar
unique among swifts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large size for a swift with conspicuous white
collar. All-black plumage. Rare vagrant
in southern US.
