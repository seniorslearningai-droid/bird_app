White-crested Elaenia (Elaenia albiceps)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small flycatcher, olive above, pale below. White crown
crest visible when raised, two wing
bars. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Migratory South American species. Very rare vagrant
to North American Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Woodland and scrub of South America. Very rare vagrant
to Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Other elaenias very similar. White crown crest when
raised.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Olive flycatcher with white crown crest visible when
raised. Two wing bars. Very rare
South American vagrant.
