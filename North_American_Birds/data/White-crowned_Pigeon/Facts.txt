White-crowned Pigeon (Patagioenas leucocephala)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large dark gray pigeon with brilliant white crown.
Iridescent neck feathers with scaly pattern.
Red eye ring. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on fruit, especially poisonwood and other
tropical trees. Nests in mangroves. Found
in Florida Keys and Everglades.

Habitat
-------
Coastal mangrove forests and tropical hardwood
hammocks in Florida Keys and Everglades.
Common in Caribbean.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scaly-naped Pigeon darker and lacks the
brilliant white crown cap.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant white crown cap contrasting with dark
gray body unmistakable. Large pigeon
found in Florida Keys and Caribbean.
