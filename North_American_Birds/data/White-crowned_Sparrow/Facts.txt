White-crowned Sparrow (Zonotrichia leucophrys)
===========================

Description
-----------
Bold black and white striped crown with gray face and breast; pinkish or orangish bill; streaked brown back.

Behavior
--------
Scratches in leaf litter; sings whistled songs varying by subspecies; common migrant and winter feeder bird.

Habitat
-------
Brushy areas, thickets, roadsides, and gardens; breeds in tundra and mountain meadow edges across North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-throated Sparrow has white throat patch and yellow lore; Golden-crowned Sparrow has yellow crown center.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black and white crown stripes; gray face; pinkish bill; streaked brown back; very common at feeders in winter.
