White-eared Hummingbird (Basilinna leucotis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Male has iridescent green body,
purple crown, red bill with black tip, bold white
stripe behind eye. About 3.75 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar; visits mountain flowers. Rare summer
visitor to Arizona mountain canyons from Mexico.
Males sing from exposed perches.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak mountain forests of Mexico and Central
America. Rare visitor to Chiricahua Mountains
and other Arizona sky islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Broad-billed Hummingbird similar green. White-eared's
bold white eye stripe unique.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white stripe behind eye diagnostic. Red bill
with black tip. Male's purple crown and
green body. Rare in Arizona mountains.
