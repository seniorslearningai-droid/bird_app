White-eyed Parakeet (Psittacara leucophthalmus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large parakeet, green with red scattered on face and
wings, white eye ring, long
tail. About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious and noisy. Feral population in Florida.
Forages in fruiting trees.

Habitat
-------
Forest and woodland. Feral populations in Florida.
Native to South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mitred Parakeet more red on face. Blue-crowned Parakeet
has blue crown. White eye
ring distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eye ring on green parakeet. Scattered red on
face and wings. Long tail.
Florida feral.
