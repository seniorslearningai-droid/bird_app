White-eyed Vireo (Vireo griseus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small vireo with white eye, yellow spectacles, two white wing bars, yellow flanks, and olive-green upperparts.

Behavior
--------
Sings loud explosive quick-with-the-beer-quick song in dense thickets; secretive but vocal; mimics other birds.

Habitat
-------
Dense brushy thickets, shrubby forest edges, and overgrown fields of eastern and southeastern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bell's Vireo has faint spectacles and darker eye; other vireos lack white eye and yellow spectacles combination.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White iris; yellow spectacles around white eye; yellow flanks; two wing bars; explosive song from dense thickets.
