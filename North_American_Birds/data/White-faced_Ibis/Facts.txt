White-faced Ibis (Plegadis chihi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Dark glossy maroon-bronze ibis with long downcurved bill; breeding adult has white feathering bordering bare red face.

Behavior
--------
Probes mud with curved bill for invertebrates; flocks fly in lines and V formations; colonial nester.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater and alkaline marshes, flooded fields, and wetlands of western US and Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Glossy Ibis lacks white face border and has blue facial skin; both very similar in non-breeding plumage.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White facial border in breeding; glossy maroon plumage; red facial skin; long downcurved bill; western US marshes.
