White-faced Storm-Petrel (Pelagodroma marina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Unique storm-petrel, pale gray and brown above with
white face and underparts, dark eye
stripe. Bouncing sea-skimmer. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Unique feeding posture: skips along water surface
with feet pushing off. Rare vagrant
to North American Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic and Indian Oceans; breeds on islands. Rare
vagrant to offshore North American
Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other storm-petrel has white face and underparts
with this bold patterning.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White face and underparts with dark eye stripe. Unique
bouncing walking on water surface
feeding method.
