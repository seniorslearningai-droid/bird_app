White-fronted Amazon (Amazona albifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium parrot, green with white forehead, red lores,
blue crown on male. About
10 inches. Smallest Amazona.

Behavior
--------
Active and vocal in flocks. Forages in trees for
fruit. Small feral populations
in Florida.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forest and dry woodland. Small feral populations
in Florida. Native to
Mexico and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-lored Amazon larger. Lilac-crowned Amazon has
lilac crown. Small size distinctive
among Amazon parrots.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White forehead with red lores on green parrot. Smallest
Amazon parrot. Florida
feral populations.
