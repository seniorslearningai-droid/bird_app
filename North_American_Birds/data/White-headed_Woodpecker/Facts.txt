White-headed Woodpecker (Leuconotopicus albolarvatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Striking black woodpecker with entirely white head and large white wing patch visible in flight; male has red nape.

Behavior
--------
Pries bark scales from ponderosa pine to extract seeds; quiet for a woodpecker; local and declining species.

Habitat
-------
Ponderosa pine forests of the mountains of California, Oregon, Washington, and Idaho.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other North American woodpecker has all-white head with black body; distinctive and unmistakable.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white head on black body; large white wing patch; ponderosa pine forest; male's small red nape patch.
