White-necked Petrel (Pterodroma cervicalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gadfly petrel, pale gray above with dark M-pattern,
white underparts, white collar around
nape. About 17 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Kermadec Islands north of New Zealand.
Migrates into North Pacific; very
rare vagrant off Pacific coast.

Habitat
-------
North Pacific in non-breeding season. Very rare vagrant
to North American Pacific
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Juan Fernandez Petrel similar but lacks white collar.
White-necked has distinctive white
nape collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Distinctive white collar around nape. Large pale gadfly
petrel. Very rare visitor to
North Pacific coast.
