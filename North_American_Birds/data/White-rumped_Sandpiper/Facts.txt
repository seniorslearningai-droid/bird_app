White-rumped Sandpiper (Calidris fuscicollis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sandpiper with long wings extending beyond
tail. Brown streaked plumage, white rump, streaked
breast. About 7.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Long-distance migrant; winters in southern South America.
Forages on mudflats. Common in eastern North
America during migration.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra. Migrates through Great Plains
and Atlantic coast. Winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Baird's Sandpiper similar wings-past-tail shape. White
rump distinguishes White-rumped.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White rump patch visible in flight. Wings extend
past tail at rest. Rusty scapulars
in breeding plumage.
