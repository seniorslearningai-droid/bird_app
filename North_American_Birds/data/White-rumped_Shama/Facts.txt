White-rumped Shama (Copsychus malabaricus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bird, male glossy black with chestnut belly,
white rump, long graduated tail.
About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Superb singer with rich, varied song. Bold and
curious. Introduced to Hawaii;
competes with native species.

Habitat
-------
Dense forest undergrowth. Introduced to Hawaii, found
on Oahu, Kauai, and
other islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No similar black-and-chestnut bird in Hawaii. Long
tail and white rump
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Glossy black with chestnut belly and white rump.
Long tail. Superb loud
song. Hawaiian introduced species.
