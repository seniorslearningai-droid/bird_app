White-tailed Eagle (Haliaeetus albicilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Huge eagle with very short white wedge-shaped tail,
massive yellow bill. Brown body, pale
head. About 37 inches.

Behavior
--------
Soars over coasts and large water bodies. Hunts fish,
waterbirds, and mammals. Rare vagrant
to Alaska and Atlantic coast.

Habitat
-------
Coastlines and large lakes of Europe and Asia. Rare
vagrant to Alaska; rare
on Atlantic coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Bald Eagle similar but has longer tail and black body.
White-tailed Eagle very short broad tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Short white wedge-shaped tail, massive yellow bill,
very broad wings. Old World counterpart
to Bald Eagle.
