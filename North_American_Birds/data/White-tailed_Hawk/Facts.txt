White-tailed Hawk (Geranoaetus albicaudatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large hawk, gray above, white below with rufous shoulder
patches, short white tail with
dark subterminal band. About 23 inches.

Behavior
--------
Soars over coastal prairies in Texas. Attracted to
grass fires to catch fleeing prey.
Resident of South Texas.

Habitat
-------
Open coastal prairies and grasslands of southern Texas.
Regular resident in sparse populations
in southernmost Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Swainson's Hawk lacks white tail and rufous shoulders.
White-tailed distinctive pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White tail with dark terminal band, rufous shoulder
patches. Coastal prairie specialist
of southern Texas.
