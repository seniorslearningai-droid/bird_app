White-tailed Kite (Elanus leucurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant white and gray raptor with black shoulder patches, red eyes, and long white tail; falcon-like proportions.

Behavior
--------
Hovers kestrel-like over grasslands hunting voles; roosts communally in winter; gives high whistled calls.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, marshes, and agricultural fields of California, Texas, and Pacific Coast lowlands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mississippi Kite is gray throughout; Swallow-tailed Kite is black and white with forked tail; harriers have different shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White body; black shoulder patches; hovers persistently over grasslands; long white tail; red eye; gray wings.
