White-tailed Ptarmigan (Lagopus leucura)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small grouse; white in winter with black bill and eye; mottled brown and white in summer; white tail always visible.

Behavior
--------
Lives above treeline year-round; roosts in snow; feeds on alpine willow buds and leaves; very tame bird.

Habitat
-------
Alpine tundra above treeline in Rocky Mountains and Cascades; year-round resident in harsh mountain environments.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Willow and Rock Ptarmigan have black outer tail feathers; both are larger; White-tailed always shows white tail.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white tail in all seasons; very small size among ptarmigan; rocky alpine habitat; entirely white in winter.
