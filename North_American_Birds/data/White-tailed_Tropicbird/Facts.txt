White-tailed Tropicbird (Phaethon lepturus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Elegant white seabird with very long white central
tail streamers, black eye stripe, yellow
or orange bill. About 30 inches including tail.

Behavior
--------
Plunge-dives for fish. Floats with tail elevated.
Breeds on tropical islands; found offshore
in Atlantic and Pacific.

Habitat
-------
Tropical oceans worldwide. Breeds on Bermuda and
Caribbean islands. Offshore in
Gulf of Mexico and Atlantic.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-billed and Red-tailed Tropicbirds differ in bill
color and tail streamer color.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very long white tail streamers, white plumage, black
eye stripe. Yellow or orange
bill. Graceful aerial seabird.
