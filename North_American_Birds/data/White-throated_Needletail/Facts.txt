White-throated Needletail (Hirundapus caudacutus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large swift. Dark brown with white throat,
white undertail coverts, and white on back forming
horseshoe shape. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Extremely fast flier; said to be one of the fastest
birds in level flight. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska and Pacific islands from Asia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in East Asian and Siberian forests. Migrates
to Australia and Southeast Asia. Rare vagrant
to western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-collared Swift has white collar. Needletail
larger than other swifts with distinctive shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large size among swifts. White throat and
horseshoe-shaped white back pattern. Needle-
like tail visible at close range.
