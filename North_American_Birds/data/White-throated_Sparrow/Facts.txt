White-throated Sparrow (Zonotrichia albicollis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Brown-streaked sparrow with bold white throat patch, yellow lore spot, and black and white or brown and tan crown stripes.

Behavior
--------
Scratches loudly in leaf litter; sings clear Oh sweet Canada Canada whistled song; common feeder bird in winter.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forests for breeding; brushy thickets, forest edges, and gardens throughout eastern US in winter.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-crowned Sparrow lacks white throat patch and yellow lore; Golden-crowned Sparrow lacks distinct throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White throat patch; yellow lore spot; black and white crown stripes; song translates as Oh sweet Canada Canada.
