White-throated Swift (Aeronautes saxatalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Fast-flying swift with black and white plumage; white throat and center of belly; black sides; white flanks patch.

Behavior
--------
Extremely fast flight; forages aerially for insects; nests in cliff crevices; chatters loudly in fast flocks.

Habitat
-------
Rocky cliffs, canyons, and mountain faces throughout western North America; forages widely over open country.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Vaux's Swift is all dark brown; Black Swift is all black; no other western swift has bold black and white pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black and white contrasting pattern; white throat and belly center; black sides; very fast flight near cliffs.
