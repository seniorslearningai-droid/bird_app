White-throated Thrush (Turdus assimilis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium thrush, gray-brown above, white throat with
brown streaks, orange-yellow bill.
About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in forest understory for fruit. Rare vagrant
to extreme southern Texas from
Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Humid forest of Mexico and Central America. Rare
vagrant to southern Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Clay-colored Thrush lacks white throat. Rufous-backed
Robin has rufous back.
American Robin has red breast.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White throat with dark streaking contrasts with
brown upperparts. Orange bill.
Rare Texas vagrant from Mexico.
