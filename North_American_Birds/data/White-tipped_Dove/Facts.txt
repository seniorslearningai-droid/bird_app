White-tipped Dove (Leptotila verreauxi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium-sized dove. Brown above, pale pinkish-buff
below, iridescent neck patch, white tail tips
visible in flight, red eye ring. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks on ground foraging for seeds and fruit.
Relatively tame. Resident in southern Texas;
common in Central and South America.

Habitat
-------
Dense scrub, forests, and woodland edges in
southern Texas (Lower Rio Grande Valley).
Common in Mexico and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-winged Dove larger with prominent wing patches.
White-tipped has distinctive pale underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Plain brown bird with white tail corners visible
in flight, bare red orbital ring,
and pale pinkish underparts.
