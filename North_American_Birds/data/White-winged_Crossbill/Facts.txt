White-winged Crossbill (Loxia leucoptera)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch with crossed mandibles; male pink-red with black wings and two bold white wing bars; female greenish.

Behavior
--------
Pries open spruce cones with crossed bill; nomadic following cone crops; chatters in fast flocks through spruces.

Habitat
-------
Boreal spruce forests of Canada and northern mountains; irrupts south when cone crops fail.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red Crossbill lacks white wing bars; Pine Grosbeak is much larger; Common Redpoll is streaked without crossed bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Crossed bill tips; two bold white wing bars; pink-red male; nomadic boreal forest; spruce cone specialist.
