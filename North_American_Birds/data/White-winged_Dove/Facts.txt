White-winged Dove (Zenaida asiatica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Grayish-brown dove with bold white wing stripe, blue orbital ring around orange-red eye, and rounded tail with white tips.

Behavior
--------
Gives who cooks for you song; expanding northward from desert Southwest; common at feeders and urban areas.

Habitat
-------
Desert scrub, riparian woodlands, and urban areas; expanding range northward and eastward from core range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mourning Dove has pointed tail without white tips; Inca Dove is much smaller and scaly; Eurasian Collared-Dove has collar.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold white wing stripe on folded wing; blue eye-ring; orange eye; rounded tail with white corners; deserts and cities.
