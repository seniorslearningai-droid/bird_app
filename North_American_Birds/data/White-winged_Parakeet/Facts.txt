White-winged Parakeet (Brotogeris versicolurus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small green parakeet with distinctive white and yellow
wing patches. Short pointed tail,
yellow bill. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral population in Miami and Los Angeles
areas. Forms flocks in suburban
areas.

Habitat
-------
Feral in suburban South Florida and Los Angeles.
Original range is South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-chevroned Parakeet very similar but has yellow-only
wing chevron.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing patch with yellow. Small green parakeet.
Feral in Miami and Los Angeles
suburban areas.
