White-winged Scoter (Melanitta deglandi)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large sea duck; male black with teardrop white eye patch and white wing speculum; orange bill with black knob.

Behavior
--------
Dives for mussels and clams; large winter flocks on Great Lakes and coastal waters; breeds on boreal lakes.

Habitat
-------
Boreal lakes and rivers for nesting; winters on both coasts and Great Lakes of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Surf Scoter lacks white wing patch; Black Scoter is all black with yellow bill knob; all scoters dark-bodied.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White speculum visible in flight and at rest; white teardrop eye mark; black body; orange bill with black knob.
