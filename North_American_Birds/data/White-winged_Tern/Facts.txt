White-winged Tern (Chlidonias leucopterus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small marsh tern. Breeding adult has black body,
white wings, red bill. Non-breeding gray and
white. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages over marshes picking insects and small fish
from surface. Rare vagrant to North America from
Eurasia.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Eurasian freshwater marshes. Winters in
Africa and Asia. Rare vagrant to both
coasts of North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black Tern similar. Breeding White-winged has white
wing coverts contrasting with black body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Breeding bird's white wings contrasting with black
body unmistakable. Non-breeding similar to
Black Tern but paler.
