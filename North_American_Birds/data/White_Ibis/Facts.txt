White Ibis (Eudocimus albus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium wading bird, all white plumage, bright red
downturned bill and face, red legs.
About 25 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in shallow water and mudflats. Highly gregarious;
flocks rest and nest in trees.
Common in southeastern US.

Habitat
-------
Coastal marshes, mangroves, and freshwater wetlands
of southeastern US. Common from
Carolina to Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Scarlet Ibis bright red; hybrids can be intermediate.
Wood Stork larger and black-winged.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All-white plumage with red downturned bill and red
face. Common wading bird in
southeastern US coastal marshes.
