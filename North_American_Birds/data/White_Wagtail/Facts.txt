White Wagtail (Motacilla alba)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium wagtail, black and white pattern, gray back,
white face with black throat
and cap. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Walks rapidly on ground wagging long tail constantly.
Breeds in western Alaska.
Bold and conspicuous.

Habitat
-------
Open areas near water, towns, and open ground.
Breeds in western Alaska near
human habitation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-backed Wagtail very similar subspecies. Eastern
Yellow Wagtail yellow below.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black-and-white pattern with long wagging tail. White
face, black throat. Western
Alaska breeder and vagrant.
