Whooper Swan (Cygnus cygnus)
===========================

Description
-----------
A large white swan with a long, straight neck and distinctive yellow-and-black bill pattern. Adults weigh 7-12 kg with a 2-meter wingspan.

Behavior
--------
Grazes on aquatic vegetation, grasses, and crops. Highly vocal with a trumpeting call. Migrates in family groups over long distances.

Habitat
-------
Breeds near lakes and rivers in northern Eurasia. Winters on coastal bays, flooded fields, and large lakes in temperate regions.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Very similar to Trumpeter Swan in North America. Bewick's Swan is smaller with less yellow on bill. Mute Swan has orange bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large yellow patch on bill extending to a point (not rounded), straight neck posture, loud bugling call, and lack of knob on bill distinguish it.
