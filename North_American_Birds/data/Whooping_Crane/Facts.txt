Whooping Crane (Grus americana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large white crane with red and black head, black wingtips visible in flight; tallest North American bird.

Behavior
--------
Endangered; loud whooping calls carry miles; dances in courtship; migrates between Texas and Canada.

Habitat
-------
Aransas National Wildlife Refuge in Texas for wintering; Wood Buffalo National Park in Canada for breeding.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sandhill Crane is gray; Snow Goose is smaller; Trumpeter Swan lacks red crown and black face mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tall all-white bird with red crown and black mask; black wingtips; very rare and endangered; loud whooping call.
