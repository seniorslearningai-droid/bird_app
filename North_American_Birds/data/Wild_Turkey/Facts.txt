Wild Turkey (Meleagris gallopavo)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very large ground bird; male iridescent bronze and black with bare blue and red head, spurs, and fanned tail display.

Behavior
--------
Males gobble and strut in spring; forages for acorns and insects; roosts in trees; large flocks in winter.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous and mixed forests, open woodlands, and brushy edges throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Domestic turkey is very similar but tends to be fatter; no other bird could be confused with a Wild Turkey.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very large size; bronze iridescent body; bare red-blue head; fan tail display in male; gobbling call.
