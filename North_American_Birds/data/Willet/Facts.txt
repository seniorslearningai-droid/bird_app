Willet (Tringa semipalmata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gray-brown shorebird appearing plain at rest but revealing bold black and white wing pattern in flight.

Behavior
--------
Calls pill-will-willet loudly; aggressive near nest; probes mud for invertebrates; common coastal shorebird.

Habitat
-------
Coastal beaches, marshes, and mudflats; interior birds breed on prairie wetlands; two distinct subspecies.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Greater Yellowlegs has yellow legs and spotted back; Godwits have upturned or straight longer bills.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black and white wing stripe; plain gray-brown at rest; thick straight bill; loud pill-will-willet call.
