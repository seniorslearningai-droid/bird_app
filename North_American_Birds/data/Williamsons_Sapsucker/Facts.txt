Williamson's Sapsucker (Sphyrapicus thyroideus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male black with white wing patches, red throat, and yellow belly; female looks completely different with brown barring.

Behavior
--------
Drills sap wells in conifers; licks sap and catches insects attracted to it; quiet compared to other sapsuckers.

Habitat
-------
Mature coniferous forests of Rocky Mountains and Sierra Nevada mountains of western North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Male unlike any other woodpecker; female resembles Northern Flicker; Yellow-bellied Sapsucker different pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Male's black body with white wing patches and red throat; yellow belly; extreme sexual dimorphism in this species.
