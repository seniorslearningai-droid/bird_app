Willow Flycatcher (Empidonax traillii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plain olive-brown Empidonax with faint eye-ring, two white wing bars, and olive-gray breast; no distinctive markings.

Behavior
--------
Sings fitz-bew! or sneeze-like song; identified almost exclusively by voice; nests in willows near water.

Habitat
-------
Willow and alder thickets near streams, wet meadows, and marsh edges throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Alder Flycatcher is essentially identical but sings fee-bee-o; other Empidonax flycatchers require voice for ID.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Fitz-bew call essential for identification; faint eye-ring; willow habitat; olive-brown plumage; two wing bars.
