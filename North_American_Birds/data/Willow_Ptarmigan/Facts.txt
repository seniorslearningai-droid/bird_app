Willow Ptarmigan (Lagopus lagopus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest ptarmigan; male brick-red head and white body in spring; all white in winter; black outer tail feathers.

Behavior
--------
Males cackle and display in spring; forms large winter flocks; forages for willow buds; walks on snow via feathered feet.

Habitat
-------
Arctic and subarctic tundra; associated with willow shrub thickets; widespread across northern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Rock Ptarmigan is grayer with black lore line in male; White-tailed Ptarmigan has white outer tail feathers.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black outer tail feathers; red combs over eyes in male; brick-red head in spring breeding plumage; willow tundra.
