Willow Warbler (Phylloscopus trochilus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small leaf warbler, olive-green above, pale yellowish
below with pale supercilium.
About 4.5 inches. Flesh-colored legs.

Behavior
--------
Active insect forager in tree canopy. Beautiful
descending song. Rare vagrant to
western and eastern Alaska.

Habitat
-------
Deciduous woodland, scrub, and forest edges across
Europe and Asia. Rare vagrant
to Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Chiffchaff nearly identical but has dark legs.
Arctic Warbler has wing bar.
Yellow-browed Warbler has two wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Flesh-colored legs (vs black in Chiffchaff). Plain
wings no bars. Pale yellowish
below. Very rare Alaska vagrant.
