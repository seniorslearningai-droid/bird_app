Wilson's Phalarope (Phalaropus tricolor)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium shorebird; breeding female has black and chestnut neck stripe on gray back; white underparts; needle-like bill.

Behavior
--------
Female is brightly colored; polyandrous; spins in circles on water to stir up invertebrates; swims while feeding.

Habitat
-------
Prairie potholes and marshes for breeding; large saline lakes during migration; winters in South America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-necked Phalarope is smaller with shorter bill; Red Phalarope is stockier with broader bill; both more oceanic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Needle-like thin bill; female's bold black and chestnut neck stripe; spinning feeding behavior on water surface.
