Wilson's Plover (Charadrius wilsonia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium plover with single broad breast band, disproportionately thick black bill, and pinkish legs; stocky build.

Behavior
--------
Forages for fiddler crabs and ghost crabs on beaches; calls soft wit notes; nests in open beach scrapes.

Habitat
-------
Sandy beaches, tidal flats, and barrier islands along Atlantic and Gulf coasts of southeastern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Semipalmated Plover has orange bill base and smaller bill; Piping Plover is paler with orange bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Very thick black bill; wide black breast band; pinkish legs; single breast band; sandy coastal beach habitat.
