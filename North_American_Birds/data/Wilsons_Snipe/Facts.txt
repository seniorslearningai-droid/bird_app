Wilson's Snipe (Gallinago delicata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Plump brown shorebird with extremely long straight bill, bold buffy crown stripes, and streaked brown camouflage.

Behavior
--------
Probes deep in mud for earthworms; flushes explosively with scaipe call; male makes winnowing display flight.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes, wet meadows, and boggy fields throughout North America; secretive among dense vegetation.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
American Woodcock is plumper with eyes set far back; dowitchers are longer necked and flock-flying birds.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Extremely long bill; bold buffy head stripes; explosive flushing; camouflaged brown plumage; wet field habitat.
