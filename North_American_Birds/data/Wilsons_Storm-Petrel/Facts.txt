Wilson's Storm-Petrel (Oceanites oceanicus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small dark storm-petrel with white rump, square-cut
tail, long legs with yellow-webbed feet
visible in flight. About 7 inches.

Behavior
--------
Very abundant seabird worldwide. Patters on water
surface feeding. Breeds in Antarctic;
migrates north in summer.

Habitat
-------
Atlantic and Pacific Oceans. Common Atlantic coast
in summer when Southern Hemisphere
breeders migrate north.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Leach's Storm-Petrel larger with forked tail. Wilson's
has square tail and longer legs.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Square tail, yellow-webbed feet dangling in flight.
White rump. Small dark seabird
very common in Atlantic summer.
