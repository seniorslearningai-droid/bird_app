Wilson's Warbler (Cardellina pusilla)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small yellow warbler; male has black cap on bright yellow face and underparts; female may have faint dark cap.

Behavior
--------
Very active and energetic; flicks wings; catches insects aerially; sings rapid staccato chi-chi-chi song.

Habitat
-------
Streamside willows, alder thickets, and moist mountain shrubs; migrates throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow Warbler lacks black cap; Hooded Warbler male has black hood not just cap; Common Yellowthroat has mask.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow face and underparts; round black cap in male; energetic flicking wing behavior; willow thickets.
