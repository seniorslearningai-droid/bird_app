Winter Wren (Troglodytes hiemalis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny dark brown wren with very short upright tail, barred plumage, and pale eyebrow; round compact body shape.

Behavior
--------
Creeps mouse-like through dense undergrowth; sings extraordinarily long complex song for its tiny size.

Habitat
-------
Dense coniferous forest undergrowth, windfall tangles, and forested ravines of eastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pacific Wren is nearly identical western counterpart; Marsh Wren and Sedge Wren prefer open wetlands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Tiny size; very short upright stubby tail; dark brown barred plumage; dense forest floor habitat; loud complex song.
