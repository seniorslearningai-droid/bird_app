Wood Duck (Aix sponsa)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male has iridescent green and purple crested head with white facial stripes; female gray-brown with white eye-ring.

Behavior
--------
Nests in tree cavities; ducklings jump from nest on first day; male's rising squeaky call; perches in trees.

Habitat
-------
Wooded swamps, beaver ponds, and flooded forests throughout eastern and Pacific Coast North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Mandarin Duck male similar but different head pattern; female resembles other female ducks but has white eye-ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Ornate iridescent male; crested head with white facial lines; female's white teardrop eye-ring; wooded wetland.
