Wood Sandpiper (Tringa glareola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium sandpiper. Brown spotted upperparts, white
supercilium, white underparts with streaked breast,
yellow legs. About 8 inches.

Behavior
--------
Bobs tail. Forages in freshwater wetlands. Rare
but regular vagrant to western Alaska; rare
elsewhere in North America.

Habitat
-------
Breeds in Eurasian boreal forests and bogs. Winters
in Africa and Asia. Rare vagrant to
western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Solitary Sandpiper very similar. Wood Sandpiper has
white rump and different leg color.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White spotted brown back, white rump in flight,
yellow legs. Regular vagrant in western
Alaska.
