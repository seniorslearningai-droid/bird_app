Wood Stork (Mycteria americana)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large white wading bird with bare dark gray head and neck, black flight feathers, and thick down-curved bill.

Behavior
--------
Uses tactile feeding method swinging bill through water; colonial nester in cypress trees; soars on thermals.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater and coastal wetlands, cypress swamps of southeastern US; expanding nesting northward.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Great Egret is all white including flight feathers; Whooping Crane is taller with different profile in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bald dark head; black flight feathers on white body; thick decurved bill; soars with neck extended; southeastern US.
