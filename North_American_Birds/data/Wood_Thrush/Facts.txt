Wood Thrush (Hylocichla mustelina)
===========================

Description
-----------
Reddish-brown head and back with white underparts boldly spotted with large black spots; orange-red eye-ring.

Behavior
--------
Sings beautiful flute-like ee-oh-lay phrases; forages on leaf litter; declining due to forest fragmentation.

Habitat
-------
Moist deciduous forests of eastern North America; requires large intact forest tracts for breeding success.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Hermit Thrush is smaller with rufous tail; Veery has less distinct spotting; Swainson's has buffy eye-ring.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Reddish-brown head contrasting with spotted white breast; large dark spots; orange eye-ring; forest interior.
