Wood Warbler (Phylloscopus sibilatrix)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium leaf warbler with bright yellow supercilium
and throat, white belly, green
back. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages high in deciduous canopy. Distinctive song
with spinning trill. Very rare
vagrant to northeastern North America.

Habitat
-------
Mature deciduous forest in Europe. Very rare vagrant
to northeastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-browed Warbler smaller with wing bars. Willow
Warbler lacks bright yellow
supercilium.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow supercilium and throat contrast with
white belly. Green back. Very
rare European vagrant.
