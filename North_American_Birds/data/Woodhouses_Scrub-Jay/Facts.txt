Woodhouse's Scrub-Jay (Aphelocoma woodhouseii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium scrub-jay, blue above, gray-brown back,
whitish below with incomplete blue breast
band. About 11 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground and in shrubs for acorns,
insects, and berries. Caches food.
Often found in pinyon-juniper habitat.

Habitat
-------
Arid scrub, pinyon-juniper woodland, and chaparral
of interior western United States
from Oregon south to Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
California Scrub-Jay coastal and brighter blue.
Florida Scrub-Jay has whitish
forehead and different range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Interior western range. Dull blue with grayish
back. Incomplete blue breast band.
Pinyon-juniper habitat preference.
