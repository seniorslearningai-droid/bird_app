Worm-eating Warbler (Helmitheros vermivorum)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium warbler, olive-green above, buff below, bold
black crown stripes on buff
head. About 5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground and in dead leaf clusters searching
for caterpillars. Walks more
than it hops.

Habitat
-------
Mature deciduous forest on hillsides in eastern United
States. Winters in Caribbean
and Central America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Swainson's Warbler has plain crown. Ovenbird has orange
crown stripe. Bold head
striping distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bold black-and-buff striped head. Olive above, buff
below. Dead leaf cluster forager.
Hillside forest specialist.
