Worthen's Sparrow (Spizella wortheni)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small sparrow, rusty cap, gray face, plain pale
below with white wing bars.
Similar to Field Sparrow. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in open grassland and scrub. Very rare vagrant
to New Mexico from Mexico.
Globally endangered.

Habitat
-------
Grasslands and shrubby areas of northeastern Mexico.
Very rare vagrant to
New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Field Sparrow has pink bill and eye ring. Clay-colored
Sparrow has brown cheek patch.
Nearly identical to Field Sparrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Nearly identical to Field Sparrow. Very rare Mexico
vagrant to New Mexico. Dark
loral area helpful.
