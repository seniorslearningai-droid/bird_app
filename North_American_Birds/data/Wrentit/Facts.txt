Wrentit (Chamaea fasciata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small brown bird with long rounded tail, short bill, white eye, and lightly streaked breast; tit-like and wren-like.

Behavior
--------
Very secretive; rarely seen despite loud bouncing-ball song; pairs stay together year-round in dense chaparral.

Habitat
-------
Coastal chaparral and dense scrub of California and southern Oregon; highly resident in small territories.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wrens are more boldly barred; titmice have different body shape; Bewick's Wren has more prominent supercilium.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White eye in brown face; long round tail; staccato bouncing-ball song accelerating into trill; coastal chaparral.
