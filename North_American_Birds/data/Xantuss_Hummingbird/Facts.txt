Xantus's Hummingbird (Basilinna xantusii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hummingbird. Male has green body, cinnamon-
rufous belly, white stripe behind eye, red bill.
Female similar but duller. About 3.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Feeds on nectar and small insects. Endemic to Baja
California; extremely rare vagrant to California.
Active and territorial at feeders.

Habitat
-------
Endemic to Baja California Sur, Mexico, in scrub
and woodland. Very rare vagrant to
southern California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-eared Hummingbird similar. Xantus's rufous belly
distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Rufous belly and white eye stripe unique combination.
Red bill. Very rare vagrant
to California from Baja California.
