Yellow-bellied Flycatcher (Empidonax flaviventris)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small Empidonax with yellow underparts including throat, yellow eye-ring, olive back, and two yellowish wing bars.

Behavior
--------
Sings soft per-wee or che-bunk calls; found in boreal bogs; most yellow-tinged of Empidonax flycatchers.

Habitat
-------
Boreal forest bogs, spruce-fir forests, and moist northern woodlands; migrates through eastern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Acadian Flycatcher has yellow only on sides; other Empidonax lack yellow throat; Yellow Warbler has different shape.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow throat and belly; yellow eye-ring; olive back; greenish tones throughout; boreal bog breeding habitat.
