Yellow-bellied Sapsucker (Sphyrapicus varius)
===========================

Description
-----------
Black and white woodpecker; male has red crown and red throat; female has white throat; yellow belly wash; white wing stripe.

Behavior
--------
Drills rows of sap wells in trees; many species drink sap from these wells; drumming is irregular and halting.

Habitat
-------
Mixed and deciduous forests of eastern North America; breeds in northern forests; winters in southeastern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Williamson's Sapsucker is different; Red-naped Sapsucker has red nape patch; Hairy Woodpecker lacks yellow belly.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White wing stripe; yellow belly; red crown; irregular halting drumming; rows of sap wells drilled in tree trunks.
