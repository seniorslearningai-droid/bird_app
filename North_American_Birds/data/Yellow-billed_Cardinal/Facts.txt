Yellow-billed Cardinal (Paroaria capitata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bird, red head, black back, white below, bright
yellow bill. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds near water. Introduced
to Hawaii; present on several
islands.

Habitat
-------
Open areas near water, gardens, and parks. Introduced
to Hawaii.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-crested Cardinal has crest and gray back. Northern
Cardinal has all-red body.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow bill with red head and black back. White
belly. Introduced to Hawaii.
No crest unlike Red-crested Cardinal.
