Yellow-billed Cuckoo (Coccyzus americanus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Slender brownish bird with white underparts, yellow bill base, rufous wing flash, and bold white tail spots below.

Behavior
--------
Eats caterpillars especially tent caterpillars; slow deliberate movements; calls hollow ka-ka-ka-kow series.

Habitat
-------
Dense riparian woodlands, thickets, and forest edges of North America; declining in western US populations.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-billed Cuckoo has red eye-ring and smaller tail spots; Mangrove Cuckoo has buffy underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow lower mandible; rufous flash in wing; bold white tail spots below; slender build; hollow wooden call series.
