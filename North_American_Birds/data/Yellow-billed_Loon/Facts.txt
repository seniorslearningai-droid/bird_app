Yellow-billed Loon (Gavia adamsii)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest loon. Distinctive ivory-yellow upturned bill,
black and white checkered back in breeding.
About 34 inches.

Behavior
--------
Dives for fish in deep Arctic lakes. Rare but regular
winter visitor on Pacific and occasionally
Atlantic coasts.

Habitat
-------
Breeds on Arctic tundra lakes in North America and
Siberia. Winters primarily on Pacific
coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Loon very similar. Yellow-billed Loon's pale
ivory-yellow upturned bill distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Pale ivory-yellow bill that appears upturned at tip.
Largest loon species. Rare winter
visitor on Pacific coast.
