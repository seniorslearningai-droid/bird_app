Yellow-billed Magpie (Pica nuttalli)
===========================

Description
-----------
Black and white magpie with distinctive bright yellow bill and yellow facial skin patch; very long graduated tail.

Behavior
--------
Social and noisy; caches food; forages in oak woodlands and orchards; corvid intelligence; flocks in winter.

Habitat
-------
Endemic to Central Valley of California; oak woodlands, farmlands, and riparian areas only in California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-billed Magpie is identical in shape and pattern but has dark bill; no other yellow-billed magpie exists.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow bill; yellow facial skin; long black tail with iridescent sheen; endemic only to California's Central Valley.
