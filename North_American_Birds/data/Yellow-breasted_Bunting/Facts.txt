Yellow-breasted Bunting (Emberiza aureola)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male chestnut above, bright yellow
below with chestnut breast band.
Female streaked yellow-buff. About 5.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds. Rare vagrant to western
Alaska. Globally endangered
due to trapping in Asia.

Habitat
-------
Wet scrub and meadows of Siberia and East Asia.
Rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pine Bunting has white head not yellow underparts.
Yellow Warbler much smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow underparts with chestnut breast band
on male. Rare Alaska vagrant.
Globally endangered species.
