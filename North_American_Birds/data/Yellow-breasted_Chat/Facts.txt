Yellow-breasted Chat (Icteria virens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Largest North American warbler; yellow breast, olive-green back, white spectacles, black lores, and heavy bill.

Behavior
--------
Sings bizarre mix of whistles, cackles, and hoots; often sings at night; very secretive in dense thickets.

Habitat
-------
Dense brushy thickets, overgrown fields, and shrubby streamside areas throughout temperate North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
No other warbler-like bird has yellow breast with white spectacles and black lore; Common Yellowthroat much smaller.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large size for warbler; bright yellow breast; white spectacles with black lore; bizarre varied song; dense thickets.
