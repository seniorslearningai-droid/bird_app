Yellow-browed Bunting (Emberiza chrysophrys)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting, streaked brown with yellow supercilium,
black head with white crown
stripe. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds. Very rare vagrant to
western Alaska and Aleutian
Islands from Siberia.

Habitat
-------
Mixed forest of Siberia. Very rare vagrant to western
Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-throated Bunting has yellow throat. Rustic Bunting
has chestnut tones.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow supercilium on black head with white crown
stripe. Very rare Alaska vagrant.
