Yellow-browed Warbler (Phylloscopus inornatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small leaf warbler, olive-green above with two
yellow-white wing bars, bold supercilium.
About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Active and restless forager in canopy. Increasingly
common vagrant to eastern North
America in fall.

Habitat
-------
Forests of Siberia and Asia. Increasingly regular
vagrant to northeastern North America
in fall migration.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Pallas's Leaf Warbler has crown stripe and rump
patch. Hume's Warbler buffier
with less distinct wing bars.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Two bold pale wing bars with prominent supercilium.
Small olive-green warbler. Increasingly
regular fall vagrant.
