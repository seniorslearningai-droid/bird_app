Yellow-chevroned Parakeet (Brotogeris chiriri)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small green parakeet with distinctive yellow wing
patches. Short pointed tail, pale bill.
About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Established feral populations in Miami and Los Angeles.
Often mixes with White-winged
Parakeet.

Habitat
-------
Feral in suburban South Florida and Los Angeles.
Replaces White-winged Parakeet in
much of Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
White-winged Parakeet has white in wing patch. Yellow-
chevroned has only yellow chevron.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow-only wing chevron, no white. Small green parakeet.
Feral Miami and Los Angeles
suburban areas.
