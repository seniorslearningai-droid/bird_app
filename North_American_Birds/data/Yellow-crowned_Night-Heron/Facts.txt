Yellow-crowned Night-Heron (Nyctanassa violacea)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gray heron with black head, white cheek patch, yellow crown, and red eye; stout bill; adults distinctive.

Behavior
--------
Specializes in catching crabs and crayfish; more crepuscular and nocturnal; gives barking call in flight.

Habitat
-------
Coastal mangroves, wooded swamps, and freshwater marshes of southeastern US and Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-crowned Night-Heron has white and black head pattern and different crown color; immatures both similar brown.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Gray body; black face with white cheek patch; pale yellowish crown; red eye; stout bill for crushing crustaceans.
