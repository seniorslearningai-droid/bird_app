Yellow-eyed Junco (Junco phaeonotus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium junco with bright yellow iris, gray head,
rusty back, white outer tail
feathers. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in pine-oak forest. Year-round
resident in southeastern Arizona
mountains.

Habitat
-------
Pine-oak forests of the Madrean sky islands in
southeastern Arizona and New Mexico
into Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Dark-eyed Junco has dark eye. No other junco has
yellow iris in range.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow iris distinctive. Rusty back. Pine-oak
forest in Arizona sky islands.
Year-round resident.
