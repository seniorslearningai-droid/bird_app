Yellow-faced Grassquit (Tiaris olivaceus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Tiny finch. Male olive-green with bright yellow face
markings and black breast.
Female duller. About 4 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages for grass seeds in open areas. Rare vagrant
to Florida and Texas from
Caribbean and Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands and weedy areas. Rare visitor to
southern Florida from Caribbean
and Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-faced Grassquit male blacker with less yellow.
Bananaquit different pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow face markings and black breast on olive-green
body. Tiny size. Rare Florida
and Texas vagrant.
