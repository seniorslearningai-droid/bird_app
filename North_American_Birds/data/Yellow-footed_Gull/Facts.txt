Yellow-footed Gull (Larus livens)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, very dark gray back, bright yellow feet
and legs, yellow bill with red spot.
About 26 inches.

Behavior
--------
Breeds on Gulf of California islands; post-breeding
birds move to Salton Sea in summer.
Closely related to Western Gull.

Habitat
-------
Gulf of California and Salton Sea. Post-breeding
visitors make Salton Sea their specialty
summer location in California.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Western Gull almost identical but has pink legs.
Yellow-footed Gull's bright yellow legs diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow legs and feet on large dark-backed gull.
Salton Sea in summer. Very
similar to Western Gull otherwise.
