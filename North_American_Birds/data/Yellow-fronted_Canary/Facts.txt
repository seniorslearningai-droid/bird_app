Yellow-fronted Canary (Crithagra mozambica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small finch, olive-green above with yellow supercilium,
yellow-green below, gray cheeks.
About 4.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Gregarious seed-eater in open areas. Introduced to
Hawaii; common in dry open
areas.

Habitat
-------
Open grasslands, scrub, and gardens. Introduced to
Hawaii, particularly common on
Oahu.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Warbling White-eye has white eye ring. Anianiau
smaller and brighter in Hawaii.
Yellow supercilium distinctive.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow supercilium and yellow-green underparts. Gray
cheeks. Introduced to Hawaiian
open grasslands. Seed-eater.
