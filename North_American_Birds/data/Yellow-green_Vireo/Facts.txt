Yellow-green Vireo (Vireo flavoviridis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium vireo, olive-green above, yellow-green below
and on sides. Gray crown, white
supercilium, red eye. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages slowly through canopy, picking insects
from leaves. Persistent singer like Red-eyed
Vireo. Rare vagrant to Texas.

Habitat
-------
Tropical forests of Central America and Mexico.
Rare but regular vagrant along
Texas Gulf Coast in fall.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Red-eyed Vireo very similar but whiter below with
less yellow. Yellow-throated Vireo
has wing bars and yellow throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow-green coloration on underparts and sides.
Red iris. Gray crown with
white supercilium. Texas vagrant.
