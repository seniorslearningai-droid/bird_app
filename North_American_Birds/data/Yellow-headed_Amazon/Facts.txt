Yellow-headed Amazon (Amazona oratrix)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium parrot, green with yellow head increasing with
age. Red wing patch, yellow-orange
on wing bend. About 15 inches.

Behavior
--------
Critically endangered in Mexico; established small feral
populations in California and Florida.
Loud screeching calls.

Habitat
-------
Feral in Los Angeles and possibly Florida. Critically
endangered in native Mexican and
Central American range.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-naped Amazon has yellow nape only. Yellow-headed
has more extensive yellow head.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow head with red wing patch. Grows more yellow
with age. Critically endangered
Mexican species, feral in California.
