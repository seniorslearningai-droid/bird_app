Yellow-headed Blackbird (Xanthocephalus xanthocephalus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Male unmistakable with brilliant yellow head and breast on black body with white wing patches; female brownish.

Behavior
--------
Nests over water in reeds; males sing harsh mechanical grinding songs; forages in large flocks on grain fields.

Habitat
-------
Freshwater marshes with tall emergent vegetation; breeds in western North America; winters in southwestern US.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Male unmistakable; female resembles other blackbirds but has yellow wash on face and throat; no similar species.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant yellow head and breast on black body; white wing patches in male; unmistakable in fresh water marshes.
