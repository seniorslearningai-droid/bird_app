Yellow-headed Caracara (Daptrius chimachima)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium caracara with yellowish head and underparts,
brown back and wings, streaked face.
About 16 inches.

Behavior
--------
Opportunistic feeder; eats ticks off cattle and scavenges.
Very rare vagrant from Central
America to Florida and Texas.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, pastures and roadsides of Central and
South America. Very rare vagrant
to Florida and Texas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Crested Caracara darker with crest. Yellow-headed smaller
with yellow face and underparts.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow head and underparts, brown back. Small caracara.
Very rare Central American vagrant
to Florida and Texas.
