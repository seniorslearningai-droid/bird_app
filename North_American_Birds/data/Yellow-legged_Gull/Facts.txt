Yellow-legged Gull (Larus michahellis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large gull, pale gray back, bright yellow legs,
yellow bill with red spot. Similar to
Herring Gull. About 24 inches.

Behavior
--------
Mediterranean and Atlantic European species. Very rare
vagrant to northeastern North America.
Bold scavenger at coasts and harbors.

Habitat
-------
Mediterranean Sea, Atlantic coast of Europe, Azores,
Canary Islands. Very rare vagrant to
northeastern North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Herring Gull almost identical but has pink legs.
Yellow-legged Gull's yellow legs diagnostic.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow legs on large pale-backed gull. Very rare
Atlantic vagrant. Similar to Lesser
Black-backed but paler back.
