Yellow-rumped Warbler (Setophaga coronata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray warbler with yellow rump, yellow crown patch, and yellow shoulder patch; white or yellow throat depending on race.

Behavior
--------
Most abundant warbler; eats bayberries enabling winter residency farther north; often in large flocks.

Habitat
-------
Coniferous forests for breeding; widespread in migration; winters throughout southern US in shrubby areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Magnolia Warbler has yellow rump but different pattern; no other common warbler shows bold yellow rump in flight.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow rump patch most conspicuous; yellow crown and shoulder patches; Audubon's has yellow throat.
