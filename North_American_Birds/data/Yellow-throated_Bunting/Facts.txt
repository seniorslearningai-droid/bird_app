Yellow-throated Bunting (Emberiza elegans)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium bunting. Male has black crest and face, bright
yellow throat, white supercilium.
Female duller. About 6 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground in woodland. Very rare vagrant
to western Alaska from East
Asia.

Habitat
-------
Woodland and scrub of Korea, China, and Japan. Very
rare vagrant to western Alaska.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-browed Bunting lacks yellow throat. No other
bunting has black face with
yellow throat.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow throat with black face and crest.
Very rare Alaska vagrant from
East Asia.
