Yellow-throated Vireo (Vireo flavifrons)
===========================

Description
-----------
Stocky vireo with bright yellow spectacles, bright yellow throat and breast, two white wing bars, and gray rump.

Behavior
--------
Sings slow deliberate burry phrases; gleans caterpillars from canopy foliage; builds neat lichen-covered nest.

Habitat
-------
Mature deciduous forests and forest edges of eastern North America; riparian trees favored.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Yellow-throated Warbler has black and white face and different body shape; Pine Warbler lacks yellow spectacles.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Bright yellow spectacles; yellow throat and breast; gray back; two bold white wing bars; slow deliberate song.
