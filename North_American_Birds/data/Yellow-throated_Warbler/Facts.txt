Yellow-throated Warbler (Setophaga dominica)
===========================

Description
-----------
Gray-backed warbler with yellow throat, white supercilium, black face mask, and bold black streaked white breast.

Behavior
--------
Creeps along bark of tree branches like a nuthatch; probes Spanish moss and bark crevices for insects.

Habitat
-------
Mature forests with Spanish moss, sycamore and pine forests in southeastern US; year-round in Florida.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Grace's Warbler is western and has different face pattern; Yellow-throated Vireo has yellow spectacles.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Yellow throat with bold black and white face pattern; white supercilium; creeps along large branches; southeastern US.
