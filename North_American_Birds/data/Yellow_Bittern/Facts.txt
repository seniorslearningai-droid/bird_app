Yellow Bittern (Ixobrychus sinensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small bittern with yellow-buff body, dark brown wings,
black cap. Similar in shape to Least
Bittern. About 14 inches.

Behavior
--------
Stalks prey in reeds like other bitterns. Asian species;
very rare vagrant to Alaska and
Hawaiian Islands.

Habitat
-------
Reed marshes and wetlands of East Asia. Very rare
vagrant to Alaska; rare in
Hawaiian Islands.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Least Bittern similar shape but has rufous neck.
Yellow Bittern paler buff overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Small bittern with yellow-buff plumage and dark brown
wings. Asian vagrant to Alaska
and Hawaii.
