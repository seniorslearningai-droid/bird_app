Yellow Grosbeak (Pheucticus chrysopeplus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large grosbeak. Male brilliant yellow with black wings
and white wing patches. Female
streaked yellow. About 9.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages in trees for seeds and insects. Rare vagrant
to Arizona and New Mexico
from Mexico.

Habitat
-------
Riparian woodland and thorn forest of western Mexico.
Rare vagrant to Arizona
and New Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Evening Grosbeak smaller with different wing pattern.
American Goldfinch much smaller.
Brilliant yellow with black wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Brilliant yellow with black wings and white patches.
Massive grosbeak bill. Rare
Arizona vagrant from Mexico.
