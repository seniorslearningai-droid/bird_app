Yellow Rail (Coturnicops noveboracensis)
===========================

Description
-----------
Very small secretive rail with buffy-yellow streaked plumage, short stubby bill, and white wing patches in flight.

Behavior
--------
Extremely secretive; rarely flushes; white wing patches visible when flushed; clicks like stones knocked together.

Habitat
-------
Wet sedge meadows, shallow marshes, and grassy fields; very difficult to observe; breeds in northern prairies.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Sora is larger with gray breast and yellow bill; LeConte's Sparrow has similar habitat but is a sparrow.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
White secondaries visible when flushed; buffy yellow streaked plumage; tiny size; ticking call at night in marshes.
