Yellow Warbler (Setophaga petechia)
===========================

Description
-----------
Bright yellow warbler; male has reddish streaks on breast; females paler; yellow spots in tail; beady black eye.

Behavior
--------
Sings sweet-sweet-I'm-so-sweet; buries Cowbird eggs under new nest layers; common in willows near water.

Habitat
-------
Willow and alder thickets, shrubby streamside habitats, and gardens throughout North America.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Wilson's Warbler has black cap; Orange-crowned Warbler is olive-dull; Prothonotary Warbler has gray wings.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
All yellow with reddish breast streaks in male; yellow tail spots; black eye stands out; willow thicket habitat.
