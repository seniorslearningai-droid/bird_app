Yucatan Vireo (Vireo magister)
===========================

Description
-----------
Large plain vireo, brown-gray above, buffy-white
below with buffy flanks. Large bill,
pale eye. About 6.5 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages deliberately through dense scrub and forest.
Rare vagrant to Texas coast. Song
is a slow deliberate repetition.

Habitat
-------
Scrub forests and mangroves of Yucatan Peninsula.
Very rare vagrant to Texas
Gulf Coast.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Black-whiskered Vireo similar but has black whisker
mark. Red-eyed Vireo smaller
with different eye color.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Large vireo with brownish-gray tones and pale eye.
Buffy flanks. Very rare
Texas vagrant from Yucatan.
