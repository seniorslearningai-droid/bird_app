Zebra Dove (Geopelia striata)
===========================

Description
-----------
Small slender dove with brown upperparts, pale
pinkish-buff underparts finely barred with black
giving zebra pattern. Long tail. About 9 inches.

Behavior
--------
Forages on ground for seeds in pairs or small
groups. Tame and approachable. Introduced to Hawaii
from Southeast Asia; very common there.

Habitat
-------
Open areas, gardens, roadsides, and parks. Very
common on all Hawaiian Islands. Native to
Southeast Asian grasslands and cultivated areas.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Common Ground Dove similar small size. Zebra Dove
has longer tail and barred pattern.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Fine black barring on underparts giving zebra pattern.
Small size and very long tail. Tame
and common in Hawaiian urban areas.
