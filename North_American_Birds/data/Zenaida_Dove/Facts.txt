Zenaida Dove (Zenaida aurita)
===========================

Description
-----------
A medium-sized dove with warm brown plumage, iridescent neck patch,
white trailing edge on wings, and a rounded tail with white tips.

Behavior
--------
Walks on the ground to forage for seeds. Often tame around humans.
Produces a soft, mournful cooing call similar to the Mourning Dove.

Habitat
-------
Found in open woodlands, scrub, coastal areas, and gardens across
the Caribbean islands and parts of the Yucatan Peninsula.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Closely resembles the Mourning Dove but has a shorter, rounded tail
and white wing patches visible in flight. Stockier build overall.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Look for the white trailing edge on the secondary wing feathers and
the short rounded tail with white tips — key differences from Mourning Dove.
