Zino's Petrel (Pterodroma madeira)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium gadfly petrel, gray-brown above, white underparts,
gray breast band. Critically endangered.
About 13 inches.

Behavior
--------
Critically endangered; breeds only on Madeira. Very
rare in North Atlantic; extremely
unlikely vagrant to North America.

Habitat
-------
North Atlantic Ocean. Extremely rare vagrant. Breeds
only on central Madeira mountains;
critically endangered.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Fea's/Desertas Petrel very similar but larger. Zino's
smaller with smaller bill.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Smaller than Fea's/Desertas Petrel. Critically endangered
species breeding only on Madeira.
Virtually impossible vagrant.
