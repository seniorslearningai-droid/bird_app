Zone-tailed Hawk (Buteo albonotatus)
===========================

Description
-----------
Medium hawk resembling Turkey Vulture; black body with banded tail; two-toned wings held in dihedral; yellow cere.

Behavior
--------
Mimics Turkey Vulture in flight to approach prey undetected; preys on lizards and birds; vocal in canyons.

Habitat
-------
Desert canyons, mountain slopes, and riparian areas of southwestern US and Mexico.

Similar Looking Birds
---------------------
Turkey Vulture has naked red head and different proportions; Common Black Hawk is stouter with different tail bands.

Best Ways to Identify
---------------------
Black hawk with tail bands; holds wings in V-dihedral like Turkey Vulture; yellow cere; canyon habitat southwest.
